'''''Sometimes I Think About Dying''''' is a 2023 American [[romantic film|romantic]] [[comedy drama]] film directed by Rachel Lambert, and written by Kevin Armento, Stefanie Abel Horowitz, and Katy Wright-Mead. It is based on the 2014 play ''Killers'' by Armento, and a short film that was released in 2019, directed and co-written by Horowitz. The film stars [[Daisy Ridley]], [[Dave Merheje]], [[Parvesh Cheena]], Marcia DeBonis, [[Meg Stalter]], [[Brittany O'Grady]], and [[Bree Elrod]].

The film premiered at the [[2023 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 19, 2023, and was released on January 26, 2024.

==Premise==
Fran is a socially awkward office worker who spends most of her time in isolation and daydreams of her own death, until a new colleague pricks the bubble of her own isolation.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://festival.sundance.org/program/film/638a16eed406b2047df2c83e|title=Sometimes I Think About Dying|website=[[Sundance Film Festival]]}}</ref>

==Production==
In October 2021, it was reported that [[Daisy Ridley]] secretly shot an independent drama in [[Astoria, Oregon]];<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.dailyastorian.com/news/local/film-crew-shoots-independent-movie-in-astoria-featuring-star-wars-actress/article_3b5581c4-3349-11ec-a3e3-b3caf4bd38aa.html |title=Film crew shoots independent movie in Astoria featuring 'Star Wars' actress|website=The Astorian|date=October 22, 2021}}</ref> the project would later be announced in December 2021 in which Ridley also produced the film with Rachel Lambert directing.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2021/12/daisy-ridley-sometimes-i-think-about-dying-1234891282/|title=Daisy Ridley To Star And Produce Indie 'Sometimes I Think About Dying'|website=Deadline Hollywood|date=December 15, 2021 }}</ref> The screenplay by Kevin Armento is a partial adaptation of his own 2013 play ''Killers'', with Stefanie Abel Horowitz, who directed a 2019 short of the same name, also a screenwriter along with Katy Wright-Mead. All three had also been credited with screenwriting the 2019 short and Horowitz had also directed the 2013 play.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://collider.com/daisy-ridley-sometimes-i-think-about-dying/|title= Daisy Ridley to Star in, Produce Indie Drama 'Sometimes I Think About Dying'|website=Collider|date= December 15, 2021}}</ref>

The film is set in a small city on the coast of Oregon.<ref>{{Cite web |date=January 19, 2023 |title=Sometimes I Think About Dying' Review: Daisy Ridley Tries To Come Out Of Her Shell [Sundance] |url=https://theplaylist.net/sometimes-i-think-about-dying-review-daisy-ridley-tries-to-come-out-of-her-shell-sundance-20230119/ |website=Theplaylist.net}}</ref> Ridley told a crowd at the [[Sundance Film Festival]] that she related to Fran saying "Like, sometimes I feel like a piece of fucking shit. And sometimes I feel, like, great…What was interesting with Fran was sometimes she's sort of obnoxious. Sometimes, she's like, 'I don't want to play your games. I don't want to talk about food. I'm good, I'm apart from this.' And other times, she's like, 'How do I become part of this?' So I resonated with that."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.moviemaker.com/daisy-ridley-sometimes-i-think-about-dying/|title= Daisy Ridley Relates to Her Character in Sometimes I Think About Dying: 'Sometimes I Feel Like a Piece of F—ing Shit'|website=MovieMaker.com|date= January 20, 2023}}</ref>

==Release==
''Sometimes I Think About Dying'' premiered at the [[2023 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 19, 2023.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/2023/01/sometimes-i-think-about-dying-review-daisy-ridley-1234798010/|title= Sometimes I Think About Dying' Review: Daisy Ridley's Wondrous, Whimsical New Journey|website=Indiewire|date= January 20, 2023}}</ref> In April 2023, [[Oscilloscope (company)|Oscilloscope]] acquired the US distribution rights to the film.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2023/04/daisy-ridleys-sundance-sometimes-i-think-about-dying-oscilloscope-1235330607/|title=Daisy Ridley's Sundance Pic 'Sometimes I Think About Dying' Lands At Oscilloscope|website=Deadline Hollywood|first=Justin|last=Kroll|date=April 19, 2023|access-date=April 19, 2023}}</ref> The UK distribution rights were acquired by [[Vertigo Releasing]] in May 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Barraclough |first=Leo |date=2023-05-18 |title=Daisy Ridley's 'Sometimes I Think About Dying,' Which Premiered at Sundance, Sells to U.K.'s Vertigo (Exclusive) |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/global/daisy-ridley-sometimes-i-think-about-dying-sundance-vertigo-1235615859/ |access-date=2023-06-07 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> It was released in the United States on January 26, 2024,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Echebiri |first=Makuochi |date=2023-11-13 |title=Daisy Ridley-Led ''Sometimes I Think About Dying'' Gets Release Date & New Poster |url=https://collider.com/sometimes-i-think-about-dying-release-date-poster/ |access-date=2023-11-14 |website=[[Collider (website)|Collider]]}}</ref> and in the United Kingdom on April 19.<ref name="BBFC"/>

==Reception==
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|82|6.8|130|consensus=Departing from corporate comedy, ''Sometimes I Think About Dying'' modestly explores critical human connection through a superbly melancholic Daisy Ridley.|access-date=November 23, 2024|ref=y}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 68 out of 100, based on 26 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Sometimes I Think About Dying'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/sometimes-i-think-about-dying |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=November 23, 2024 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

[[Helen O'Hara (journalist)|Helen O'Hara]] in ''[[Empire (magazine)|Empire]]'' described a "beautiful, subdued Daisy Ridley performance" and wrote "if you’re tired of blockbuster bombast, this could be the antidote".<ref>{{cite web|first=Helen|last=O'Hara|website=Empire|url= https://www.empireonline.com/movies/reviews/sometimes-i-think-about-dying/|date=15 April 2024|accessdate=15 April 2024|title=Sometimes I Think About Dying review}}</ref> Peter Debruge in ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' said, "Movies tend to cut these bits out, to focus on the escapist stuff, but every once in a while one comes along, searching for poetry in the mundane." Ridley's character Fran's daydreams are shown as images, and Debruge noted that those "scenes are unexpected, surreal, accompanied by a lovely, meditative string score from composer Dabney Morris. Fran doesn't seem suicidal, but she isn't particularly engaged in life either. The character’s personality is so understated, it's strange to find someone of Ridley's stature drawn to such a self-effacing role".<ref>{{Cite web |date=January 19, 2023 |title=Sometimes I Think About Dying' Review: Daisy Ridley's Dreamy Sundance Indie Leaves Room to Think About Other Things |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/sometimes-i-think-about-dying-review-daisy-ridley-1235495211/ |website=Variety}}</ref> Lovia Gyarkye said in ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'', "Loneliness is the subject of a poetic exploration…Fran is too distinctively drawn to be just an avatar, but the impressions of her solitude are aching reminders of how modern life nurtures an unsettling separateness… ''Sometimes I Think About Dying'', then, is a graceful treatise on how challenging — but liberating — it can be to make connections."<ref>{{Cite web|last=Gyarkye|first=Lovia|access-date=January 20, 2023|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/sometimes-i-think-about-dying-review-daisy-ridley-1235304660/|title=Sometimes I Think About Dying' Review: Daisy Ridley Carries a Poetic Meditation on Isolation|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|date=January 20, 2023 }}</ref>
