{{Short description|1977 film by Marva Nabili}}
'''''The Sealed Soil''''' is a 1977 film by [[Marva Nabili]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmlinc.org/nyff2024/films/the-sealed-soil/|title=The Sealed Soil|website=Film at Lincoln Center}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.bam.org/film/2024/nyff-the-sealed-soil|title=The Sealed Soil|website=BAM.org}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nytimes.com/1978/06/09/archives/mideast-feminism-views-of-female-film-makers-feminist-films-trying.html|title=Mideast Feminism: Views of Female Film Makers (Published 1978)|date=June 9, 1978}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Erickson |first1=Steve |title=Film Reviews: New York Film Festival -- A Trio of Memorable Recent Restorations - The Arts Fuse |url=https://artsfuse.org/298986/film-reviews-new-york-film-festival-a-trio-of-memorable-recent-restorations/ |website=artsfuse.org |access-date=22 April 2025 |date=4 October 2024}}</ref>

The film traces the "passive revolt" of a young girl who refuses to marry,<ref name=":2">{{cite magazine|first=Paola |last=Raiman |title=Marva Nabili |magazine=[[Cahiers du cinéma]] |number=757 |date=July–August 2019 |issn= |url=https://www.cahiersducinema.com/produit/juillet-aout-2019-n757/ |pages=39 }}</ref> a transformation that can be seen as a metaphor for Iran's transition from tradition to modernity.<ref>{{cite magazine|language=en |first=Hamid |last=Naficy |title=Iranian Writers, the Iranian Cinema, and the Case of Dash Akol |magazine=Iranian Studies |date=1985 |url=https://www.scholars.northwestern.edu/en/publications/iranian-writers-the-iranian-cinema-and-the-case-of-dash-akol |accessdate=9 September 2019 |pages=231–251 }}</ref> She smuggled the original negative out of Iran and edited it at her university in New York.<ref name=":0">{{cite book|access-date=9 September 2019 |date=5 June 2018 |first1=Hamid |isbn=978-0-691-18621-4 |language=en |last1=Naficy |page=73 |publisher=[[Princeton University Press]] |title=An Accented Cinema: Exilic and Diasporic Filmmaking |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=2_5ZDwAAQBAJ&dq=Marva+Nabili&pg=PA73}}<!-- auto-translated from French by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> The film was shot without sound. Nabili later added dubbing and sound effects.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://nyunews.com/arts/film/2024/10/07/nyff-sealed-soil-review/ | title=NYFF 62 Review: Patriarchy, repression and routine in 'The Sealed Soil' | date=7 October 2024 }}</ref> ''The Sealed Soil'' met with international critical acclaim, notably winning an award at the London Film Festival in 1977.<ref>{{cite web |last=dos Santos |first=Júlia |date=18 August 2017 |title=The Sealed Soil: Meet the classic female-headed Iranian woman |url=https://londonfeministfilmfestival.files.wordpress.com/2016/12/delirium-nerd-cultura-e-nerdices-sob-um-viecc81s-feminista-english.pdf |access-date=10 September 2019 |work=Delirium Nerd}}</ref> The film is the second feature film made by a female director in Iran.<ref name=":2" /> The film was never shown in Iran and was finally officially released in the United States in 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/28/movies/the-sealed-soil-bam.html|title='The Sealed Soil': Modesty and Its Discontents|work=The New York Times |date=28 May 2025 |last1=Hoberman |first1=J. }}</ref>
