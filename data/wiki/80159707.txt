'''''There There''''' is a 2022 American romantic comedy film written and directed by [[Andrew Bujalski]] and starring [[Jason Schwartzman]], [[Lili Taylor]], [[Lennie James]], [[Molly Gordon]], Annie La Ganga and [[Avi Nash]].<ref name=slant/>

==Plot==
{{more plot|section|date=June 2026}}

A series of short scenes featuring two characters at a time, playing with our perceptions and expectations in a disorienting reality not far removed from our own. A lover's doubt in the harsh light of morning leads to a chain of awkward intimacies—confidants, disruptors, peacemakers, and instigators—each seeking a measure of faith rewarded.

== Production ==

=== Cinematography ===
The film was shot remotely during the COVID-19 pandemic using iPhone 12 Pro Max smartphones, with director [[Andrew Bujalski]] and cinematographer [[Matthias Grunsky]] never physically present on set. Each scene was filmed with actors in separate locations, often thousands of miles apart and weeks or months apart in time, with the two performers never meeting in person. While Bujalski directed the actors from his home in Texas, cinematographer Grunsky instructed the minimal on-location crew via Zoom from Munich, Germany, controlling exposure, focus, and color temperature through the Filmic Pro app.<ref name="Matthias Grunsky">{{cite web |url=https://matthias-grunsky.com/there-there.html |title=THERE THERE – Working Remotely on iPhones |author=Matthias Grunsky |access-date=2025-02-05}}</ref>

The production method reflected the film's thematic concerns with isolation and mediated communication. Grunsky embraced the iPhone's technical characteristics—including its deep depth of field, compression artifacts, and noise in low light—as part of the film's aesthetic language rather than attempting to hide the limitations of smartphone cinematography.<ref name="Matthias Grunsky"/>

This marked the seventh collaboration between Bujalski and Grunsky, continuing their pattern of experimental approaches to camera technology following ''[[Computer Chess (film)|Computer Chess]]'' (2013), which was shot on 1970s [[Video camera tube|video tube cameras]].

==Release==
In June 2022, it was announced that [[Magnolia Pictures]] acquired U.S. distribution rights to the film, which premiered at the 2022 [[Tribeca Film Festival]].<ref>{{cite web|last2=Lang|first2=Brent|last1=Keslassy|first1=Elsa|title=Magnolia Buys Andrew Bujalski's Tribeca Film 'There There' Starring Jason Schwartzman, Lili Taylor (EXCLUSIVE)|date=June 23, 2022|magazine=Variety|url=https://variety.com/2022/film/global/magnolia-andrew-bujalskis-tribeca-there-there-jason-schwartzman-1235301547/|accessdate=June 8, 2025|archive-date=June 8, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250608184723/https://variety.com/2022/film/global/magnolia-andrew-bujalskis-tribeca-there-there-jason-schwartzman-1235301547/|url-status=live}}</ref>  The film was then released in theaters and on demand on November 18, 2022.<ref>{{cite web|last=Tsai|first=Robert|title='There There' Review: Andrew Bujalski Leaps Backward to Mumblecore-Via-Zoom|date=November 17, 2022|website=TheWrap|url=https://www.thewrap.com/there-there-film-review-andrew-bujalski-jason-schwartzman-lili-taylor/|accessdate=June 8, 2025|archive-date=June 8, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250608185634/https://www.thewrap.com/there-there-film-review-andrew-bujalski-jason-schwartzman-lili-taylor/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Reception==
On the [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], 46% of 28 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 5.3/10. The website's consensus reads, "''There There''{{'}}s unique high concept might be noteworthy, but it isn't strong enough to carry this series of baggy vignettes."<ref>{{cite web|title=There There|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/there_there|accessdate=June 25, 2026|archive-date=February 6, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250206205430/https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/there_there|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 57 out of 100, based on 11 critics, indicating "mixed or average" reviews.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/there-there/|title=There There|website=[[Metacritic]]|accessdate=June 25, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251227233144/https://www.metacritic.com/movie/there-there/|archive-date=December 27, 2025|url-status=live}}</ref>
