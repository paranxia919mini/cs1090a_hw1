The '''76th annual [[Cannes Film Festival]]''' took place from 16 to 27 May 2023.<ref>{{cite web |title=Cannes 2023: accreditations are open! |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/infos-communiques/info/articles/cannes-2023-accreditations-are-open |website=[[Cannes Film Festival]] |access-date=24 February 2023 |date=3 February 2023 |archive-date=24 February 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230224041532/https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/infos-communiques/info/articles/cannes-2023-accreditations-are-open |url-status=live }}</ref> Swedish filmmaker [[Ruben Östlund]] served as jury president.<ref name="CannesJury"/> With the French film ''[[Anatomy of a Fall]]'' winning the [[Palme d'Or]], the festival's top prize,<ref name=":4" /> [[Justine Triet]] became the third female director to achieve the prize, after [[Jane Campion]] in [[1993 Cannes Film Festival|1993]], and [[Julia Ducournau]] in [[2021 Cannes Film Festival|2021]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Murray |first=Miranda |date=28 May 2023 |title=France's Triet becomes third female director to win Cannes' top prize |work=Reuters |url=https://www.reuters.com/lifestyle/wes-anderson-ken-loach-among-contenders-cannes-film-festivals-top-prize-2023-05-27/ |access-date=28 May 2023 |archive-date=28 May 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230528200129/https://www.reuters.com/lifestyle/wes-anderson-ken-loach-among-contenders-cannes-film-festivals-top-prize-2023-05-27/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

The official poster for the festival featuring actress [[Catherine Deneuve]] was created by Lionel Avignon and Stefan de Vivies of the design studio Hartland Villa from a photo taken by Jack Garofalo during the shooting of ''[[La Chamade (film)|La Chamade]]'' (1968). The poster was chosen to pay tribute to Deneuve for her contributions to film.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Goodfellow |first=Melanie |date=20 April 2023 |title=Cannes Film Festival Unveils 2023 Poster |url=https://deadline.com/2023/04/cannes-film-festival-2023-poster-catherine-deneuve-1235331733/ |access-date=19 April 2023 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |language=en-US |archive-date=19 April 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230419183106/https://deadline.com/2023/04/cannes-film-festival-2023-poster-catherine-deneuve-1235331733/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

During the festival, two [[Honorary Palme d'Or]] were awarded: the first one during the opening ceremony for [[Michael Douglas]], and the second one was awarded on short notice for [[Harrison Ford]] before the world premiere of ''[[Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny]]''.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=18 May 2023 |title='Indiana Jones' swings into Cannes Film Festival; Harrison Ford honored before joyous festivalgoers |url=https://apnews.com/article/indiana-jones-dial-destiny-cannes-premiere-c94df09d6299a181eb2d713cc1959746 |access-date=19 May 2023 |website=Associated Press |archive-date=19 May 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230519090552/https://apnews.com/article/indiana-jones-dial-destiny-cannes-premiere-c94df09d6299a181eb2d713cc1959746 |url-status=live }}</ref>
