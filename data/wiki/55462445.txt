'''''The Last Knight''''', also known as '''''The Last Warrior'''''<ref>{{cite news |last1=Kozlov |first1=Vladimir |title=Disney Resumes Local-Language Movie Production in Russia After 7-Year Break |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/disney-russia-local-language-movies-885954 |accessdate=July 30, 2018 |work=The Hollywood Reporter |date=April 20, 2016 |language=en}}</ref> ({{langx|ru|Последний богатырь|Poslyedniy bogatyr'|lit='''''The Last Bogatyr'''''}}) is a 2017 Russian-language [[Fantasy comedy|fantasy comedy film]] directed by [[Dmitriy Dyachenko]].<ref name=thr>{{cite news |last1=Vladimir |first1=Kozlov |title=Russia Box Office: Disney Film Becomes Top Local-Language Release of All Time |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/russia-box-office-disney-film-becomes-top-local-language-release-all-time-1061467 |accessdate=July 30, 2018 |work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |date=November 27, 2017 |language=en}}</ref> The story develops around [[Baba Yaga]] and [[Koschei]], both villains in traditional [[Russian fairy tale]]s (as well as the fairy tales of other [[Slavic countries]]). The film was produced by the American film companies [[The Walt Disney Company CIS]] together with the Russian film companies [[Yellow, Black and White]]<ref name=thr/> and Kinoslovo.<ref name="kms">{{cite news |title=Disney in the tale hit |url=https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/2967461 |accessdate=July 30, 2018 |work=Kommersant |date=19 April 2016 |pages=1}} [https://translate.google.com/translate?sl=ru&tl=en&js=y&prev=_t&hl=en&ie=UTF-8&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.kommersant.ru%2Fdoc%2F2967461&edit-text= Translation].</ref> The film stars [[Viktor Khorinyak]], [[Mila Sivatskaya]], [[Elena Yakovleva]], [[Ekaterina Vilkova]], [[Konstantin Lavronenko]] and Yevgeny Dyatlov.

It was scheduled to have a wide release on October 26, 2017. As of November 26, 2017, the film became the highest-grossing local-language release of all time in Russia, surpassing ''Stalingrad'' with 1.68 billion rubles ($28.8 million)<ref name=thr/> and topping out at $30.5 million. In Russia, the film holds the 12th place biggest [[Box-office bomb|box-office]] audiences.<ref name="thr0">{{cite news |last1=Kozlov |first1=Vladimir |title=Disney to Make Sequel to 'Last Knight,' Russia's Top-Grossing Local-Language Film |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/disney-make-sequel-last-knight-russias-top-grossing-local-language-film-1086464 |accessdate=July 30, 2018 |work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |date=February 20, 2018 |language=en}}</ref> This was Disney Russia's second film produced specifically for the Russian market, with the first being ''[[The Book of Masters]]'' (2009).<ref name=thr/>

A sequel, titled ''[[The Last Warrior: Root of Evil]]'', was released in January 2021 and a third film, ''[[The Last Warrior: A Messenger of Darkness]]'', was released in December 2021.

==Plot==
In Belogorie, a parallel version of [[Ancient Russia]], Sorceress Varvara hunts down [[Alyosha Popovich]] whom she believes to be the last [[Bogatyr]] (Hero or Warrior). She [[Petrification|petrifies]] and throws him into the sea, where hundreds of other victims rest.

In modern [[Moscow]], Ivan Naydenov is a young orphan who makes his way in life by being a con artist. He poses as a "white mage", stars in rigged psychic contest shows, and performs "rites" for clients. He starts having doubts about his life choices, however, when asked to cure a [[coma]]tose boy.

One day, during an escape from a client's husband, Ivan miraculously ends up in Belogorie. This magical world is inhabited by [[Russian fairy tale]]s characters and creatures. A [[volkhv]] appears and claims that Ivan is the lost son of [[Ilya Muromets]], hidden on Earth many years ago.

Ivan is then captured by Varvara's warriors and brought to prince [[Dobrynya Nikitich]]. Dobrynya is friendly, but princess Varvara imprisons Ivan in her dungeon without her husband's knowledge. There, Ivan meets a disembodied [[Koschei]], who tells him the magical [[Sword Kladenets]] is his only way back to Earth. Koschei himself had used Kladenets to become immortal, but is overthrown and imprisoned by Bogatyrs. After the forces of good had won, most of the magical creatures were imprisoned or killed, then the Bogatyrs themselves suffered the same fate. If Ivan truly is Ilya's son, he can uncover the Sword and use it to restore power to the surviving magical creatures, and to return home.

[[Baba Yaga]] is captured and thrown into the same dungeon. However, this was premeditated: she brought [[Vasilisa the Wise]] in frog form, who easily releases the prisoners. Koschei, Baba Yaga and Vasilisa decide to take Ivan with them to find the Sword. Escaping the dungeon, they enlist [[Vodyanoy]] the Merman. Koschei tells Ivan of Vasilisa's backstory: after she caught the eye of Dobrynya, the jealous Varvara turned everyone in her village into frogs. Baba Yaga could alleviate the curse by helping her to occasionally turn back into human form. Despite bonding with Vasilisa, Ivan still wishes to get back to his own world.

Varvara eventually captures the protagonist, petrifies Vodyanoy and forces Ivan to get the Sword for her, holding his companions hostage. Ivan recovers the Sword and overpowers his foes, but Koshei turns out to be in league with Varvara. Moreover, Dobrynya turns out to be the mastermind, planning to use the Sword to become immortal like Koschei. Reasoning that he no longer needs his help, Dobrynya betrays Koschei, and Ivan uses the Sword to escape to Earth.

Back in Moscow, Ivan realizes he cannot abandon his beloved Vasilisa, and decides to get back. He finds the volkhv and returns to Belogorie. After setting free [[Zmey Gorynych]] he uses a modern handgun to fight his way to Dobrynya. However, the latter has already become immortal and prepares to execute Vasilisa and Baba Yaga. Using Gorynych as a distraction, Ivan challenges Dobrynya to a duel, hoping to give his companions time to destroy the magical stone and undo the Immortals' invulnerability. During the fight, Dobrynya reveals that it was his envy of Ilya that led to betrayal of Bogatyrs.

Koschei eventually gets his hands on the magical stone. Touched by the selfless heroism of his companion, he decides to shatter it, killing himself and Dobrynya. Varvara flees Belogorie, and her petrified victims come back to life. Among them was Ilya, who reunites with Ivan. Rather than return to the life of a fake "mage", Ivan stays in Belogorie and marries Vasilisa. He briefly comes to Earth to heal the comatose boy.

A mid-credit scene shows Varvara on Earth, meeting her mother Galina, Ivan's housekeeper. The two had planned to enthrall Dobrynya and rule Belogorye. That plan have failed, they will now have to make a different move.

==Production==
=== Development ===
In May 2016, production began in Moscow and other locations. The premier was set for October 19, 2017, on the eve of school holidays with potential for being released in the [[IMAX]] format. The production companies applied to the [[Cinema Foundation of Russia|Cinema Foundation]] for state support.<ref name="kms" /> Filming took place in [[Moscow]] and near [[Sochi]].<ref>[https://www.kinoafisha.info/movies/8326460/ The Last Warrior, 2017]</ref> As of November 26, 2017, the film became the highest-grossing local-language release of all time in Russia, surpassing ''Stalingrad'' with 1.68 billion rubles ($28.8 million)<ref name=thr/>

===Casting===
The main character of the film, Ivan Naydenov, performed by Viktor Khorinyak, was conceived as a more serious hero, although there was also something left of the character of Kostya Anisimov from the TV series ''[[Kitchen (TV series)|Kitchen]]'', for example, the ability to quickly find a way out of a difficult situation.

=== Filming ===
[[Principal photography]] took place in [[Moscow]] and near [[Sochi]],<ref>{{cite web |url=https://tvkultura.ru/article/show/article_id/155153/ |title=Съемки сказочного фильма "Последний богатырь" проходят в Москве |language=Russian |trans-title=The filming of the fairy tale film ''The Last Warrior'' takes place in Moscow |date=October 8, 2016 |work=[[Russia-K]] |access-date=June 17, 2020 |archive-date=October 30, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191030012534/https://tvkultura.ru/article/show/article_id/155153/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> and basically all the scenes were shot in real scenery, and not on a [[chroma key]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.mirf.ru/kino/dmitry-dyachenko-poslednij-bogatyr-interview/ |title=Режиссёр "Последнего богатыря": "Наша сказка достаточно суровая" |language=Russian |trans-title=Director of ''The Last Warrior'': "Our tale is quite severe" |last=Serebryanskiy |first=Sergey |date=October 25, 2017 |work=[[Mir Fantastiki]]}}</ref> Most of the filming took place in the [[Republic of Abkhazia]]. Abkhazian production company Alexandra Basariya (Sandro) Abkhaz-Production LLC hospitably received the film crew and beloved Russian actors. The shooting took place in such cities and areas as: the road to [[Lake Ritsa]], Auadhara [[Ritsa Strict Nature Reserve|Nature Reserve]], Alpine Meadows, the village of [[Otapi]] cave in [[Ochamchira District|Ochamchyrsky District]], the town of [[Pitsunda]], [[Gagra]] and [[Gudauta]].

==Release==
The film is scheduled to be released in the [[Russian Federation]] on October 29, 2017, by [[Sony Pictures Releasing#International arrangements|Walt Disney Studios Sony Pictures Releasing]] (WDSSPR). The television premiere took place on January 1, 2018, on the [[Russia-1]] TV channel.

==Reception==
===Budget===
The exact budget for the movie is unknown but it's estimated to be somewhere between $6<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20171027170722/http://kinodata.pro/vse-o-kino/novosti-rus-kino/22835-pervonachalnyj-byudzhet-skazki-pervyj-bogatyr-uzhe-prevyshen.html The original budget of the fairy tale "The Last Warrior" has already been exceeded] - Kinodata, 01.10.2016 {{in lang|ru}}</ref> and $8 million.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20171027170808/http://www.kinomania.ru/film/798159 The Last Warrior] - Kinomania, as of 10/27/2017 {{in lang|ru}}</ref> In 2017 production company Yellow, Black and White (YBW) revealed that the overall production and promotion budget was 370 million Russian Rubles (~$8 million), equally distributed between Disney and YBW. The film is partially sponsored by Russian government through [[Cinema Foundation of Russia|Cinema Fund]], which gave 100 million Russian Rubles to YBW, although half of that sum was returned to state-owned [[Cinema Foundation of Russia|Cinema Fund]] thanks to the movie's commercial success.<ref name="vedomosti">[https://web.archive.org/web/20191013004119/https://www.vedomosti.ru/technology/articles/2017/11/09/741035-disney-zarabativat-na-rossiiskom-kino Disney начала зарабатывать на российском кино]. vedomosti.ru, Nonmember 9, 2017 {{in lang|ru}}</ref>

===Box office===
''The Last Knight'' in less than two weeks of rental broke the bar of 1 billion [[ruble]]s and became the highest-grossing domestic film of 2017, according to the Unified Automated Information System (UAIS) (this record was broken after a short time by the sports drama [[Going Vertical|''Going Vertical'' (2017 film)]], which earned at the box office 3 billion rubles).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.igromania.ru/news/72240/Posledniy_bogatyr_stal_samym_kassovym_rossiyskim_filmom.html |title="Последний богатырь" стал самым кассовым российским фильмом |language=Russian |trans-title=''The Last Knight'' became the highest grossing Russian film |last=Kozhevnikov |first=Yevgeniy |date=November 27, 2018 |work=[[Igromania]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kino-teatr.ru/kino/news/y2017/11-5/13431/ |title="Последний богатырь" стал миллиардером |language=Russian |trans-title=''The Last Knight'' became a billionaire |date=November 5, 2017 |work=Kino-Teatr.Ru}}</ref> In total, ''The Last Knight'' collected more than 1.7 billion rubles at the box office, having recovered its budget in 11 days.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.forbes.ru/karera-i-svoy-biznes/392075-zolotoy-holop-kak-sozdateli-glavnoy-komedii-goda-zarabotali-v-prokate |title=Золотой "Холоп": как создатели главной комедии года заработали в прокате под 3 млрд рублей |language=Russian |trans-title=Golden [[Serf (film)|''Serf'' (2019 film)]]: how the creators of the main comedy of the year earned at the box office under 3 billion rubles |last=Melnikova |first=Kseniya |date=January 29, 2020 |work=Forbes (Russia) |accessdate=June 17, 2020}}</ref>

The reason for success is found both in a positive [[Word-of-mouth marketing|word of mouth]], and in the active support of distributors.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.kinopoisk.ru/media/article/3099228/ |title=Как последний стал первым: Причины кассового успеха "Последнего богатыря" |language=Russian |trans-title=How the latter became the first: Reasons for the box office success of ''The Last Knight'' |last=Kornatskiy |first=Nikolay |date=January 7, 2018 |work=[[KinoPoisk]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.igromania.ru/news/73494/Film_Posledniy_bogatyr_stal_samym_kassovym_v_Rossii_za_2017_god.html |title=Фильм "Последний богатырь" стал самым кассовым в России за 2017 год |language=Russian |trans-title=The film ''The Last Knight'' became the highest grossing in Russia in 2017 |last=Surepin |first=Sergey |date=February 7, 2018 |work=[[Igromania]]}}</ref>

===Critical response===
Critics' opinions about the film were divided, it received conflicting ratings - but mostly average, neutral. According to the "Criticism" aggregator, ''The Last Knight'' received an average rating of 67/100 in the press (based on 29 reviews).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://kritikanstvo.ru/movies/poslednijbogatyir/ |title=Рецензии на фильм Последний богатырь (2017), отзывы |language=Russian |trans-title=Reviews on the movie ''The Last Knight'' (2017), reviews |work=Kritikanstvo.Ru}}</ref> [[Igromania]] magazine stated that the film is straightforward and that the reason why the villains of fairy tales such as Koschey or Baba Yaga went over to the good side was not explained in the film, but he praised the film for good humor.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.igromania.ru/news/71467/Russkiy_folklor_dobrye_zlodei_i_yumor_Kuhni_chem_udivit_Posledniy_bogatyr.html |title=Русский фольклор, добрые злодеи и юмор "Кухни": чем удивит "Последний богатырь"? |language=Russian |trans-title=Russian folklore, good villains and the humor of ''[[Kitchen (TV series)|Kitchen]]'': what will surprise ''The Last Knight''? |last=Kozhevnikov |first=Yevgeniy |date=October 20, 2017 |work=[[Igromania]]}}</ref> Magazine "[[Mir Fantastiki]]" gave the film six out of ten stars and stated that the film could not escape the cliche with the main character, which could be ridiculed in ''[[Shrek]]'' fifteen years ago, because of which the film suffers from a lack of originality, and also stated that the reason why the villains save the world of fairy tales was not disclosed in the film. However, the magazine also praised the film for humor.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.mirf.ru/kino/poslednij-bogatyr/ |title=Последний богатырь: русская сказка от Disney |language=Russian |trans-title=''The Last Knight'': Russian fairy tale from Disney |last=Shepelyov |first=Dmitriy |date=October 17, 2017 |work=[[Mir Fantastiki]]}}</ref>

According to the results of a survey organized by the [[Kinopoisk]] website on the [[VKontakte]] social network, users called ''The Last Knight'' the best Russian film of 2017.<ref name=Gazeta>{{cite web |url=https://www.kinopoisk.ru/media/news/3102799/ |title=Пользователи "ВКонтакте" выбрали лучшие фильмы 2017-го вместе с КиноПоиском |language=Russian |trans-title=VKontakte users have chosen the best films of 2017 along with KinoPoisk |date=January 9, 2018 |work=news on KinoPoisk}}</ref>
