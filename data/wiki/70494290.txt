'''''Road to Boston'''''  ({{Korean|hangul=1947 보스톤|lit='''''1947 Boston'''''}}) is a 2023 South Korean [[Biographical film|biographical]] [[sports film]] written and directed by [[Kang Je-gyu]] starring [[Ha Jung-woo]], [[Yim Si-wan]], [[Bae Seong-woo]] and [[Kim Sang-ho (actor)|Kim Sang-ho]]. It tells the story of Korean athletes who participated in the [[Boston Marathon|Boston International Marathon]] in 1947, the first international marathon held since World War II. It was released theatrically on September 27, 2023, coinciding with [[Chuseok|Korea's Chuseok holiday]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Han Hyeon-jeong |date=January 17, 2023 |script-title=ko:하정우·임시완·배성우 '1947 보스톤', 9월 추석 개봉[공식] |trans-title=Ha Jung-woo, Lim Si-wan, and Bae Sung-woo's '1947 Boston' to be released on Chuseok in September [Official] |url=https://entertain.naver.com/now/read?oid=009&aid=0005075630 |access-date=January 17, 2023 |website=Maeil Business |language=ko |archive-date=January 17, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230117000050/https://entertain.naver.com/now/read?oid=009&aid=0005075630 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Jung Hee-yeon |date=August 14, 2023 |title='1947 보스톤' 9월 27일 추석 개봉 확정 [공식] |trans-title='1947 Boston' to be released on Chuseok on September 27th [Official] |url=https://entertain.naver.com/read?oid=382&aid=0001068932 |access-date=August 14, 2023 |publisher=Sports Donga |language=ko |via=[[Naver]]}}</ref>

== Plot ==
In the [[1936 Berlin Olympics]], marathon gold medalist [[Sohn Kee-chung]] set a world record. On the podium, as Japan's national anthem "[[Kimigayo]]" played, he covered the [[Japanese flag]] on his uniform with a potted plant. Overnight, he became a national hero to Koreans, but under Japanese colonial rule, he was forbidden to run again.

In 1947, post-liberation [[Seoul]], rising star [[Suh Yun-bok]] is seen as the second Sohn Kee-chung. Suh is suddenly approached by the legendary runner himself, who suggests they compete in the [[Boston Marathon]]. Sohn wants to reclaim the glory lost in Berlin, this time running proudly under the Korean flag. Though they couldn't even afford a single pair of running shoes, the Korean marathoners embark on an unforgettable journey to [[Boston]].
