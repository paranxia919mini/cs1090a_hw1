'''''Vagabond''''' ({{langx|fr|Sans toit ni loi}}, literally "without roof or law") is a 1985 French [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]] written and directed by [[Agnès Varda]] and starring [[Sandrine Bonnaire]]. Beginning with the discovery of a young female [[vagabond (person)|vagabond]] in a ditch, the film tells—via [[Flashback (narrative)|flashbacks]]—the story of her last winter, which she spent wandering around the [[Languedoc-Roussillon wine]] country.

The film premiered at the [[42nd Venice International Film Festival]], where it won the [[Golden Lion]], and was nominated for four [[César Awards]], with Bonnaire winning [[César Award for Best Actress|Best Actress]]. It was the 36th highest-grossing film of the year in France, with a total of 1,080,143 admissions.<ref name="JPBox Office">{{cite web|url=http://www.jpbox-office.com/fichfilm.php?id=6419&affich=france|title=Sans toît ni loi (1985)- JPBox-Office|last=JP|website=www.jpbox-office.com}}</ref>

==Plot==
On a cold winter morning in the vineyards of a small village in the [[Gard|Gard region]] of France, the contorted body of a young woman, Mona Bergeron, is discovered in a ditch. The local [[National Gendarmerie|gendarmes]] quickly determine she is a vagrant who froze to death, and she is buried in a [[potter's field]].

Mona's last winter is revealed through flashbacks framed by interviews with those who crossed paths with her. She wanders the country alone, hitchhiking, sleeping in a tent, and doing odd jobs to survive. She endures hunger, thirst, cold, and a scarcity of tobacco and [[Cannabis (drug)|cannabis]]. On her journey, Mona meets a housekeeper, a family of goat farmers, a professor specialising in [[Platanus|plane tree]]s, a [[Tunisians|Tunisian]] vineyard worker, and a group of homeless youths who spend their time drinking, doing drugs, and committing petty crimes at a railway station. She tells one companion that she left her life as a secretary in Paris to seek freedom and life without responsibility.

Mona's boots fall apart, and after she loses her tent and sleeping bag in a fire at a [[Squatting|squat]] in [[Nîmes]], she is left with only a blanket for warmth. She stumbles across a bizarre harvest festival, where she is daubed with wine dregs by men in strange costumes. After escaping, she wanders into a vineyard, where she falls into a ditch. Tired, cold, wet, and now injured, she does not get up, and succumbs to the elements.

==Title==
The film's original French title, ''Sans toit ni loi'' ("without roof or law"), is [[word play]] based on a common French idiom, "sans foi ni loi" ("with neither faith nor law"). It also a pun on ''sans toi'' ("without you").

==Style==
''Vagabond'' combines straightforward narrative scenes, in which we see Mona living her life, with [[pseudo-documentary]] sequences in which people who knew Mona turn to the camera and say what they remember about her. Significant events are sometimes left unshown, so that the viewer must piece information together to gain the full picture. It was filmed in the departments of [[Gard]], [[Hérault]], and [[Bouches-du-Rhône]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0089960/locations|title=Vagabond (1985) - IMDb|via=www.imdb.com}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The film was acclaimed by critics. [[Roger Ebert]] gave it four stars out of four, writing: "like so many of the greatest films, it tells us a very specific story, strong and unadorned, about a very particular person".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ebert |first=Roger |date=August 1, 1986 |title=Vagabond |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/vagabond-1986}}</ref> On the [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], 100% of 26 critics' reviews of ''Vagabond'' are positive.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/vagabond/|title=Sans Toit ni Loi (Vagabond) (Without Roof or Rule)|website=www.rottentomatoes.com|access-date=December 14, 2025}}</ref>
