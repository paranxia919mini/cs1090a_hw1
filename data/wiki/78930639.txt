'''''Hysteria''''' is a 2025 German [[thriller drama film]] written and directed by Mehmet Akif Büyükatalay. Starring [[Devrim Lingnau]], the film story follows a film shoot as it takes a dark turn when the burning of a Quran throws the crew into turmoil.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/film/458077/ |title= Hysteria |author= Mehmet Akif Büyükatalay |date=16 January 2025|access-date=20 January 2025 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref><ref name="ten:crew">{{Cite web|url=https://www.crew-united.com/en/Hysteria__286910.html |title=Hysteria, Feature Film, 2022-2024, Drama, Thriller, Germany |website= Crew United |date= 5 December 2024|access-date=20 January 2025|language=en}}</ref>

The film was selected in the [[Berlin International Film Festival#Festival programme|Panorama]] section of the Berlinale and had its premiere at the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]] on 15 February 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/472376 |title= Diverse perspectives and limitless creativity abound in the Berlinale Panorama|author= Vassilis Economou |date=16 January 2025|access-date=20 January 2025 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.plutofilm.de/news/hysteria-premieres-at-berlinale/0143 |title= Hysteria Premieres at Berlinalei |first= Mehmet|last= Akif Büyükatalay |date=16 January 2025|access-date=20 January 2025 |website= Pluto Film Distribution Network |language=en}}</ref>

==Synopsis==

The film is set amidst the chaotic environment of a film production on a film about the racist attacks in Germany of the 1990s. The plot thickens when a [[Quran]] is burned on set, and all evidence of the incident vanishes. The assistant director finds herself entangled in a complex situation, attempting to uncover the truth. Various individuals involved in the production, including the film's producer, director, and several actors from a refugee home, each have their own version of events.

==Production==

Principal photography began on 15 December 2022 on locations in [[Cologne|Köln]], [[Rüsselsheim]] in Germany,<ref name="ten:crew"/> and wrapped up filming on 12 April 2023 in  Rüsselheim, Cologne and [[Hürth]], cities located in the regions of Hesse and [[North Rhine-Westphalia]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/448699/ |title= Mehmet Akif Büyükatalay wraps production on his second feature, Hysteria|first= Teresa |last= Vena |date=31 August 2023|access-date=20 January 2025 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Release==

''Hysteria'' had its World premiere in the Panorama section of the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]] on 15 February 2025.<ref name="pan:sd">{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/berlinale-reveals-special-panorama-generation-and-forum-titles/5200856.article |title=Berlinale reveals Special, Panorama, Generation and Forum titles |first= Ben |last= Dalton |date=16 January 2025 |access-date=20 January 2025 |website=[[ScreenDaily]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.berlinale.de/en/2025/programme/202516943.html |title= Hysteria |work=Berlinale |date=4 February 2025 |access-date=15 February 2025}}</ref>

On 5 October 2025, it was presented in Panorama section of [[2025 Vancouver International Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://viff.org/series/panorama/ |title= Panorama|date=27 August 2025|access-date=28 August 2025|website= [[Vancouver International Film Festival]]}}</ref>

==Reception==
On [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 93% based on 14 reviews, with an average rating of 6.6/10.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/hysteria_2025|title=Hysteria|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|access-date=23 June 2026}}</ref>
