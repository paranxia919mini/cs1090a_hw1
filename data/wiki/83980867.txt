'''''Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island''''' ({{Langx|ja|映画ちいかわ 人魚の島のひみつ}}) is a 2026 [[Japan]]ese [[Anime|animated]] film directed by Kei Oikawa, based on the [[manga]] series ''[[Chiikawa]]'' by [[Nagano (illustrator)|Nagano]]. It is the first film in the ''Chiikawa'' merchandise line. It was released in Japanese theatres by [[Toho]] on 24 July 2026.

Once upon a time, [[Siren (mythology)|Siren]], a [[legendary creature]] whose song could work magic for the plants, came to an island with three [[Mermaid#Japanese folklore|mermaids]]. The islanders offered tributes of food to the Siren, and she rewarded them with harvests by singing to crops help them grow. One day, the Siren was playing with the mermaids and unknowingly destroyed a boat with two islanders, Hitoha and Futaba on board. They had previously know that [[Ningyo#Yao Bikuni|anyone who ate a mermaid could gain eternal life]].

Futaba was badly injured, leading Hitoha to kill a mermaid and cook it at home. Futaba was revived and healed by gaining eternal life after being fed a piece of mermaid by Hitoha, but they were horrified at the thought of immortality with no one else around and made Hitoha eat it as well. Hitoha and Futaba then discovered a [[battery holder|battery slot]] on their bottoms. They quickly understood the meaning of "eternal life" – when their "life" ran out, they could be "revived" by replacing the [[Electric battery|battery]]. They promised to keep the secret forever.

Meanwhile, furious at the death of one of her mermaids, Siren abducted and ate the islanders until the culprit was found. None of the other islanders knew about the disappearance of the mermaid or the reason for Siren's aggression. As a result, the islanders forbade anyone from contacting Siren and invited visitors from outside to help suppress Siren. In response, Siren also attacked visitors who tried to suppress her.

Usagi, one of Chiikawa's friends, receives an invitation letter to the island, which offered various treasures – including meals. Many of the residents ([[Chiikawa#Characters|recurrent characters of the series]]: Chiikawa, Hachiware, Usagi, Momonga, Furuhonya, and Rakko) then follow to visit the island. There, they enjoy the meals and celebrate. At night, Chiikawa, Hachiware and Usagi accidentally enter the Siren's cave and learn about what happened to them.

The next day, an islander announces the mission to suppress Siren and find missing villagers, while most promises are lies, scaring off visitors and leaving only Chiikawa and friends to defend. Siren then attacks the village and visitors, and Chiikawa is the only one who manages to escape. Chiikawa encounters Shimajirō, the owner of a restaurant in a remote area who makes [[clam soup]] and spicy [[Japanese curry|curry]] rice. Chiikawa, with Shimajirō's help, frees all the Siren's captives, including the two villagers who had previously disappeared. Shimajirō holds off the Siren, allowing everyone to escape. Convinced Siren would attack again, Rakko tries to conjure up a plan. Chiikawa, who ate Shimajirō's curry rice, suggests making a very spicy curry rice to disable Siren's singing attack. 

Siren appears the next day. Chiikawa and friends force the spicy curry rice they prepared into her mouth, but Siren enjoys the spiciness and unaffected by the curry. Then, Shimajirō protects others from Siren's attack and offers the [[Carolina Reaper]], saying that it was the only [[Chili pepper|pepper]] that would be overly spicy for her. After forcing the Carolina Reaper-laced curry into her mouth, Siren begins writhing in pain and hits Hachiware and Chiikawa with her tail. Futaba protects them, though the Siren knocks away her battery cover. Noticing the battery cover, Siren acknowledges Hachiware's plea to stop attacking the islanders and retreats to the waters to ease the spiciness in her mouth.

While nursing Futaba in their home, Chiikawa witnesses Hitoha replacing Futaba's battery and combined with the knowledge of the mermaid scale in their home before, Chiikawa figures out Hitoha and Futaba were the ones who killed and ate the mermaid. At the celebration of the Siren's defeat, Chiikawa tries to talk to Hitoha and Futaba, but decides to keep the secret and returns to enjoy the party. The next day, Chiikawa and friends depart the island with gifts and cheers from the islanders. On the ship, Usagi sees and takes a mermaid scale from Hitoha's home in Chiikawa's pochette. As the ship moves, the scale drifts back towards the island by wind from Chiikawa's hand. Seeing the scale flying off, Chiikawa starts chatting with friends.

In the [[post-credits scene]], Hitoha and Futaba are revealed to have fled the island; they arrive on a new island and decide to reside in a cave. In the nearby ocean, Siren and her two surviving mermaids swim towards the island, closing in on the two immortal fugitives.

== Production ==
The film is based on the manga's "Siren Arc" ({{Langx|ja|セイレーン編}}) or "Island Arc", which was serialised from March to November 2023. The screenplay was written by [[Nagano (illustrator)|Nagano]], credited as the original story. Under Nagano's supervision, Kei Oikawa of [[Cygames Pictures]] was selected as the director. Production companies QTORY, [[Cypic]], [[Fuji Television|Fuji Animation Studio]] and [[Toho]] are the film's distributors.<ref name="ann-encyclopedia" /><ref name="Hazra-2026">{{cite news |last1=Hazra |first1=Adriana |title=Chiikawa Anime Film Opens at #1, 5th Live-Action Kingdom Film Drops to #3 |url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-07-30/chiikawa-anime-film-opens-at-no.1-5th-live-action-kingdom-film-drops-to-no.3/.240044 |access-date=15 August 2026|work=Anime News Network |date=2026-08-14 }}</ref>

The opening and ending theme songs, respectively titled {{Nihongo|"Kutsuzure"|くつずれ|{{lit}} "Shoe Sore"}}, performed by Makoto Tanaka,<ref name="Hazra-2026" />  {{Nihongo|"Tsukue Sasuru"|机さする|{{lit}} "Desk Rubbing"}}, performed by Haruka Aoki, are both written by Nagano.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-08-02|title=『映画ちいかわ』エンディング曲「机さする」から"その後"を考察 隠された"いつか"の意味 |url=https://realsound.jp/movie/2026/08/post-2477841.html |access-date=23 August 2026|website=Real Sound｜リアルサウンド 映画部 |language=ja}}</ref>

Siren's visual design is based on an ''[[wiktionary:inu hariko|inu hariko]]'' ([[papier-mâché]] dog) for the upper body and a [[goldfish]] for the lower body.<ref>{{Cite web|date=17 August 2026|title=OFFISIAL INTERVIEW 原作・脚本:ナガノ|url=https://chiikawa.toho-movie.jp/interview/nagano.html|access-date=4 September 2026|website=「映画ちいかわ 人魚の島の秘密」公式サイト|language=ja}}</ref> The ''inu hariko'' is a [[talisman]] believed to have originated in [[Nagoya]]'s [[Atsuta Shrine]], and is used to pray for [[fertility]] and safe childbirth, while the goldfish is a speciality product of [[Yatomi]], [[Aichi Prefecture]]; This work features other elements evocative of Aichi, such as [[soy sauce]] and [[miso]]; the local preference for strongly flavoured dishes; curry reminiscent of [[Ichibanya|CoCo Ichibanya]], a company based in Aichi; [[Ruditapes philippinarum|Japanese manila clam]]s, a seafood product for which Aichi boasts the highest catch volume in Japan; [[Kasugai, Aichi|Kasugai City]] in Aichi is said to be the birthplace of {{Ill|Yao Bikuni|jp|八百比丘尼}}, who ate mermaid meat; and {{Ill|Shiruko Sand|jp|しるこサンド}} used as [[bait]] to lure out the mermaid.

"[[AA battery]]" in [[Japanese language|Japanese]] is usually [[Clipping (morphology)|shortened]] as {{Nihongo||単三|tansan}}, which is a [[homophone]] of the short name for [[carbonated beverage]]s {{Nihongo||炭酸|tansan}}; It is also a soundalike of {{Nihongo|[[minor third]]|短三度|Tan-Sando}}, the [[music interval]] sung by a slain mermaid.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2026-08-06|title=「こんな悲しいキャッチコピーある？」　ちいかわの"単三電池"にネット騒然　「ひ…人の心ない……」「闇の深いグッズで震える」「いやああああああああああ」|url=https://nlab.itmedia.co.jp/research/articles/3961732/|access-date=6 September 2026|website=[[ITmedia|ねとらぼ]]|language=ja}}</ref>

The promotional song for the food stall that the islanders had been singing ever since the Siren arrived contains an [[acrostic]] message {{Nihongo|"We apologize—please help us."|おわびじゃ たすけて|Owabija. Tasukete}}.

The list of [[Okinawa Prefecture|Okinawa]] topics performed by Shisa: [[Kōrēgusu]] is a kind of [[Hot sauce|chili sauce]] common in Okinawa made by [[Infusion|infused]] chili peppers in [[awamori]] (the [[Liquor|distilled spirit]]); Yushi-Dohu is hand-made [[Shima tofu]]; 'Yuntaku' means chatting with chill in [[Ryukyuan languages]]; The song Shisa sang is UNION([[:ja:フレッシュプラザ ユニオン|ja]])'s commercial jingle, a supermarket chain that operates only on [[Okinawa Island]].

A cited song {{Nihongo|'Farewell to This Day'|今日の日はさようなら|Kyou no hi ha sayounara}}([[:ja:今日の日はさようなら|ja]]) is a well-known Japanese choral piece that was also used in [[Evangelion: 2.0 You Can (Not) Advance]].

A second commercial released after the film's screening features the caption: "That friendship, that sin, and that life—forever."<ref>{{Cite web|date=2026-07-28|title=『映画ちいかわ 人魚の島のひみつ』TVCMいつまでも篇【大ヒット上映中】|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-1nKzOF8-_Q|access-date=19 September 2026|website=[[YouTube]]|language=ja}}</ref>
