'''''Red, White and Blue''''' is a 2020 [[Historical drama|historical drama film]] directed by [[Steve McQueen (director)|Steve McQueen]] and co-written by McQueen and [[Courttia Newland]]. It stars [[John Boyega]] as [[Leroy Logan]], an officer in the [[London Metropolitan Police]] who founded the [[National Black Police Association (United Kingdom)|Black Police Association]] and attempted to reform the police force from within. The film was released as part of the anthology series ''[[Small Axe (miniseries)|Small Axe]]'' on [[BBC One]] on 29 November 2020, and released on [[Amazon Prime Video]] on 4 December 2020. It premiered as an opening film at the 58th [[New York Film Festival]] on 24 September 2020.<ref>{{cite web|last=Fleming|first=Mike Jr.|date=3 August 2020|title=New York Film Festival Sets Steve McQueen's 'Lovers Rock' For Opening Night; Drive-Ins, Virtual Showings To Supplement Possible Lincoln Center Screenings|url=https://deadline.com/2020/08/new-york-film-festival-opening-night-film-lovers-rock-steve-mcqueen-director-amazon-prime-video-bbc-one-drive-in-theaters-1203002156/|access-date=3 August 2020|website=Deadline Hollywood}}</ref>

== Release ==
The film premiered at the [[2020 New York Film Festival]], which was held virtually, shown alongside two other films from Steve McQueen's ''Small Axe'' series, ''[[Mangrove (film)|Mangrove]]'' and ''[[Lovers Rock (2020 film)|Lovers Rock]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Chang|first=Justin|date=2020-10-12|title=Even from home, this year's New York Film Festival was a virtual celebration of cinema's power|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/movies/story/2020-10-12/new-york-film-festival-mangrove-french-exit|access-date=2020-11-29|website=Los Angeles Times|language=en-US}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
[[Review aggregator]] [[Metacritic]] assigned the film a weighted average score of 85 out of 100, based on 14 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Red, White and Blue Reviews |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/red-white-and-blue |access-date=5 December 2020 |website=[[Metacritic]] }}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> On [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 97% based on 58 reviews, with an average rating of 8.2/10. The site's critics consensus reads, "An urgent and timely biopic that's as sumptuous as it is searing, ''Red, White, and Blue'' is a triumph that gives the undeniably talented John Boyega the starring role he deserves."<ref>{{Cite web|title=Red, White and Blue (2020) |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/small_axe/s05|access-date= 5 December 2020|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]}}</ref>

Critics praised the film for evoking a sense of urgency and anger. K. Austin Collins of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' wrote that "The tense urgency we feel throughout this movie owes in great part to its structure, which, particularly in some of the argumentative family scenes, has a way of dredging up a thousand things at once, because any one rift risks giving everyone the excuse to lay bare all the despair and disagreement that'd been suppressed to that point."<ref>{{Cite magazine|last=Collins|first=K. Austin|date=2020-12-05|title=In 'Red White, and Blue,' Steve McQueen Exhibits One of His Most Exciting Modes as a Director: Cool Anger|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/movies/movie-reviews/steve-mcqueen-small-axe-red-white-blue-amazon-prime-video-1096202/|access-date=2020-12-24|magazine=Rolling Stone|language=en-US}}</ref> Odie Henderson, reviewing the film for ''[[RogerEbert.com]]'', wrote that it "is not a coddling film. It's an angry one, a tricky meditation that forces you to put yourself in the shoes of someone you might actually consider a traitor or a fool. Boyega, who is fantastic, wears the burden of Leroy's loneliness very well, and you feel his desire for an ally, a friend or, most notably, a running buddy who looks like him."<ref>{{Cite web|last=Henderson|first=Odie|date=December 4, 2020|title=Small Axe: Red, White and Blue movie review (2020) {{!}} Roger Ebert|url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/small-axe-red-white-and-blue-movie-review-2020|access-date=2020-12-24|website=RogerEbert.com|language=en}}</ref>

John Boyega's lead performance received acclaim. [[Peter Bradshaw]] of ''[[The Guardian]]'' wrote that Boyega "takes his career to the next level with a heroic and even tragic portrayal of Logan," and compared the performance to [[Al Pacino]]'s in ''[[Serpico]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Bradshaw|first=Peter|date=2020-10-03|title=Red, White and Blue review – Steve McQueen and John Boyega hit gold|url=http://www.theguardian.com/film/2020/oct/03/red-white-and-blue-review-steve-mcqueen-john-boyega|access-date=2020-12-24|website=The Guardian|language=en}}</ref> Kevin Maher of ''[[The Times]]'' called it Boyega's "most commanding" performance, commending his "simmering restraint, with wide-eyed naivety and with brief moments of fulminating rage."<ref>{{Cite news|last=Maher|first=Kevin|date=October 5, 2020|title=Red, White and Blue review — brilliant John Boyega shows his star quality in real-life role|language=en|work=[[The Times]]|url=https://www.thetimes.com/culture/tv-radio/article/red-white-and-blue-review-brilliant-john-boyega-shows-his-star-quality-in-real-life-role-dvbj0ndh8|access-date=2020-12-24|issn=0140-0460}}</ref>

Writing for ''[[The Ringer (website)|The Ringer]]'', Justin Charity observed that the film "hinges on the classic contest within minority groups: assimilation versus subversion," and compared its complex portrayal of bigoted police officers favorably to the anthology series ''[[Lovecraft Country (TV series)|Lovecraft Country]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Charity|first=Justin|date=2020-12-07|title=Assimilation or Subversion in Steve McQueen's 'Red, White, and Blue'|url=https://www.theringer.com/tv/2020/12/7/22154025/steve-mcqueen-small-axe-john-boyega-amazon-bbc|access-date=2020-12-24|website=The Ringer|language=en}}</ref> Sonia Saraiya of ''[[Vanity Fair (magazine)|Vanity Fair]]'' described the film as "a frustratingly inconclusive story" which does not depict the later successes of Logan's career, noting that McQueen and Newland "don't allow Logan's story to ripen long enough to see the fruits of his persistence." Nevertheless, she opined that the father-and-son dynamic at the heart of the film was "beautifully and subtly told."<ref>{{cite web |last1=Saraiya |first1=Sonia |title=Small Axe: Red, White, and Blue Is a Bitter Pill to Swallow |url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2020/12/small-axe-red-white-blue-john-boyega-review |website=Vanity Fair |access-date=8 July 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201204165347/https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2020/12/small-axe-red-white-blue-john-boyega-review |archive-date=4 December 2020 |date=4 December 2020}}</ref>

The film appeared on several critics' top ten lists of best films from 2020.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Dietz|first=Jason|date=January 9, 2020|title=Best of 2020: Film Critic Top Ten Lists|url=https://www.metacritic.com/feature/film-critics-pick-10-best-movies-of-2020|access-date=2021-01-11|website=Metacritic|language=en|archive-date=15 December 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201215081138/https://www.metacritic.com/feature/film-critics-pick-10-best-movies-of-2020|url-status=dead}}</ref>
