'''''Hardly a Criminal''''' ({{langx|es|'''Apenas un delincuente'''}}) is a 1949 Argentine [[crime]] [[drama film|drama]] directed by [[Hugo Fregonese]] and written by [[Raimundo Calcagno]] and [[Israel Chas de Cruz]]. The film started the director's Hollywood film directing career. It is one of the most celebrated films of the [[Golden Age of Argentine cinema|classical era of Argentina cinema]] and was re-released in theatres a few times during the 21st century.

In a survey of the [[The 100 Greatest Films of Argentine Cinema|100 greatest films of Argentine cinema]] carried out by the [[Museo del Cine Pablo Ducrós Hicken]] in 2000, the film reached the 10th position.<ref>{{cite journal|url=https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ITZbOc_XZnbJ1W2a9Lq9Zk8xJRrWrzPg/view|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221121182959/https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ITZbOc_XZnbJ1W2a9Lq9Zk8xJRrWrzPg/view|archive-date=21 November 2022|accessdate=21 November 2022|year=2000|issue=3|work=La mirada cautiva|location=Buenos Aires|publisher=[[Museo del Cine Pablo Ducrós Hicken]]|via=[[The 100 Greatest Films of Argentine Cinema|Encuesta de cine argentino 2022]] on [[Google Drive]]|pages=6–14|title=Las 100 mejores del periodo 1933-1999 del Cine Argentino}}</ref> In a new version of the survey organized in 2022 by the specialized magazines ''La vida útil'', ''Taipei'' and ''La tierra quema'', presented at the [[Mar del Plata International Film Festival]], the film reached the 27th position.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://encuestadecineargentino.com/top-100/|title=Top 100|language=es|accessdate=13 November 2022|date=11 November 2022|publisher=[[The 100 Greatest Films of Argentine Cinema|Encuesta de cine argentino 2022]]}}</ref>

==Plot==
Jorge Salcedo is a bank employee of modest means who wants the good things in life and fast. His girlfriend, who has just finished her law exams, tells him that the maximum imprisonment for embezzlement is six years. He is willing to pay that price in exchange for comforts of life, planning to hide the money and using it after serving his jail term. He steals half a million pesos. In the prison he faces not just his paranoia about the safety of his money bit the authorities want to recover the missing money as do many criminals in prison with him.<ref>{{Cite web |title=User-submitted review of "Apenas un delincuente" |url=https://www.imdb.com/review/rw9504322/?ref_=tturv_perm_3 |access-date=2024-11-04 |website=IMDb}}</ref><ref>{{Cite AV media |url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0143110/reviews/?ref_=tt_urv_sm |title=Hardly a Criminal (1949) - User reviews - IMDb |language=en-US |access-date=2024-11-04 |via=www.imdb.com}}</ref>

==Production==
It was mostly filmed on streets in [[Buenos Aires]].<ref name="Lexington">{{cite news |date=2 November 1949 |title='Criminal' Film Boosted |url=https://www.newspapers.com/clip/74389291/lexington-herald-leader/ |work=[[Lexington Herald-Leader]] |location=Lexington, Kentucky |access-date=26 March 2021}}</ref> [[James Mason]] decided to act in Fregonese's 1950 film ''[[One Way Street]]'', when it had the [[working title]] ''Death on a Side Street'', once he watched ''Hardly a Criminal''.<ref>{{cite news |last=Hopper |first=Hedda |author-link=Hedda Hopper |date=22 November 1949 |title=In Hollywood |url=https://www.newspapers.com/clip/74388819/the-newark-advocate/ |work=[[The Advocate (Newark)|The Newark Advocate]] |location=Newark, Ohio |via=Newspapers.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.tcm.com/tcmdb/title/85766/one-way-street#overview |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140103102820/http://www.tcm.com/tcmdb/title/85766/One-Way-Street/#overview |url-status=dead |archive-date=January 3, 2014 |title=One Way Street |website=[[Turner Classic Movies]] |access-date=26 March 2021}}</ref>

==Release==
The film's producers hoped that it would be successful in the United States with English subtitles.<ref name="Lexington"/> After the film's 1949 showing at the [[American Academy of Arts and Sciences]], the director and his wife [[Faith Domergue]] held a party for multiple guests.<ref>{{cite news |date=5 August 1949 |title=Here and there |url=https://www.newspapers.com/clip/74389676/daily-news/ |work=[[Los Angeles Daily News|Daily News]] |location=Los Angeles, California |via=Newspapers.com}}</ref>

In 2014, the film and ''One Way Street'' was released at the Noir City: Hollywood, 16th Annual Festival of Film Noir program at the [[Grauman's Egyptian Theatre]].<ref name="Cinematheque">{{cite web |url=http://americancinemathequecalendar.com/content/hardly-a-criminal-one-way-street |title=''Hardly a Criminal'' / ''One Way Street'' |website=[[American Cinematheque]] |access-date=26 March 2021 |archive-date=25 June 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220625044429/http://americancinemathequecalendar.com/content/hardly-a-criminal-one-way-street |url-status=dead }}</ref> It was a part of the Death is My Dance Partner: Film Noir in Postwar Argentina program, which had six features that were filmed within the time of [[Peronism]] (1949–1956), at the [[Museum of Modern Art]] in 2016.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.artforum.com/film/nick-pinkerton-on-death-is-my-dance-partner-at-the-museum-of-modern-art-58064 |title=Nick Pinkerton on "Death Is My Dance Partner" at the Museum of Modern Art |last=Pinkerton |first=Nick |date=10 February 2016 |website=[[Artforum]] |access-date=26 March 2021}}</ref> It was released with [[35 mm movie film]] and a new English dubbing by the [[UCLA Film and Television Archive]]'s Film Noir Foundation Collection.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.moma.org/calendar/events/1772 |title=''Apenas un delincuente'' (''Hardly a Criminal'') |website=[[MoMA PS1]] |access-date=26 March 2021}}</ref> In 2020, the film was a selection at the 18th Annual San Francisco Film Noir Festival.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://offscreen.com/view/noir-city-international-the-18th-annual-san-francisco-film-noir-festival-part-2 |title=Noir City International: the 18th Annual San Francisco Film Noir Festival, 24 January – 2 February, 2020, "It's a bitter little world" Part 2 |last=Rist |first=Peter |date=July 2020 |website=Offscreen |access-date=26 March 2021}}</ref>

==Reception==
Critics from Buenos Aires in 1949 said that it was Argentina's "outstanding film of the year".<ref name="Lexington"/> The film started the director's Hollywood film directing career. The [[American Cinematheque]] said that the film is "the best Argentine noir of the 1940s" and that it "is an audacious blending of ''Naked City'' and ''Brute Force''".<ref name="Cinematheque"/> Nicolas Rapold of ''[[Film Comment]]'' wrote, "While lacking the snap of American counterparts, its laying bare of masculine pride and familial shame was hard to shake."<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.filmcomment.com/blog/festivals-venice-2015/ |title=Festivals: Venice |last=Rapold |first=Nicolas |date=18 November 2015 |website=[[Film Comment]] |access-date=26 March 2021}}</ref>
