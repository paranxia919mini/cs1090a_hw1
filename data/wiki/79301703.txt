'''''Holding Liat''''' is a 2025 American [[documentary]] film, directed by Brandon Kramer. Produced by [[Darren Aronofsky]] under his [[Protozoa Pictures]] banner. It follows the reaction of [[Liat Beinin Atzili]]'s family after she and her husband are kidnapped from their home in a [[kibbutz]] near the [[Gaza Strip|Gaza]] border during the [[October 7 attacks]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/video/holding-liat-clip-berlin-film-festival/|title='Holding Liat' First Look: Berlin Film Festival World Premiere Documents Family Of Hostage Seized By Hamas On October 7|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Matthew|last=Carey|date=February 14, 2025|access-date=February 22, 2025}}</ref>

The film had its world premiere at the Forum section of the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]] on February 16, 2025, where it won the Berlinale Documentary Film Award.

==Plot==
During the [[October 7 attacks]] Liat Beinin Atzili and her husband [[Aviv Atzili]] were kidnapped from their home in a [[kibbutz]] near the border. Liat parents, Yehuda and Chaya Beinin, deal with the fear of their daughter's fate. Alongside many other relatives dissatisfied with [[Benjamin Netanyahu]]'s government disregard of the hostages, Yehuda intensifies his public campaign against the [[Gaza war]], and the inevitability of casualties among the hostages.

Yehuda travels alongside Tal, his youngest daughter, to the United States hoping to gather support against the Israeli government inaction, yet he finds that [[United States support for Israel in the Gaza war|American politicians unconditional support for Israel]] is also reflected upon Netanyahu. In Portland, ceasefire demonstrations take place and American academic and historian [[Joel Beinin]], Yehuda's brother, gives a lecture about the [[Nakba]]. Through video chat, the brothers' old disagreement over the basic principles of the [[Zionism|state of Israel]] is revealed, as Joel argues that the Kibbutzniks' original socialist ideals were coopted by a colonialist agenda.

Blood found in the Kibbutz is confirmed to be from Aviv, but the IDF still don't know the whereabouts of his body. The kibbutz members mourns their losses, but a feeling of outrage still grips Israeli society. As the [[2023 Gaza war ceasefire|first ceasefire]] is announced, relatives await the final list of released hostages. Tal express her dissatisfaction with the Israeli and American position in a public meeting about the ceasefire. Liat is ultimately released by Hamas. Yehuda and Chaya receive a call from American President Joe Biden, to whom they express gratitude.

Liat reunites with her parents at the hospital, whereupon she realizes something bad must have happened to Aviv. At his funeral, Liat gives a speech and the family dance in his memory. Returning to her kibbutz, she watches through TV the ceasefire demonstrations in [[Tel Aviv]]. Three months later, in a Holocaust memorial, Liat talks about the ongoing war and the Palestinian lives on the "other side of the fence".

==Release==
The film had its World Premiere at the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]] on February 16, 2025, where it won the Berlinale Documentary Film Award.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/berlinale-reveals-special-panorama-generation-and-forum-titles/5200856.article|title=Berlinale reveals Special, Panorama, Generation and Forum titles|website=[[Screen International]]|first1=Ben|last1=Dalton|first2=Tim|last2=Dams|date=January 16, 2025|access-date=February 22, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/berlin2025-festival-winners-1236143785/|title=Norwegian Queer Love Story 'Dreams' Wins 75th Berlin Film Festival (Full Winners List)|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|first=Scott|last=Roxborough|date=February 22, 2025|access-date=February 23, 2025}}</ref> Soon after, it had its North American Premiere at [[Tribeca Festival]] on June 9, 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://tribecafilm.com/films/holding-liat-2025|title=Holding Liat|website=[[Tribeca Festival]]|access-date=April 26, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2025/04/tribeca-festival-2025-lineup-movies-1236369631/|title=Tribeca Festival Unveils 2025 Film Lineup With Miley Cyrus, Demi Lovato, Bryan Cranston & 'How To Train Your Dragon'|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Jill|last=Goldsmith|date=April 16, 2025|access-date=April 26, 2025}}</ref>

The film was first released theatrically by MetFilm in the United Kingdom on September 12, 2025. It is scheduled for theatrical release in the United States in January 2026, beginning with New York's [[Film Forum]].<ref>{{Cite web |last= |title=Past, Present and Future Releases: September 2025 |url=https://filmdistributorsassociation.com/release-schedule/past-present-and-future-releases/ |access-date=October 20, 2025 |website=Film Distributors’ Association |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Morfoot |first=Addie |date=2025-10-17 |title=Israeli Hostage Survivor and Subject of Doc 'Holding Liat' on Recent Hostage Release: 'After a War of This Scale We Can't Have Things Go Back to What They Were' |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/news/holding-liat-documentary-israeli-hostage-survivor-release-1236555054/ |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Wiseman |first=Andreas |date=2025-08-29 |title=Darren Aronofsky-Produced Berlin Winner 'Holding Liat', About Gaza War & Israeli-American Hostage, Sets U.S. & International Release Plan; Watch Trailer |url=https://deadline.com/2025/08/holding-liat-gaza-hostage-film-gets-distribution-awards-run-1236501126/ |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> In Israel, the theatrical run will be coordinated by Rotem Heyman followed by a television premiere on [[Yes (company)|Yes]].<ref name=":0" />
