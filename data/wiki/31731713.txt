{{nihongo|'''''Ginza Cosmetics'''''|銀座化粧|Ginza keshō}} is a 1951 Japanese [[drama film]] directed by [[Mikio Naruse]]. It is based on a novel by Tomoichirō Inoue.<ref name="jmdb" /><ref name="kinenote" />

==Plot==
''Ginza Cosmetics'' follows the life of hostess Yukiko, single mother of a young boy, in the lively [[Tokyo]] quarter of [[Ginza]].
