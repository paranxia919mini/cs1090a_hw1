'''''The Magnificent Seven''''' is a 2016 American [[Western (genre)|Western]] action film directed by [[Antoine Fuqua]] and written by [[Nic Pizzolatto]] and [[Richard Wenk]]. It is a [[remake]] of the [[The Magnificent Seven|1960 film]], which itself was a remake of [[Akira Kurosawa]]'s film ''[[Seven Samurai]]''. The film stars [[Denzel Washington]], [[Chris Pratt]], [[Ethan Hawke]], [[Vincent D'Onofrio]], [[Lee Byung-hun]], [[Manuel Garcia-Rulfo]], [[Martin Sensmeier]], and [[Peter Sarsgaard]]. It was the final film of composer [[James Horner]], who died in 2015 after composing part of the score. His friend [[Simon Franglen]] completed the music. [[Principal photography]] began on May 18, 2015, north of [[Baton Rouge, Louisiana]].

''The Magnificent Seven'' premiered on September 8, 2016, at the [[2016 Toronto International Film Festival]] as the opening film, and was released in the United States on September 23, 2016, by [[Sony Pictures Releasing]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-37307798|title=Toronto 2016: Magnificent Seven diversity 'not a statement', says director|first=Tim|last=Masters|work=BBC News|date=September 9, 2016|access-date=June 21, 2018|archive-date=July 21, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180721123536/https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-37307798|url-status=live}}</ref> The film received mixed reviews from critics, where the cast, action sequences, and the score were praised, but the story and screenplay received criticism. It was a moderate success, grossing $162&nbsp;million worldwide on a production budget of $90&nbsp;million.

== Plot ==
 <!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summary should be between 400 to 700 words. -->         
In 1879, [[Robber baron (industrialist)|robber baron]] and [[Gold mining|gold-mining]] [[Business magnate|tycoon]] Bartholomew Bogue, accompanied by an army of [[Mercenary|Blackstone Agents]], seizes control of the [[American frontier|frontier]] town of Rose Creek and forces the residents to work as cheap labor for his mine, while polluting their water supply. Being denounced by the residents, Bogue has their church torched and several of the townsfolk, including farmer Matthew Cullen, brutally murdered in public view. Bogue then gives them a 3 week notice to turn over the deeds to their land at the offer he granted.

Matthew's widow Emma and her friend Teddy leave in search of [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunters]] to help save the town. Their first recruit is [[Wichita, Kansas|Wichita]]-based bounty-hunter Sam Chisholm, who expresses interest in Bogue's involvement. He in turn recruits six others: gambler and [[Exhibition shooting|trick-shooter]] Joshua Faraday; pardon-seeking outlaw Vasquez; former [[Confederate States Army|Confederate]] [[sharpshooter]] Goodnight Robicheaux; his friend, assassin Billy Rocks; legendary [[mountain man]] Jack Horne; and [[Comanche]] warrior Red Harvest.

The Seven enter Rose Creek and eliminate twenty-two Blackstone agents guarding the town, while their lead agent, McCann, flees on horseback. Chisholm apprehends the corrupt [[Sheriffs in the United States|sheriff]], Mr. Harp, sending him away to challenge Bogue. Chisholm tells the terrified townspeople that they now have seven days before Bogue's army arrives. Although some flee, most are determined to fight.

The Seven liberate the men held at the mine, take the explosives stockpiled there and spend the next week fortifying the town and training the townsfolk in combat. Meanwhile, Bogue kills Harp and orders McCann and his Comanche enforcer Denali, to assemble an army. Robicheaux, haunted by his experiences in the [[American Civil War|Civil War]] and fearing his own death if he kills again, leaves the town the night before Bogue's arrival. Emma volunteers to take his place.

At dawn, Bogue's army attacks Rose Creek. A large number are killed by various [[booby trap|traps]] and ambushes. Faraday is wounded by McCann, who is killed by Vasquez; the defensive line collapses, and Robicheaux returns to join the battle, warning the others of an incoming [[Gatling gun]]. The gun tears through both the townspeople and Bogue's men alike. Outgunned, the Seven evacuate the survivors to the burnt-out church and mount a [[last stand]].

Horne protects a wounded Teddy from gunfire, but is killed by Denali. Denali then prepares to kill Emma. An enraged Red Harvest kills Denali, calling him "a disgrace". Robicheaux and Billy are killed by a second round of Gatling gunfire while covering Faraday from the church steeple, who charges up the hill and destroys the gun with [[dynamite]] at the cost of his own life.

Chisholm confronts and guns down the two remaining mercenaries before disarming Bogue. A wounded Bogue retreats into the church, where Chisholm reveals that his family in [[Lincoln County, Kansas|Lincoln, Kansas]] was [[Lynching in the United States|lynched]] by ex-Confederate soldiers whom Bogue hired to remove [[Southern Homestead Act of 1866|homesteaders]]. After imploring Bogue to repent, Chisholm begins to [[garrote]] him. Bogue tries to draw a hidden revolver, but Emma kills him.

The townspeople do return to Rose Creek and thank Chisholm, Vasquez, and Red Harvest for their help as they ride off. Faraday, Robicheaux, Billy and Horne are buried near the town and honored as heroes. Emma, in [[Voice-over|voice-over narration]], reflects fondly on the noble sacrifice that made them "magnificent".

As with his previous films, Washington would start off the day by kneeling down and praying, asking for forgiveness for all his wrongdoings. "For me," he said, "this is more than just making movies. The producers were skeptical whether Washington would take the job since it was a Western film, but Fuqua flew to New York City to negotiate with the actor, who accepted the offer.<ref name="Q&A"/><ref name="TW"/>

[[Chris Pratt]] described his character Josh Faraday as "a bit of a fox, a trickster. He's a gambler, a drinker. He loves the ladies. But he's deadly in a fire fight."<ref name="CB">{{cite web|url=https://comicbook.com/2016/09/03/the-magnificent-seven-chris-pratt-and-denzel-washington-characte/|title=The Magnificent Seven Chris Pratt And Denzel Washington Character Vignettes|first=Jay|last=Jayson|work=Comicbook.com|date=September 3, 2016|access-date=September 7, 2016|archive-date=September 6, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160906223422/http://comicbook.com/2016/09/03/the-magnificent-seven-chris-pratt-and-denzel-washington-characte/|url-status=live}}</ref> Like Washington, it is also Pratt's first Western film.<ref name="EWW"/> Pratt began watching Western films at the age of 31, while filming in London, and credits [[Gary Cooper]] for revitalizing his perspective towards the genre.<ref name="THRV"/><ref name="EWW"/> Fuqua, aware of Pratt's penchant towards Westerns, reached out to him, and Pratt called back in a few days singing "[[Oh Shenandoah]]", to which Fuqua replied, "He's it. He's [[Steve McQueen]]."<ref name="Q&A"/>

Fuqua's approach to [[Ethan Hawke]]'s character Goodnight Robicheaux, a former Confederate soldier, was to picture him as if [[Christopher Walken]]'s character Nikanor "Nick" Chevotarevich in ''[[The Deer Hunter]]'' was an emotionally shattered [[American Civil War|Civil War]] veteran.<ref name="GM"/> Hawke was the first person to come on board after the project was finalized.<ref name="SIntl"/> He had stumbled upon Fuqua and Washington during the New York City premiere of ''[[The Equalizer (film)|The Equalizer]]'' in 2014 and, upon learning that a remake was in the works, asked Fuqua to cast him.<ref name="GM" /> Like Washington, ''The Magnificent Seven'' marks the third collaboration between Hawke and Fuqua, after ''[[Training Day]]'' (2001) and ''[[Brooklyn's Finest]]'' (2009).<ref name="GM">{{cite web|url=https://www.theglobeandmail.com/arts/awards-and-festivals/tiff/how-ethan-hawke-survived-hollywood/article31725063/|title=How Ethan Hawke survived Hollywood|first=Barry|last=Hertz|work=[[The Globe and Mail]]|date=September 7, 2016|access-date=September 7, 2016|archive-date=September 7, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160907023951/http://www.theglobeandmail.com/arts/awards-and-festivals/tiff/how-ethan-hawke-survived-hollywood/article31725063/|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[Manuel Garcia-Rulfo]] described his character Vasquez, a bandit on the run from the law, as someone who loves gunfighting.<ref name="Pop">{{cite web|url=https://pop.inquirer.net/2016/08/watch-the-magnificent-seven-vignettes-the-outlaw-the-warrior/|title=Watch "The Magnificent Seven" vignettes: the outlaw, the warrior|work=Pop Inquirer|date=August 19, 2016|access-date=September 7, 2016|archive-date=September 15, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160915060411/https://pop.inquirer.net/2016/08/watch-the-magnificent-seven-vignettes-the-outlaw-the-warrior/|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[Martin Sensmeier]] auditioned several times to land the role of Red Harvest, a native [[Comanche]] warrior.<ref name="Casting7">{{cite web|url=http://www.universityherald.com/articles/39815/20160907/meet-the-magnificent-seven-each-characters-teased-in-vignettes-videos.htm|title=Meet 'The Magnificent Seven' With These New Character Vignettes|work=University Herald|date=September 7, 2016|access-date=September 7, 2016|archive-date=September 14, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160914213701/http://www.universityherald.com/articles/39815/20160907/meet-the-magnificent-seven-each-characters-teased-in-vignettes-videos.htm|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Pop"/> He stayed off social media and studied intently to portray his part.<ref name="MS">{{cite web|url=http://indiancountrytodaymedianetwork.com/2016/09/06/magnificent-seven-actor-martin-sensmeier-getting-chewed-out-oscar-nominee-165691|title=Magnificent Seven Actor Martin Sensmeier On Getting Chewed Out by an Oscar-Nominee|work=[[Indian Country Today Media Network]]|access-date=September 7, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170704093936/http://indiancountrytodaymedianetwork.com/2016/09/06/magnificent-seven-actor-martin-sensmeier-getting-chewed-out-oscar-nominee-165691|archive-date=July 4, 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref> Scotty Augere, who had previously worked on ''[[Dances with Wolves]]'', taught Sensmeier how to ride a horse bareback and rode with him two hours a day.<ref name="MS"/>

[[Vincent D'Onofrio]] was cast as [[mountain man]] Jack Horne on the urging of co-stars Chris Pratt and Ethan Hawke.<ref name="http://lrmonline.com/news/the-lrm-interview-vincent-donofrio-on-the-magnificent-seven-and-daredevil">{{cite web|last1=Douglas|first1=Edward|title=The LRM Interview: Vincent D'Onofrio on The Magnificent Seven and Daredevil|url=http://lrmonline.com/news/the-lrm-interview-vincent-donofrio-on-the-magnificent-seven-and-daredevil|website=Latino Review Media|date=July 27, 2017|access-date=January 16, 2017|archive-date=April 20, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170420231146/http://lrmonline.com/news/the-lrm-interview-vincent-donofrio-on-the-magnificent-seven-and-daredevil|url-status=live}}</ref> For the role, D'Onofrio developed a raw, high-pitched voice to give the impression of a man who has lived in the wilderness for years without speaking to people. When he asked to test the voice for Fuqua, the director refused to listen, instructing the actor to surprise him with it in his first scene, which resulted in a delighted Fuqua laughing so hard that he almost ruined the take.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Davis|first1=Erik|title=The Funny Story Behind Vincent D'Onofrio's Crazy High-Pitched Voice|url=http://www.fandango.com/movie-news/the-funny-story-behind-vincent-donofrios-crazy-high-pitched-voice-in-the-magnificent-seven-751358|website=Fandango|date=September 26, 2016 |access-date=January 16, 2017|archive-date=November 27, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161127001231/http://www.fandango.com/movie-news/the-funny-story-behind-vincent-donofrios-crazy-high-pitched-voice-in-the-magnificent-seven-751358|url-status=live}}</ref>

The climactic battle between the Seven and a small army led by Bogue took three weeks to film. Since his grandmother was the prime influence on the film, every day after filming, Fuqua asked himself if she would have had fun watching it.<ref name="ECOT"/>

== Reception ==
=== Box office ===
''The Magnificent Seven'' grossed $93.4&nbsp;million in the United States and Canada, and $68.9&nbsp;million in other countries, for a worldwide total of $162.4&nbsp;million, against a net production budget of $90&nbsp;million.<ref name=BOM/> The film had a global 2D IMAX opening of $4.3&nbsp;million from 606 theaters.<ref name="Overseas41stOpening"/>

According to the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'', the film was released in theaters at a time when the Western genre had been struggling to attract wide audiences and accrue lucrative revenues, as it has shown considerable downfall in interest among patrons since the 1970s. The genre has had several recent [[box office bomb|box office flops]], such as ''[[Cowboys & Aliens]]'' (2011) and ''[[The Lone Ranger (2013 film)|The Lone Ranger]]'' (2013), but has also found success in films like ''[[True Grit (2010 film)|True Grit]]'' (2010) and ''[[Django Unchained]]'' (2012).<ref name="LAbox"/> Its strong debut in North America was partly attributed to the presence of [[Denzel Washington]] and [[Chris Pratt]], who industry analysts say are two of only a handful of movie stars today who consistently draw large audiences to theaters,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2016/09/26/movies/denzel-washington-box-office.html?rref=collection%2Fsectioncollection%2Fmovies&action=click&contentCollection=movies&region=rank&module=package&version=highlights&contentPlacement=1&pgtype=sectionfront&_r=0|title=The Box Office Power of Denzel Washington|first=Mekado|last=Murphy|work=[[The New York Times]]|date=September 26, 2016|access-date=September 28, 2016|archive-date=February 19, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180219031122/https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2016/09/26/movies/denzel-washington-box-office.html?rref=collection%2Fsectioncollection%2Fmovies&action=click&contentCollection=movies&region=rank&module=package&version=highlights&contentPlacement=1&pgtype=sectionfront&_r=0|url-status=live}}</ref> and also to Fuqua's direction.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/movies/la-et-ct-weekend-box-office-magnificent-seven-20160925-snap-story.html#nt=oft07a-2gp1|title='Magnificent Seven' wins box office shoot-out over 'Storks'|first=Tre'vell|last=Anderson|work=[[Los Angeles Times]]|date=September 26, 2016|access-date=September 28, 2016|archive-date=September 27, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160927212341/http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/movies/la-et-ct-weekend-box-office-magnificent-seven-20160925-snap-story.html#nt=oft07a-2gp1|url-status=live}}</ref>

''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' pointed out that the production budget also plays an important role in determining a film's success. By comparison to other recent Westerns, ''The Magnificent Seven'' cost $90&nbsp;million to make, before promotion and marketing costs are included. The site pointed out that "the trick is keeping their budgets reasonable," unlike ''The Lone Ranger'', which cost $215&nbsp;million to make, and ''Cowboys & Aliens'', which cost $163&nbsp;million.<ref name="DEAD1"/> The film became a financial recoverer for [[Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer]], after the studio lost a great deal of money with ''[[Ben-Hur (2016 film)|Ben-Hur]]'' the previous month, and was the studio's second hit of the year, following the sleeper ''[[Me Before You (film)|Me Before You]]'', released in June.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://mobile.nytimes.com/2016/09/26/movies/the-magnificent-seven-dominates-weekend-north-american-box-office.html?referer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nytimes.com%2Finteractive%2F2016%2F09%2F26%2Fmovies%2Fdenzel-washington-box-office.html%3Frref%3Dcollection%2Fsectioncollection%2Fmovies&action=click&contentCollection=movies&region=rank&module=package&version=highlights&contentPlacement=1&pgtype=sectionfront&_r=0|title='The Magnificent Seven' Dominates Weekend North American Box Office|first=Brooks|last=Barnes|work=[[The New York Times]]|date=September 25, 2016|access-date=September 28, 2016|archive-date=April 19, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170419201108/https://mobile.nytimes.com/2016/09/26/movies/the-magnificent-seven-dominates-weekend-north-american-box-office.html?referer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nytimes.com%2Finteractive%2F2016%2F09%2F26%2Fmovies%2Fdenzel-washington-box-office.html%3Frref%3Dcollection%2Fsectioncollection%2Fmovies&action=click&contentCollection=movies&region=rank&module=package&version=highlights&contentPlacement=1&pgtype=sectionfront&_r=0|url-status=live}}</ref>

==== Outside North America ====
The film was projected to gross around $100&nbsp;million, with foreign box office prognosticators expecting a total similar to ''[[The Hateful Eight]]''{{'s}} $101.6&nbsp;million.<ref name="Overseas41stOpening"/><ref name="DEAD1"/> It was released in South Korea, its first international market, on September 14, 2016, and delivered an opening of $5.1&nbsp;million, finishing in third place at the box office, behind local film ''[[The Age of Shadows]]'' and Hollywood tentpole ''[[Ben-Hur (2016 film)|Ben-Hur]]''.<ref name="VVV1">{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2016/film/asia/korea-box-office-shadows-wins-holiday-weekend-1201864335/|title=Korea Box Office: 'Shadows' Holds Holiday Weekend|first=Sonia|last=Kil|work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|date=September 18, 2016|access-date=September 19, 2016|archive-date=September 19, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160919231438/http://variety.com/2016/film/asia/korea-box-office-shadows-wins-holiday-weekend-1201864335/|url-status=live}}</ref> The following weekend, it opened in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, Spain and Russia,<ref name="Var"/> and grossed $19.2&nbsp;million from 63 markets, including Korea. IMAX made up $1.4&nbsp;million from 234 theaters.<ref name="Overseas41stOpening">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2016/09/storks-magnificent-seven-bridget-jones-baby-finding-dory-overseas-weekend-results-international-box-office-1201825820/|title='Bridget Jones's Baby' Tops Overseas Again As 'Magnificent 7' Corrals $19.2M & 'Storks' Bundles $18.3M – Intl Box Office|first=Nancy|last=Tartaglione|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=September 25, 2016|access-date=September 26, 2016|archive-date=September 26, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160926154829/http://deadline.com/2016/09/storks-magnificent-seven-bridget-jones-baby-finding-dory-overseas-weekend-results-international-box-office-1201825820/|url-status=live}}</ref>

It opened in first place in Russia ($1.8&nbsp;million), Spain ($1.1&nbsp;million) and Malaysia ($560,000), and second in the United Kingdom and Ireland ($2.6&nbsp;million), Germany ($1.4&nbsp;million) and Brazil ($1.1&nbsp;million),<ref name="Overseas41stOpening"/> and had similar openings in Australia ($2.8&nbsp;million) and France ($1.9&nbsp;million).<ref name="Overseas42ndOpening">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2016/10/miss-peregrine-deepwater-horizon-ms-dhoni-untold-story-india-lord-china-finding-dory-bridget-jones-baby-overseas-weekend-results-international-box-office-1201829684/|title='Miss Peregrine' Books $36.5M; 'Finding Dory', 'Bridget Jones' Reach Milestones; Big Frame For Local Pics – Intl Box Office|first=Nancy|last=Tartaglione|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=October 2, 2016|access-date=October 3, 2016|archive-date=October 3, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161003055418/http://deadline.com/2016/10/miss-peregrine-deepwater-horizon-ms-dhoni-untold-story-india-lord-china-finding-dory-bridget-jones-baby-overseas-weekend-results-international-box-office-1201829684/|url-status=live}}</ref> China is not yet determined.<ref name="Overseas41stOpening"/>

=== Critical response ===
''The Magnificent Seven'' received mixed<!--PURELY MIXED, per source and scores--> reviews, with critics praising the cast, action sequences, and score, but stating that the film did not offer much originality or innovation.<ref name=reviews>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.ew.com/article/2016/09/08/magnificent-seven-reviews-denzel-washington-chris-pratt |title=Magnificent Seven reviews: Remake falls short of its title at TIFF|first=Oliver|last=Gettell |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|date=September 8, 2016|access-date=September 9, 2016}}</ref> On [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film has an approval rating of 64%, based on 315 reviews, with an average rating of 6/10. The website's critical consensus reads: "''The Magnificent Seven'' never really lives up to the superlative in its title – or the classics from which it draws inspiration – but remains a moderately diverting action thriller on its own merits."<ref name="RT">{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_magnificent_seven_2016/|title=The Magnificent Seven (2016)|work=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|publisher=[[Fandango Media|Fandango]]|access-date=January 14, 2025|archive-date=August 20, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150820170835/http://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_magnificent_seven_2016/|url-status=live}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], which assigns a normalized rating to reviews, the film has a score of 54 out of 100, based on 50 critics, indicating "mixed or average reviews".<ref name=Meta>{{cite web |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-magnificent-seven |title=The Magnificent Seven Reviews |work=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[CBS Interactive]] |access-date=October 21, 2018 |archive-date=November 12, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191112223735/https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-magnificent-seven |url-status=live }}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average of "A−" on an A+ to F scale.<ref name="CinemaScore">{{cite web |url=https://pbs.twimg.com/media/CtF3pPjVMAAD5Yn.jpg |title=CinemaScore |work=cinemascore.com |via=Twitter |access-date=April 8, 2017 |archive-date=April 9, 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170409174652/https://pbs.twimg.com/media/CtF3pPjVMAAD5Yn.jpg |url-status=live }}</ref>

''[[IGN]]'' critic Terri Schwartz gave the film a 6.7/10, and summarized her review with: "''The Magnificent Seven'' ends up being a bit too predictable to reach its full potential, but the fun the cast clearly had making it allows the movie to be an enjoyable ride while it lasts. Fuqua does his best to update the Western for the modern audience, but doesn't capture what made those films great in the process. The action is big and sleek, the characters are charismatic and the film looks beautiful, but this won't be a movie that stays with you long after you leave the theater".<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.ign.com/articles/2016/09/23/the-magnificent-seven-review |title=The Magnificent Seven Review |author=Schwartz, Terri |date=September 22, 2016 |access-date=October 1, 2016 |archive-date=October 2, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161002025155/http://www.ign.com/articles/2016/09/23/the-magnificent-seven-review |url-status=live }}</ref>

''[[Chicago Sun-Times]]''{{'}} [[Richard Roeper]] praised the film, giving it a score of three stars out of four, and writing: "Overall, this is a rousing, albeit sometimes cheesy, action-packed Western bolstered by Denzel Washington's baddest-of-the-baddasses lead performance, mostly fine supporting work, and yep, some of the most impressively choreographed extended shootout sequences in recent memory."<ref>{{cite news |url=http://chicago.suntimes.com/entertainment/lightweight-magnificent-seven-entertains-with-guns-blazing/ |title=Lightweight 'Magnificent Seven' entertains with guns blazing |author=Roeper, Richard |work=Chicago Sun-Times |date=September 20, 2016 |access-date=October 1, 2016 |archive-date=September 26, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160926201502/http://chicago.suntimes.com/entertainment/lightweight-magnificent-seven-entertains-with-guns-blazing/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

[[James Berardinelli]] of ''Reelviews'' gave the film a score of two stars out of four, writing: "The original ''[[The Magnificent Seven]]'' found a perfect balance between moments of grand triumph and the understated, solemn denouement. This ''The Magnificent Seven'' has the dour ending without the high points preceding it. With two better versions of this story readily available, why bother with this mediocre re-telling? 'Currently recognizable actors' hardly seems like a good justification."<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.reelviews.net/reelviews/magnificent-seven-the |title=Magnificent Seven, The (United States, 2016) |author=Berardinelli, James |date=September 22, 2016 |access-date=October 1, 2016 |archive-date=September 26, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160926193124/http://www.reelviews.net/reelviews/magnificent-seven-the |url-status=live }}</ref>

[[MTV]]'s Amy Nicholson decried the film, writing: "Fuqua's made two clean piles separating good and evil, and in doing so, he's thrown away the film's point. Now we can trade our conscience for a bucket of popcorn. Today's ''The Magnificent Seven'' is just another superhero flick that spends half its running time assembling a band of bulletproof daredevils. Which makes sense — the original inspired ''The Avengers'', which published [[The Avengers (comic book)|its first comic]] three years after it was a hit."<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2935220/magnificent-seven-denzel-washington-chris-pratt-review/ |title=The Magnificent Seven gallops boldly into mediocrity |work=MTV |author=Nicholson, Amy |date=September 23, 2016 |access-date=October 1, 2016 |archive-date=November 28, 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171128024257/http://www.mtv.com/news/2935220/magnificent-seven-denzel-washington-chris-pratt-review/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

[[Peter Travers]] of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' gave the film three out of four stars, saying: "The new ''Seven'' isn't aiming for cinema immortality. It's two hours of hardcore, shoot-em-up pow and it's entertaining as hell."<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Travers|first1=Peter |title='The Magnificent Seven' Review: A Classic, Newly Diversified Western Rides Again|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/movies/reviews/peter-travers-the-magnificent-seven-movie-review-w441136 |magazine=Rolling Stone|access-date=October 16, 2016}}</ref>
