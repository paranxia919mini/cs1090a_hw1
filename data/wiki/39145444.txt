'''''Partner''''' is a 1968 [[Italy|Italian]] [[drama film]] directed by [[Bernardo Bertolucci]].

Based on the 1846 novella [[The Double (Fyodor Dostoyevsky novel)|''The Double'']] by [[Fyodor Dostoyevsky]], it entered the 29th [[Venice Film Festival]]<ref>{{cite book|last=T. Jefferson Kline|title=I film di Bernardo Bertolucci|publisher=Gremese Editore, 1994|isbn=8876057978}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|last=Leo Pestelli|title=Molte idee (meno cinema) nel "Partner" di Bertolucci|newspaper=La Stampa|date=8 September 1968|page=9}}</ref> and the [[Quinzaine des Réalisateurs]] section at the [[1969 Cannes Film Festival|22nd Cannes Film Festival]].<ref>{{cite book|last=Pierre-Henri Deleau|title=Cinéma en liberté: la Quinzaine des réalisateurs à Cannes|publisher=Ed. de La Martinière, 1993}}</ref>

The film's US debut was limited and only showed at the 1968 New York Film Festival.<ref name=":0" />

==Plot ==   
This film follows a college student who has a routine life and who encounters a twin he is not related to. Along the way, he discovers that the twin friend has many qualities he doesn't have.
