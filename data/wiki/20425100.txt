'''''Night and Day''''' ({{korean|밤과 낮}}) is a [[List of South Korean films of 2008|2008 South Korean]] [[comedy-drama]] film written and directed by [[Hong Sang-soo]], starring [[Kim Young-ho (actor)|Kim Young-ho]] and [[Park Eun-hye]]. The film competed for the [[Golden Bear]] at the [[58th Berlin International Film Festival]].

== Storyline ==
In the summer of 2007, Kim Seong-nam, a painter in his forties, travels to Paris, France, to escape arrest for smoking marijuana, leaving his wife behind in Korea. While there he meets an ex-girlfriend, Min-seon, and is introduced to a small community of Korean artists.
