'''''The Royal Road''''' is a 2015 documentary film directed by [[Jenni Olson]]. The film premiered in the New Frontier section of the [[2015 Sundance Film Festival]]. In the film's voiceover, Olson reflects on her [[butch and femme|butch]] identity and experiences of unrequited love. The film went on to earn the award for Best LGBTQ Film at the 2015 [[Ann Arbor Film Festival]]. Consisting entirely of 16mm urban landscape shots and a lyrical stream of consciousness voiceover, the film touches on a wide range of topics from reflections on classic Hollywood film to the history of the Spanish colonization of California and the [[Mexican American War]].

A voice-over cameo appearance by Pulitzer Prize-winning playwright [[Tony Kushner]] serves as the centerpiece for the film's focal point segment entitled, "In Defense of Nostalgia."  Jenni Olsen states that "In the same way that outdoorsy people experience feelings of calm and wholeness from spending time in nature, there are also those of us who discover a profound serenity in the man-made environment of yesterday."<ref>{{Cite AV media |url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3062620/quotes/?ref_=tt_dyk_qu |title=The Royal Road (2015) - Quotes - IMDb |language=en-US |access-date=2025-03-29 |via=www.imdb.com}}</ref>

==Production==
Olson cited ''[[Sherman's March (1986 film)|Sherman's March]]'' and [[Alfred Hitchcock]]'s ''[[Vertigo (film)|Vertigo]]'' as influences.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Iwasaki|first1=Scott|title=Film connects past with the present|url=http://www.parkrecord.com/filmfestival/ci_27358654/olsons-royal-road-is-poetic-love-letter-san|website=The Park Record|accessdate=February 6, 2015}}</ref> [[San Francisco Film Society]] was the film's fiscal sponsor. The film had its Bay Area premiere on April 29, 2015, at the SFFS's [[San Francisco International Film Festival]]. Olson also crowdfunded $24,038 towards the production of the film.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Olson|first1=Jenni|title=The Royal Road - a film by Jenni Olson|url=https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/jenniolson/the-royal-road-a-film-by-jenni-olson|website=Kickstarter|accessdate=February 3, 2015}}</ref> Olson's wife, [[Julie Dorf]], produced the film.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Grady|first1=Pam|title=Jenni Olson's 'The Royal Road' on its way to Sundance|url=http://www.sfgate.com/movies/article/Jenni-Olson-s-The-Royal-Road-on-its-way-to-5979901.php|website=SF Gate|accessdate=February 15, 2015}}</ref>

==Reception==
Of the film, Dennis Harvey wrote in ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' that "The pic could hardly be smaller or quieter by conventional standards, assembly on all levels is serenely accomplished."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Harvey|first1=Dennis|title=Sundance Film Review: 'The Royal Road'|url=https://variety.com/2015/film/reviews/sundance-film-review-the-royal-road-1201411607/|website=Variety|date=23 January 2015 |accessdate=February 15, 2015}}</ref>
