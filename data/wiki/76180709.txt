'''''No Other Land''''' ({{Langx|ar|لا أرض أخرى|Lā arḍ ukhrá|italic=no}}) is a 2024 [[documentary film]] directed by [[Basel Adra]], [[Hamdan Ballal]], [[Yuval Abraham]], and [[Rachel Szor]], in their directorial debut. A Palestinian-Israeli collective of activists, the four conceived and produced the film in what they describe as an act of resistance on the path to justice in the [[Israeli–Palestinian conflict]]. The film was recorded between 2019 and 2023 and shows the destruction of [[Masafer Yatta]], a Palestinian community in the [[Israeli occupation of the West Bank|occupied West Bank]], which had been resisting forced displacement after an [[Israeli firing zones in the West Bank|Israeli "firing zone"]] was declared on their land.<ref>{{Cite web |url= https://cineuropa.org/film/456695/ |title= ''No Other Land'' |first= Basel |last= Adra |date= 22 January 2024 |access-date= 25 February 2024 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en |archive-date=24 February 2024 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20240224183932/https://cineuropa.org/film/456695/ |url-status= live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url= https://publications.idfa.nl/idfa-bertha-fund-activity-report-2022/in-the-spotlight-hamdan-balal-basel-adra-no-other-land |title= Camera as a weapon of proof |first=Edo |last=Dijksterhuis |access-date=25 February 2024 |website=[[International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam]] |language=en |archive-date=6 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240206181724/https://publications.idfa.nl/idfa-bertha-fund-activity-report-2022/in-the-spotlight-hamdan-balal-basel-adra-no-other-land |url-status=live }}</ref>

A co-production between [[Palestine]] and [[Norway]], the film was selected for the Panorama section at the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 16 February 2024,<ref>{{Cite web |url= https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/454943 |title= The Panorama strand of the Berlinale to open with Levan Akin's ''Crossing'' |first= Davide |last= Abbatescianni |date= 17 January 2024 |access-date= 24 February 2024 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language= en}}</ref> winning the Panorama Audience Award for Best Documentary Film<ref name= "Roxborough-2024"/> and the Berlinale Documentary Film Award.<ref name= "Abbatescianni-2024"/> The film also won the [[Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature Film]] at the [[97th Academy Awards]].<ref>{{Cite web |url= https://www.nytimes.com/2025/03/02/movies/no-other-land-oscars-documentary.html |title= 'No Other Land,' Whose Politics Deterred Distributors, Wins Best Documentary |date= March 2, 2025 |last= Tracy |first= Mark |work=[[The New York Times]] |access-date= March 3, 2025}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
{{Further|Israeli settlement|Israeli settler violence}}
[[Basel Adra]], a young [[Palestinians|Palestinian]] activist, has been resisting the [[forced displacement]] of his people by Israel's military in [[Masafer Yatta]], a region in the occupied [[West Bank]], since he was a child. He records the gradual destruction of his homeland, where Israeli soldiers are tearing down homes and evicting their inhabitants to enforce a court order maintaining that the area's designation as an [[Israeli firing zones in the West Bank|Israeli military firing zone]] was legal under Israeli law.<ref>[https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%5C13/130/004/N89&fileName=13004130.N89&type=4 בג"ץ 413/13 מוחמד מוסא שחאדה אבו עראם ואחרים נ' שר הבטחון ואחרים (4 במאי 2022)]</ref> He befriends Yuval Abraham, a Jewish Israeli journalist who helps him in his struggle. They form an unexpected bond, but their friendship is challenged by the [[Israeli apartheid|huge gap]] between their living conditions: Adra faces constant oppression and violence, while Abraham enjoys freedom and security.

The film follows Adra and the residents of Masafer Yatta as they confront [[Israel Defense Forces|Israeli forces]] carrying out demolition orders. During one such confrontation, Masafer Yatta resident Harun Abu Aram is shot by an Israeli soldier and paralyzed from the neck down while trying to prevent them from stealing his electric generator. Due to the destruction of his home, which was illegal under Israeli law, he is forced to live in a cave. Abu Aram eventually dies as a result of his injuries and inability to receive proper care.

Abraham attempts to raise awareness of the situation in Masafer Yatta, with limited results. Adra chides him for wanting results too quickly and tells Abraham to be patient. Adra ponders the limits of raising awareness, rhetorically asking Abraham what will happen after people see the footage they have captured. The film ends by showing Adra's cousin Zakaria al-Adra, who was unarmed, being shot by an Israeli settler in the days following the [[October 7 attacks]].<ref name="Resch-2025">{{cite web |last1=Resch |first1=Max |date=7 May 2025 |title=Any student with belief in free speech should have their eyes on 'No Other Land' |url=https://www.michigandaily.com/arts/film/any-student-with-belief-in-free-speech-should-have-their-eyes-on-no-other-land/ |access-date=8 May 2025 |website=[[The Michigan Daily]]}}</ref><ref name="Rosen-2024">{{cite web |last1=Rosen |first1=Maya |date=16 April 2024 |title=Co-Resistance at a Crossroads |url=https://jewishcurrents.org/co-resistance-at-a-crossroads-masafer-yatta-west-bank-resistance |access-date=8 May 2025 |website=[[Jewish Currents]]}}</ref>

==Production==
[[File:Basil al-Adra, the lead director of the Oscar-winning film I Have No Other Land, pictured with Odha Muhammad Khalil Hadhalin, Hamdan Blel and activist Ali Awad during their documentation of life and resilience in Masafer Yatta.jpg|thumb|Left to right: Hamdan Ballal and Basel Adra, co-directors of ''No Other Land'', pictured with [[Awdah Hathaleen]] and Ali Awad in 2021]]
In an interview at the Berlinale, Adra and Abraham spoke with ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' about the film. Adra said of its development, "Yuval and Rachel, who are Israelis, came five years ago to write about things—Yuval is journalist. We met and we became friends but also activists together, working on articles about the area. [...] And then we got the idea of doing this, of creating this movie." About filming, Abraham said:<ref>{{Cite news |url= https://variety.com/2024/film/global/palestinian-israeli-no-other-land-doc-eradication-palestinian-villages-political-solution-1235910522/ |title= Palestinian, Israeli Activists Talk ''No Other Land'' Doc on Eradication of Palestinian Villages and Hopes It Can Help 'Find a Political Solution' |first= Nick |last= Vivarelli |work= [[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |date= 16 February 2024 |access-date= 25 February 2024 |language= en |archive-date= 25 February 2024 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20240225111839/https://variety.com/2024/film/global/palestinian-israeli-no-other-land-doc-eradication-palestinian-villages-political-solution-1235910522/ |url-status= live }}</ref>

<blockquote>{{sic|Basal's|expected=Basel's}} family and neighbors had a huge archive of videos that were filmed over the course of 20 years. And then we as activists, we were there on the ground together, working together for almost five years, and we filmed a lot. We had Rachel, the cinematographer and co-director of the film, who was shooting us. So there was an abundance of footage. The military entered {{sic|Basal's|nolink=y|expected=Basel's}} home twice and confiscated computers and cameras. So we were always very, very stressed. It was complicated logistically and quite stressful, but in the end we managed.</blockquote>The documentary was filmed over four years between 2019 and 2023, wrapping in October 2023.<ref>{{Cite AV media |url= https://www.nziff.co.nz/2024/tamaki-makaurau-auckland/no-other-land/ |title= No Other Land |language= en-GB |access-date= 2024-08-13 |via= www.nziff.co.nz |archive-date= 13 August 2024 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20240813034053/https://www.nziff.co.nz/2024/tamaki-makaurau-auckland/no-other-land/ |url-status= live }}</ref>

=== Criticisms and concerns ===
The film was condemned by both Israeli officials and the [[Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions]] (BDS) movement, for starkly different reasons.<ref name="Estrin-2025">{{cite news |last=Estrin |first=Daniel |date=2025-03-03 |title=Israel's culture minister calls a Palestinian-Israeli film's Oscar a 'sad moment' |url=https://www.npr.org/2025/03/03/nx-s1-5316043/israel-palestinian-reaction-no-other-land-documentary-oscars-2025 |access-date=2025-03-09 |work=NPR}}</ref><ref name="Tress-2025">{{cite web |last1=Tress |first1=Luke |last2=Magid |first2=Jacob |last3=Berman |first3=Lazar |last4=Freiberg |first4=Nava |date=2025-03-07 |title=BDS movement rejects Israeli-Palestinian Oscar winner 'No Other Land' |url=https://www.timesofisrael.com/bds-movement-rejects-israeli-palestinian-oscar-winner-no-other-land/?utm_source=The+Weekend+Edition&utm_campaign=weekend-edition-2025-03-09&utm_medium=email |access-date=2025-03-09 |website=The Times of Israel}}</ref>

==== Berlinale ====
[[File:MKr354249 Basel Adra and Yuval Abraham (Dokumentarfilmpreis, Berlinale 2024).jpg|thumb|upright|Basel Adra and Yuval Abraham holding their Berlinale Documentary Award]]
At the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]], ''No Other Land'' won the Berlinale Documentary Award and the Panorama Audience Award for Best Documentary Film.<ref name="Roxborough-2024" /> During his acceptance speech for the Berlinale Documentary Award, Abraham criticized Israel, saying:<ref>{{Cite web |last=Axelrod |first=Toby |date=2024-02-26 |title=Israeli journalist criticizes West Bank 'apartheid' at Berlin film festival ceremony marked by Gaza war protests |url=https://www.jta.org/2024/02/26/global/israeli-journalist-criticizes-west-bank-apartheid-at-berlin-film-festival-ceremony-marked-by-gaza-war-protests |access-date=2024-04-06 |website=Jewish Telegraphic Agency |language=en-US |archive-date=6 April 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240406130125/https://www.jta.org/2024/02/26/global/israeli-journalist-criticizes-west-bank-apartheid-at-berlin-film-festival-ceremony-marked-by-gaza-war-protests |url-status=live }}</ref>

{{Blockquote|text=We are standing in front of you now—me and Basel are the same age. I am Israeli; Basel is Palestinian. And in two days we will go back to a land where we are not equal. I am living under a civilian law and Basel is under military law. We live 30 minutes from one another, but I have voting rights; Basel is not having voting rights. I'm free to move where I want in this land; Basel is, like millions of Palestinians, locked in the occupied West Bank. This situation of apartheid between us, this inequality, it has to end.}}

In his acceptance speech, Adra said:<ref>{{Cite web |last=Goodman |first=Amy |date=April 5, 2024 |title=Israeli Director of "No Other Land" Slams Claims of Antisemitism After Berlin Film Fest Controversy |url=https://www.democracynow.org/2024/4/5/no_other_land |access-date=2024-04-06 |website=Democracy Now! |language=en |archive-date=6 April 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240406131104/https://www.democracynow.org/2024/4/5/no_other_land |url-status=live }}</ref>

{{Blockquote|text=It's our first movie since many years. My community, my family has been filming our community being erased by this brutal occupation. I am here celebrating the award, but also very hard for me to celebrate when there are tens of thousands of my people being slaughtered and massacred by Israel in Gaza. Masafer Yatta, my community, is being also razed by Israeli bulldozers. I ask one thing: for Germany, as I am in Berlin here, to respect the U.N. calls and stop sending weapons to Israel.}}

There were numerous [[Palestinian nationalism|pro-Palestine]] protests during the acceptance speeches and on the red carpet at the Berlinale—including from [[Golden Bear]] winner [[Mati Diop]].<ref name="Abbatescianni-2024" /><ref name="Ntim-2024" /> After the closing ceremony on 25 February 2024, an [[Instagram]] account linked to the Panorama section published an allegedly official statement from the festival organizers, demanding German authorities to [[arms embargo|withdraw arms supplies]] to Israel. Shortly afterward, the [[Berlinale]]'s main Instagram account stated that the Panorama account had been hacked and announced plans to "file criminal charges against unknown persons".<ref>{{cite news |last=Frater |first=Patrick |date=2024-02-26 |title=Berlin Film Festival Files Criminal Charges After Anti-Semitic Hacking, Criticizes Award-Winner Statements |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/news/berlin-festival-anti-semitic-hacking-award-winner-statements-1235923611/ |access-date=2024-02-26 |work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> [[Governing Mayor of Berlin|Berlin Mayor]] [[Kai Wegner]] said, "Anti-Semitism has no place in Berlin, and that also applies to the art scene", without specifying what parts of the festival he was referring to. On [[Twitter]], Wegner posted, "Berlin is firmly on Israel's side." In their speeches, Adra and Abraham called for a political solution to end the occupation, accused Israel of massacre, and criticised Germany for selling arms to Israel.<ref name="Ntim-2024">{{Cite news |last=Ntim |first=Zac |date=2024-02-26 |title=Berlin Mayor Criticizes "Anti-Semitic" Berlinale Closing Ceremony As Hackers Post Ceasefire Message To Fest's Social |url=https://deadline.com/2024/02/berlin-film-festival-hackers-gaza-ceasefire-kai-wegner-anti-semitism-1235838268/ |access-date=2024-02-26 |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |language=en-US |archive-date=27 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240227105413/https://deadline.com/2024/02/berlin-film-festival-hackers-gaza-ceasefire-kai-wegner-anti-semitism-1235838268/ |url-status=live }}</ref> The organizers said the "filmmakers' statements were independent and should be accepted as long as they respect the [[legality of Holocaust denial#Germany|legal framework]]".<ref>{{Cite news |date=2024-02-26 |title=Berlinale film festival marred by 'antisemitic' protests |url=https://www.euronews.com/culture/2024/02/26/berlinale-film-festival-marred-by-antisemitic-protests |access-date=2024-02-26 |work=[[euronews]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Vicente |first=Álex |date=2024-02-20 |title=The Berlinale on the war front: How the Israel–Palestine conflict undermined the film festival |url=https://english.elpais.com/culture/2024-02-20/the-berlinale-on-the-war-front-how-the-israel-palestine-conflict-undermined-the-film-festival.html |access-date=2024-02-26 |work=[[El País]] |language=en-us |archive-date=26 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240226171221/https://english.elpais.com/culture/2024-02-20/the-berlinale-on-the-war-front-how-the-israel-palestine-conflict-undermined-the-film-festival.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

Abraham told ''[[The Guardian]]'', "To stand on German soil as the son of Holocaust survivors and call for a ceasefire—and to then be labelled as antisemitic is not only outrageous, it is also literally putting Jewish lives in danger",<ref name="Oltermann-2024">{{Cite news |last=Oltermann |first=Philip |date=2024-02-27 |title=Israeli director receives death threats after officials call Berlin film festival 'antisemitic' |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2024/feb/27/israeli-director-receives-death-threats-after-officials-call-berlinale-antisemitic |access-date=2024-03-19 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref> and reported that his family in Israel had evacuated their home after "a right-wing Israeli mob" came in search of him.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Pita |first=Antonio |date=2024-03-02 |title='We will be waiting for you': Israeli filmmaker targeted by death threats after speech in Berlin |url=https://english.elpais.com/international/2024-03-02/we-will-be-waiting-for-you-israeli-filmmaker-targeted-by-death-threats-after-speech-in-berlin.html |access-date=2024-03-19 |website=El País English |language=en-us}}</ref> He was also concerned for the safety of Adra, who had since returned to the West Bank.<ref name="Oltermann-2024" />

==== Pro-Israeli criticism ====
Israeli culture minister [[Miki Zohar]] denounced the film's 2025 Oscar win as a "sad moment for the world of cinema",<ref name="Estrin-2025"/> adding: "Freedom of expression is an important value, but turning the defamation of Israel into a tool for international promotion is not art—it is sabotage against the State of Israel, especially in the wake of the October 7th massacre and the ongoing war".<ref>{{cite web |last=Fuster |first=Jeremy |date=2025-03-07 |title='No Other Land' Becomes Political Lightning Rod After Oscar Best Doc Win |url=https://www.thewrap.com/no-other-land-oscar-backlash/ |access-date=2025-03-09 |website=TheWrap}}</ref><ref name="Tress-2025"/> Roni Aboulafia of the [[Israeli Documentary Filmmakers Forum]] called Zohar's remarks "shameful", saying they reflected growing restrictions on free speech in Israel.<ref name="Estrin-2025"/> [[Jonathan Sacerdoti]] decried the film's use of an Israeli song, "{{Ill|Ein Li Eretz Acheret|he|אין לי ארץ אחרת}}" ("I Have No Other Land"), in what he deemed [[cultural appropriation]] and [[historical negationism|erasure]] of Jewish historical [[land claim]]s in the region.<ref>{{cite web |last=Sacerdoti |first=Jonathan |date=2025-03-05 |title=No Other Land isn't what it seems |url=https://www.spectator.co.uk/article/no-other-land-isnt-what-it-seems/ |access-date=2025-03-09 |website=[[The Spectator]]}}</ref> Israeli NGO Regavim said the film used a "concoction of misrepresentations and outright fabrications".<ref>{{cite web |last=Akiva |first=Kassy |date=2025-03-03 |title=The Truth About The Oscar-Winning Anti-Israel Documentary 'No Other Land' |url=https://www.dailywire.com/news/the-truth-about-the-oscar-winning-anti-israel-documentary-no-other-land |access-date=2025-04-28 |website=[[The Daily Wire]]}}</ref>

==== Pro-Palestinian criticism ====
The [[Palestinian Campaign for the Academic and Cultural Boycott of Israel]] (PACBI) declared that the film violates the BDS movement's [[anti-normalization]] guidelines on interaction with Israelis, saying, "Palestinians do not need validation, legitimation or permission from Israelis to narrate our history, our present, our experiences, our dreams, and our resistance."<ref name="Tress-2025" /> PACBI also argued that Hollywood has long dehumanized Palestinians, Arabs, Muslims, Black people, and Indigenous communities.<ref>{{cite web |last=Feigenbaum |first=Noa |date=2025-03-06 |title=BDS movement criticizes 'No Other Land,' citing Israeli collaboration in film's production |url=https://www.jpost.com/bds-threat/article-845064 |access-date=2025-03-09 |website=[[The Jerusalem Post]]}}</ref> In an interview with ''[[+972 Magazine]]'', the head of the Masafer Yatta village council, Nidal Younis, said he respected PACBI's criticism, but believed that "the pros outweigh the cons, and the film should not be boycotted. It tells our story, the Palestinian story. There is no Israeli story in it. Yuval is a true partner, and so are all the international and Jewish activists who sleep in Masafer Yatta and defend us from settler and army attacks."<ref name="Salaime-2025">{{Cite web |last=Salaime |first=Samah |date=2025-03-07 |title='No Other Land' is a victory for the Palestinian struggle |url=https://www.972mag.com/no-other-land-bds-masafer-yatta/ |access-date=2025-03-13 |website=[[+972 Magazine]] |language=en-US}}</ref> Other activists criticized PACBI's statement and endorsed the film, with the head of the [[Khirbet Susya]] village council, Jihad Al-Nawaja, saying she did not know "what the BDS people are talking about", calling Abraham "far more Palestinian than most of these online commentators attacking him", and asserting they would not be boycotting the film if they had actually come to live in the village.<ref name="Salaime-2025" />

== Violent incidents related to film ==
The settler who shoots Zakaria al-Adra at the end of the film was identified as Yitzhak Nir. Nir said he was acting in [[self-defense]] and was not charged by the police. Nir's father and uncle perpetrated the [[1983 Hebron University attack]] that killed three Palestinians and wounded 33 more. The Israeli military bulldozed Zakaria al-Adra's home in May 2024.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Nathaniel |first1=Jasper |author-link=Jasper Nathaniel |title=A Video Shows an Israeli Settler Shooting a Palestinian Point-Blank. 10 Months Later, No One Has Been Arrested |url=https://zeteo.com/p/israeli-settler-violence-palestinian-masafer-yatta |website=[[Zeteo]] |access-date=8 May 2025 |date=20 August 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Al Adara |first1=Shoug |title=A settler shot my husband.
