'''''Steal This Story, Please!''''' is a 2025 American documentary film which explores the life and career of [[Public service journalism|independent]] [[investigative journalist]] [[Amy Goodman]] and her work on the news program ''[[Democracy Now!]]''. It was directed by [[Academy Awards|Oscar]]-nominated, [[Emmy Awards]]-winning filmmakers [[Tia Lessin]] and [[Carl Deal]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://filmmakermagazine.com/132359-the-film-is-a-portrait-of-amy-goodman-but-its-also-a-celebration-of-resistance-carl-deal-and-tia-lessin-on-their-doc-nyc-and-idfa-debuting-steal-this-story-please/|title="The Film Is a Portrait of Amy Goodman, But It's Also a Celebration of Resistance": Carl Deal and Tia Lessin on their DOC NYC and IDFA-debuting Steal This Story, Please!|first=Lauren|last=Wissot|date=November 13, 2025|website=Filmmaker Magazine}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://forward.com/culture/film-tv/783022/steal-this-story-please-amy-goodman-jewish-democracy-now-documentary-review/|title=Amy Goodman has been a fearless journalist for more than four decades; she says her Jewish roots made her that way|first=Olivia|last=Haynie|date=November 12, 2025}}</ref> Musician and political activist [[Tom Morello]] ([[Rage Against the Machine]], [[Audioslave]]), as well as actresses and political activists [[Jane Fonda]]<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/jane-fonda-documentary-steal-this-story-please-1236544489/ |title=Jane Fonda Joins Documentary 'Steal This Story, Please!' as Executive Produce |last=Gardner |first=Chris |date= March 23, 2026 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |access-date=April 13, 2026}}</ref> and [[Rosario Dawson]], are among the executive producers.<ref name=world_premiere />

For its world premiere, ''Steal This Story, Please!'' opened the DC/DOX film festival in [[Washington, D.C.]] on June 12, 2025.<ref name=world_premiere>{{Cite web|url=https://dcdoxfest.com/films/steal-this-story-please/|title=Steal This Story, Please!|website=DC/DOX Film Festival 2025}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://variety.com/2025/film/news/democracy-now-amy-goodman-documentary-dc-dox-festival-1236377423/|title=Film About Democracy Now! Journalist Amy Goodman To Open Third Annual DC/DOX Festival (EXCLUSIVE)| date=April 30, 2025|website=Variety.com}}</ref> The international premiere took place on November 15, 2025, at the [[International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam]].<ref name=international_premiere>{{Cite web|url=https://festival.idfa.nl/en/film/a24a929a-fc4a-4059-aa0e-cbddc6ae9548/steal-this-story-please!/|title=Steal This Story, Please!| date=November 16, 2025|website=IDFA}}</ref> In both instances, the screening was followed by an in-person discussion involving Amy Goodman and filmmakers Carl Deal and Tia Lessin, about the importance of [[Public service journalism|independent journalism]].<ref name=world_premiere /><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.democracynow.org/events/2025/11/amy_goodman_at_idfa_screening_of_steal_this_story_please_1595|title=Amy Goodman at IDFA Screening of "Steal This Story, Please!"|website=DemocracyNow.org}}</ref>

==Release==
''Steal This Story, Please!'' was screened at the [[52nd Telluride Film Festival]] on August 31, 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-06-12 |title=52nd Telluride Film Festival Announces Lineup|url=https://www.telluridefilmfestival.org/ |access-date=2025-12-05 |website= Telluride Film Festival|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Davis |first=Clayton |date=2025-08-28 |title=Telluride Festival Lineup: 'Hamnet,' 'Springsteen' and 'Ballad of Small Player' Set for Premieres With Tributes for Ethan Hawke and Noah Baumbach |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/festivals/telluride-festival-lineup-2025-hamnet-springsteen-1236498020/ |access-date=2025-12-05 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film was presented in the True Stories section of the [[37th Palm Springs International Film Festival]] on January 3, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://psfilmfest.org/film-festival-2026/film-finder/steal-this-story-please |title= Steal This Story, Please!|date=3 December 2025|access-date=5 December 2025|website= [[Palm Springs International Film Festival]] |language=en}}</ref>
