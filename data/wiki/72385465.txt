{{nihongo|'''''The Island Closest to Heaven'''''|天国にいちばん近い島|Tengoku ni ichiban chikai shima|lead=yes}} is a 1984 [[Cinema of Japan|Japanese film]] directed by [[Nobuhiko Obayashi]], based on an original story by {{ill|Katsura Morimura|fr|Katsura Morimura}}.<ref name="Films"/>

==Plot==
After her father's sudden death, a Japanese [[Secondary education in Japan|high school]] girl travels to the southwest Pacific archipelago of [[New Caledonia]] in search of the location, which they had agreed to visit together, of the film's title. From her encounters with Japanese [[émigré]]s and locals, she learns where the real island closest to heaven may be found.<ref name="Films"/><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.radiotimes.com/movie-guide/b-jynq4p/the-island-closest-to-heaven/ |title=The Island Closest to Heaven |publisher=Radio Times |access-date=30 November 2022}}</ref>
