'''''The Woman Who Ran''''' ({{Korean/auto|hangul=^도망친 여자|rr=yes}}) is a 2020 South Korean [[drama film]] written, produced, directed, edited and scored by [[Hong Sang-soo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.newsis.com/view/?id=NISX20200214_0000919579|script-title=ko:홍상수 '도망친 여자' 단발머리 김민희 스틸 공개|date=14 February 2020|publisher=[[Newsis]]|language=ko|trans-title=Hong Sang-soo's 'The Woman Who Ran' Stills Reveal Short-Haired Kim Min-hee|access-date=14 February 2020}}</ref> It was selected to compete for the [[Golden Bear]] in the main competition section at the [[70th Berlin International Film Festival]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.berlinale.de/en/press/press-releases/detail_34516.html|title=The 70th Berlinale Competition and Further Films to Complete the Berlinale Special|publisher=[[Berlinale]]|access-date=29 January 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2020/film/festivals/berlin-film-festival-main-competition-lineup-1203485048/|title=Berlin Competition Lineup Revealed: Sally Potter, Kelly Reichardt, Eliza Hittman, Abel Ferrara|last=Dams|first=Tim|date=29 January 2020|work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=29 January 2020}}</ref> At Berlin, [[Hong Sang-soo]] won the [[Silver Bear for Best Director]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2020/film/news/berlin-film-festival-2020-award-winners-1203519894/ | title=Berlin Film Festival 2020: There Is No Evil Wins Golden Bear |work=Variety | date=29 February 2020 |access-date=1 March 2020}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
According to the [[review aggregator]] [[Metacritic]], which gave the film an average score of 79 out of 100 based on 10 critic reviews, the film received "generally favorable reviews".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-woman-who-ran |title=The Woman Who Ran |work=Metacritic |access-date=1 March 2020}}</ref> On review aggregator website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 98% based on 45 reviews, with an average rating of 8,1/10. The site's critical consensus reads, "Narratively slight yet cumulatively absorbing, ''The Woman Who Ran'' finds writer-director Hong Sang-soo continuing to work in a beguilingly minor key."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_woman_who_ran|title=The Woman Who Ran|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|publisher=[[Fandango Media|Fandango]]|access-date={{RT data|access date|df=dmy}}}}</ref>
