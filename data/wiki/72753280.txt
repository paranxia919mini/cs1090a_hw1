'''''Longlegs''''' is a 2024 American [[Psychological horror (film and television)|psychological horror film]] written and directed by [[Osgood Perkins]]. The film stars [[Maika Monroe]], [[Blair Underwood]], [[Alicia Witt]], and [[Nicolas Cage]]. Set in the 1990s, the story follows a [[Federal Bureau of Investigation]] (FBI) agent assigned to hunt down an occult-obsessed [[serial killer]] responsible for a string of family murders across [[Oregon]]. The killer is dubbed Longlegs, and baffles the FBI with his perfect ability to seemingly leave zero trace of involvement at the crime scenes.

''Longlegs'' premiered in Los Angeles at the [[Grauman's Egyptian Theatre]] on July 8, 2024, and was released theatrically in the United States by [[Neon (company)|Neon]] on July 12, 2024. It received positive reviews and grossed $128 million worldwide on a budget of less than $10 million,<ref name="Opening"/> becoming Neon's highest-grossing film domestically, the highest-grossing independent film of 2024, and Perkins's highest-grossing film.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Saperstein |first=Pat |date=August 16, 2024 |title='Longlegs' Crosses $100 Million at Box Office to Become 2024's Highest-Grossing Indie Film |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/box-office/longlegs-100-million-box-office-highest-grossing-indie-1236109098/ |access-date=August 20, 2024 |website=Variety |archive-date=August 17, 2024 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20240817033132/https://variety.com/2024/film/box-office/longlegs-100-million-box-office-highest-grossing-indie-1236109098/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Plot==
In 1974 [[Oregon]], a young girl with a [[Polaroid camera]] follows a mysterious voice and encounters an erratic man in pale makeup.

Twenty years later, [[FBI]] agent Lee Harker is assigned by her supervisor, William Carter, to a case involving a series of [[murder–suicide]]s in Oregon. Each case consists of a father killing his family, then himself, leaving behind a letter with [[Satanism|Satanic]] glyphs signed "Longlegs", whose handwriting belongs to none of the family members. Lee exhibits [[Psychic detective|possible clairvoyance]] and manages to decode Longlegs' letters.

Further investigation reveals that each family had a 9-year-old daughter born on the 14th day of the month. The murders all occurred within six days before or after said birthday, and their respective dates form an [[occult]] triangle symbol on a calendar, with one date missing. While at home talking on the phone to her mother, Ruth, Lee receives a coded birthday card from Longlegs, warning her that revealing its source will lead to her mother's murder.

Following a clue, Lee and William discover a doll containing a high-energy metal orb inside its head. After visiting a [[Psychiatric hospital|mental hospital]] to question Carrie Anne Camera, the sole survivor of Longlegs' murders who was visited previously by someone using Lee's name, William suspects Lee has a connection to Longlegs. Discovering that Ruth had filed a police report of an intruder approaching Lee the day before her 9th birthday, William instructs Lee to visit her. Ruth directs Lee to her childhood belongings, where she finds a Polaroid that reveals Longlegs to be the man who visited a young Lee on her birthday in the opening sequence.

Lee submits the photo, leading to Longlegs' arrest. Realizing the missing calendar date is that day, Lee fears an unknown accomplice of Longlegs will carry out the murder. In an interrogation room, Longlegs claims to serve "[[Satan|the man downstairs]]" and hints at Ruth's involvement in the murders before [[Suicide|taking his own life]] by repeatedly bashing his face and forehead onto the metal table. Shortly after leaving the interrogation room, Lee is informed by William that Carrie Anne has committed suicide.

Agent Browning drives Lee to Ruth's home. Browning waits in the car outside while Lee searches the house, but Ruth approaches the car and fatally shoots Browning before destroying a doll resembling a young Lee, causing Lee to lose consciousness. In a vision, Lee discovers that she had been threatened by Longlegs as a child, who only spared her after Ruth pleaded for her life. In return for sparing Lee, Longlegs demanded Ruth's servitude; Ruth agreed, allowing Longlegs to live in the Harker basement and create Satanic dolls that Ruth, posing as a [[nun]], delivered to households, causing each family's patriarch to commit [[familicide]]. Lee's doll blocked her memories of Longlegs while influencing her with his magic.

Lee awakens in the basement and answers the phone, where a demonic voice warns her about William's daughter Ruby's 9th birthday party, scheduled for that day. Lee rushes to save the Carters, whose deaths would complete Longlegs' triangle. She finds Ruth has already delivered the doll and possessed the family. After William murders his wife, Anna, Lee fatally shoots him to protect Ruby. Ruth lunges at Ruby with a dagger, forcing Lee to kill her. Lee tries to destroy the doll, but her revolver is now empty. Lee puts her gun down, telling Ruby they must go, but both stare continuously at the doll. 
Longlegs, alone in his cell where he previously took his own life, giddily sings "Happy Birthday" to Lee Harker. Closing his eyes, he then says "Hail, Satan" and smooches the air.

==Production==
The film was announced in November 2022, with [[Osgood Perkins]] as writer and director.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wiseman |first=Andreas |date=November 11, 2022 |title=Nicolas Cage To Star In Horror-Thriller 'Longlegs' For C2, Automatik & Cage's Saturn Films; 'Sinister', 'La La Land' Producers & 'Joker' Exec Among Team |url=https://deadline.com/2022/11/nicolas-cage-horror-thriller-longlegs-sinister-la-la-land-joker-producers-execs-1235166010/ |access-date=January 14, 2023 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |archive-date=January 15, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230115020309/https://deadline.com/2022/11/nicolas-cage-horror-thriller-longlegs-sinister-la-la-land-joker-producers-execs-1235166010/ |url-status=live}}</ref> He drew inspiration from several sources; the plot device of the evil dolls that cause fathers to murder their families was loosely inspired by the [[killing of JonBenét Ramsey]]: "The murder took place approaching Christmas, and one present that the parents had gotten for JonBenét was a life-size replica doll of herself, wearing one of her pageant dresses. It was in a cardboard box in the basement, 15 feet from where she was killed, and there was something so insane about that, I'd cataloged it away."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://screenrant.com/longlegs-real-life-inspiration-director-explained/|work=[[Screen Rant]]|title=Real-Life Inspiration For Major Longlegs Twist Explained By Director Osgood Perkins|last=Northrup|first=Ryan|date=July 15, 2024|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240817033958/https://screenrant.com/longlegs-real-life-inspiration-director-explained/|archive-date=August 17, 2024|url-status=live}}</ref>

Perkins told ''[[People (magazine)|People]]'' magazine that the film was inspired by his mother's treatment towards his father [[Anthony Perkins|Anthony Perkins']] sexuality, shielding the public and the Perkins children from knowledge of his homosexual relationships. Oz Perkins was quoted, saying, "Your mother can protect you from a truth that she thinks is unsavory{{nbs}}[...] and then you just build out a crazy movie around that."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Smart |first=Jack |date=July 14, 2024 |title=Longlegs Director Explains Movie's Personal Connection to His Dad, Psycho Horror Icon Anthony Perkins (Exclusive) |url=https://people.com/how-longlegs-personal-osgood-perkins-exclusive-8677416 |access-date=October 3, 2024 |website=People.com |language=en |archive-date=February 2, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250202150502/https://people.com/how-longlegs-personal-osgood-perkins-exclusive-8677416 |url-status=live }}</ref>

=== Box office ===
''Longlegs'' grossed $74.3 million in the United States and Canada, and $53.6 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $128&nbsp;million.<ref name="BOM">{{Cite Box Office Mojo|id=tt23468450|title=Longlegs|access-date=November 2, 2024}}</ref><ref name="NUM">{{Cite The Numbers|id=Longlegs-(2024)|title=Longlegs – Financial Information|access-date=November 1, 2024|website=[[The Numbers (website)|The Numbers]]}}</ref> ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' calculated the film made a net profit of $48{{nbsp}}million.<ref name=profit>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2025/04/most-profitable-movies-2024-biggest-returns-1236379096/|title=Smaller Movies, Big Profit: Horror Is All The ROI Rage In 2024's Most Valuable Blockbuster Tournament|date=April 29, 2025|first=Anthony|last=D'Alessandro|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|access-date=April 30, 2025}}</ref>

In the United States and Canada, ''Longlegs'' was released alongside ''[[Fly Me to the Moon (2024 film)|Fly Me to the Moon]]'', and was projected to gross $7–9 million from 2,510 theaters in its opening weekend.<ref>{{cite web |last=D'Alessandro |first=Anthony |date=July 9, 2024 |title=Gru To Rule With $33M+ As 'Fly Me To The Moon', 'Longlegs' Provide Depth To Weekend Box Office – Preview |url=https://deadline.com/2024/07/channing-tatum-box-office-fly-me-to-the-moon-despicable-me-4-1236004684/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240714211041/https://deadline.com/2024/07/channing-tatum-box-office-fly-me-to-the-moon-despicable-me-4-1236004684/ |archive-date=July 14, 2024 |access-date=July 11, 2024 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]}}</ref> After making $10 million on its first day (including $3 million from Thursday night previews, both records for Neon), weekend estimates were raised to $20–23 million.<ref>{{cite news |last=Moreau |first=Jordan |date=July 12, 2024 |title=Box Office: 'Longlegs' Makes $3 Million in Previews, Surpassing 'Immaculate' to Set Neon Record |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/box-office/box-office-longlegs-previews-neon-1236068814/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240712161039/https://variety.com/2024/film/box-office/box-office-longlegs-previews-neon-1236068814/ |archive-date=July 12, 2024 |access-date=July 12, 2024 |publisher=Variety}}</ref> It went on to debut to $22.4 million, finishing second at the box-office behind holdover ''[[Despicable Me 4]]''.<ref>{{cite web |title=Domestic 2024 Weekend 28 |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2024W28/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240715185340/https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2024W28/ |archive-date=July 15, 2024 |access-date=July 16, 2024 |website=[[Box Office Mojo]]}}</ref> The opening marked the best opening weekend for Neon and the biggest total for an original 2024 horror film. It was Monroe's best domestic opening as lead (excluding 2016's ''[[Independence Day: Resurgence]]'', for which she was [[Billing (performing arts)|billed]]) and Cage's first live-action film to open above $20 million since ''[[Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance]]'' in 2012.<ref name="Opening"/> In its second weekend, the film made $12 million, a drop of 46.6% to finish in fourth.<ref>{{cite web |title=Domestic 2024 Weekend 29 |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2024W29/ |access-date=July 28, 2024 |website=[[Box Office Mojo]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=D'Alessandro |first=Anthony |date=July 22, 2024 |title='Twisters' Even Bigger With $81M+ Opening – Monday AM Box Office Update |url=https://deadline.com/2024/07/box-office-twisters-1236015127/ |access-date=July 22, 2024 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |archive-date=July 22, 2024 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20240722022436/https://deadline.com/2024/07/box-office-twisters-1236015127/ |url-status=live }}</ref> In its third weekend, it became Neon's highest-grossing film after surpassing their earnings for 2019's ''[[Parasite (2019 film)|Parasite]]'' ($53.37 million) and added $6.8 million.<ref>{{cite news |last=Goldsmith |first=Jill |date=July 28, 2024 |title=It's Official, ''Longlegs'' Is Neon's Top Grossing Film Ever; Sean Wang's ''Didi'' Sees Nice Open In Limited Release – Specialty Box Office |url=https://deadline.com/2024/07/indie-film-box-office-longlegs-neon-didi-the-fabulous-four-1236025156/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://archive.today/20240804194112/https://deadline.com/2024/07/indie-film-box-office-longlegs-neon-didi-the-fabulous-four-1236025156/ |archive-date=August 4, 2024 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Domestic 2024 Weekend 30 |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2024W30/ |access-date=August 5, 2024 |website=[[Box Office Mojo]] |archive-date=August 5, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240805041943/https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/2024W30/ |url-status=live }}</ref> On August 16, 2024, the film had surpassed $100 million worldwide, making it the highest-grossing independent film of the year to date.<ref name=":0" />

===Audience reception===
Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average grade of "C+" on an A+ to F scale. Those polled by [[PostTrak]] gave it a 70% overall positive score, with an average 3 out of 5 stars.<ref name="Opening" /> 
 
A correspondent of [[LGBT]] magazine ''[[Them (website)|Them]]'' made note of how some audience members perceived the characterization of the film's villain to be [[homophobic]] or [[transphobic]] due to the character's androgynous characteristics.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Samantha |date=July 17, 2024 |title=Is Nicolas Cage's New Movie Longlegs "Queerphobic"? An Investigation |url=https://www.them.us/story/longlegs-movie-queerphobic-criticism-oz-perkins |access-date=October 3, 2024 |website=[[Them (website)|Them]] |language=en-US |archive-date=October 1, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241001062516/https://www.them.us/story/longlegs-movie-queerphobic-criticism-oz-perkins |url-status=live }}</ref> A CNN opinion piece accused the film of transphobia, comparing Longlegs to [[Buffalo Bill (The Silence of the Lambs)|Buffalo Bill]] of ''[[The Silence of the Lambs (film)|The Silence of the Lambs]]'', and emphasizing the [[LGBTQ themes in horror fiction|horror genre's complicated history of LGBT themes]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Berlatsky |first=Noah |date=July 13, 2024 |title=Opinion: It's time more horror films push back against queer stereotypes |url=https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/13/opinions/longlegs-trans-queer-identities-stereotypes-hollywood-berlatsky/index.html |access-date=October 3, 2024 |website=[[CNN]] |language=en |archive-date=October 7, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241007094726/https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/13/opinions/longlegs-trans-queer-identities-stereotypes-hollywood-berlatsky/index.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

In a [[Reddit]] [[r/IAmA|AMA]] interview with director Oz Perkins, a fan inquired if the writing of ''Longlegs'' was intended to be a negative portrayal of a transgender person. Perkins responded by denouncing anti-trans behavior, but did not specifically elaborate about any element of the film.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Monteil |first=Abby |date=August 15, 2024 |title='Longlegs' Director Says "Anyone Who is Anti-Trans Is a F**king Piece of Sh*t Idiot" |url=https://www.them.us/story/longlegs-director-oz-perkins-queerphobic-discourse-statement-reddit |access-date=September 13, 2024 |website=[[Them (website)|Them]] |language=en-US |archive-date=October 7, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241007091553/https://www.them.us/story/longlegs-director-oz-perkins-queerphobic-discourse-statement-reddit |url-status=live }}</ref>

Director [[Guillermo del Toro|Guillermo Del Toro]] complimented the film, praising "[Perkins'] metronome, his meticulous composition and his uncanny sense of evil and impending doom." [[Paul Schrader]] commented on the film on social media, questioning why directors like Perkins and [[Ti West]] were "confined to the horror genre ghetto."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bergeson |first=Samantha |date=July 16, 2024 |title=Guillermo del Toro Praises 'Longlegs': 'A Great Oz Perkins Film' and '100 Percent His Vibe' |url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/general-news/guillermo-del-toro-praises-longlegs-oz-perkins-1235022277/ |access-date=October 3, 2024 |website=[[IndieWire]] |language=en-US}}</ref>
