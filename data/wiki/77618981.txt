'''''The Beast Within''''' is a 2024 British [[Mystery fiction|mystery]] [[Thriller film|horror-thriller film]] directed by Alexander J. Farrell, co-written by Greer Ellison, Alexander J. Farrell, and produced by Merlin Merton, Martin Owen, Karl Hall, Ryan Hamilton and Jack Christian. The film stars [[Kit Harington]], [[Ashleigh Cummings]], James Cosmo, and [[Caoilinn Springall]]. It was released on 26 July 2024.

== Synopsis ==

"After a series of strange events leads her to question her family’s isolated life on a fortified compound deep in the English wilds, 10-year-old Willow follows her parents on one of their secret late-night treks to the heart of the ancient forest. But upon witnessing her father undergo a terrible transformation, she too becomes ensnared by the dark ancestral secret they’ve tried so desperately to conceal."<ref>{{cite web | url=https://wellgousa.com/films/beast-within | title=The Beast Within | work=wellgousa.com | publisher=[[Well Go USA Entertainment]] | accessdate=February 23, 2026 }}</ref>

== Production ==
''The Beast Within'' was filmed in Harewood Woods and Castle in West [[Yorkshire]] England, chosen for its atmospheric and eerie setting with its Structures dating back to the 14th century.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Harvey |first=Dennis |date=2024-07-26 |title='The Beast Within' Review: Kit Harington Gets Hairy (and Bare) in a Handsome English Werewolf Thriller |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/the-beast-within-review-1236080828/ |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref> The film's director, Alexander J. Farrell, aimed to create a sense of dread and suspense, using practical effects and minimal [[Computer animation|''CGI'']] to bring the beast to life. The script, written by Greer Ellison, draws inspiration from classic horror films and folklore, blending elements of mystery and psychological terror.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Nichols |first=Tyler |date=2024-07-23 |title=The Beast Within Review (Fantasia 2024) |url=https://www.joblo.com/the-beast-within-review-fantasia-2024/ |access-date=2024-08-13 |website=JoBlo |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Filming ===
[[Principal photography]] for ''The Beast Within'' began in Yorkshire, England with the working title "What Remains of Us" in May 2023 and continued over several months.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=2024-07-22 |title=The Beast Within (2024) Filming Locations and Cast Information |url=https://www.filmiloc.com/the-beast-within-2024-filming-locations-and-cast-information/ |access-date=2024-08-14 |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Release and reception ==
''The Beast Within'' premiered at the [[Fantasia International Film Festival|Fantasia Film Festival]] on 26 July 2024, where it received positive reviews for its suspenseful storytelling and effective use of practical effects. Critics praised the performances of the lead actors, particularly [[Kit Harington]]'s portrayal of Noah and [[Caoilinn Springall]]'s role as Willow.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mintzer |first=Jordan |date=2024-07-26 |title='The Beast Within' Review: Kit Harington's Psychological Werewolf Flick Has More Bark Than Bite |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/the-beast-within-review-kit-harington-1235952897/ |access-date=2024-08-13 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Baughan |first=Nikki |title='The Beast Within': Fantasia Review |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/the-beast-within-fantasia-review/5195640.article |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=Screen |language=en}}</ref>

Tyler Nichols of ''[[JoBlo.com]]'' praised Caoillinn Springall's performance, which is as impressive.<ref name=":0" /> Aaron B. Peterson of ''The Hollywood Outsider'' gave the film 2/5 stars.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Peterson |first=Aaron B. |date=2024-07-25 |title=The Beast Within (2024) {{!}} Film Review |url=https://www.thehollywoodoutsider.com/the-beast-within-2024-film-review/ |access-date=2024-08-13 |website=The Hollywood Outsider - Film and Television Podcast {{!}} Reviews |language=en-US}}</ref>  Film pundit, John Dotson of ''The Cosmic Circus'' described the film as a Gothic Tale of Childhood Trauma.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dotson |first=John |date=2024-08-05 |title=Fantasia Fest 2024 Movie Review: The Beast Within |url=https://thecosmiccircus.com/fantasia-fest-2024-review-the-beast-within-kit-harrington/ |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=The Cosmic Circus |language=en-US}}</ref>

On the review aggregator website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], ''The Beast Within'' holds an approval rating of 40% based on 42 reviews, with an average rating of 5.3/10. The site's consensus states: "Somewhere inside ''The Beast Within'' lies a clever riff on the werewolf genre, but it struggles to claw through a lack of incident and specificity."<ref>{{Cite web |title=''The Beast Within'' |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_beast_within_2024 |access-date=November 21, 2024 |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]] |publisher=[[Fandango Media]] |language=en}}</ref>

The film's soundtrack, composed by Jack Halama and Nathan W Klein, features an eerie and haunting score that enhances the film's suspenseful atmosphere. The soundtrack was released on digital streaming platforms and received critical acclaim for its contribution to the film's mood and tone.<ref>{{Cite web |title=The Beast Within (2024) - Soundtrack.Net |url=https://www.soundtrack.net/movie/the-beast-within-2024/ |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=www.soundtrack.net}}</ref>
