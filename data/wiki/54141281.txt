'''Claes Kasper Bang''' ({{IPA|da|ˈkʰlɛˀs ˈpɑŋˀ|lang}}; born 28 April 1967<ref name=lowdown />) is a Danish actor and musician. He is best known for playing the leading role of Christian in [[Ruben Östlund]]'s 2017 film ''[[The Square (2017 film)|The Square]]'', which won the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[Cannes Film Festival]], and for the role of Fjölnir in [[Robert Eggers]]'s 2022 film ''[[The Northman]]''.

In 2019, he played the role of Sasha Mann for the final season of ''[[The Affair (TV series)|The Affair]]'', and he rose to further international recognition by starring as [[Count Dracula]] in the [[BBC]]/[[Netflix]] series ''[[Dracula (2020 TV series)|Dracula]]'' (2020). For his role in ''The Square'', he became the first Dane to win the [[European Film Award for Best Actor]].

Bang has also developed a music career under the moniker of '''This Is Not America'''.

==Early life and education ==
Claes Kasper Bang<ref>{{Cite web |last=Yuan |first=Jada |date=2017-11-03 |title=Meet Claes Bang, Star of The Square and Your New James Bond–ian Danish Crush |url=https://www.vulture.com/2017/11/meet-the-squares-claes-bang-your-new-danish-crush.html |access-date=2025-07-28 |website=Vulture |language=en}}</ref> was born in [[Odense]] on the Danish island of [[Funen]]. After his parents' divorce, he mostly lived with his father and moved home frequently, attending several schools while growing up.<ref name=":7">{{Cite web|title=Big Bang |url=http://camillaalfthan.com/wp-content/uploads/ClaesBang.pdf|last=Alfthan |first=Camilla|date=2018|language=no|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref><ref name=":8">{{Cite web|title=Appetize Magazine No. 1 |url=https://issuu.com/dna1/docs/appetize_aalborg01_2010_web|date=17 February 2010|language=da|format=PDF|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Claes Bang: Der Späti|url=https://www.zeit.de/zeit-magazin/2018/08/claes-bang-schauspieler-the-square-maennermode|date=14 February 2018|website=Zeit Magazin|language=de|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref> 

During his third year of high school, Bang participated in the musical ''[[Hair (musical)|Hair]]'', that began his interest in acting, and after he left school he continued to do amateur theatre. At the age of 23, he made an application to the [[Danish National School of Theatre and Contemporary Dance|Danish National School of Theatre]] after discovering that the age limit for applying is 25. He was unsuccessful but applied again the next year and was accepted five days before his 25th birthday. He attended the school in 1992 and graduated with a diploma in 1996.<ref name=":8" /><ref name=":7" /><ref>{{Cite web|title=Claes Bang: Niemals stillstehen|url=https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/kultur-vergnuegen/claes-bang-niemals-stillstehen-li.79612|last=Zeitung|first=Berliner|date=29 March 2020|website=Berliner Zeitung|language=de-DE|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref>

==Acting career==
[[File:Claes Bang 2015-09-10.jpg|thumb|Bang in 2015]]
Bang's first theatre role after graduation was at Børneteatret Skægspire in the play ''Tudseakvariet'', and he received his first breakthrough role in the Danish 1997 television show ''[[Taxa (TV series)|Taxa]]''.<ref name=lowdown>{{Cite web|last=Clarke|first=Emma|url=https://www.standard.co.uk/showbiz/celebrity-news/claes-bang-dracula-actor-age-movies-tv-shows-a4324551.html|title=The lowdown on Dracula star Claes Bang|date=2 January 2020|website=Evening Standard|language=en|access-date=12 January 2020}}</ref> 

From 2001 to 2004, he was a permanent member of the cast at [[Aalborg Teater]]. In 2002, he asked the theatre director for permission to perform a monologue called ''Ondskaben'', which is based on [[Jan Guillou]]'s novel ''[[Evil (novel)|Evil]]''. After leaving the theatre in 2004, Bang bought the rights to the work so he could perform the monologue at other theatres. He has performed the monologue almost 400 times, including an English language version in Denmark, Sweden, the UK, Germany, and South Africa.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Claes Bang optræder i folks lejligheder med sin bedste rolle |url=https://www.bt.dk/content/item/1363706|date=20 December 2019|website=www.bt.dk|language=da|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref><ref name=":8" /><ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.dfi.dk/Service/English/News-and-publications/News/May-2017/Claes-Bang-in-The-Square-Ruben-Ostlund.aspx|title=Claes Bang in The Square|publisher=[[Danish Film Institute]]|date=11 May 2017|access-date=25 May 2017}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Fierce Fresh Fabulous |url=http://theatreartsadmincollective.weebly.com/fierce-fresh-fabulous.html |website=Theatre Arts Admin Collective|language=en|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref>

Bang starred in numerous Danish television series and films early in his career. From 2006 to 2008, he gained wide recognition (particularly in Germany) for his role in the Danish police drama ''[[Anna Pihl]]''.<ref name=":8" /> He subsequently starred in several German language roles both on television (''[[Küstenwache (TV series)|Küstenwache]]'', ''[[SOKO Wismar]]'', ''[[Notruf Hafenkante]]'', ''Sibel & Max'', and ''[[Cologne P.D.]]'') and in film (''[[Jungle Child (film)|Jungle Child]]'', ''Rettet Raffi!'', ''Alle unter einer Tanne'', and ''Überleben an der Scheidungsfront'').{{cn|date=July 2025}} 

He played the leading role of the art museum curator Christian in [[Ruben Östlund]]'s 2017 film ''[[The Square (2017 film)|The Square]]'', which won the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2017 Cannes Film Festival]].<ref name="screen">{{Cite web|last=Mitchell|first=Wendy|date=23 May 2017 |url=http://www.screendaily.com/features/cannes-interview-the-square-actor-claes-bang/5118362.article?blocktitle=FEATURES&contentID=43731|title=Cannes Interview: 'The Square' actor Claes Bang|work=[[Screen Daily]]|access-date=25 May 2017}}</ref> He also won the [[European Film Award for Best Actor]], becoming the first Dane to do so.<ref>{{cite web|author1=/ritzau/|title=Claes Bang Wins Europe's Version of the Oscars' "Best Actor Award" |url=https://www.b.dk/globalt/claes-bang-vinder-europas-svar-paa-en-oscar-for-bedste-mandlige-hovedrolle|website=B.dk|publisher=Berlingske|access-date=10 December 2017|language=da|date=9 December 2017}}</ref>

In November 2018, Bang was cast to play [[Count Dracula]] in the [[BBC]] mini-series ''[[Dracula (2020 TV series)|Dracula]],'' created by the film-makers involved in ''[[Sherlock (TV series)|Sherlock]]''.<ref>[https://deadline.com/2018/11/claes-bang-dracula-1202510162/ 'The Girl in the Spider's Web' Star Claes Bang Stakes Claim As Dracula In Hartswood's Netflix & BBC Drama]</ref> In an interview for ''[[The Guardian]]'', it was observed that, while "sexy" and "insatiable", it is also "a very witty performance" from Bang.<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.theguardian.com/tv-and-radio/2019/dec/17/claes-bang-dracula-interview-sherlock|title=Claes Bang on playing a sexy Dracula: 'He's insatiable – and not just for blood!'|last=Gilbey|first=Ryan|date=17 December 2019|work=The Guardian|access-date=12 January 2020|language=en-GB|issn=0261-3077}}</ref>

On 14 April 2020, it was announced that Bang would be working on the pilot of ''Little Room'' (previously called ''The Agoraphobics Detective Society''), which was produced by Maggie Montieth and directed/written by his ''Dracula'' co-star [[Dolly Wells]], with Suzi Ewing and Heidi Greensmith. Bang and the other cast members filmed themselves from home on [[Zoom Video Communications|Zoom]], and were responsible for the props, wardrobe, hair and makeup, and sound.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Claes Bang, Brian Cox and more to film whodunnit series pilot from home|url=https://www.radiotimes.com/news/tv/2020-04-14/brian-cox-claes-bang-agoraphobics-detective-society-pilot/|date=14 April 2020|website=Radio Times|language=en|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Little Room – A Whodunnit For Today |url=https://pinpointpresents.com/littleroom/|language=en-GB|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Brian Cox, Claes Bang And More Starring In The Agoraphobics Detective Society|url=https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/brian-cox-claes-bang-agoraphobics-society/|date=15 April 2020|website=Empire|language=en|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref> The pilot was released online by Pinpoint Presents on 27 May 2020, and viewers were asked to donate to [[The Film and Television Charity]] and the [[Motion Picture & Television Fund]], to help those in the film, TV and cinema industries who have been financially impacted by the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Dracula's Claes Bang and Brian Cox star in new charity lockdown pilot |url=https://www.radiotimes.com/news/on-demand/2020-05-27/little-room-claes-bang-brian-cox/|date=27 May 2020|website=Radio Times|language=en|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref>

In 2022, Bang starred in the [[Apple TV (streaming service)|Apple TV]] comedy drama series ''[[Bad Sisters]]'' as the cruel husband John Paul (JP), whose funeral the series revolves around.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Hævnfantasier gjort til virkelighed: Hvad gør man, når man har fået verdens værste svoger? During the shooting period, Bang lived in isolation in his apartment in [[Belfast]], and only went outside for shooting the film.<ref name=":10" /> 

In 2024, Bang starred in the [[Apple TV (streaming service)|Apple TV]]s drama series ''[[The New Look (TV series)|The New Look]]'' where he portrayed nazi officer and [[Coco Chanel]]s lover, [[Hans Günther von Dincklage]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Den stjernespækkede ’The New Look’ med Ben Mendelsohn og Claes Bang serverer modedrama for alle pengene – se traileren|url=https://soundvenue.com/film/2024/01/den-stjernespaekkede-the-new-look-med-ben-mendelsohn-og-claes-bang-serverer-modedrama-for-alle-pengene-se-traileren-554384|website=SOUNDVENUE.COM|date=2024-01-18|access-date=2026-01-29|language=da-DK|first=Owen|last=Murphy}}</ref> He appeared in six out of ten episodes in the series.

In 2024, Bang portrayed the [[Switzerland|Swiss]] mythical figure [[William Tell]] in [[Nick Hamm]]‘s [[William Tell (2024 film)|movie with the same name]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Se Claes Bang som hævngerrig mesterskytte i traileren til ‘William Tell’|url=https://soundvenue.com/film/2025/01/se-claes-bang-som-haevngerrig-mesterskytte-i-william-tell-605784|website=SOUNDVENUE.COM|date=2025-01-16|access-date=2026-01-29|language=da-DK|first=Mathilde Johanne|last=Kristiansen}}</ref> Bang later stated that he was happy to have been cast for the part and play the hero after having starred in ''[[Bad Sisters]]'' (2022) and ''[[The Northman]]'' (2022).<ref name=":7" /> For the film, Bang had to learn how to use and shoot a [[crossbow]].<ref name=":7" />  

In 2025, Bang portrayed the [[Danish Realm|Danish]] architect [[Johan Otto von Spreckelsen]] in the historic drama ''[[The Great Arch]]'' which was based on the creation of the [[Grande Arche]] in Paris in the 1980s.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Den nye triumfbue|url=https://www.ekkofilm.dk/anmeldelser/den-nye-triumfbue/|website=www.ekkofilm.dk|access-date=2026-01-29|language=da}}</ref> Bang had to learn French for the part, a skill which he was later praised for by his co-actor [[Sidse Babett Knudsen]] who herself is fluent in French.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Sidse Babett Knudsen om dansk kollega i ny fransk film: "Han er jo vanvittig" {{!}} Kino.dk|url=https://kino.dk/nyheder/sidse-babett-knudsen-om-dansk-kollega-i-ny-fransk-film-han-er-jo-vanvittig|website=kino.dk|access-date=2026-01-29}}</ref> The movie received critical acclaim,<ref>{{Cite web|title='Sådan var min far ikke': Ny film om dansk arkitekt får hård kritik af hans egen søn|url=https://www.dr.dk/nyheder/kultur/saadan-var-min-far-ikke-ny-film-om-dansk-arkitekt-faar-haard-kritik-af-hans-egen-soen|website=DR|date=2025-12-19|access-date=2026-01-29|language=da-DK}}</ref> and Bang received his first nomination for the [[César Award]]s [[César Award for Best Actor|Best Actor]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=César 2026: 18 films. 61 nominations. Congratulations to the 2025 Official Selection!|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/press/press-releases/cesar-202618-films-61-nominations-congratulations-to-the-2025-official-selection/|website=Festival de Cannes|date=2026-01-28|access-date=2026-01-29|language=en-US}}</ref>

In 2026, Bang starred in [[Ángel Manuel Soto|Angel Manuel Soto]]’s action comedy ''[[The Wrecking Crew (2026 film)|The Wrecking Crew]]'', where he as the leader of a [[yakuza]]-gang acted opposite [[Jason Momoa]] and [[Dave Batista]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Se Claes Bang som skurk over for Jason Momoa og Dave Bautista i trailer til ny actionbasker|url=https://soundvenue.com/musik/2026/01/se-claes-bang-som-skurk-over-for-jason-momoa-og-dave-bautista-i-traileren-til-actionbaskeren-the-wrecking-crew-657564|website=SOUNDVENUE.COM|date=2026-01-09|access-date=2026-01-29|language=da-DK}}</ref>

==Music==
This Is Not America is the name of Bang's musical side project.<ref name=":0">{{cite web|title=Sceneliv Magasinet |url=http://skuespillerforbundet.dk/fileadmin/user_upload/documents/Nyheder/Sceneliv/2010/Sceneliv_Februar_2010.pdf|date=February 2010|publisher=Dansk Skuespillerforbund|language=da|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> He describes it as "a sanctuary and a creative playground where there is no director making the decisions."<ref>{{Cite web|title=This Is Not America|url=https://open.spotify.com/artist/2VlxJZZG5aPHnlPzbtNflE|website=Spotify|language=en|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> Bang writes and composes the songs himself, and his musical style can be described as a mix of [[Gangway (band)|Gangway]], [[Pet Shop Boys]] and [[Depeche Mode]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite news|last=Gilbey|first=Ryan|date=17 December 2019|title=Claes Bang on playing a sexy Dracula: 'He's insatiable – and not just for blood!'|language=en-GB|work=The Guardian |url=https://www.theguardian.com/tv-and-radio/2019/dec/17/claes-bang-dracula-interview-sherlock|access-date=18 May 2020|issn=0261-3077}}</ref><ref name=":2">{{Cite web|title="this is not america" – synthpop som i 80'erne |url=https://kulturforunge.dk/2009/03/12/this-is-not-america/|date=12 March 2009 |website=Kulture for Unge|language=da-DK|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> Music videos and album art for This Is Not America are produced with help from his wife Lis Kasper Bang, née Louis-Jensen, who does the filming and photography.<ref name=":1" /><ref>{{Cite web|title=ART — Lis Kasper|url=https://liskasper.dk/ART|website=liskasper.dk|language=en|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Facebook post|url=https://www.facebook.com/claesbang/posts/844696455712775|last=Claes|first=Bang|date=7 April 2018|website=facebook.com|language=da|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref>

Bang started composing music for fun with a recording studio on his computer in 2002.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web|title=Jeg tager det hele|url=https://www.berlingske.dk/content/item/1297965|date=7 November 2008|website=Berlingske.dk|language=da|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> He records the piano and guitar pieces himself (he learnt these instruments when he was younger) and then programs them with other sounds on his computer.<ref name=":2" /><ref name=":4">{{cite AV media |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HvWjeTphr_I&feature=youtu.be|title=Claes Bang 'tricked' Danish radio to get airplay|date=8 December 2017|publisher=Associated Press|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> Bang chose the alias This Is Not America to circumvent [[DR (broadcaster)|DR]]'s policy of not broadcasting tracks by actors, and to prevent his acting career from influencing how people perceive his music.<ref name=":3" /><ref name=":5">{{Cite web|title=Dansk stjerne har kæmpe succes i USA - men får ingen tilbud fra Danmark|url=https://ekstrabladet.dk/flash/dkkendte/dansk-stjerne-har-kaempe-succes-i-usa-men-faar-ingen-tilbud-fra-danmark/7115912|date=17 April 2018|website=ekstrabladet.dk|language=da|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Q&A med Claes Bang: "Jeg var nødt til at opfinde et alias til min musik, så jeg kunne svindle P3" |url=https://heartbeats.dk/qa-med-claes-bang-selv-om-jeg-forsoeger-at-saette-mig-ned-og-lave-et-eller-andet-helt-moderne-saa-gaar-der-fem-minutter-og-saa-lyder-det-som-noget-fra-1981/|date=5 February 2020|website=heartbeats|language=da-DK|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> The name comes from a [[This Is Not America|song]] by [[David Bowie]], who is one of Bang's great role models.<ref name=":2" /> His first three singles "Don't Come Crying", "Shameless", and "Run" were played on [[DR P3|P3]] for a year before the station found out that they were made by the actor,<ref name=":4" /><ref name=":6">{{Cite web|title=ELLE mand: Claes Bang |url=https://www.elle.dk/kultur/nyheder/elle-mand-claes-bang|date=16 April 2010|website=ELLE|language=da|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> however his songs were still played on various DR radio stations for a few more years.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Claes Bang {{!}} Musik {{!}} DR|url=https://www.dr.dk/musik/kunstner/claes-bang/10354063|website=www.dr.dk|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref> These singles were later released on the debut album ''Dislocated'' on 7 April 2010.<ref name=":9">{{Cite web|title=Claes Bang: 'Det er både fantastisk og flovt at blive friet til'|url=https://www.femina.dk/kultur/kendte/claes-bang-det-er-baade-fantastisk-og-flovt-blive-friet-til|date=20 April 2010|website=Femina|language=da|access-date=18 May 2020}}</ref><ref name=":6" />

Two of Bang's co-stars have featured on some This Is Not America tracks. In 2015, he released the single "Still of the Night", featuring [[Charlotte Munck (actress)|Charlotte Munck]], his co-star in the Danish police drama ''Anna Pihl''. While filming ''The Square'', Bang discovered that co-star and musician [[Marina Schiptjenko]] shared the same taste in music, so he showed her some of his music and sent her a few demos. Once filming finished, they recorded five songs together in his home studio in [[Copenhagen]].<ref name=":5" /> So far, the duo has released "Who Am I", "I Don't Care" and "Here We Go".{{cn|date=July 2025}}

==Personal life==
