'''''Alex Wheatle''''' is a 2020 [[Historical drama|historical drama film]] directed by [[Steve McQueen (director)|Steve McQueen]] and co-written by McQueen and Alastair Siddons. It stars Sheyi Cole as [[Alex Wheatle]], a Black British novelist who was sentenced to a term of imprisonment after the [[1981 Brixton riot|1981 Brixton uprising]]. The film was released as part of the anthology series ''[[Small Axe (miniseries)|Small Axe]]'' on [[BBC One]] on 6 December 2020 and on [[Amazon Prime Video]] on 11 December 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kanter |first=Jake |title='Small Axe': Steve McQueen's Anthology Drama Gets BBC Premiere Date |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=7 October 2020 |url=https://deadline.com/2020/10/small-axe-steve-mcqueen-anthology-drama-gets-bbc-premiere-date-1234592880/ |access-date=29 November 2020}}</ref>

== Plot ==
{{Hook|date=February 2025}}
After enduring a childhood in a predominantly white [[Foster care|foster home]] devoid of love or familial bonds, Alex Wheatle, a young [[Black British people|Black]] man, finds himself in [[Brixton]]. Here, he not only experiences a sense of community for the first time in his life but also uncovers a profound passion for music. However, Brixton proves to be a tinderbox, and following [[1981 Brixton riot|the three days of unrest in April 1981]], he is incarcerated. Yet, within the confines of prison, Alex stumbles upon unexpected opportunities. Encouraged by his fellow inmate Simeon to broaden his horizons, the young man discovers a love for literature and begins to cultivate a certain level of education.<ref>{{Cite web |first=Lucy |last=Popescu |title=Small Axe: Alex Wheatle review |website=cine-vue.com |date=2020-12-06 |url=https://cine-vue.com/2020/12/small-axe-alex-wheatle-review.html |access-date=2025-02-04}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |first=Todd |last=McCarthy |title=Film Review: Steve McQueen's 'Alex Wheatle' From 'Small Axe' Anthology |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=2020-11-29 |url=https://deadline.com/2020/11/alex-wheatle-review-steve-mcqueen-small-axe-amazon-1234622905/ |access-date=2021-10-21}}</ref>
