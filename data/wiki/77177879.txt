'''''Die My Love''''' is a 2025 American [[psychological drama]] film directed by [[Lynne Ramsay]], who co-wrote the screenplay with [[Enda Walsh]] and [[Alice Birch]]. Based on [[Die, My Love|the 2012 novel]] by [[Ariana Harwicz]], it follows a young mother ([[Jennifer Lawrence]]) in rural Montana whose severe [[postpartum depression]] and [[Postpartum psychosis|psychosis]] unravel her marriage to her husband ([[Robert Pattinson]]) and her grip on reality. The supporting cast includes [[LaKeith Stanfield]], [[Nick Nolte]], and [[Sissy Spacek]].

''Die My Love'' had its world premiere at the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival]] on May 17, 2025, where it was nominated for the [[Palme d'Or]]. It was theatrically released in the United States by [[Mubi (streaming service)|Mubi]] on November 7, 2025. It received generally positive reviews from critics, with praise for Lawrence's performance. At the [[83rd Golden Globe Awards]] she was nominated for [[Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama|Best Actress]].

==Plot==
 <!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summary should be between 400 to 700 words. -->
Jackson's uncle leaves his [[Montana]] home to him after his death. Jackson decides to move there from [[New York City|New York]] with his pregnant girlfriend, Grace. Soon after the couple moves in, Grace gives birth to a baby boy. While initially happy and playful in their rural abode, Grace and Jackson begin to have problems. He is away at work for long periods, and her feelings of boredom, loneliness, and abandonment worsen when she finds condoms in his car and assumes he has been sleeping with other women. 

In the months leading up to the baby's birth, Jackson's father, Harry, passes away. During a conversation with Jackson's mother, Pam, now a widow, she learns that Jackson's uncle had died by suicide inside the couple's home by shooting himself. To Grace's chagrin, Jackson brings home an untrained, constantly barking dog without any prior discussion. One night, after the dog is injured in a car accident, she demands that Jackson shoot it. When Jackson refuses, saying he will take it to the vet the next day, Grace shoots it herself.

As time goes on, Grace's behavior grows erratic. She walks around with knives, begins an affair with a married stranger who drives by the house on a motorcycle, and throws herself through a glass door. In the midst of all this, Grace and Jackson fight continuously, leading Grace to spin out and react more intensely. Despite their constant fighting, Jackson asks Grace to marry him.

The wedding and reception start off well, but as the night continues, Jackson rebuffs Grace when she repeatedly asks him to kiss her and leaves her to dance on her own. In the bridal suite without Jackson, Grace has a concierge sing and play guitar for her in her hotel room. She bashes her head into a mirror and walks home on foot in the morning with the baby in a stroller. Jackson finds her and commits Grace to a psychiatric institution. The psychiatrist suggests that she has abandonment issues that stem from the death of her parents when she was a child. When a seemingly improved Grace is released, she learns that Jackson has cleaned, remodeled, and repainted the house, been given a new car, and named their baby Harry, after his deceased father.

When the family hosts a welcome-back party for Grace, she snaps after hearing how "healthy" and "well" she looks and tries to leave. Jackson takes her on a drive, during which she expresses that she does not want to go back and that Jackson does not see her. When they reach a stopping point, they sit in silence for some time, before Grace kisses Jackson and says, "Enough," walking into the nearby forest. Grace sets fire to the book she had been writing. She leaves the journal to burn while she removes her dress. Jackson realizes that something is wrong and runs into the forest just in time to see Grace walk into the rapidly spreading fire.

The film held an awards screening at the [[73rd San Sebastián International Film Festival]] following Lawrence's [[Donostia Award]] ceremony,<ref>{{cite web |last1=Hopewell |first1=John |title=Jennifer Lawrence to Receive San Sebastián's Highest Honor |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/global/jennifer-lawrence-san-sebastian-donostia-award-1236498546/ |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |access-date=26 August 2025 |date=26 August 2025}}</ref> with additional screenings planned for at the 2025 [[Vienna International Film Festival]],<ref>{{cite web |title=Features - Viennale |url=https://www.viennale.at/en/preview/features |website=Viennale.at |access-date=26 August 2025}}</ref> the [[2025 BFI London Film Festival|69th BFI London Film Festival]] as a Gala selection,<ref>{{cite web |title=Die My Love - BFI London Film Festival 2025 |url=https://whatson.bfi.org.uk/lff/Online/default.asp?BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::permalink=die-my-love-lff25&BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::context_id= |website=BFI Southbank |access-date=13 September 2025}}</ref> the [[20th Rome Film Festival]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ciakmagazine.it/news/festa-del-cienma-di-roma-2025-il-programma-completo-diretta/ |title= Festa del Cinema di Roma 2025, il programma completo (diretta) |trans-title=Rome Film Fest 2025: Full program (live)|date=19 September 2025|access-date=19 September 2025|website= [[Ciak Magazine]] |language=it}}</ref>  and the [[2025 Stockholm International Film Festival]].<ref name="sff:hr">{{Cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/alexander-skarsgard-benny-safdie-get-stockholm-fest-honors-1236395666/|title=Stockholm Fest to Honor Alexander Skarsgård, Benny Safdie |author=Scott Roxborough  |website= [[The Hollywood Reporter]] |date= 8 October 2025 |access-date=31 October 2025|lang=en}}</ref>

=== Box office ===
''Die My Love'' grossed $5.5 million in the United States and Canada, and $6.7 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $12.2 million.<ref name=BOM/>

''Die My Love'' made $2.6 million from 1,983 theaters in its opening weekend, finishing in eighth at the box office.<ref name="DH-Specialty">{{cite web |last1=Goldsmith |first1=Jill |title='Nuremberg' & 'Sentimental Value' Standout Debuts With Moviegoing Season In Full Swing – Specialty Box Office |url=https://deadline.com/2025/11/indie-film-box-office-nuremberg-russell-crowe-sentimental-value-1236612210/ |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |access-date=November 19, 2025 |date=November 9, 2025}}</ref> In the same weekend, the film became Lynne Ramsay's highest grossing film in the United States.<ref name="DH-Specialty" /> In its second weekend, it grossed $891,010, finishing in 12th.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Goldsmith |first1=Jill |title=Busy Neon Gifting Weekend Moviegoers Box Sets To See 4 Of Its Films Playing AMC Burbank — Specialty Box Office |url=https://deadline.com/2025/11/indie-film-box-office-neon-amc-burbank-fourplex-1236619607/ |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |access-date=November 19, 2025 |date=November 16, 2025}}</ref> By the end of its run, the film became Ramsay's highest grossing movie worldwide.<ref name="BOM" />

=== Critical response ===
{{Multiple image
| total_width       = 280
| image1            = Jennifer Lawrence SDCC 2015 X-Men.jpg
| image2            = Lynne Ramsay at ADIFF 2018.jpg
| footer            = Lawrence and Ramsay received critical acclaim for their work, with many citing the former as having given a career-best performance.

''Die My Love'' received generally positive reviews from critics following its premiere at the 2025 Cannes Film Festival, with particular acclaim for Jennifer Lawrence's intense portrayal of postpartum psychosis and Lynne Ramsay's stylistic direction, albeit audience responses were more polarized. <!--{{As of|June 2026}}-->{{RT prose|score=75|2=7|count=268|4=A frenzied depiction of a common but oft-ignored experience, ''Die My Love'' might be too stylistically mannered to fully connect but gifts Jennifer Lawrence with one of her most vivid roles yet.|ref=yes|access-date=June 6, 2026}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 72 out of 100, based on 54 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Die My Love'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/die-my-love |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=January 3, 2026 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average grade of "D+" on its A+ to F scale.<ref>{{cite web |title=CinemaScore |url=https://www.cinemascore.com |website=[[CinemaScore]] |access-date=November 19, 2025}}</ref>

Many critics singled out Lawrence's performance, which ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' described as deserving of an [[Academy Award]], and Ramsay's direction for praise.<ref name=":1">{{cite web |last1=Wiseman |first1=Andreas |title=Critics Herald Jennifer Lawrence's "Mesmerizing" Performance In Cannes Drama ''Die My Love'': 'Expect Her To Show Up On That Oscar Stage Again' |url=https://deadline.com/2025/05/jennifer-lawrence-robert-pattinson-die-my-love-cannes-1236402539/ |website=Deadline |date=17 May 2025|access-date=18 May 2025}}</ref> Tim Grierson of ''[[Screen International]]'' declared Lawrence "the match that lights Lynne Ramsay's gripping, slow-burn fifth feature", highlighting its layered examination of mental health and volatile relationships.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/die-my-love-review-jennifer-lawrence-sparks-lynne-ramsays-portrait-of-a-troubled-marriage/5205177.article|website=[[Screen International]]|first=Tim|last=Grierson|date=18 May 2025|title=''Die, My Love'' review: Jennifer Lawrence sparks Lynne Ramsay's portrait of a troubled marriage|access-date=18 May 2025}}</ref> Nicholas Barber of the [[BBC]] wrote that Lawrence "is better than ever".<ref>{{Cite web|website=[[BBC]]|title=Die, My Love review: Jennifer Lawrence is 'better than ever' in a searing portrait of motherhood|url=https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20250517-die-my-love-review-jennifer-lawrence-is-better-than-ever-in-a-searing-portrait-of-motherhood|first=Nicholas|last=Barber|date=17 May 2025|access-date=18 May 2025}}</ref>

Rafa Sales Ross of ''The Playlist'' called Lawrence the "undeniable propulsive force" of the film, noting her "rare ability to swing from effortless charm... straight into the dark abyss."<ref>{{cite web |last1=Sales Ross |first1=Rafa |title='Die, My Love' Review: Lynne Ramsay Returns With Thorny Psychological Thriller Crowned By Brilliant Jennifer Lawrence [Cannes] |url=https://theplaylist.net/die-my-love-review-lynne-ramsay-returns-with-thorny-psychological-thriller-crowned-by-brilliant-jennifer-lawrence-cannes-20250520/ |website=The Playlist |access-date=26 August 2025 |date=20 May 2025}}</ref> [[Stephanie Zacharek]] of ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' raved that it's "the kind of performance you go to the movies for, one that connects so sympathetically with... human suffering that it scares you a little."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://time.com/7287323/die-my-love-review-jennifer-lawrence/ |title=Jennifer Lawrence Gives Her Best Performance Yet in the Postpartum Fever Dream Die, My Love |date=21 May 2025 |access-date=26 August 2025 |magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]] |last=Zacharek |first=Stephanie}}</ref>

Dave Calhoun of ''[[Time Out (magazine)|Time Out]]'' labeled it a "deeply raw and honest film" with a "musical, black-comic, big-hearted spirit that pulls you through the despair."<ref>{{cite web |last1=Calhoun |first1=Dave |title=Cannes review: Lynne Ramsay's 'Die, My Love' is a ferocious trip into a fracturing psyche |url=https://www.timeout.com/movies/die-my-love-review-2025 |website=[[Time Out (magazine)|Time Out]] |access-date=26 August 2025 |date=18 May 2025}}</ref> However, [[Owen Gleiberman]] of ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' viewed it as "reckless on the surface but overdetermined", critiquing its thesis-like approach to motherhood's burdens.<ref>{{Cite web|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Owen|last=Gleiberman|title=''Die My Love'' Review: Jennifer Lawrence Is a Mother Grappling with Postpartum Depression (and Punk-Rock Angst) in Lynne Ramsay's Showy Mess of a Marital Psychodrama|date=May 17, 2025|url=https://variety.com/2025/film/reviews/die-my-love-review-jennifer-lawrence-robert-pattinson-1236401527/|author-link=Owen Gleiberman|access-date=18 May 2025}}</ref> In a review for ''[[The Washington Post]]'', Sonia Rao awarded the film 2.5 stars, praising Lawrence as "brilliant" but faulting the lack of postpartum exploration as "baffling at best and exploitative at worst".<ref name=":2">{{Cite news |last=Rao |first=Sonia |date=2025-11-07 |title=Jennifer Lawrence is brilliant in an otherwise underbaked 'Die My Love' |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/entertainment/movies/2025/11/07/die-my-love-jennifer-lawrence-review/ |access-date=2025-11-07 |newspaper=The Washington Post |language=en-US |issn=0190-8286}}</ref>

Sheila O'Malley of ''[[RogerEbert.com]]'' awarded the film three-and-a-half stars out of four, comparing Lawrence's performance to [[Catherine Deneuve]] in ''[[Repulsion (film)|Repulsion]]'' and calling the film "a wild and worthwhile ride".<ref name="Ebert">{{cite web |last1=O'Malley |first1=Sheila |date=November 7, 2025 |title=''Die My Love'' movie review & film summary (2025) |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/die-my-love-jennifer-lawrence-robert-pattinson-film-review-2025 |access-date=January 2, 2026 |website=[[RogerEbert.com]]}}</ref>
