'''''Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk''''' ({{langx|ar| ضع روحك على يدك وامشِ}}) is a 2025 [[documentary film]] directed by [[Sepideh Farsi]], depicting life in [[Gaza Strip|Gaza]] during the ongoing [[Israeli invasion of the Gaza Strip|Israeli military campaign]], captured through Farsi's video calls with Palestinian [[Photojournalism|photojournalist]] [[Fatima Hassouna]].<ref name="Cannes">{{Cite web |last=Roxborough |first=Scott |date=15 April 2025 |title=Cannes: ACID Sidebar Unveils 2025 Lineup |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/cannes-2025-acid-sidebar-lineup-1236191033/ |access-date=17 April 2025 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref>

A co-production between France, Palestine and Iran, the documentary premiered at the [[ACID (Cannes)|ACID]] parallel section of the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival]] on 15 May.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Balaga |first=Marta |date=15 April 2025 |title=Cannes Section ACID Announces Selection, From a 'Feel-Good Movie About Depression' to a New Drama With Paris Olympics 'Blue Man' |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/global/cannes-acid-announces-selection-1236369584/ |access-date=17 April 2025 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> It was theatrically released in France on 24 September by New Story.

Hassouna was killed in an Israeli airstrike alongside nine members of her family on 16 April 2025, the day after the film was selected for the ACID section.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dalton |first=Ben |date=17 April 2025 |title=Cannes ACID responds to death of Fatma Hassona, subject of 2025 film selection, killed in Gaza |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/cannes-acid-responds-to-death-of-fatma-hassona-subject-of-2025-film-selection-killed-in-gaza/5204061.article |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250422173915/https://www.screendaily.com/news/cannes-acid-responds-to-death-of-fatma-hassona-subject-of-2025-film-selection-killed-in-gaza/5204061.article |archive-date=22 April 2025 |access-date=17 April 2025 |website=[[Screen International]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Roxborough |first=Scott |date=17 April 2025 |title=Palestinian Journalist and Protagonist in Cannes-Bound Documentary Killed in Gaza |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/palestinian-journalist-subject-cannes-doc-killed-gaza-1236193771/ |access-date=17 April 2025 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Balaga |first=Marta |date=17 April 2025 |title=Fatma Hassona, the Palestinian Protagonist of Cannes-Bound 'Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk,' Killed in Israeli Missile Strike |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/global/fatma-hassona-palestinian-protagonist-cannes-acid-doc-killed-1236371607/ |access-date=17 April 2025 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> The festival released an official statement expressing condolences and criticizing the ongoing [[Gaza war|violence in Gaza]].<ref>{{Cite press release |date=23 April 2025 |title=Tribute to Fatma Hassona |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/press/press-releases/tribute-to-fatma-hassona/ |access-date=23 April 2025 |website=Festival de Cannes |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
During early 2024, Sepideh Farsi, an Iranian filmmaker living in exile in [[Paris]], travels to [[Cairo]] hoping to cross the [[Rafah Border Crossing|Egyptian-Palestinian]] border and document the ongoing [[Gaza war|war in Gaza]], but is prevented to enter in [[Rafah]] due to the [[Blockade of the Gaza Strip]] imposed by [[Israel]].

Farsi, instead, documents the war through video calls interviews with photojournalist [[Fatima Hassouna]], who lives alongside her family in a small apartment in the devastated Northern Gaza. Describing the harsh living conditions imposed to the Gaza population and the civilians casualties, including the killing of her own relatives, Hassouna is constantly interrupted by poor internet connection. The video calls are intertwined with Hassouna's photojournalism work, which was mainly focused in the children's suffering, and news reports providing context of the international response to the ongoing [[Gaza genocide|genocide]].

As the war intensifies in late 2024, Hassouna continues to documents successive aerial bombings and military activity near her home in [[Gaza City]], which resulted in the death of friends and her displacement to a shelter nearby.

On 15 April 2025, Farsi share the news of the film's selection to the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival]] and Hassouna express her desire to attend its world premiere. Farsi ends the call requesting Hassouna's passport. On the following morning, 16 April 2025, Hassouna and nine relatives were killed in their sleep by an Israeli airstrike on their home.

By May 2025, [[Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights|OHCHR]] had confirmed the killing of 211 journalists in Gaza since [[October 7 attacks|7 October]], including 28 women.

==Production==
The film was produced by Javad Djavahery of Rêves d'Eau Productions and co-produced by Annie Ohayon Dekel of 24images Production.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Pedersen |first=Lise |date=8 April 2025 |title=Brazil's Anti-Femicide Patrol and Mexico's Queer Rodeo Scene Among Tales Told in Visions du Réel's Work in Progress Section |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/global/visions-du-reel-work-in-progress-projects-1236362691/ |access-date=17 April 2025 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref>
[[File:Aerial view of destruction in Beach refugee camp, Gaza Strip.jpg|thumb|271x271px|Gaza destruction in 2024, during the filming of the documentary]]
Unable to enter Gaza, the producers turned to [[Fatima Hassouna]], a young photographer in the north of the territory, to document life under Israeli siege.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dalton |first=Ben |title=Cannes ACID responds to death of Fatma Hassona, subject of 2025 film selection, killed in Gaza |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/cannes-acid-responds-to-death-of-fatma-hassona-subject-of-2025-film-selection-killed-in-gaza/5204061.article |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[Screen International]] |language=en}}</ref> In April 2024, director Sepideh Farsi had traveled to Cairo, where she filmed Palestinian refugees in the Egyptian capital. In Cairo, a man who had just left Gaza told her about Fatima Hassouna, a "young, brilliant and talented photographer". Farsi contacted Hassouna and after just two conversations with her, the idea arose to make a film from her point of view, about her life and that of the people trapped in the regularly bombarded coastal strip. The film is based on this almost year-long video exchange between the two women.<ref>{{Cite news |date=17 April 2025 |title=Gaza photojournalist Fatma Hassona killed in a strike with 10 of her relatives |url=https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2025/04/17/gaza-photojournalist-fatima-hassouna-killed-in-a-strike-with-10-of-her-relatives_6740338_4.html |access-date=16 May 2025 |work=Le Monde |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Mintzer |first=Jordan |date=15 May 2025 |title='Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk' Review: An Intimate Portrait of Life and Death in Gaza, Haunted by a Tragic Twist |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/put-your-soul-on-your-hand-and-walk-review-gaza-documentary-1236206777/ |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

The original title of the film comes from a description by Fatima, revealed in one of her [[Videotelephony|video conversations]] with Sepideh, about the ever-present danger of observing that every second she went out in the Gaza streets, "you put your soul on your hands and walk."<ref>{{Cite web |author=Julian Borger |date=6 August 2025 |title='I must document everything': the film about the Palestinian photographer killed by Israeli missiles in Gaza |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2025/aug/06/fatma-hassouna-sepideh-farsi-put-your-soul-on-your-hand-and-walk-documentary-gaza-palestinian-photographer |access-date=11 December 2025 |website=Guardian |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=12 November 2025 |title="Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk": New Film on Gaza Photojournalist Killed in Israeli Strike |url=https://www.democracynow.org/2025/11/12/put_your_soul_on_your_hand |access-date=11 December 2025 |website=Democracy Now |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025 |title=Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk |url=https://tiff.net/films/put-your-soul-on-your-hand-and-walk |access-date=11 December 2025 |website=TIFF |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Release==
The film was selected to be screened in the [[ACID (Cannes)|ACID]] section at the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival|78th Cannes Film Festival]], where it premiered on 15 May 2025.<ref name="Screenings Guide">{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2025/the-screenings-guide-of-the-78th-festival-de-cannes/|title=The Screenings Guide of the 78th Festival de Cannes|date=8 May 2025|website=Festival de Cannes|access-date=8 May 2025}}</ref> It released in French theaters on 24 September 2025, distributed by New Story.<ref>{{Cite news |date=16 May 2025 |title=Cannes Film Festival: Emotions run high at screening of documentary on photojournalist Fatma Hassona, killed in Gaza |url=https://www.lemonde.fr/en/culture/article/2025/05/16/cannes-film-festival-emotions-run-high-at-screening-of-documentary-on-photojournalist-fatma-hassona-killed-in-gaza_6741340_30.html |access-date=16 May 2025 |work=Le Monde |language=en}}</ref>

The film had its UK premiere at the [[Edinburgh International Film Festival]] on 19 August 2025, and was theatrically released in the UK on 22 August 2025 by [[Dogwoof]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk: heartbreaking portrait of resistance |url=https://www.theskinny.co.uk/film/new-releases/put-your-soul-on-your-hand-and-walk |access-date=22 August 2026 |website=www.theskinny.co.uk |language=en}}</ref> [[Kino Lorber]] gave the film a [[limited theatrical release]] in the United States beginning at [[IFC Center]] in New York City on 5 November 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Carey |first=Matthew |date=8 October 2025 |title=Kino Lorber Announces Theatrical Dates, Drops Trailer For Gaza-Themed Oscar Contender 'Put Your Soul On Your Hand And Walk' |url=https://deadline.com/2025/10/put-your-soul-on-your-hand-and-walk-trailer-exclusive-1236573224/ |access-date=22 August 2026 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Reception==
An open letter<ref>{{Cite web |title=Artists for Fatem |url=https://artists4fatem.org/ |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=artists4fatem.org}}</ref> published in the run-up to the Cannes opening [[Richard Gere]], [[Susan Sarandon]], [[Joaquin Phoenix]], [[Guillermo del Toro]], [[Guy Pearce]], [[Ralph Fiennes]], [[David Cronenberg]], [[Viggo Mortensen]] and [[Javier Bardem]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2025/05/richard-gere-susan-sarandon-david-cronenberg-letter-gaza-1236394571/ |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]] |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Ritman |first=Alex |date=13 May 2025 |title=Mark Ruffalo, Guy Pearce, Melissa Barrera and Ralph Fiennes Among 350+ Figures to Sign Letter About Killing of Palestinian Protagonist of Cannes-Bound Doc: 'We Are Ashamed' of Industry 'Passivity' (EXCLUSIVE) |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/global/letter-about-killing-of-fatma-hassona-1236391687/ |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Vojdani |first=Jasmine |date=13 May 2025 |title=Filmmakers Denounce Industry 'Silence' About the Killing of Cannes Film Subject |url=https://www.vulture.com/article/fatma-hassona-cannes-open-letter-who-signed.html |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[Vulture (website)|Vulture]] |url=https://uk.news.yahoo.com/more-hollywood-stars-join-protest-100853059.html |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=Yahoo News |language=en-GB}}</ref> Juliette Binoche, the jury president of the film festival, honoured Hassouna at the opening of the festival with the words: "She should have been here among us this evening [...] Art remains. It is a powerful testimony of our lives and dreams; and we, the audience, embrace it."<ref>{{Cite web |date=16 May 2025 |title=Cannes honours Palestinian photojournalist Fatima Hassouna at documentary premiere |url=https://www.the-independent.com/arts-entertainment/films/news/cannes-2025-fatima-hassouna-palestine-israel-gaza-b2752277.html |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[The Independent]] |language=en}}</ref>

Jordan Mintzer's review in ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' called the film "intimate portrait of a gifted young woman living through hell ... Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk is ultimately less a documentary exposé than a piece of raw unfiltered evidence, bearing witness to a tragedy that continues to unfold as this review is being written. The film, and Hassona's eye-opening photographs, will one day be added to the historical record detailing what happened in Gaza – which, in a broader sense, is also about what has happened to our civilization."<ref name=":0" />

Allan Hunter of ''[[Screen International]]'' commented, "There is no hint of self-pity in Hassona's words as she describes the death of a friend or an aunt whose head is found in a different street to her body. She constantly thinks of others, helping to distribute any available aid to hungry children. Her cheerful optimism in the face of terrible conditions is inspirational. [...] Hassona's optimism even endures in their last call of April 15th, 2025  as she receives the news that their film has been accepted for Cannes. Hassona and members of her family were killed the following day. Farsi's film now stands as a powerful memorial to someone who was both ordinary and extraordinary."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hunter |first=Allan |title='Put Your Soul On Your Hand And Walk' review: Gaza documentary is a poignant final testimony |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/put-your-soul-on-your-hand-and-walk-review-gaza-documentary-is-a-poignant-final-testimony/5204429.article |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[Screen International]] |language=en}}</ref>

[[France 24]] reported that few films had attracted more attention at the 2025 Cannes Film Festival than the premiere of ''Put Your Soul On Your Hand and Walk'', which "drew tears and a lengthy standing at an emotional premiere".<ref>{{Cite web |date=15 May 2025 |title=Louder than bombs: Cannes screens tribute to Gaza photojournalist who refused a quiet death |url=https://www.france24.com/en/culture/20250515-cannes-screens-tribute-gaza-photojournalist-fatma-hassona-refused-quiet-death |access-date=16 May 2025 |website=[[France 24]] |language=en}}</ref>
