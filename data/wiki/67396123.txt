'''Rosine Mbakam''' (born in 1980)<ref>{{Cite web|title=Les Deux Visages d'une femme Bamiléké - Tënk|url=https://www.tenk.fr/brouillon-d-un-reve/les-deux-visages-d-une-femme-bamileke.html|access-date=2021-04-14|website=www.tenk.fr|language=fr}}</ref> is a Cameroonian film director based in [[Belgium]]. She has directed many [[short film]]s and full-length [[Documentary film|documentaries]].<ref>{{Cite magazine|title=The Films Of Rosine Mbakam|url=https://www.newyorker.com/goings-on-about-town/movies/the-films-of-rosine-mbakam|access-date=2021-04-14|magazine=The New Yorker|language=en}}</ref> ''Les deux visages d'une femme Bamiléké/The Two Faces of a [[Bamileke people|Bamiléké]] Woman'' (2016) and ''[[Chez Jolie Coiffure]]'' (2018) won a number of international prizes.<ref>{{Cite news|last=Bugbee|first=Teo|date=2019-10-15|title=Review: Two Films From Rosine Mbakam Explore West African Women's Identity|language=en-US|work=The New York Times|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2019/10/15/movies/chez-jolie-coiffure-and-the-two-faces-of-a-bamileke-woman-review.html|access-date=2021-04-14|issn=0362-4331}}</ref> ''Les prières de Delphine/Delphine’s Prayers'' (2021) gained international attention, including reviews in [[The New York Times]], [[The New Yorker]], [[Los Angeles Times|LA Times]], [[Variety (magazine)|Variety]], and [[NPR]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Meet The Filmmaker Reinventing How African Women Are Portrayed In Movies|url=https://www.npr.org/sections/goatsandsoda/2019/11/10/775115620/meet-the-filmmaker-reinventing-how-african-women-are-portrayed-in-movies|access-date=2021-04-14|website=NPR.org|language=en}}</ref> With ''Mambar Pierrette'', Mbakam made her feature film debut in 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mambar Pierrette |url=https://frauenfilmfest.com/en/movie/mambar-pierrette/ |access-date=2024-04-13 |website=Internationales Frauen Film Fest |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Biography ==
Rosine Mbakam was born in Cameroon in 1980.<ref name=":0" /> She grew up in a traditional Bamiléké family. She spent her childhood in [[Yaoundé]], the capital city of [[Cameroon]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Rosine Mbakam {{!}} IFFR|url=https://iffr.com/en/persons/rosine-mbakam|access-date=2021-04-15|website=iffr.com}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web|last=DELVOSALLE|first=PHILIPPE|title=" Chez jolie coiffure " : interview de Rosine Mbakam {{!}} Nord/Sud|url=https://www.pointculture.be/magazine/articles/focus/chez-jolie-coiffure-interview-de-rosine-mbakam/|access-date=2021-04-15|website=www.pointculture.be}}</ref>

Rosine Mbakam made her debut in cinema in 2001 and gained an education in thorough audiovisual media in Cameroon between 2000 and 2004 at the center of COE (Centro orientamento Educativo), an Italian nongovernmental organization (NGO) based in Yaoundé.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|title=Mbakam, Rosine {{!}} African Film Festival, Inc.|url=https://africanfilmny.org/directors/rosine-mbakam/|access-date=2021-04-15|language=en-US}}</ref><ref name=":3">{{Cite web|title=Les Prières de Delphine|url=https://www.cinemadureel.org/film/les-prieres-de-delphine/?lang=en|access-date=2021-04-15|website=Cinéma du réel|language=en-US}}</ref> She then joined the private Cameroonian television channel Spectrum Télévision (STV) where she worked as a photojournalist and directed programs from 2003 to 2007<ref name=":2" /><ref>{{Cite web|title=film-documentaire.fr - Portail du film documentaire|url=http://www.film-documentaire.fr/4DACTION/w_liste_generique/C_95936_F|access-date=2021-04-15|website=www.film-documentaire.fr}}</ref> 10. In 2007, when she was 27, she left Cameroon for Belgium and enrolled in Institut Supérieur des Arts (INSAS) in [[Brussels]], where she continued her studies in film and audiovisual production.<ref name=":1" /> She graduated from INSAS in 2012.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Rosine MBAKAM|url=https://en.notrecinema.com/communaute/stars/stars.php3?staridx=343587|access-date=2021-04-15|website=notreCinema.com|language=en}}</ref>

== Career ==
Rosine Mbakam directed her first films in 2009 while she was still a student. This includes a few short films: ''Un cadeau'' released in 2010 and ''Les Portes du Passé'' released in 2011.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Director {{!}} WordPress|url=http://lesdeuxvisagesdunefemmebamileke.com/?page_id=8&lang=en|access-date=2021-04-15|website=lesdeuxvisagesdunefemmebamileke.com}}</ref> With Mikro Popvitch in 2011, she co-directed the film Mavambu, which is a portrait of the Congolese artist, [[:fr:Freddy Tsimba|Freddy Tsimba]], and was produced by [[:de:Africalia|Africalia]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Chez Jolie Coiffure|url=http://www.dctvny.org/events/chezjoliecoiffure|access-date=2021-04-15|website=DCTV|language=en}}</ref> After obtaining her degree in 2012, she directed and edited films and documentaries for the society of Africalia production at the same time as directing her own.<ref name=":2" /> In 2014, she co-founded the production company.<ref name=":1" /> As an extension of her own filmmaking career, Mbakam serves as an instructor to students at The [[Royal Academy of Fine Arts (KASK)]].<ref name=":3" />

=== ''The Two Faces of a Bamiléké Woman'' ===
In 2016, Rosine Mbakam directed her first feature film, a creative documentary titled ''Les deux visages d'une femme Bamiléké ([[The Two Faces of a Bamiléké Woman]]).'' The 76-minute film is a personal documentary in which the director focuses on her return to her native country with her French husband and their son, seven years after she left.<ref>{{Cite journal|last=Garritano|first=Carmela|date=March 2020|title=Rosine Mbakam, director. The Two Faces of a Bamiléké Woman. 2016. 76 min. French and Bamiléké, with English subtitles. Cameroon and France. Icarus Films. $348.|journal=African Studies Review|language=en|volume=63|issue=1|pages=E40–E42|doi=10.1017/asr.2019.56|s2cid=204364128|issn=0002-0206|doi-access=free}}</ref> The film is built by a series of conversations mainly between Mbakam and her mother on varied subjects connected to family, gender, and also politics.<ref name=":0" /> The [[International Film Festival Rotterdam|International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR)]] and [[Panafrican Film and Television Festival of Ouagadougou|Panafrican Film and Television Festival of Ouagadougou (FESPACO)]] are among the sixty-plus film festivals at which this movie has been screened.<ref name=":3" />

=== ''Chez Jolie Coiffure'' ===
In 2018, she directed ''Chez Jolie Coiffure''. This 71-minute documentary concerns the immigration, daily life, and difficulties experienced by African immigrants in Europe.<ref name=":0" /> The film takes place in a hair salon in the neighborhood of Matonge in Brussels and focuses on the stories of the owner, a Cameroonian immigrant woman seeking residency along with her friends.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Chez Jolie Coiffure + Q&A|url=https://opencitylondon.com/events/chez-jolie-coiffure/|access-date=2021-04-15|website=Open City Documentary Festival|language=en-US}}</ref>

=== ''Delphine’s Prayers'' ===
In 2021, Rosine Mbakam directed a third documentary called ''Les prières de Delphine (Delphine’s Prayers''). This film is a portrayal of Delphine, a young Cameroonian woman who has been caught in sex work in Cameroon. She was abandoned by her father after the death of her mother, and by age 13, she was raped and pregnant with a daughter. Sex work becomes her ticket to Belgium after she makes a man who is 3 times her age her husband.<ref>{{Cite web|title=film-documentaire.fr - Portail du film documentaire|url=http://www.film-documentaire.fr/4DACTION/w_fiche_film/62011_1|access-date=2021-04-15|website=www.film-documentaire.fr}}</ref><ref name=":4" />

=== ''Mambar Pierrette'' ===
In 2023, Mbakam released her first narrative feature, ''Mambar Pierrette'', centered on a talented seamstress and single mother in Douala which premiered at the [[Toronto International Film Festival|Toronto Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mambar Pierrette |url=https://www.screenslate.com/articles/mambar-pierrette |access-date=2024-04-27 |website=Screen Slate |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Girish |first=Devika |date=2023-05-26 |title=Interview: Rosine Mbakam on Mambar Pierrette |url=https://www.filmcomment.com/blog/interview-rosine-mbakam-on-mambar-pierrette-cannes-2023-directors-fortnight/ |access-date=2024-04-27 |website=Film Comment |language=en}}</ref>

== Personal life ==
In 2014, she visited her home in Cameroon for the first time in seven years after leaving for Belgium and marrying her husband. They now have two sons.<ref name=":4">{{Cite web|title=Whatever Happened To ... The Filmmaker Focusing On A New Face For African Women?|url=https://www.npr.org/sections/goatsandsoda/2020/09/08/910701931/whatever-happened-to-the-filmmaker-focusing-on-a-new-face-for-african-women|access-date=2021-04-15|website=NPR.org|language=en}}</ref>
