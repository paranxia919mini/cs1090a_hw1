'''''Everyone Is Lying to You for Money''''' is a 2025 American [[documentary film]] which explores the [[cryptocurrency]] industry. It was produced, written, and directed by [[Ben McKenzie]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2026/04/ben-mckenzie-interview-everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money-1236863673/|title=Ben McKenzie On His Crypto Documentary And Trump Family Coin Cash|first=Matthew|last=Carey|date=April 16, 2026|website=[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]]}}</ref><ref name="V">{{Cite web|url=https://variety.com/2026/film/news/ben-mckenzie-cryptocurrency-documentary-sells-the-forge-1236680125/|title=Ben McKenzie Cryptocurrency Documentary ‘Everyone Is Lying to You for Money’ Acquired by The Forge (EXCLUSIVE)|first=Brent|last=Lang|date=March 5, 2026|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> It was given a [[limited theatrical release]] on April 17, 2026.<ref name="V"/>

== Production ==
Production took place over three years, helmed by the duo of actor-turned-filmmaker McKenzie and producer Giorgio Angelini.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wiseman |first=Andreas |last2=Tartaglione |first2=Nancy |date=2025-04-29 |title=‘Gotham’ & ‘The O.C.’ Star Ben McKenzie Makes Film Directing Debut On Crypto Doc ‘Everyone Is Lying To You For Money’, Which Will Launch At SXSW London |url=https://deadline.com/2025/04/ben-mckenzie-makes-directing-debut-on-crypto-documentary-1236379778/ |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> The film was self-financed by McKenzie.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Solomon |first=Dan |date=June 13, 2025 |title=Best Documentaries in Years. Will Anyone Get to See It? |url=https://www.texasmonthly.com/arts-entertainment/ben-mckenzie-crypto-documentary-everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money/ |access-date=April 18, 2026 |website=Texas Monthly}}</ref>

A highly personal project ([[Morena Baccarin]], McKenzie's wife appears in the movie as well), McKenzie directed, wrote, produced, and starred in the documentary. The film was initiated as part of research for the 2023 book ''Easy Money: Cryptocurrency, Casino Capitalism, and the Golden Age of Fraud'', which McKenzie wrote with journalist Jacob Silverman, building off McKenzie's efforts and growing profile as a [[cryptocurrency skeptic]] and critic.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kemmerle |first=Karen |date=September 9, 2025 |title=‘Everyone Is Lying To You For Money’ Filmmaker Ben McKenzie Used His ‘The O.C’ Fame To Score An Interview With Scammer Samuel Bankman-Fried: “His Assistant Was A Fan” |url=https://decider.com/2025/09/29/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money-ben-mckenzie-interview/ |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=Decider |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Release ==

The film had its world premiere on June 6, 2025, as part of the inaugural [[SXSW London]] festival.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Everyone is Lying To You For Money {{!}} SXSW London 2025 Ses... |url=https://sxswlondon.comsession/2025/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money-edf747e5 |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=sxswlondon.comsession |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Wiseman |first=Andreas |last2=Tartaglione |first2=Nancy |date=2025-04-29 |title=‘Gotham’ & ‘The O.C.’ Star Ben McKenzie Makes Film Directing Debut On Crypto Doc ‘Everyone Is Lying To You For Money’, Which Will Launch At SXSW London |url=https://deadline.com/2025/04/ben-mckenzie-makes-directing-debut-on-crypto-documentary-1236379778/ |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> It also played at the 2025 [[Doc NYC|DOC NYC]] film festival, the 2025 [[Mill Valley Film Festival]], and the [[Miami Film Festival]] in April 2026.<ref>{{Cite web |title=EVERYONE IS LYING TO YOU FOR MONEY |url=https://www.docnyc.net/film/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money/ |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=DOC NYC |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025 |title=Everyone is Lying to You for Money |url=https://app.mvff.com/film/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=Mill Valley Film Festival}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-12-10 |title=Everyone Is Lying to You for Money – featuring Ben McKenzie (Marquee) |url=https://miamifilmfestival.com/program/event/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money-featuring-ben-mckenzie-marquee/ |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=Miami Film Festival |language=en}}</ref>

In March 2025, the independent [[Film distribution|distributor]] The Forge acquired distribution and sales rights. The film received a limited theatrical release beginning April 17, 2026.<ref name="V" />

== Reception ==
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|100||21}}<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/everyone_is_lying_to_you_for_money|title=Everyone Is Lying to You for Money|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|access-date=August 24, 2026}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 72 out of 100, based on six critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Everyone Is Lying to You for Money'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/ |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date= |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money/|title=Everyone Is Lying to You for Money|website=[[Metacritic]]|access-date=May 10, 2026}}</ref>

[[Owen Gleiberman]] of ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' wrote that the film is "a lively, knife-sharp, impeccably researched and reported documentary that answers every conceivable question you've ever had about crypto, and does so in a way that's brisk and funny and illuminating rather than intimidating."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2026/film/reviews/everyone-is-lying-to-you-for-money-review-ben-mckenzie-1236725504/|title=‘Everyone Is Lying to You for Money’ Review: Ben McKenzie’s Knife-Sharp Documentary Takedown of Cryptocurrency|first=Owen|last=Gleiberman|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|date=April 20, 2026|access-date=April 21, 2026}}</ref>
