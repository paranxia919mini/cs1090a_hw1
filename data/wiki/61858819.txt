'''''The World Before Your Feet''''' is a 2018 American [[documentary film]] directed by filmmaker [[Jeremy Workman]] about Matt Green's mission to walk every street of New York City, a journey of over 8,000 miles.<ref name="NYT-20181121">{{cite news |last=Kenigsberg |first=Ben|title='The World Before Your Feet' Review: Walk a Mile — or 8,000 — in Another Man's Shoes|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2018/11/20/movies/the-world-before-your-feet-review.html|date=November 21, 2018 |work=The New York Times |access-date=November 21, 2018}}</ref> The film also marked the producing debut of actor [[Jesse Eisenberg]].<ref name="Setoodeh">{{Cite news|url=https://variety.com/2018/film/news/jesse-eisenberg-the-world-before-your-feet-lex-luthor-1202723448/|title=Jesse Eisenberg on SXSW Doc 'The World Before Your Feet,' Returning to Lex Luthor|last=Setoodeh|first=Ramin|date=March 11, 2018|work=Variety|access-date=March 12, 2018 |url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180316215616/http://variety.com/2018/film/news/jesse-eisenberg-the-world-before-your-feet-lex-luthor-1202723448/|archive-date=March 16, 2018}}</ref>

''The World Before Your Feet'' premiered at the [[South by Southwest]] film festival, and was released theatrically on November 21, 2018, through [[Greenwich Entertainment]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://greenwichentertainment.com/film/the-world-before-your-feet/|title=The World Before Your Feet &#124; Greenwich Entertainment|website=greenwichentertainment.com}}</ref> Widely praised upon its release, the documentary holds {{a or an|{{RT data|score}}}} rating on [[Rotten Tomatoes]].<ref name="tomatoes" />

==Release==
The documentary premiered in competition at the 2018 [[South by Southwest|South by Southwest Film Festival]], where it was acquired by distributor [[Greenwich Entertainment]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Greenwich Acquires Jeremy Workman's SXSW Documentary THE WORLD BEFORE YOUR FEET|url=https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Greenwich-Acquires-Jeremy-Workmans-SXSW-Documentary-THE-WORLD-BEFORE-YOUR-FEET-20180625|publisher=Broadway World|access-date=25 June 2018|ref=1}}</ref> It was released theatrically on November 21, 2018,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://quadcinema.com/film/the-world-before-your-feet/|title=The World Before Your Feet|website=Quad Cinema}}</ref> screening in over 70 cities across the United States and Canada<ref>{{cite web|title=Get Tickets|url=https://theworldbeforeyourfeet.com/tickets/|website=The World Before Your Feet Official Website|access-date=21 November 2018}}</ref> and playing in theaters for over a year.<ref>{{Cite web |title=The World Before Your Feet |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/release/rl3624371713/ |access-date=2024-03-16 |website=Box Office Mojo}}</ref>

Following its theatrical run, the film was subsequently made available on multiple streaming sites.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.theguardian.com/film/2020/nov/28/true-story-new-documentary-film-streaming-platform-the-world-before-your-feet|title=Streaming: in praise of new documentary platform True Story|date=November 28, 2020|website=the Guardian}}</ref> A special edition DVD/Blu-ray was released through [[Kino Lorber]].<ref>{{cite web|title=The World Before Your Feet|url=https://www.kinolorber.com/product/the-world-before-your-feet/|website=Kino Lorber}}</ref> The film was also widely released in Germany<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.kino-zeit.de/film-kritiken-trailer-streaming/new-york-die-welt-vor-deinen-fuessen-2018|title = New York - die Welt vor deinen Füßen (2018) &#124; Film, Trailer, Kritik}}</ref> with distributor Happy Entertainment<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.filme.de/news/einer-der-wohl-laengsten-spaziergaenge-der-welt-new-york-die-welt-vor-deinen-fuessen-happy-entertainment/|title = Einer der wohl längsten Spaziergänge der Welt: New York – die Welt vor deinen Füßen &#124; Happy Entertainment|date = 12 December 2019}}</ref> under the title ''New York Die Welt vor deinen Füßen'' <ref>{{Cite news|url=https://taz.de/Kino-Doku-Die-Welt-vor-deinen-Fuessen/!5667521/|title = Kino-Doku "Die Welt vor deinen Füßen": Spaziergang mit einem Unbekannten|newspaper = Die Tageszeitung: Taz|date = 13 March 2020|last1 = Caldart|first1 = Isabella}}</ref>

The documentary then returned to American movie theaters in 2025, following Matt Green's completion of his New York City walking project.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.westnewtoncinema.com/movie/the-world-before-your-feet |access-date=2025-06-16 |website=www.westnewtoncinema.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=The World Before Your Feet - A Movie and A Walk |url=https://www.assemblytheatre.org/event-details/the-world-before-your-feet-a-movie-and-a-walk |access-date=2025-06-16 |website=The Assembly Theatre |language=en}}</ref>

==Reception==
The film was widely praised upon its release. It has {{a or an|{{RT data|score}}}} positive rating on [[Rotten Tomatoes]], based on {{RT data|count}} reviews.<ref name="tomatoes">{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_world_before_your_feet|title=The World Before Your Feet|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|publisher=[[Fandango Media|Fandango]]|access-date={{RT data|access date|df=dmy}}}}</ref> The website's critics consensus reads, "''The World Before Your Feet'' offers a perspective on New York City that might be entrancingly unfamiliar even to residents -- and beckons viewers toward a more attentive way of life."<ref name="tomatoes" /> On [[Metacritic]], it has a [[weighted average]] score of 78 out of 100, indicating "favorable reviews".<ref>{{Cite web|title=The World Before Your Feet |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-world-before-your-feet |website=[[Metacritic]] }}</ref>

The film was a ''Critic's Pick'' in ''[[The New York Times]]'', in which critic Ben Kenigsberg stated that "after watching ''The World Before Your Feet'', it’s difficult to look at the city the same way."<ref name="NYT-20181121" />  It was a ''Critic's Choice'' of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'', in which critic Michael Rechtshaffen stated that the film is "inspiring and enlightening... Filmmaker Workman, who's behind [Green] every step of the way without ever getting in the way, allows the city and its colorful denizens to take center stage."<ref>{{cite web |last=Rechtshaffen |first=Michael |title=Man walks New York step by step, borough by borough in the uplifting documentary 'The World Before Your Feet'|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/movies/la-et-mn-the-world-before-your-feet-review-20181120-story.html |newspaper=[[Los Angeles Times]] |date=20 November 2018 }}</ref> ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' called the film "an eye-opening stroll with a stranger"<ref>{{cite web |last=Schwartz |first=Dana |title=The World Before Your Feet is an eye-opening stroll with a stranger: EW review|url=https://ew.com/movie-reviews/2018/11/21/the-world-before-your-feet-is-an-eye-opening-stroll-with-a-stranger-ew-review/|magazine=Entertainment Weekly}}</ref> while The Playlist described it as "an utterly compelling joy."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Garrison|first1=Gary|title='The World Before Your Feet' Is A Delightful Stroll Through The Pleasures Of Walking|date=16 March 2018 |url=https://theplaylist.net/world-before-feet-review-20180316//|publisher=The Playlist}}</ref> ''[[San Francisco Chronicle]]'', in a positive review, said, "It's hard to see Green in action and not think of traditional pilgrims on holy journeys and, perhaps, hermits or monks or others who have withdrawn from regular life".<ref>{{cite web |last=Addiego |first=Walter |title=He's out to walk the streets of New York — all of them|url=https://datebook.sfchronicle.com/movies-tv/hes-out-to-walk-the-streets-of-new-york-all-of-them/|newspaper=San Francisco Chronicle}}</ref> [[Rex Reed]] of ''[[The New York Observer]]'' stated, "New York, New York, it's a wonderful town. This movie proves it like none other."<ref>{{cite web|last=Reed|first=Rex|title=Meet The 'Independently Homeless' Guy Who Walked Every Single New York City Street|url=https://observer.com/2018/11/the-world-before-your-feet-review-rex-reed/|work=The New York Observer|date=20 November 2018}}</ref>
