'''''Twilight of the Warriors: Walled In''''' ({{zh|t_hk=九龍城寨之圍城|l=The Siege of Kowloon Walled City}}) is a 2024 [[Hong Kong action cinema|Hong Kong action film]] directed by [[Soi Cheang]],<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/presse/communiques/les-films-de-la-selection-officielle-2024/ |title=Les films de la Sélection officielle 2024 |date=11 April 2024 |access-date=12 April 2024 |archive-date=11 April 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240411160938/https://www.festival-cannes.com/presse/communiques/les-films-de-la-selection-officielle-2024/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="variety-rev" /> and starring [[Louis Koo]], [[Sammo Hung]], [[Richie Jen]], [[Raymond Lam]], [[Terrance Lau]], [[Kenny Wong]], [[Philip Ng]], [[Tony Wu (actor)|Tony Wu]] and German Cheung. The film was based on the novel ''City of Darkness'' by Yuyi and the [[manhua]] of the same name by [[Andy Seto]].<ref name=screen/>

Having been in development since the 2000s, production for the film officially began in November 2021<ref name="progress">{{cite web|url=https://www.facebook.com/1088589954568290/posts/4608713109222606/|title=九龍城寨·圍城 - 大家好耐無見啦，有無掛住我哋？😉 俾幾日時間你哋估下有咩演員 🤫|website=[[Facebook]] }}</ref> and wrapped in April 2022. The film was released in Hong Kong and mainland China on 1 May 2024, and was selected to be in the Official Selection (Midnight Screening) of the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival]], as part of screened out of competition.<ref name=cannes>{{Cite web |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/presse/communiques/les-films-de-la-selection-officielle-2024/ |title=Les films de la Sélection officielle 2024 |date=11 April 2024 |access-date=12 April 2024 |archive-date=11 April 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240411160938/https://www.festival-cannes.com/presse/communiques/les-films-de-la-selection-officielle-2024/ |url-status=live }}</ref> The film received critical acclaim.

The film became the [[List of highest-grossing domestic films in Hong Kong|second-highest-grossing domestic film of all time in Hong Kong]],<ref>{{cite news |title=《九龍城寨之圍城》 票房突破8251萬 榮登香港電影史上華語電影總票房第2位 |url=https://hk.news.yahoo.com/%E3%80%8A%E4%B9%9D%E9%BE%8D%E5%9F%8E%E5%AF%A8%E4%B9%8B%E5%9C%8D%E5%9F%8E%E3%80%8B-%E7%A5%A8%E6%88%BF%E7%AA%81%E7%A0%B48251%E8%90%AC-%E6%A6%AE%E7%99%BB%E9%A6%99%E6%B8%AF%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1%E5%8F%B2%E4%B8%8A%E8%8F%AF%E8%AA%9E%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1%E7%B8%BD%E7%A5%A8%E6%88%BF%E7%AC%AC2%E4%BD%8D-064459611.html |access-date=28 May 2024 |work=Yahoo News |date=28 May 2024 |language=zh-Hant-HK}}</ref> and won [[Hong Kong Film Award for Best Film|Best Film]] in the [[43rd Hong Kong Film Awards]]. The film was selected as [[List of Hong Kong submissions for the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film|Hong Kong's official entry]] for [[Academy Award for Best International Feature Film|Best International Feature Film]] at the [[97th Academy Awards]], but was not nominated.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rosser |first=Michael |title=Hong Kong enters 'Twilight Of The Warriors: Walled In' to Oscar race |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/hong-kong-enters-twilight-of-the-warriors-walled-in-to-oscar-race/5197383.article |date=20 September 2024 |access-date=20 September 2024 |website=[[Screen International]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Plot==
[[Kowloon Walled City]] is a densely populated, anarchic enclave in Hong Kong. It is also a hotbed of criminal activity controlled by gang leader Lui Chen-tung and his enforcer, Jim “King of Killers” Chan until an alliance of gangs led by the formidable martial arts master Cyclone deposed Lui and killed Jim, bringing a kind of peace to the walled city.

Years later, in the mid-1980s, [[Refugee wave from the People's Republic of China to British Hong Kong|refugee]] Chan Lok-kwan lives a downtrodden existence in Hong Kong’s periphery, working odd jobs and competing in illegal pit fights arranged by a powerful crime lord, Mr. Big. After Mr. Big swindles Chan, Chan desperately steals a bag of drugs from Mr. Big and flees Mr. Big’s henchmen, led by the sadistic and seemingly invincible King.

Chan narrowly escapes by entering the walled city, which is protected by Cyclone, whose skill and authority deter even the local triads from entering lightly. However, when Chan tries to sell his stolen drugs within the walled city, Cyclone’s gang members angrily intervene. After an initial brawl, Cyclone subdues Chan and takes pity on his wretched state, offering him a place to stay.

Chan begins working various jobs within Kowloon Walled City’s dense urban maze of illegal workshops and businesses. But Chan soon fits in, befriending Cyclone’s lieutenants Shin, AV, and Twelfth Master, a lieutenant of the Temple Street Gang. Together, Chan, Shin, AV, and Twelfth Master become known as the Kowloon 4.

Meanwhile, Dik Chau, the walled city’s biggest landlord and a high-ranking triad member, calls a meeting with Cyclone and Tiger, leader of the Temple Street Gang. All three are long-time allies, going back to when they united to depose Lui Chen-tung and kill Jim. Unbeknownst to Chau and Tiger, Cyclone and Jim had struck an unlikely friendship, and even though Cyclone killed Jim, he also helped Jim’s wife flee Hong Kong with her newborn son. Chau, however, swore an oath to destroy Jim’s family to avenge the loss of his own, whom Jim murdered. Now, at long last, Chau announces he has discovered Jim’s son, who turns out to be Chan.

The Kowloon 4 try to hustle Chan safely out of the walled city, before Chau and Tiger’s gangs try to kill him, but they are too late. A fierce brawl erupts, in which Cyclone does not take part, and Chan narrowly defends himself against Chau and Tiger. 

Feeling betrayed by Cyclone, Chau seeks Mr. Big’s help to kill Chan. Mr. Big’s gang assaults the walled city, and in the mayhem that follows, King kills the ailing Cyclone while other residents of the walled city also perish in the fighting. All members of the Kowloon 4 suffer dire wounds while evacuating Chan from the walled city.

King kills Mr. Big and imprisons Chau, ruling the walled city tyrannically until Chan recovers and returns to challenge him. With the timely aid of the Kowloon 4, Chan defeats King’s gang and after a protracted battle, King himself. Shin delivers the killing blow to avenge his old master Cyclone.

Afterwards, the Kowloon 4 look out over Hong Kong and note that while the city constantly changes, their friendship never will.

==Production==
The project had been in development since the 2000s, where it was set to be co-directed by [[John Woo]] and [[Johnnie To]] and star an all-star cast of [[Chow Yun-fat]], [[Andy Lau]], [[Tony Leung Chiu-wai|Tony Leung]], [[Sean Lau]], [[Louis Koo]], [[Anthony Wong]], [[Sun Honglei]], [[Anita Yuen]] and [[Zhang Jingchu]], with [[Nicolas Cage]], [[James McAvoy]], [[Li Baotian]], [[Jang Dong-gun]], [[Li Bingbing]] and [[Zhang Fengyi]] making special appearances, as shown on a teaser poster, although nothing was confirmed.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://hkmdb.com/db/movies/image_detail.mhtml?id=17545&image_id=255770&display_set=eng|title=Kowloon Walled City teaser poster|access-date=10 February 2022|archive-date=8 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211208064114/https://hkmdb.com/db/movies/image_detail.mhtml?id=17545&image_id=255770&display_set=eng|url-status=live}}</ref>

On 13 April 2013, [[Media Asia Entertainment Group|Media Asia]] announced the project was to be titled ''Dragon City'' and set to be directed by [[Derek Kwok]] and starring [[Donnie Yen]], who would also serve as [[stage combat|action director]] and producer through his production company, Super Hero Films, and was set to begin production in September of that year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/two-donnie-yen-projects-coming-438209/|title=Hong Kong's Media Asia Announces Two Donnie Yen Action Vehicles|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|date=12 April 2013|access-date=10 February 2022|archive-date=8 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211208070109/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/two-donnie-yen-projects-coming-438209/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://budomate.com/dragon-city/|title=ACE COP FIGHTING CRIME IN DRAGON CITY|access-date=10 February 2022|archive-date=8 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211208064119/https://budomate.com/dragon-city/|url-status=live}}</ref>

== Future ==
Director Soi Cheang has announced that he is working on two projects for the franchise: a prequel titled ''Twilight of the Warriors: Dragon Throne'' and a sequel, ''Twilight of the Warriors: The Final Chapter'', both of which are slated to be filmed simultaneously.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Brzeski |first=Patrick |date=2024-08-13 |title=‘Twilight of the Warriors: Walled In’ Director Soi Cheang on Reviving Hong Kong Action Cinema, Plans for Prequel and Sequel |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/twilight-of-the-warriors-walled-in-director-soi-cheang-prequel-sequel-1235972082/ |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Wong |first=Silvia |title=‘Twilight of The Warriors: Walled In’ set for a prequel and sequel (exclusive) |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/twilight-of-the-warriors-walled-in-set-for-a-prequel-and-sequel-exclusive/5193724.article |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Screen |language=en}}</ref>

In October 2025, the film's official social media accounts announced that ''Twilight of the Warriors: The Final Chapter'' had officially begun production. The plot of the movie would be set in the 1980s and 1990s, and the team invited former residents of Kowloon Walled City to contribute objects and memorabilia representative of that era to the film. As a token of appreciation, the production would be giving away limited-edition Kowloon Walled City: The Final Chapter tote bags, emphasizing that this gift was exclusive to those who donated.<ref>{{Cite web |last=許育民 |date=2025-10-21 |title=九龍城寨之終章｜正式籌備 電影公司呼籲「寨民」伸出援手有獎賞 |url=https://www.hk01.com/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1/60287383/%E4%B9%9D%E9%BE%8D%E5%9F%8E%E5%AF%A8%E4%B9%8B%E7%B5%82%E7%AB%A0-%E6%AD%A3%E5%BC%8F%E7%B1%8C%E5%82%99-%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E5%91%BC%E7%B1%B2-%E5%AF%A8%E6%B0%91-%E4%BC%B8%E5%87%BA%E6%8F%B4%E6%89%8B%E6%9C%89%E7%8D%8E%E8%B3%9E |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=香港01 |language=zh-HK}}</ref>
