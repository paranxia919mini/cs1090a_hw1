'''''In a Violent Nature''''' is a 2024 Canadian [[slasher film]] written and directed by Chris Nash, and starring Ry Barrett, Andrea Pavlovic, and [[Lauren-Marie Taylor]]. Described as an "ambient slasher",<ref name="Gheciu 2024">{{cite web|url=https://www.theglobeandmail.com/arts/film/article-sundance-slasher-in-a-violent-nature-puts-northern-ontario-front-and/|title=Sundance slasher In A Violent Nature puts northern Ontario front and centre|last=Gheciu|first=Alex Nino|date=January 22, 2024|website=[[The Globe and Mail]]|access-date=January 24, 2024|archive-date=May 30, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240530175253/https://www.theglobeandmail.com/arts/film/article-sundance-slasher-in-a-violent-nature-puts-northern-ontario-front-and/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Ehrlich 2024">{{cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/criticism/movies/in-a-violent-nature-review-horror-movie-1234947847/|title='In a Violent Nature' Review: A Gory, High-Concept Slasher More Interesting in Theory Than in Execution|last=Ehrlich|first=David|date=January 24, 2024|website=[[IndieWire]]|access-date=January 24, 2024|archive-date=June 7, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240607014007/https://www.indiewire.com/criticism/movies/in-a-violent-nature-review-horror-movie-1234947847/|url-status=live}}</ref> it follows a mute killer who is accidentally resurrected from his grave in the [[Ontario]] wilderness by a group of teenagers, whom he then begins stalking and murdering. The events depicted are largely observed from the killer's perspective.<ref name="Earl 2024">{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/news/in-a-violent-nature-gory-slasher-terrence-malick-1235864450/|title=How 'In a Violent Nature' Director Chris Nash Redefined Slashers With an Ultra-Gory Throwback That Evokes Terrence Malick|last=Earl|first=William|date=January 18, 2024|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=January 24, 2024|archive-date=June 22, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240622162452/https://variety.com/2024/film/news/in-a-violent-nature-gory-slasher-terrence-malick-1235864450/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Harvey 2024">{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/in-a-violent-nature-review-1235870787/|title='In a Violent Nature' Review: A Fresh Canadian Spin on Slasher Conventions|last=Harvey|first=Dennis|date=January 22, 2024|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|archive-date=June 8, 2024|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240608222445/https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/in-a-violent-nature-review-1235870787/|url-status=live}}</ref>

''In a Violent Nature'' premiered in the Midnight section of the [[2024 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 22, 2024. The film was released theatrically in the United States and Canada by [[IFC Films]] on May 31, and was released on the streaming service [[Shudder (streaming service)|Shudder]] later in the year. Opening on 1,426 screens, it marked IFC Films's widest theatrical release to date, and grossed $3 million in its first week. It received positive reviews from film critics. A sequel, ''In a Violent Nature 2,'' is in production.

==Plot==
A group of friends—Kris, Colt, Troy, Evan, Ehren, Brodie, and Aurora—discover a locket hanging on the remains of a [[Fire lookout tower|fire tower]], which Troy pockets. Moments later, the corpse of a serial killer, identified as Johnny, rises from the ground. Enraged, he roams the woods in search of the locket and hears an argument between a hunter and a park ranger. Johnny enters the hunter's house, where he sees a necklace that he mistakes for his locket, triggering a brief memory of his father. The homeowner returns and runs away at the sight of Johnny, but is caught and killed.

Johnny approaches the group sitting around a campfire, drawn to their location by the sound of gunfire they made. Ehren recounts the legend of Johnny. Decades prior, Johnny, the developmentally delayed son of a local merchant, fell to his death from the firetower in a prank gone wrong. Despite attempts to cover it up, his father discovers the truth and is killed confronting one of the individuals involved. Subsequent murders were attributed to his vengeful spirit. Later that night, Ehren is killed by Johnny. He uses Ehren's corpse to break into and ransack a nearby [[park ranger]] office for weapons and an antique [[Firefighter's helmet#Early respirators|firefighter mask]].

The next day, Johnny kills Brodie in the water near a lake dock, and Aurora at an overlook. Later, Troy, Colt, Kris, and Evan argue about the circumstances of their friends' disappearances. As the argument escalates, Troy throws the car keys into the woods, which Johnny recovers. Colt walks into the woods to try to retrieve them, but instead finds Johnny's dropped axe. Colt picks up the axe and returns to the others, showing it to them while they continue arguing about the other strange things they've seen. While playing with the keychain, Johnny hears Troy make disparaging remarks toward Colt's deceased father. Enraged, he walks towards the group just as Colt and Kris ride off towards the [[Ranger station|range station]], and injures Troy with the axe. While inside the cabin, Evan notices this encounter, prompting him to exit and shoot Johnny before he can kill Troy. The pair run into the woods, but are both soon killed after Johnny regains consciousness and begins chasing them.

After Colt and Kris arrive at the ranger station, a park ranger investigating the scene notices the locket around Kris's neck and explains that the locket is what keeps Johnny's soul at rest. At the same time, Johnny emerges from the woods and attacks the group and kills the park ranger with a gas-powered log splitter while Colt and Kris escape.

The two return to the fire tower and plan to trap Johnny, but Colt is killed in the process. Kris, too shocked to implement the plan, leaves the locket hanging on a gas canister and flees into the woods. Kris injures her leg and limps to a nearby road where a woman picks her up. The woman notices Kris' injury and insists on stopping to apply a [[tourniquet]]. A panicked Kris pleads with her to keep driving, waiting for Johnny to appear from the nearby woods, but he does not. Back at the fire tower, the gas canister is shown, missing Johnny's locket.

==Production==
[[File:St John Ambulance Museum, Unley (8715828763).jpg|thumb|right|upright|A Vajen Bader smoke protector helmet, similar to that used by the film's villain]]Filming initially took place in the [[Kawartha Lakes]] area of [[Central Ontario]], Canada, in 2021, but Nash later recalled that the locale "just felt like it wasn't hitting the right note for us".<ref name="Gheciu 2024" /> The film was ultimately shot in [[Algoma District]], Ontario, near [[Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario|Sault Ste. Marie]].<ref name="Gheciu 2024" />

Commentators have drawn comparisons between ''In a Violent Nature'' and [[slow cinema]].<ref name="Gheciu 2024" /><ref name="Ehrlich 2024" /> Nash drew inspiration for the film's style from the [[Gus Van Sant]]-directed films ''[[Gerry (2002 film)|Gerry]]'' (2002), ''[[Elephant (2003 film)|Elephant]]'' (2003) and ''[[Last Days (2005 film)|Last Days]]'' (2005), which Nash characterizes as "slower, more methodical, more deliberate and follow characters through a scene."<ref name="Gheciu 2024" /> Nash cited the work of director [[Terrence Malick]] as an influence.<ref name="Earl 2024" /> Nash was also inspired by the Austrian horror film ''[[Angst (1983 film)|Angst]]''.<ref name="Earl 2024" />

''In a Violent Nature'' emphasizes static [[long take]] shots.<ref name="Eddy 2024">{{cite web|url=https://gizmodo.com/in-a-violent-nature-sundance-horror-review-shudder-1851179970|title=In a Violent Nature Turns the Slasher Genre on Its Head|last=Eddy|first=Cheryl|date=January 23, 2024|website=[[Gizmodo]]|access-date=January 24, 2024|archive-date=June 19, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240619105112/https://gizmodo.com/in-a-violent-nature-sundance-horror-review-shudder-1851179970|url-status=live}}</ref> "We always wanted to treat this almost like a [[nature documentary]]," Nash said of the film's stylistic approach. "It's lulling you into a sense [that] the danger isn't quite there. When you see tourists getting really close to bears, they're just like, 'Oh, no, look, it's not doing anything. It's fine.' And then all of a sudden, the bear just turns around and charges at you. You're way too defenseless and you just have no idea that they're capable of this much power and brutality."<ref name=usatoday>{{cite web|work=[[USA Today]]|last=Truitt|first=Brian|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/2024/05/31/in-a-violent-nature-movie-2024/73875234007/|url-status=live|title=Here's Johnny! Buzzy slasher movie 'In a Violent Nature' unleashes a gory kill to die for|archive-date=June 24, 2024|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240624020530/https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/2024/05/31/in-a-violent-nature-movie-2024/73875234007/}}</ref>

Nash has said that he chose to shoot the film in [[Academy ratio]] because "that's how I watched all the [[slasher film|slashers]] as a kid on [[VHS]]... That's the slasher aspect ratio."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.theringer.com/podcasts/the-big-picture/2024/06/04/hollywood-is-broken-lets-start-a-movie-studio|title=Hollywood Is Broken. Let's Start a Movie Studio.|website=www.theringer.com|access-date=8 January 2025|archive-date=January 9, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250109031900/https://www.theringer.com/podcasts/the-big-picture/2024/06/04/hollywood-is-broken-lets-start-a-movie-studio|url-status=live}}</ref> Nash chose not to use a traditional musical score, instead relying primarily on ambient sounds from the surrounding forest. He said the decision was intended to maintain an objective viewpoint and avoid influencing the audience's emotional response. <ref>https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/violent-nature-director-chris-nash-171731072.html</ref>

===Box office===
[[IFC Films]] released ''In a Violent Nature'' in North America on May 31, 2024 on 1,426 screens, marking the widest opening release of a film for the studio.<ref name=battle>{{cite web|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=June 1, 2024|url=https://deadline.com/2024/06/box-office-garfield-furiosa-haikyu-dumpster-battle-1235945804/|title='Garfield' & 'IF' Bully 'Furiosa' Into Third Place As Summer Box Office Recession Continues – Sunday AM Update|url-status=live|archive-date=June 2, 2024|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240602004611/https://deadline.com/2024/06/box-office-garfield-furiosa-haikyu-dumpster-battle-1235945804/|last=D'Alessandro|first=Anthony}}</ref> It earned $410,000 in Thursday night previews.<ref name=battle/> It went on to gross $2.1 million at the North American box office during its opening weekend, marking the second-best opening for IFC Films after ''[[Late Night with the Devil]]''.<ref name=battle/> By June 21, 2024, the film had earned $4,192,513 at the box office.<ref name=BOM/>

===Critical response===
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|79|6.6|187|consensus=As single-mindedly focused on mayhem as its demonic monster, ''In a Violent Nature'' serves up a deliciously disgusting feast for gore aficionados.|access-date=August 24, 2026|ref=y}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 68 out of 100, based on 26 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''In a Violent Nature'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/in-a-violent-nature |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=August 24, 2026 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]''{{'}}s Dennis Harvey wrote that the film's "stripped-down approach to a familiar gist has a distinctiveness that is impressive, and is sure to please fans who are always up for a new slasher film—but wish most of them weren't so interchangeable."<ref name="Harvey 2024" /> Cheryl Eddy of ''[[Gizmodo]]'' called it "a fascinating artistic experiment and genre fans should definitely give it a look. But it may also make you wish you were watching a traditional slasher—for something more fun and entertaining, may we suggest ''[[Friday the 13th Part 2]]''?—instead."<ref name="Eddy 2024" /> Jeannette Catsoulis of ''[[The New York Times]]'' praised the film's "dreamlike" atmosphere, concluding: "Nash has attempted an ambitious blend of art house and slaughterhouse whose rug-pulling ending will polarize, even as its moody logic prevails."<ref>{{cite news|work=[[The New York Times]]|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2024/05/30/movies/in-a-violent-nature-review-killing-them-softly.html|title='In a Violent Nature' Review: Killing Them Softly|last=Catsoulis|first=Jeannette|date=May 30, 2024|url-status=live|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240530152705/https://www.nytimes.com/2024/05/30/movies/in-a-violent-nature-review-killing-them-softly.html#selection-477.0-480.0|archive-date=May 30, 2024|access-date=May 29, 2026}}</ref>

[[Michael Gingold]] of ''[[Rue Morgue (magazine)|Rue Morgue]]'' wrote that the film's style "isn't quite a reinvention of the form, but it creates a different and very effective kind of tension."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://rue-morgue.com/sundance-24-movie-review-in-a-violent-nature-takes-a-different-uberbloody-look-at-a-forest-slasher/|title=Sundance '24 Movie Review: "In a Violent Nature" Takes a Different, Überbloody Look at a Forest Slasher|last=Gingold|first=Michael|author-link=Michael Gingold|date=January 23, 2024|website=[[Rue Morgue (magazine)|Rue Morgue]]|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240608222605/https://rue-morgue.com/sundance-24-movie-review-in-a-violent-nature-takes-a-different-uberbloody-look-at-a-forest-slasher/|archive-date=June 8, 2024|url-status=live|access-date=May 29, 2026}}</ref> David Ehrlich of ''[[IndieWire]]'' gave the film a grade of "C", writing that the film "derives its semi-hypnotic power from the disconnect it creates between the sheer movieness of ''what'' happens and the somnambulant nonchalance with which it all goes down. The effect is interesting in theory, but largely unsatisfying in execution (pun very much intended)."<ref name="Ehrlich 2024" />

In a list published on June 20, 2024, ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' ranked ''In a Violent Nature'' the best horror film of the year thus far, with writer William Earl noting: "Amid the outrageous kills and crisp camerawork lies a sharp [[satire]] laced with empathy, beauty and genuine fear."<ref name="Earl">{{cite web|work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|url=https://variety.com/lists/best-horror-movies-2024-ranked/|date=June 20, 2024|last=Earl|first=William|title=The Best Horror Movies of 2024 So Far, Ranked: 'Immaculate,' 'Late Night With the Devil,' 'In a Violent Nature' and More|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240621110617/https://variety.com/lists/best-horror-movies-2024-ranked/abigail/|archive-date=June 21, 2024|url-status=live|access-date=May 29, 2026}}</ref>
