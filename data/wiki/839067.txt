'''''Siberian Lady Macbeth''''' (Orig. '''''Sibirska Ledi Magbet'''''), also translated as ''Fury Is a Woman'', is a [[1962 in film|1962 film]] directed by [[Andrzej Wajda]], based on the [[novella]] ''[[Lady Macbeth of the Mtsensk District (story)|Lady Macbeth of the Mtsensk District]]'' by [[Nikolai Leskov]].

* Bojan Stupica - Izmajlow
* Miodrag Lazarević - Zinovij Izmailow 
* [[Branka Petrić]] - aunt
