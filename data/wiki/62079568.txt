'''''The Northman''''' is a 2022 American [[Epic film|epic]] [[Period film|period]] [[dark fantasy]] [[action film]] directed by [[Robert Eggers]], who {{nowrap|co-wrote}} the screenplay with [[Sjón]]. Based on the legend of [[Amleth]] from ''[[Gesta Danorum]]'' by [[Saxo Grammaticus]], the film follows Amleth, an exiled [[Vikings|Viking]] prince who sets out on a quest to avenge the murder of his father at the hands of his uncle at the height of the [[Viking Age]]. It features an [[ensemble cast]] of [[Alexander Skarsgård]], [[Nicole Kidman]], [[Claes Bang]], [[Anya Taylor-Joy]], [[Gustav Lindh]], [[Ethan Hawke]], [[Björk]], and [[Willem Dafoe]].

Eggers and Skarsgård, who both also produced, had met to discuss possible collaborations; Eggers decided to make the film his third project after meeting with Skarsgård, who had wanted to make a Viking film for several years. Much of the cast joined in October 2019 and filming took place in locations throughout Iceland, the Republic of Ireland, and Northern Ireland from August to December 2020. The film is heavily influenced by [[Norse mythology]].<ref>{{cite news|last=Sailes|first=J.S.|date=29 April 2022|title=The Northman: Every Nod to Norse Mythology, Explained|publisher=[[CBR.com]]|url=https://www.cbr.com/northman-norse-mythology-inspirations/|url-status=live|access-date=22 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220430181653/https://www.cbr.com/northman-norse-mythology-inspirations/|archive-date=30 April 2022}}</ref>

''The Northman'' premiered at [[Grauman's Chinese Theatre|TCL Chinese Theatre]] in [[Los Angeles]] on April 18, 2022, though it had already been released theatrically in some European and South American countries beginning on April 13. It was released in the United States on April 22. It received positive reviews from critics but underperformed at the box office, grossing either $75.8 or $69.6 million<ref name="BOM">{{Cite Box Office Mojo|title=The Northman|id=11138512|access-date=June 15, 2022}}</ref><ref name="TN">{{Cite The Numbers|title=The Northman|id=Northman-The-(2022)|access-date=July 10, 2022}}</ref> on a net budget of $70 million.<ref name=VarietyProj/><ref name="NewYorkerIssue" /> It later found an unexpected amount of financial success on [[Video on demand|VOD]] and home media, allowing it to recoup a large portion of its box office losses.<ref name="hollywoodreporter.com">{{cite web|last=Ritman|first=Alex|date=August 31, 2022|title=Venice: Focus Features' Production and Acquisitions Head Kiska Higgs on Todd Field's "Dazzling" 'TÁR,' Cate Blanchett Buzz: 'She Always Delivers!'|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/todd-field-tar-cate-blancett-1235206911/|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220831060115/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/todd-field-tar-cate-blancett-1235206911/|archive-date=August 31, 2022|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Mendelson |first1=Scott |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/scottmendelson/2022/09/19/as-beast-tops-vudu-how-pvod-may-help-save-the-non-franchise-movie/?sh=35b22e201c98|title=As 'Beast' Tops Vudu, How PVOD May Help Save the Non-Franchise Movie|website=[[Forbes]]}}</ref>

==Plot==
<!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summaries for feature films should be set between 400 to 700 words. -->
In [[Anno Domini|AD]] 895, in the North Atlantic, King [[Aurvandill]] "War-Raven" returns to the island of Hrafnsey, reuniting with his wife, Queen Gudrún, and his heir, Prince [[Amleth]]. To prepare Amleth for his eventual accession to the throne, the father and son participate in a ceremony overseen by Aurvandill's [[jester]], Heimir. Heimir tells Amleth that his destiny is fixed and cannot be escaped, and Amleth vows to avenge his father if Aurvandill is ever slain, rather than live his life in shame. The next morning, Amleth's bastard uncle [[Feng (chieftain)|Fjölnir]] stages a [[palace coup]], beheads Aurvandill, sacks the [[hillfort]] and carries away Gudrún. Amleth narrowly escapes Fjölnir's assassins and flees by boat, swearing vengeance.

Several years later, an adult Amleth is a type of [[berserker]], known as an [[ulfhéðinn]], within a band of Vikings. After sacking a village in [[Garðaríki]]<!-- DO NOT REMOVE THIS. The film shows "Gardariki" in runes. -->, he encounters a Seeress in the temple of [[Svetovit]] while the Vikings he accompanied led a campaign of pillage and murder; the Seeress orders Amleth to remember his oath of vengeance and tells him that his fate is intertwined with that of a Maiden-King. Soon after, he learns that "Fjölnir the Brotherless" has lost his throne to [[Harald Fairhair|Harald of Norway]] and now lives as a sheep farmer in Iceland. Branding himself as a [[thrall]], a slave, Amleth sneaks aboard a ship taking them to Iceland. He encounters an enslaved [[Early Slavs|Slavic]] woman named Olga, who claims to be a sorceress. They are taken to Fjölnir's farm, where Amleth learns his mother married Fjölnir and bore him a son named Gunnar. When most of the slaves are rejected as defective, Amleth displays his strength and aggression to impress Fjölnir, who ultimately decides to keep him and Olga due to her beauty.

One night, Amleth follows a [[vixen]] and encounters a [[warlock]], who facilitates a seance between Amleth and the skull of Heimir, whom Fjölnir has also murdered. Heimir tells Amleth about Draugr, a magical sword that can be drawn only at night or at the Gates of [[Hel (location)|Hel]]. Amleth enters a [[Tumulus#Scandinavia|barrow]] and obtains the blade from its owner, the [[Draugr|undead Mound Dweller]]. He hides the sword upon his return to the farm. The next day, Amleth is selected to compete in a game of [[knattleikr]] against another farm. The game turns violent when the leader of the opposing team brutalizes the competition until only he and Amleth are left. Gunnar (looking up to Amleth, not knowing he is his half-brother), is almost killed in trying to draw away the opposing player; but Amleth saves him, furiously beating his opponent to death. As a reward, Fjölnir's adult son, Thórir, grants him overseer duties and allows him to choose a woman.

During the evening celebrations, Amleth and Olga make love; they promise to overcome Fjölnir together. Over several nights Amleth kills prominent members of Fjölnir's estate in unnatural ways, arranging their impaled body parts into the shape of a horse in a gruesome display. This makes Fjölnir and the entire estate believe they are under attack from an evil spirit. [[Christian]] slaves are believed by Thórir to be responsible. Olga mixes the food supply with [[fly agaric]], a potent [[hallucinogen]]. This allows Amleth to enter Fjölnir's house. Amleth reveals his identity to Gudrún, who replies that she was Aurvandill's slave and that Amleth was [[Pregnancy from rape|conceived by rape]]. Gudrún also reveals that she masterminded Fjölnir's coup because she wanted both Aurvandill and Amleth dead. She tries to seduce Amleth, who, after a moment, rejects her. Enraged, Amleth kills Thórir, and cuts out his heart.

Gudrún reveals Amleth's true identity to Fjölnir and calls for Amleth's death. Fjölnir prepares to kill Olga, but Amleth offers to trade Olga's life for Thórir's heart. Amleth allows himself to be captured, and is tortured for the location of Thórir's heart. Amleth is released from his restraints by a flock of ravens sent by [[Odin]] and Olga rescues him. The two escape by boat to his kinsmen in the [[Orkney Islands]] after Amleth decides to abandon his quest for revenge. Aboard the vessel Amleth has a vision, and realizes that Olga is pregnant with twins, one of whom will be the Maiden-King prophesied by the Seeress. Realizing that Olga and their children will never be safe while Fjölnir lives, Amleth, despite Olga's pleas, jumps overboard and swims ashore reasoning that he cannot escape his fate.

Back at the farm, Amleth kills Fjölnir's remaining men and frees the slaves. While searching for Fjölnir, Amleth is attacked by Gudrún and Gunnar and kills them in self-defense after being wounded. Fjölnir, discovering his wife and son dead, coldly tells Amleth to meet him at the Gates of Hel: the crater of the volcano [[Hekla]]. Amleth and Fjölnir engage naked in a fierce [[Holmgang]] (duel) until Fjölnir is beheaded and Amleth is simultaneously stabbed. As he dies, Amleth has a vision of Olga embracing their two children, assuring him they are safe before urging him to let go. A [[valkyrie]] carries Amleth through the gates of [[Valhalla]].

===Post-production===
The film, originally planned to cost around $65 million, ended up costing between $70–90 million to produce. Eggers found the editing process to be the most difficult of his career. Feedback from [[test screening]]s indicated that the film's first act was too slow. More feedback showed that audiences found the Old Norse dialogue difficult to understand, which resulted in most of it being replaced in [[Dubbing#ADR/post-sync|ADR]] sessions. The final cut was eventually approved on November 3, 2021.<ref name="VarietyProj"/><ref name="NewYorkerIssue"/>

=== Music ===
{{Main|The Northman (soundtrack)}}
For the film's score, Eggers brought in former [[Tri Angle (record label)|Tri Angle]] record label artists, [[Robin Carolan]] and [[Vessel (solo artist)|Vessel]] (Sebastian Gainsborough), for composition and production. They researched extensively on the history of Viking music, including discussions with ethnographer Poul Høxbro,<ref>{{cite web |title=The Northman Composers Open up About Crafting a Symphony of Brutality |url=https://comicbook.com/movies/news/the-northman-movie-composers-music-score-soundtrack-interview-robin-carolan-sebastian-gainsborough/ |access-date=2022-12-07 |website=Comicbook.com |date=April 28, 2022 |publisher=Comic Book Resources}}</ref> and used instruments based on [[Nordic folk music]], such as [[Talharpa|tagelharpa]], [[langspil]], kravik lyre, and [[säckpipa]]. They further experimented with the instruments they had, to create that ethnic Nordic sound,<ref>{{cite web |last=Major |first=Michael |title=Robin Carolan & Sebastian Gainsborough Collaborate on THE NORTHMAN Soundtrack |url=https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Robin-Carolan-Sebastian-Gainsborough-Collaborate-on-THE-NORTHMAN-Soundtrack-20220412 |access-date=2022-12-07 |website=BroadwayWorld.com}}</ref> which includes a 40-member string ensemble mimicking the sound of an archaic instrument called [[bullroarer]].<ref name=":02">{{cite web |date=2022-04-21 |title=How The Northman's Composers Dreamed Up a Different Kind of Viking Movie Score |url=https://pitchfork.com/thepitch/the-northman-music-score-soundtrack/ |access-date=2022-12-07 |website=Pitchfork |language=en-US}}</ref>

The score album was released by [[Back Lot Music]] on April 15, 2022, and featured 43 tracks.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ryan |first=Danielle |date=2022-04-06 |title=The Northman Soundtrack To Be Released Digitally Next Week — Listen To A Stormy Track |url=https://www.slashfilm.com/829722/the-northman-soundtrack-to-be-released-digitally-next-week-listen-to-a-stormy-track/ |access-date=2022-12-07 |website=/Film |language=en-US}}</ref> On July 1, [[Sacred Bones Records]] released the album in CD, vinyl and cassettes.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Pearis |first=Bill |title=Vessel & Robin Carolan scored new Robert Eggers film 'The Northman' (exclusive vinyl pre-order) |url=https://www.brooklynvegan.com/vessel-robin-carolan-scored-new-robert-eggers-film-the-northman-exclusive-vinyl-pre-order/ |access-date=2022-12-07 |website=BrooklynVegan |date=April 12, 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=The Northman Soundtrack Getting Vinyl, CD, and Cassette Release |url=https://comicbook.com/horror/news/the-northman-soundtrack-music-vinyl-cd-cassette-release-score/ |access-date=2022-12-07 |website=Comic Book Resources|date=July 14, 2022 }}</ref>

=== Home media performance ===
Eggers commented on the film's underwhelming box office, stating that "[The film] met the expectations of a bad marketplace ... Am I disappointed that, three to four weeks in, we're on VOD because that's the way things are done in the post-COVID world? Yeah. But it's doing great on VOD, so there you go."<ref name=":3">{{cite news |last=Schager |first=Nick |date=May 28, 2022 |title=Robert Eggers: 'I Need to Restrategize' After 'The Northman' |newspaper=[[The Daily Beast]] |url=https://www.thedailybeast.com/robert-eggers-feels-he-needs-to-restrategize-after-the-northman?ref=scroll |url-status=live |access-date=2022-05-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220528113558/https://www.thedailybeast.com/robert-eggers-feels-he-needs-to-restrategize-after-the-northman |archive-date=2022-05-28}}</ref> In its debut weekend on [[Video on demand#Premium_video_on_demand_(PVOD)|PVOD]] in the U.S., the film was the top-rented title on [[iTunes]], third on [[Vudu]] and fourth on [[Google Play]], making about the same amount of revenue as ''The Bad Guys'' and ''[[The Lost City (2022 film)|The Lost City]]'', despite grossing much less than both in theaters. ''[[IndieWire]]'' wrote that "''The Northman'' looks like the type of film that, even with lower theatrical returns, is greatly elevated by that exposure."<ref name=":1">{{cite web |last=Brueggemann |first=Tom |date=2022-05-16 |title=Three Movies Claim #1 VOD Slots and None Are Superhero Movies: Is This What Hope Feels Like? |url=https://www.indiewire.com/2022/05/vod-charts-the-northman-bad-guys-and-the-lost-city-all-number-one-1234725139/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220527010130/https://www.indiewire.com/2022/05/vod-charts-the-northman-bad-guys-and-the-lost-city-all-number-one-1234725139/ |archive-date=2022-05-27 |access-date=2022-05-28 |website=[[IndieWire]]}}</ref> The following week, it finished third on the iTunes and Vudu charts, and fifth on Google Play.<ref name=":2">{{cite web |last=Brueggemann |first=Tom |date=2022-05-23 |title=Sony Tops All VOD Charts with 'Morbius' and a Reduced-Price 'Uncharted' |url=https://www.indiewire.com/2022/05/sony-tops-all-vod-charts-morbius-uncharted-1234727529/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220524231116/https://www.indiewire.com/2022/05/sony-tops-all-vod-charts-morbius-uncharted-1234727529/ |archive-date=2022-05-24 |access-date=2022-05-28 |website=[[IndieWire]]}}</ref>

In its debut week on the [[DVD]]/[[Blu-ray]] market, the film debuted at the No. 1 spot on both the NPD VideoScan First Alert chart (which tracks combined DVD and Blu-ray disc unit sales) and the Blu-ray disc sales chart for the week ending June 11, 2022.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Latchem |first=John |date=2022-06-16 |title='The Northman' Conquers Disc Sales Charts |url=https://www.mediaplaynews.com/the-northman-conquers-disc-sales-charts/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220618024741/https://www.mediaplaynews.com/the-northman-conquers-disc-sales-charts/ |archive-date=2022-06-18 |access-date=2022-06-20 |website=[[Media Play News]] |language=en-US}}</ref><ref name=":4">{{Cite web |last=Greenwood |first=Sean |date=2022-06-17 |title=Blu-ray Sales: June 5–11: The Northman Knocks Out The Batman |url=https://www.blu-ray.com/news/?id=30842 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220618030151/https://www.blu-ray.com/news/?id=30842 |archive-date=2022-06-18 |access-date=2022-06-20}}</ref>

On September 28, 2023, ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' reported the film "approached breakeven thanks to overindexing on Premium VOD".<ref name="deadline">{{cite web |last=D'Alessandro |first=Anthony |date=September 28, 2023 |title='The Creator' Looks To Turn Moviegoers Into Believers: How Disney Marketed Gareth Edwards' Original Sci-Fi Pic |url=https://deadline.com/2023/09/the-creator-box-office-projection-marketing-1235555695/ |access-date=September 28, 2023 |website=Deadline Hollywood}}</ref> Focus Features' president of production and acquisitions, Kiska Higgs, also stated that the film was "OK for us in the end", though she noted that costs were shared by New Regency, and Focus Features' wasn't "front and center on production of [the film]".<ref name="hollywoodreporter.com"/>

===Critical response===
The [[review aggregator]] [[Rotten Tomatoes]] reported that 90% of 383 critics gave the film a positive review, with an average rating of 7.7/10. The website's critical consensus reads: "A bloody revenge epic and breathtaking visual marvel, ''The Northman'' finds filmmaker Robert Eggers expanding his scope without sacrificing any of his signature style."<ref>{{Cite Rotten Tomatoes|id=the_northman|type=m|title=The Northman (2022) |access-date=February 25, 2023}}</ref> [[Metacritic]] assigned the film a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average score]] of 82 out of 100 based on sixty critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{Cite Metacritic |id=the-northman |type=movie |title=The Northman Reviews |accessdate=April 27, 2022}}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average grade of "B" on an A+ to F scale, while [[PostTrak]] gave it a 75% positive score (with an average 3.5 out of five stars), with 56% saying they would definitely recommend it.<ref name="opening" />
