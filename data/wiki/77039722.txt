== Plot ==
In December 1970, former Brazilian congressman [[Rubens Paiva]] lives in a house near [[Leblon]] beach in [[Rio de Janeiro]] with his wife [[Eunice Paiva|Eunice]] and their five children. Returning to his civil career, after the revocation of his tenure as a congressman following the [[1964 Brazilian coup d'état]], Paiva continues to support political expatriates without disclosing his activities to his family.

After [[Armed struggle against the Brazilian military dictatorship|revolutionary movements]] kidnap the Swiss ambassador to Brazil, the country faces looming political instability. The Paivas' friends {{ill|Fernando Gasparian|pt|lt=Fernando}} and Dalva Gasparian decide to seek refuge in [[London]], taking the Paivas' eldest daughter, Vera, with them. Vera had previously witnessed military violence while returning from the cinema with her friends. Paiva's house is raided by the military and he is [[List of people killed by and disappeared during the Brazilian military dictatorship|arrested and disappears]] in January 1971. Eunice's public inquiries about his whereabouts result in her arrest and torture for 12 days, during which she is questioned about her husband's involvement in pro-democracy movements, which she denies. Eliana, their teenage daughter, is also imprisoned but soon released.

Newspaper reports claim that Rubens fled the country into exile, but Eunice and her friends suspect otherwise. With the help of lawyer Lino Machado, she files a [[habeas corpus]] petition. She also learns from family friend Bocaiuva Cunha that Rubens had been secretly helping political exiles. A former teacher, Martha, confirms she was imprisoned with Rubens but is afraid to speak out publicly. She later writes a letter detailing her arrest. Félix, a journalist and family friend, informs Eunice that Rubens was killed, but the military authorities refuse to confirm it. Left to care for her children alone, Eunice sells their home and moves to [[São Paulo]], anticipating a new start close to her maternal family. 

Twenty five years later, in 1996, surrounded by journalists while receiving Rubens Paiva's official death certificate from the [[History of Brazil (1985–present)|Brazilian state]], now once again a democracy, Eunice calls for reparations for victims' families and accountability for the military dictatorship's crimes.

In 2014, during a family gathering surrounded by her children and grandchildren, the now 85-year-old Eunice lives with advanced [[Alzheimer's disease]]. When a news report about the [[National Truth Commission]] addresses Rubens's case, a distressed Eunice appears to remember him.

An epilogue reveals that five men were identified as responsible for the murder of Paiva, who was killed at the [[DOI-CODI]] headquarters between January 21 and 22, 1971, but none were prosecuted. The epilogue also notes that Eunice graduated from law school at 48 and became one of the few experts on [[indigenous rights in Brazil]], serving as a counselor for the [[Federal government of Brazil|federal government]], the [[World Bank]], and the [[United Nations]]. She died in 2018 at the age of 89.

== Production ==

=== Development ===
The screenplay is by Murilo Hauser and Heitor Lorega. It is adapted from the memoir ''[[Ainda Estou Aqui]]'' by [[Marcelo Rubens Paiva]], Eunice's son. Hauser also co-wrote the screenplay for [[Karim Aïnouz]]'s ''[[The Invisible Life of Eurídice Gusmão]]'' (2019), based on [[Martha Batalha]]'s novel of the same name.

A co-production between Brazil and France, the film was produced by RT Features and VideoFilmes in collaboration with [[Globoplay]], Mact Productions, Conspiração Filmes and [[Arte France Cinéma]].

=== Filming ===
[[Principal photography]] began during May and June 2023 on location in [[Rio de Janeiro]], during November and December filming took place in [[São Paulo|Sāo Paulo]], shooting for 16 weeks in total.<ref name=":7">{{Cite web |title=DP Adrian Teijido ABC used the texture of KODAK 35mm film grain to… |url=https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/blog-post/im-still-here/ |access-date=2026-01-26 |website=Kodak |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-12-16 |title=I'm Still Here Filming Locations (2024) - 4 Filming |url=https://4filming.com/im-still-here-filming-locations-2024/ |access-date=2026-01-26 |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Amando |first=Rodrigo |date=18 June 2023 |title=Começam as filmagens do longa "Ainda Estou Aqui", dirigido por Walter Salles |url=https://siterg.uol.com.br/cultura/2023/06/18/comecam-as-filmagens-do-longa-ainda-estou-aqui-dirigido-por-walter-salles/ |access-date=29 May 2024 |website=RG |language=pt-BR}}</ref> Cinematographer Adrian Teijido shot the production on [[35 mm movie film|35mm]], aiming to recreate the Brazilian 1970's mood, selected scenes were also shot in [[Super 8 film|Super 8mm]].<ref name=":7" />

==Reception==

=== Box office ===
On its opening day in Brazil, ''I'm Still Here'' brought 50,320 people to the cinemas, grossing [[Brazilian real|R$]]1.1 million.<ref>{{Cite web |date=8 November 2024 |title=Ainda Estou Aqui bate R$ 1 milhão em bilheteria em sua data de estreia |url=https://www.metropoles.com/entretenimento/cinema/ainda-estou-aqui-bate-r-1-milhao-em-bilheteria-na-sua-data-de-estreia |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=[[Metrópoles]] |language=pt-BR}}</ref> In its first weekend, even though it was the target of a frustrated boycott by the [[Bolsonarism|Brazilian far right]],<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |date=12 November 2024 |title=Perfis de direita pregam boicote a 'Ainda Estou Aqui' nas redes |url=https://f5.folha.uol.com.br/cinema-e-series/2024/11/perfis-de-direita-pregam-boicote-a-ainda-estou-aqui-nas-redes.shtml |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=F5 |language=pt-BR}}</ref> the film debuted in first place at the box office with 358,000 admissions, earning R$8.6 million, surpassing the third week of ''[[Venom: The Last Dance]]'' (R$6.6 million) and fellow new release ''[[Red One (film)|Red One]]'' (R$5.3 million).<ref>{{Cite web |date=11 November 2024 |title='Ainda Estou Aqui' estreia na liderança da bilheteria nacional e arrecada R$ 8,6 milhões {{!}} Cinema |url=https://g1.globo.com/pop-arte/cinema/noticia/2024/11/11/ainda-estou-aqui-estreia-na-lideranca-da-bilheteria-nacional-e-arrecada-r-86-milhoes.ghtml |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref>

It was released in Portugal on January 16, 2025, where it quickly became popular, surpassing 100,000 spectators within two weeks, according to the Portuguese newspaper ''Público''.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rattner |first=Jair |date=2025-01-15 |title=Ainda Estou Aqui lota salas de cinema em Portugal como blockbusters de Hollywood |url=https://www.publico.pt/2025/01/15/publico-brasil/noticia/aqui-lota-salas-cinema-portugal-blockbusters-hollywood-2118948 |access-date=2025-04-28 |website=PÚBLICO |language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Varela |first=Felipe Eduardo |date=2025-01-27 |title=Ainda Estou Aqui ultrapassa 100 mil espectadores e conquista novo prêmio |url=https://www.publico.pt/2025/01/27/publico-brasil/noticia/aqui-ultrapassa-100-mil-espectadores-conquista-novo-premio-2120287 |access-date=2025-04-28 |website=PÚBLICO |language=pt}}</ref>

Upon its release, ''I'm Still Here'' received overwhelming praise from the public, film critics, and the press; Torres's performance was particularly acclaimed.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 September 2024 |title=Atuação de Fernanda Torres em 'Ainda Estou Aqui' é elogiada por crítica internacional: 'Deve catapultá-la a prêmios' {{!}} Cinema |url=https://g1.globo.com/pop-arte/cinema/noticia/2024/09/02/atuacao-de-fernanda-torres-em-ainda-estou-aqui-e-elogiada-por-critica-internacional.ghtml |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref> {{Rotten Tomatoes prose|97|8.3|184|consensus=Carried along by Fernanda Torres' superb performance, ''I'm Still Here'' poignantly explores a nation's upheaval through one family's search for answers.|ref=yes|access-date=16 March 2025}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 85 out of 100, based on 41 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''I&#39;m Still Here'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/im-still-here-2024 |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=21 February 2025 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref> 

Jessica Kiang of ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' praised the film and its dramatic charge: "Classical in form but radical in empathy, ''I'm Still Here'' arguably does not need the follow-up sections—one set in 1996 and the other in 2014—that somewhat alter the emotional rhythm. But on the other hand, these characters are so vivid that we don't want to leave them either".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kiang |first=Jessica |date=1 September 2024 |title='I'm Still Here' Review: Walter Salles' Profoundly Moving Sense-Memory Portrait of a Family — and a Nation — Ruptured |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/im-still-here-review-walter-salles-1236121390/ |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=Variety}}</ref> For Wendy Ide of ''[[Screen Daily]]'', Salles "never over-labours the film's emotional beats, relying instead on Torres' magnificent, intricately layered performance to drive the picture"; she also praised Montenegro, "who has a brief but exceptionally powerful cameo here as the elderly Eunice".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ide |first=Wendy |title='I'm Still Here': Venice Review |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/im-still-here-venice-review/5196729.article |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=[[Screen Daily]]}}</ref>

Several international outlets applauded Torres's performance, with ''[[Collider (website)|Collider]]'' considering it one of the best of the year and "more than deserving of an [[Academy Award for Best Actress|Oscar]] nomination".<ref name=":4">{{Cite web |last=Kiely |first=Emma |date=11 October 2024 |title='I'm Still Here' Review: Family Political Drama Is One of the Year's Best {{!}} LFF 2024 |url=https://collider.com/im-still-here-2024-review/ |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=Collider}}</ref> In her review for ''[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]]'', Stephanie Bunbury calls the film a "celebration of Brazil" and praises Torres, writing that she "has an emotional delicacy as Eunice that conveys, through the smallest and subtlest signals, what it costs her to hold back her anxiety and anger for the sake of her family. It is a performance that should catapult her into the awards race, 25 years after her mother Fernanda Montenegro was Oscar-nominated for Salles' breakthrough feature, ''[[Central Station (film)|Central Station]]''".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bunbury |first=Stephanie |date=1 September 2024 |title='I'm Still Here' Review: Walter Salles' Love Letter To Brazil Is A Powerful Warning From History – Venice Film Festival |url=https://deadline.com/2024/09/im-still-here-review-walter-salles-love-letter-to-brazil-is-a-warning-from-history-venice-film-festival-1236075262/ |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]}}</ref> David Rooney in ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' highlighted the relationship between Montenegro and Torres, writing, "What makes the connection even more poignant is that she appears as the elderly, infirm version of the protagonist". He called ''I'm Still Here'' "a gripping, profoundly touching film with a deep well of [[pathos]]. It's one of Salles' best".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rooney |first=David |date=1 September 2024 |title='I'm Still Here' Review: Walter Salles Returns Home With the Powerful Story of a Broken Family's Resistance |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/im-still-here-review-walter-salles-fernanda-torres-1235989211/ |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=The Hollywood Reporter}}</ref> For [[IndieWire]], Leila Latif called Torres's performance "as spectacular as her filmography would suggest, having marked herself out as one of the South American continent's greatest actors in roles in ''[[Foreign Land (film)|Foreign Land]]'' (also directed by Salles) and won a [[Cannes Film Festival Award for Best Actress|Best Actress Award]] in ''[[Love Me Forever or Never]]''. Her Eunice possesses phenomenal strength and stoicism which make each moment of pain that peep through the chinks of her armor all the more moving". Latif also praised Torres's on-screen interaction with Selton Mello.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Latif |first=Leila |date=1 September 2024 |title='I'm Still Here' Review: The Legacy of Rubens Paiva Is Further Fortified by Walter Salles' Loving Biopic |url=https://www.indiewire.com/criticism/movies/im-still-here-review-walter-salles-1235042776/ |access-date=13 November 2024 |website=IndieWire}}</ref>

Filmmaker [[Alfonso Cuarón]] named it one of his favorite films of 2024, saying, "Watching a Walter Salles film is to be embraced in generosity, is like experiencing a gravitational pull, both lifting and grounding us at the same time with an invisible yet undeniable force. With ''I'm Still Here'', this effect is even more compelling.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Horst |first=Carole |date=2024-12-18 |title=Directors Pick Favorite Films of 2024: Christopher Nolan Praises 'Gladiator II,' Barry Jenkins on 'Nickel Boys,' Jeff Nichols on 'Sing Sing' and More |url=https://variety.com/lists/christopher-nolan-gladiator-2-michael-mann-barry-jenkins/blitz/ |access-date=2025-01-08 |website=Variety}}</ref> Many other filmmakers, including [[Nicole Holofcener]] and [[Chad Hartigan]], also cited it as among their favorite films of 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last=O'Falt |first=Chris |date=2024-12-30 |title=65 Directors Pick Their Favorite Films of 2024 |url=https://www.indiewire.com/gallery/directors-pick-best-films-2024/mcdanor-ec002/ |access-date=2025-03-03 |website=IndieWire |language=en-US}}</ref>

In 2025, ''[[Collider (website)|Collider]]'' ranked it third on its list of the "10 Essential Movies of the 2020s So Far", with Eddie Possehl writing, "With the disease of misinformation and government corruption spreading across the real world, the story told within ''I'm Still Here'' is a vital watch for people of every age as society continues to evolve and degrade at the same time."<ref>{{Cite web |date=26 January 2025 |title=10 Essential Movies of the 2020s So Far, Ranked |url=https://collider.com/2020s-movies-essential-ranked/ |access-date=23 February 2025 |website=Collider}}</ref>

''I'm Still Here'' was named one of the [[National Board of Review: Top Five International Films|Top 5 International Films]] of 2024 by the [[National Board of Review]]<ref name="NBR" /> and one of 50 Best Films of the year by British film magazine ''[[Sight and Sound|Sight & Sound]]''.<ref>{{Cite web |date=6 December 2024 |title=The 50 best films of 2024 |url=https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/polls/50-best-films-2024 |access-date=6 December 2024 |website=BFI}}</ref> In July 2025, it ranked 253rd in the "Readers' Choice" edition of ''[[The New York Times]]''{{'}}s list of "The 100 Best Movies of the 21st Century".<ref>{{cite web |title=Readers Choose Their Top Movies of the 21st Century |url=https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/movies/readers-movies-21st-century.html |website=The New York Times |access-date=2 July 2025}}</ref>

{{Main|List of accolades received by I'm Still Here (2024 film)}}The film [[List of accolades received by I'm Still Here (2024 film)|received several awards and nominations]]. Torres won in her category, becoming the first Brazilian actress to win a [[Golden Globe Awards|Golden Globe]] in an acting category.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Rubin |first=Rebecca |date=6 January 2025 |title=‘I’m Still Here’ Star Fernanda Torres Wins Golden Globe 26 Years After Her Mom Was Nominated in Same Category |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/news/fernanda-torres-golden-globes-best-actress-drama-film-1236262309/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250106111411/https://variety.com/2025/film/news/fernanda-torres-golden-globes-best-actress-drama-film-1236262309/ |archive-date=6 January 2025 |access-date=25 January 2025 |magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> The film was also nominated for [[BAFTA Award for Best Film Not in the English Language|Best Film Not in the English Language]] at the [[78th British Academy Film Awards]].<ref>{{Cite news |date=15 January 2025 |title=Bafta Film Awards 2025: The nominations list in full |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy7gkgdjn8ko |access-date=25 January 2025 |publisher=[[BBC News]]}}</ref>

=== Cultural impact ===
Brazilians widely embraced ''I'm Still Here'', the film's release coincided with the celebrations marking 40 years since the restoration of democracy in Brazil.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Rogero |first=Tiago |date=2025-03-03 |title=Jubilant Brazilians hail I’m Still Here’s Oscar as landmark in fight for justice |url=https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/mar/03/oscars-im-still-here-brazil-dictatorship |access-date=2025-04-27 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref> The country still pays reparations for the regime's victims families, following an amnesty approved during the Military dictatorship that prevented accountability for crimes committed by the regime officials.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Szalai |first=Georg |date=2025-03-03 |title=‘I’m Still Here’ Director Credits Fernanda Torres, Real-Life Inspiration in Brazil’s First International Feature Win |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/im-still-here-oscar-international-film-1236148159/ |access-date=2025-04-27 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film's success also strongly resonated during the 2025 [[Brazilian Carnival]] season. Torres was a prominent figure in the festivities. The online shopping platform [[Shopee]] reported a surge in demand for costumes inspired by her look during the Golden Globes, while in [[São Paulo]] stores along the popular shopping street "[[Rua 25 de Março]]" were filled with Oscar statues props and themed costumes.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-02-20 |title=Fernanda Torres e Globo de Ouro estão entre as fantasias mais procuradas pelos capixabas para o Carnaval 2025 |url=https://g1.globo.com/es/espirito-santo/carnaval/2025/noticia/2025/02/20/fernanda-torres-e-globo-de-ouro-estao-entre-as-fantasias-mais-procuradas-pelos-capixabas-para-o-carnaval-2025.ghtml |access-date=2025-04-28 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-02-14 |title=Oscar e Fernanda Torres viram tendência no Carnaval e dominam o comércio da 25 de Março em SP; FOTOS |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-paulo/carnaval/2025/noticia/2025/02/14/oscar-e-fernanda-torres-viram-tendencia-no-carnaval-e-dominam-o-comercio-da-25-de-marco-em-sp-fotos.ghtml |access-date=2025-04-28 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref>
