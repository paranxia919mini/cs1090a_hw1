'''''State of Firsts''''' is a 2025 documentary film directed by [[Chase Joynt]] that follows the historic election and political journey of [[United States House of Representatives|U.S. Representative]] [[Sarah McBride]], the first openly transgender person elected to the [[United States Congress]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ramirez |first=Nikki McCann |date=2025-06-06 |title='I'm More Confident Now': Sarah McBride Reflects on Trans Attacks in Congress |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/sarah-mcbride-interview-trans-lawmaker-state-of-firsts-1235357604/ |access-date=2026-04-07 |website=Rolling Stone |language=en-US}}</ref> The film premiered at [[Tribeca Festival|Tribeca Film Festival]] on June 11, 2025.<ref name="auto2">{{Cite web |title=State of Firsts {{!}} 2025 Tribeca Festival |url=https://tribecafilm.com/films/state-of-firsts-2025 |access-date=August 14, 2025 |website=Tribeca}}</ref>

== Plot ==
The film follows [[Sarah McBride]] during her campaign for Congress and the period after her historic election, documenting her efforts to navigate political responsibilities while facing public scrutiny and opposition tied to her identity. It highlights her responses to challenges in Washington, as well as the personal and professional pressures of becoming the first openly transgender member of the [[U.S. Congress]].

The film includes a conversation between McBride and fellow [[United States House of Representatives|U.S. Representative]] [[Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez]], in which they discuss the pressures of being "firsts" in Congress and the challenges of navigating public attention and political scrutiny.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Rep. Sarah McBride Details Unique Bond with AOC Over the 'Spotlight' They've Both Faced in Congress (Exclusive) |url=https://people.com/rep-sarah-mcbride-unique-bond-with-aoc-11747168 |access-date=August 14, 2025 |website=People.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Production ==
''State of Firsts'' was directed by [[Chase Joynt]] and filmed with immersive, behind-the-scenes access during Sarah McBride's 2024 congressional campaign and early tenure in office.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sarah McBride doc to premiere at Tribeca Film Festival {{!}} Advocate.com |url=https://www.advocate.com/news/sarah-mcbride-documentary-premieres |access-date=2026-04-07 |website=www.advocate.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Release ==
''State of Firsts'' made its world premiere at [[Tribeca Festival|Tribeca Film Festival]] 2025.<ref name="auto2" /> It also premiered at DC/DOX Film Festival 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |title=State of Firsts |url=https://dcdoxfest.com/films/state-of-firsts/ |access-date=August 14, 2025 |website=DC/DOX |language=en-US}}</ref> The film was released in select theaters on June 12, 2026.<ref name="19-12jun2026">{{cite news |last1=Rummler |first1=Orion |title=In new documentary, Sarah McBride navigates anti-trans hate on her historic path to Congress |url=https://19thnews.org/2026/06/sarah-mcbride-documentary-congress/ |access-date=June 12, 2026 |work=[[The 19th]] |date=June 12, 2026}}</ref>

== Reception ==
[[The Hollywood Reporter]] noted that ''State of Firsts'' presents a multifaceted portrayal of McBride, depicting her simultaneously as a public official and a symbolic figure. The review highlighted the film's focus on her efforts to balance these roles, suggesting that its exploration of the pressures and limitations of representation adds complexity beyond a purely celebratory narrative.<ref name="auto1">{{Cite web |last=Fienberg |first=Daniel |date=June 9, 2025 |title='State of Firsts' Review: Trans Congresswoman Sarah McBride Steps Into the Spotlight for a Doc That's More Than Your Average Political Puff Piece |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/state-of-firsts-review-sarah-mcbride-trans-congress-1236258455/ |access-date=August 14, 2025 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref>
