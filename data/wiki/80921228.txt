'''''The Cycle of Love''''' is a 2025 documentary film about the journey of [[P. K. Mahanandia]], a Delhi street artist who in 1977 traveled 6,000 miles by bicycle to reunite with a woman he loved. The film premiered at the [[Telluride Film Festival]] on 29 August 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Howat |first=Daniel |date=2025-08-31 |title="THE CYCLE OF LOVE" - Review |url=https://nextbestpicture.com/the-cycle-of-love/ |access-date=2025-10-27 |website=Next Best Picture |language=en-US}}</ref> It was directed by [[Orlando von Einsiedel]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2025/08/the-cycle-of-love-telluride-documentary-exclusive-clip-1236496440/|title='The Cycle Of Love' First-Look Clip: Epic True Love Story From Director Orlando Von Einsiedel & EP Priyanka Chopra Jonas To Premiere At Telluride|first=Matthew|last=Carey|date=August 28, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/hindi/bollywood/news/priyanka-chopra-feels-honored-and-delighted-as-the-cycle-of-love-is-set-to-premiere-at-telluride/articleshow/123591042.cms|title=Priyanka Chopra feels honored and delighted as 'The Cycle Of Love' is set to premiere at Telluride|via=The Economic Times - The Times of India}}</ref>

The film won the Artemis Rising Foundation Award at the [[Hamptons International Film Festival]]<ref>{{Cite web |title=HIFF33: Awards Announced! |url=https://hamptonsfilmfest.org/hiff33-awards-announced/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251107000139/https://hamptonsfilmfest.org/hiff33-awards-announced/ |archive-date=2025-11-07 |access-date=2025-11-07 |website=[[Hamptons International Film Festival]] |language=en-US}}</ref> and the Audience Award for Best Documentary at the Middleburg Film Festival<ref>{{Cite web |last=Davis |first=Clayton |date=2025-10-20 |title=‘Hamnet’ and ‘Rental Family’ Tie for Middleburg Film Festival Audience Award |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/news/hamnet-rental-family-middleburg-audience-award-2025-1236557302/ |access-date=2026-04-05 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref>.

== Reception ==

=== Critical reception ===
Reviews so far have been positive. Christopher Campbell of Nonfics said "Everything about this film is beautiful" and "It’s one of the best movies I’ve seen this year, fiction or nonfiction"<ref>https://www.nonfics.com/p/cycle-of-love-review</ref>. Daniel Howat of Next Best Picture said "''The Cycle of Love'' employs gorgeously filmed recreations to immerse viewers in an epic love story that spans continents and decades.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wiseman |first=Andreas|date=2026-05-07 |title=Orlando Von Einsiedel’s ‘The Cycle Of Love’, Exec Produced By Priyanka Chopra, Gets U.S. Deal; Dogwoof Locks In Overseas Pacts|url=https://deadline.com/2026/05/orlando-von-einsiedel-cycle-of-love-us-deal-release-1236886931/|access-date=2026-05-10 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref>. It is a moving and deeply romantic film about destiny, connection, and the journey that binds them."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Howat |first=Daniel |date=2025-08-31 |title="THE CYCLE OF LOVE" - Review |url=https://nextbestpicture.com/the-cycle-of-love/ |access-date=2026-04-05 |website=Next Best Picture |language=en-US}}</ref> Robert Daniels of Rogerebert.com said the film crafts "some of the most believable reenactments I’ve ever seen" and that the film was "Delicate and heartwarming, Orlando von Einsiedel’s stirring documentary ''The Cycle of Love'' is the kind of film whose sweet subject transcends time and borders."<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-03 |title=Telluride Film Festival 2025: The Cycle of Love, H is for Hawk, La Grazia |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/telluride-film-festival-2025-the-cycle-of-love-h-is-for-hawk-la-grazia |access-date=2026-04-05 |website=www.rogerebert.com |language=en-US}}</ref>
