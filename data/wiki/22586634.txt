'''''Aloïse''''' is a 1975 French [[drama film]] directed by [[Liliane de Kermadec]]. It was entered into the [[1975 Cannes Film Festival]].<ref name="festival-cannes.com">{{cite web |url=http://www.festival-cannes.com/en/archives/ficheFilm/id/2177/year/1975.html |title=Festival de Cannes: Aloïse |access-date=27 April 2009|work=festival-cannes.com}}</ref>

[[Tim Palmer (film historian)|Tim Palmer]] has written about this film in his article, "Enraged to Live: Reviving Liliane de Kermadec’s ''Aloïse''," in ''French Screen Studies''.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Palmer |first1=Tim |title=Enraged to Live: Reviving Liliane de Kermadec’s Aloïse |journal=French Screen Studies |date=2024 |doi=10.1080/26438941.2024.2389626 |url=https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/26438941.2024.2389626|url-access=subscription }}</ref>

==Plot==
The unsuccessful Swiss artist [[Aloïse Corbaz]] finds work at the court of the German emperor and gets infatuated. Showing frank symptoms of insanity she is hospitalised.
