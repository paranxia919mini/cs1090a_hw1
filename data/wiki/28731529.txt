'''Ben Rivers''' (born 1972) is an artist and [[experimental film]]maker based in London, England. His work has been screened at film festivals and galleries around the world and have won numerous awards. Rivers' work ranges in themes, including exploring unknown wilderness territories to candid and intimate portraits of real-life subjects.

==Life and career==
Ben Rivers studied fine art at [[Falmouth University]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.lux.org.uk/collection/artists/ben-rivers |title=Lux. Ben Rivers |access-date=9 December 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110927100049/http://www.lux.org.uk/collection/artists/ben-rivers |archive-date=27 September 2011 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref> His practice as a filmmaker treads a line between documentary and fiction. Often following and filming people who have in some way separated themselves from society, the raw film footage provides Rivers with a starting point for creating oblique narratives imagining alternative existences in marginal worlds. Rivers often employs analogue media and hand develops 16mm film, which shows the evidence of the elements it has been exposed to – the materiality of this medium forming part of the narrative.<ref>[http://www.lux.org.uk/collection/artists/ben-rivers-0] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100407064508/http://www.lux.org.uk/collection/artists/ben-rivers-0|date=7 April 2010}}</ref>

Rivers's first feature-length film, ''Two Years at Sea'', was presented in September 2011 in the [[Orizzonti]] section at the [[68th Venice International Film Festival]] and won the [[International Federation of Film Critics|FIPRESCI International Critics prize]]. His second feature, ''[[A Spell to Ward Off the Darkness]]'',<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.aspelltowardoffthedarkness.com/ |title=A Spell To Ward Off The Darkness |publisher=A Spell To Ward Off The Darkness |date=13 September 2013 |accessdate=3 March 2015 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20141008212411/http://www.aspelltowardoffthedarkness.com/ |archivedate=8 October 2014 |df=dmy-all }}</ref> was made in collaboration with American filmmaker [[Ben Russell (filmmaker)|Ben Russell]], a frequent collaborator, and premiered at [[Locarno Film Festival]] 2013. His feature film directed in collaboration with Thai filmmaker Anocha Suwichakornpong ''Krabi, 2562'' (2019) premiered at Locarno Film Festival that same year. Rivers was awarded the Pardo Verde for his feature film ''[[Mare's Nest]]'' (2025) at the [[78th Locarno Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Szalai |first=Georg |date=August 16, 2025 |title=Locarno Film Festival Top Award Goes to 'Two Seasons, Two Strangers' by Sho Miyake |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/locarno-2025-award-winners-two-seasons-two-strangers-1236345364/ |access-date=August 16, 2025 |work=Hollywood Reporter}}</ref>  

Rivers is represented by Kate MacGarry Gallery, London. He is a Media City Film Festival Chrysalis Fellowship<ref>{{Cite web |title=Media City Film Festival |url=https://mediacityfilmfestival.com/ |access-date=2021-03-08 |website=Media City Film Festival |language=en-US}}</ref> recipient (2020), after screening his work at the festival since the early aughts. His feature films are distributed in the United Kingdom by SODA Pictures,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sodapictures.com/ |title=Soda Pictures |publisher=Soda Pictures |accessdate=3 March 2015}}</ref> Cinema Guild<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cinemaguild.com/mm5/merchant.mvc?Screen=PROD&Store_Code=HE&Product_Code=5182 |title=TWO YEARS AT SEA - Cinema Guild Home Video |accessdate=2014-10-08 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20141013101531/http://cinemaguild.com/mm5/merchant.mvc?Screen=PROD&Store_Code=HE&Product_Code=5182 |archivedate=13 October 2014 |df=dmy-all }}</ref> and KimStim<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.kimstim.com/spell-theatrical.html |title=spell-theatrical |publisher=Kimstim.com |accessdate=3 March 2015 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150410053244/http://www.kimstim.com/spell-theatrical.html |archivedate=10 April 2015 }}</ref> in North America.
