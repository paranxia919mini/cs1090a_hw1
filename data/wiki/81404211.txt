'''''Naked Acts''''' is a 1996 American [[Drama (film and television)|drama]] film written and directed by [[Bridgett M. Davis]].

==Synopsis==
A young actress who just landed a part in her first movie. However it requires her to perform a nude scene, which she does not want to do due to an insecurity stemming from her mother's career in [[Blaxploitation]] films.{{citation needed|date=October 2025}}

==Cast==
* Jake-Ann Jones as Cicely
* Patricia DeArcy as Lydia Love
* John McKie  as Marcel Brown
* [[Ron Cephas Jones]] as Joel
* [[Renee Cox]] as Diana

==Release==
The film had trouble finding a distributor when it was first made, so director Bridgett M. Davis distributed it herself. It would later see a distribution via Milestone films and Kino Lorber.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://milestonefilms.com/products/bridgett-davis-naked-acts|title=NAKED ACTS|website=Milestone Films}}</ref> The movie was released on Blu-ray Feb 18, 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.blu-ray.com/movies/Naked-Acts-Blu-ray/377874/|title=Naked Acts Blu-ray|via=www.blu-ray.com}}</ref>

==Reception==

Unpon the re-release Richard Brody of the New Yorker praised the movies themes, saying "it boldly embodies grand archetypes and underexplored legacies of Black cinema".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/the-front-row/the-rediscovery-of-naked-acts-expands-film-history|title=The Rediscovery of “Naked Acts” Expands Film History|first=Richard|last=Brody|date=June 12, 2024|via=www.newyorker.com}}</ref> It was included in the 2001 book The 50 Most Influential Black Films<ref>https://bridgettdavis.com/naked-acts/</ref> by [[S. Torriano Berry]].
