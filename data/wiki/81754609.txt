'''''Hovering Over The Water''''' ({{langx|pt|À Flor do Mar}}) is a 1986 Portuguese [[Experimental film|avant-garde]] [[Art film|arthouse]] [[Romance film|romantic drama]] film written, directed and produced by [[João César Monteiro]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://thecinematheque.ca/films/2025/hovering-water|title=Folk Tales & Divine Comedies: Films by João César Monteiro {{!}} Hovering Over the Water (À Flor do Mar)|website=The Cinemateque|language=en|access-date=December 3, 2025 }}</ref> Starring [[Laura Morante]]. It tells the story of Laura, an Italian woman spending her vacation in Portugal when her idyllic hot summer takes an unexpected turn.

==Synopsis==
Laura Rossellini, a widow from Rome, vacations on the Algarve coast one hot summer. One day while sunbathing, she finds a wounded man named Robert drifting in the surf on a rubber raft. She takes him home, and, after he is revived, learns his story. As they talk, their mutual attraction grows, until a group of armed men suddenly arrive looking for Robert.

==Production==
The film was shot in various locations in [[Algarve]] between September and November 1985.<ref name=cineubi />

==Analysis==
There is a significant number of references to other films and arts, including to the Italian Renaissance painter [[Piero della Francesca]], to [[Kenji Mizoguchi]]'s film ''[[The Crucified Lovers]]'', and to [[Roberto Rossellini]].<ref name=benard>{{cite web |first=João|last=Bénard da Costa|title=À FLOR DO MAR, João César Monteiro, 1986 |url=https://www.focorevistadecinema.com.br/FOCO1/benard-flor.htm |access-date=December 3, 2025 |work=Foco - Revista de Cinema}}</ref><ref name=publico>{{cite web |first=Mário|last=Jorge Torres|title=À Flor do Mar estreia-se 10 anos depois... : Bruscamente nos Verões passados |url=https://www.publico.pt/1996/06/21/culturaipsilon/noticia/flor-mar-joao-cesar-monteiro-estreiase-10-anos-depois-bruscamente-veroes-passados-1859531# |date=June 21, 1996  |access-date=December 3, 2025 |work=Público}}</ref>

==Release==
The film had a preview screening at the [[Cinemateca Portuguesa]] on October 2, 1986<ref name=cineubi /> and was only released in theaters ten years later, in 1996.<ref name=publico />

The film was screened as part of a complete retrospective on João César Monteiro's work by the [[Museum of Modern Art]], in October 2025.<ref name=moma>{{cite web |title=João César Monteiro: Symphonies of a Libertine |url=https://press.moma.org/film-media/joao-cesar-monteiro/ |access-date=December 6, 2025 |work=Museum of Modern Art}}</ref>

==Reception==
José Navarro de Andrade wrote a positive review after its premiere in 1996, saying that "never have the yellow of the sandstone, the blue of the sea, and that undeniable light of the Algarve had such a glorious celebration as in ''Hovering Over the Water''".<ref name=navarro>{{cite web |first=José|last=Navarro de Andrade|title=Crítica ao filme À Flor do Mar de João César Monteiro |url=https://www.publico.pt/1996/07/04/culturaipsilon/critica/critica-flor-mar-joao-cesar-monteiro-1859536 |date=July 4, 1996  |access-date=December 3, 2025 |work=Público}}</ref>

João Bénard da Costa called the title "beautiful".<ref name=benard />

===Awards===
The film won the Special Jury Prize in 1987 at the Salso Film & TV Festival.<ref name=cineubi />
