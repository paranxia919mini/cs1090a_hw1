'''''Familiar Touch''''' is a 2024 American [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]], written and directed by [[Sarah Friedland]]. It stars [[Kathleen Chalfant]], [[Carolyn Michelle Smith]], [[Andy McQueen]] and [[H. Jon Benjamin]].

''Familiar Touch'' had its world premiere at the [[81st Venice International Film Festival]] on September 3, 2024, where it won the [[Lion of the Future]], and the ''[[Orizzonti]]'' section Best Director and Best Actress prizes.<ref>{{Cite web |first1=Andreas |last1=Wiseman |first2=Nancy |last2=Tartaglione |date=2024-09-07 |title=Venice Winners: Pedro Almodóvar's 'The Room Next Door' Wins The Golden Lion; Also Wins For Nicole Kidman, Brady Corbet, 'I'm Still Here' & More |url=https://deadline.com/2024/09/venice-film-festival-2024-awards-winners-full-list-1236080995/ |access-date=2025-11-08 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> It was released in the United States on June 20, 2025, by [[Music Box Films]], to critical acclaim.

==Premise==
Billed as a coming of (old) age film, ''Familiar Touch'' follows an octogenarian woman (Kathleen Chalfant) as she transitions to life in assisted living contending with her relationship to herself and her caregivers while dealing with cognitive decline.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/memento-international-moves-in-for-venice-horizons-title-familiar-touch-exclusive/5196580.article |title=Memento International moves in for Venice Horizons title 'Familiar Touch' (exclusive) |website=[[Screen International]] |first=Rebecca |last=Leffler |date=August 28, 2024 |access-date=August 30, 2024 |archive-date=August 29, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240829173807/https://www.screendaily.com/news/memento-international-moves-in-for-venice-horizons-title-familiar-touch-exclusive/5196580.article |url-status=live}}</ref>

==Production==
[[Principal photography]] took place at the Villa Gardens retirement community in [[Pasadena, California]], with residents participating in a filmmaking workshop and acting in the film.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/festivals/familiar-touch-movie-about-old-age-dementia-exclusive-clip-1235041942/ |title=Venice's 'Familiar Touch' Is a Rare Coming-of-Old-Age Character Study — Watch Exclusive Clip |website=[[IndieWire]] |first=Ryan |last=Lattanzio |date=August 30, 2024 |access-date=August 30, 2024 |archive-date=August 30, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240830143640/https://www.indiewire.com/news/festivals/familiar-touch-movie-about-old-age-dementia-exclusive-clip-1235041942/ |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wmm.com/sponsored-project/familiar-touch/ |title=Familiar Touch |website=[[Women Make Movies]] |access-date=August 30, 2024 |archive-date=August 30, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240830153005/https://www.wmm.com/sponsored-project/familiar-touch/ |url-status=live}}</ref> Residents made their own biographical films, and only after everyone had been immersed in the experience, they became cast and crew in the feature. Although the film is about dementia, Friedland did not film the residents in the community's memory care wing, due to ethical considerations about their ability to give consent, so some roles (including the lead) were played by professional actors.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Rizov |first=Vadim |date=2023-09-20 |title=Sarah Friedland - Filmmaker Magazine |url=https://filmmakermagazine.com/people/sarah-friedland/ |access-date=2024-09-08 |website= |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Release & awards==
''Familiar Touch'' had its world premiere at the [[81st Venice International Film Festival]] on September 3, 2024.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2024/07/venice-film-festival-lineup-2024-full-list-1236018399/ |title=Venice Film Festival Lineup: 'Joker: Folie à Deux', Almodovar, Guadagnino, Kurzel, Larrain & More In Competition – Full List |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |first=Nancy |last=Tartaglione |date=July 23, 2024 |access-date=August 6, 2024 |archive-date=July 23, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240723193927/https://deadline.com/2024/07/venice-film-festival-lineup-2024-full-list-1236018399/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

In December 2024, [[Music Box Films]] acquired distribution rights to the film.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/global/music-box-familiar-touch-memento-international-1236243163/ |title=Music Box Buys Venice Prizewinning Film 'Familiar Touch' Directed by Rising Filmmaker Sarah Friedland (EXCLUSIVE) |website=Variety |first=Elsa |last=Keslassy |date=December 9, 2024 |access-date=December 9, 2024}}</ref> It was released on June 20, 2025.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://filmforum.org/film/familiar-touch |title=Familiar Touch |website=[[Film Forum]] |access-date=March 18, 2025}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical reception===
On the review aggregator website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], 98% of 54 critics' reviews are positive.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/familiar_touch |title=Familiar Touch |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]] |access-date=October 24, 2025}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 87 out of 100 based on 21 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/familiar-touch/ |title=Familiar Touch |website=[[Metacritic]] |access-date=October 24, 2025}}</ref>

===Accolades===
At the [[81st Venice International Film Festival]], the film won the [[Lion of the Future|Luigi De Laurentis Award for a Debut Film]], as well as Best Director and Best Actress in the [[Orizzonti]] section.<ref name=igcif>[https://deadline.com/2024/09/venice-awards-ceremony-winners-voice-support-palestinian-people-1236081073/ Israel-Gaza Conflict In Focus At Venice Awards Ceremony As Multiple Winners Voice Support For Palestinian People]</ref> In her acceptance speech for the first award, Friedland made a political statement, saying: "As a Jewish American artist working in a time-based medium, I must note, I'm accepting this award on the 336th day of [[Gaza genocide|Israel's genocide in Gaza]] and 76th year of occupation. I believe it is our responsibility as film workers to use the institutional platforms through which we work to redress Israel's impunity on the global stage. I stand in solidarity with the people of Palestine and their struggle for liberation."<ref name="igcif" />

At the [[Gotham Independent Film Awards 2025|35th Annual Gotham Film Awards]], the film was nominated for [[Gotham Independent Film Award for Best Feature|Best Feature]] and [[Gotham Independent Film Award for Breakthrough Director|Breakthrough Director]].

At the [[40th Independent Spirit Awards]], the film won the [[Someone to Watch Award]]. It was also eligible to compete in [[41st Independent Spirit Awards|the following year's ceremony]], where it was nominated for [[Independent Spirit Award for Best Lead Performance|Best Lead Performance]] and the [[Independent Spirit John Cassavetes Award|John Cassavetes Award]].
