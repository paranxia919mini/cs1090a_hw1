'''''One Two Three''''' is a 2008 Indian [[Hindi]]-language [[comedy film]] directed by [[Ashwni Dhir|Ashwini Dhir]].<ref>{{cite web |title=One Two Three |url=https://www.sify.com/movies/one-two-three-review-bollywood-pclw7Abbefegb.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220429031053/https://www.sify.com/movies/one-two-three-review-bollywood-pclw7Abbefegb.html |archive-date=29 April 2022 |website=[[Sify]]}}</ref> The film stars [[Sunil Shetty]], [[Paresh Rawal]], [[Tushar Kapoor]], [[Esha Deol]], [[Sameera Reddy]], [[Neetu Chandra]], [[Upen Patel]] and [[Tanishaa|Tanisha Mukerji]]. It revolves around three men who share the same name — Laxmi Narayan. The film performed moderately well at the box office and was an above average grosse movie especially in the rural areas and suburban areas and some tier 2 cities.<ref>{{cite news | url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/hindi/movie-reviews/one-two-three/movie-review/2908085.cms | title=One Two Three Movie Review {2/5}: Critic Review of One Two Three by Times of India | website=[[The Times of India]] }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/movie/one-two-three/critic-review/one-two-three-movie-review/ | title=One Two Three Review 1.5/5 {{pipe}} One Two Three Movie Review {{pipe}} One Two Three 2008 Public Review {{pipe}} Film Review | website=[[Bollywood Hungama]] | date=28 March 2008 }}</ref><ref>{{cite news | url=https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/media/entertainment/one-two-three-movie-review/articleshow/2907999.cms?from=mdr | title=One Two Three: Movie Review | newspaper=The Economic Times | last1=Malani | first1=Gaurav }}</ref>

==Premise==
Laxmi 1 lives a poor man's lifestyle in Mumbai with his widowed mother Kanta, who wants him to be a gangster and would like him to kill people, make money, then marry Chota Khujli's daughter, Meena Khujli. To fulfill his mother's wish, he accepts a contract to kill D'Mello Yadav, a Pondi-based gangster who has stolen a diamond.

Laxmi 2, is the detailed and obedient Secretary of D.M. Pipat. He wants him to buy a vintage car from a Pondi-based used car dealer, Laila.

Laxmi 3 sells undergarments and runs a business, "Bulbul Lingerie," with his son, Sonu; he travels to Pondi to meet with his new supplier, Jiya.

The trio book hotel rooms next to each other in Blue Diamond Hotel. Their respective lives are hilariously turned upside down when their names cause all three to end up in the wrong places. They are also hiding from Inspector Mayawati Chautala, who also has a crush on Laxmi 2.
