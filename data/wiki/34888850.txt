'''''Only Lovers Left Alive''''' is a 2013 [[Gothic film|Gothic]] [[Fantasy film|fantasy]] [[drama]] film written and directed by [[Jim Jarmusch]], starring [[Tilda Swinton]], [[Tom Hiddleston]], [[Mia Wasikowska]], [[Anton Yelchin]], [[Jeffrey Wright]], [[Slimane Dazi]] and [[John Hurt]]. An international co-production between the United Kingdom and Germany, the film focuses on the romance between two [[vampire]]s and was nominated for the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2013 Cannes Film Festival]].

In 2016, the film was ranked among the [[BBC's 100 Greatest Films of the 21st Century]] by 177 critics around the world.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/culture/story/20160819-the-21st-centurys-100-greatest-films|title=The 21st Century's 100 greatest films|date=23 August 2016|website=[[BBC Culture]]|access-date=23 June 2018}}</ref> In late 2019, it was named the fourth greatest film of the 2010s by ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]''{{'}}s chief film critic [[Todd McCarthy]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last=McCarthy|first=Todd|author-link=Todd McCarthy|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/lists/10-best-films-decade-1260056|title=The 10 Best Films of the Decade|magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|date=6 December 2019|access-date=2019-12-06}}</ref>

==Plot==
Married for centuries and now living half a world apart, two vampires wake as the sun goes down. Adam sits holding a [[lute]], in his cluttered Detroit [[Victorian house|Victorian]] mansion, as Eve wakes up in her bedroom in [[Tangier]], surrounded by books. Rather than feeding on humans directly, they are dependent on local suppliers of the "good stuff", for fear of blood contaminated by the 21st-century environment. Adam, still a famous musician, also fears exposure, visiting a local blood bank in the dead of night in disguise as "[[Faust|Dr. Faust]]", bribing "[[Dr. Watson]]" for his coveted [[O negative]]. Eve relies on their old friend, the author [[Christopher Marlowe]], who faked his death in 1593 and now lives under the protection of a protégé.

Despite having influenced the careers of countless famous musicians and scientists, Adam has become withdrawn and suicidal. His desire to reconnect through his music is at odds with the danger of recognition as well as his contempt for the corrupt and foolish humans he refers to as "zombies". He spends his nights recording his compositions on outdated studio equipment and lamenting the state of the modern world, while collecting vintage instruments.

Adam pays Ian, a naïve young music fan, to procure vintage guitars and other assorted curiosities, including a custom-made wooden bullet with a brass casing he thinks of using to kill himself. Having acquired much scientific knowledge over the years, Adam has built contraptions to power his home using technology originally pioneered by [[Nikola Tesla]]. His reclusive nature adds to his mystique as a musician and composer; he is upset when some intrepid fans turn up on his doorstep. Ian promises to discreetly spread rumors about Adam living elsewhere to draw them away.

When Eve phones, she recognizes Adam is despondent and decides to come to Detroit to comfort him. Soon after she arrives, Adam goes out for more blood, and she discovers his revolver hidden under the bed with the wooden bullet. Her vampire senses reveal to her that the bullet is new, and she becomes worried. Eve confronts Adam when he returns, chiding him for wasting the life and opportunities he has to enjoy and appreciate the good things in the world, as well as their relationship. They spend their nights cruising the empty streets of Detroit, listening to music, and playing chess.

The couple's idyll is shattered by the arrival of Eve's younger sister, Ava, who gorges herself on their stash of the "good stuff". Hungry for excitement, Ava persuades them to go out to a local club with Ian, where they hear Adam's music played by the band [[White Hills (band)|White Hills]]. Ava offers Ian a hit off the flask she secretly filled with blood and brought to the club, but Adam snatches it from her with supernatural speed and insists they leave. Before dawn, Ava accidentally kills Ian by drinking too much of his blood, for which Adam throws her out of the house.

Adam and Eve dispose of Ian's corpse in an acid pool in an abandoned factory. Ian's murder and the appearance of another group of Adam's fans at the house compel the couple to hastily return to Tangier with only what they can carry onto the plane. Desperately hungry, they visit Marlowe and learn that their old friend and mentor has been poisoned by accidentally drinking contaminated blood. They reminisce with Marlowe about his long ruse, successfully hiding that he was the true author of most of [[William Shakespeare]]'s plays, before he dies.

The couple return to the streets with the last flask of the "good stuff." When Eve takes Adam's cash and promises to return with a gift, he is captivated by the music from a nearby club, where Lebanese singer [[Yasmine Hamdan]] is finishing a haunting song. Eve reappears with a beautiful [[oud]], and as they sit together contemplating their likely demise, they spot a pair of young lovers kissing. Adam and Eve approach the young couple with their fangs out.

==Production==
In August 2010, Jarmusch said that [[Tilda Swinton]], [[Michael Fassbender]], [[Mia Wasikowska]], and [[John Hurt]] had agreed to join the film, described by Jarmusch in May 2011 as a "crypto-vampire love story," but said that he did not have financing yet.<ref name=screendaily/><ref name=villagevoice/> Financing the film was a difficult process for the director and he explained at the world premiere at the [[Cannes International Film Festival]] in May 2013 that "it's getting more and more difficult for films that are a little unusual, or not predictable, or don't satisfy people's expectations of something."<ref name="Trek">{{cite news|last=Pulver|first=Andrew|title=Cannes 2013: Only Lovers Left Alive a seven-year trek says Jim Jarmusch|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2013/may/25/jim-jarmusch-vampires-only-lovers-left-alive-cannes|access-date=21 February 2014|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|date=25 May 2013}}</ref>

Jarmusch revealed in 2014 that, after seven years of frustration, Swinton said "that's good news, it means that now is not the time. It will happen when it needs to happen."<ref>{{cite news|last=Ehrlich|first=David|title=Jim Jarmusch: 'Women are my leaders'|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2014/feb/20/jim-jarmusch-women-leaders-only-lovers-left-alive|access-date=21 February 2014|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|date=20 February 2014}}</ref> Jarmusch eventually received a [[United States dollar|US$]]7&nbsp;million budget from the German NRW Filmstiftung <small>([[:de:Film- und Medienstiftung NRW|de]])</small>.<ref name="Trek" /><ref name=mfo120716/> Producer Jeremy Thomas later said that Jarmusch is "one of the great American independent filmmakers – he's the last of the line. People are not coming through like that any more."<ref name="Jon" />

In January 2012, [[Tom Hiddleston]] replaced Fassbender prior to filming.<ref name=thr120130/> Shooting began that June in numerous locations, the [[Brush Park]] district of Detroit, Michigan, U.S., [[Tangier]], Morocco, [[Hamburg]] and [[Cologne]] in Germany.<ref name=olv120715/><ref name=Tangiers>{{cite magazine|title=Tom Hiddleston on winning the Total Film Hotlist Awards: interview|url=http://www.totalfilm.com/news/tom-hiddleston-on-winning-the-total-film-hotlist-awards-interview|magazine=[[Total Film]]|access-date=27 August 2012|date=4 August 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121221113755/http://www.totalfilm.com/news/tom-hiddleston-on-winning-the-total-film-hotlist-awards-interview|archive-date=21 December 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=ha120718/><ref>{{cite news|url=http://blogs.montrealgazette.com/2012/08/01/if-there-were-oscar-awards-for-being-nice-to-fans-tom-hiddleston-would-surely-win-lots-of-them/|title=If there were Oscar awards for being nice to fans – Tom Hiddleston would surely win lots of them|newspaper=[[Montreal Gazette]]|first=Liz|last=Ferguson|date=1 August 2012|access-date=9 January 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140109165023/http://blogs.montrealgazette.com/2012/08/01/if-there-were-oscar-awards-for-being-nice-to-fans-tom-hiddleston-would-surely-win-lots-of-them/|archive-date=9 January 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> Filming lasted seven weeks.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/movies/news/new-jim-jarmusch-film-explores-the-emotional-lives-of-vampires-20130915|title=New Jim Jarmusch Film Explores the Emotional Lives of Vampires|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|first=Karen|last=Bliss|date=15 September 2013}}</ref>

The film is one of several Jarmusch productions, with films such as ''[[Night on Earth]]'', in which the action mainly occurs at night. Swinton said after the film's release that "Jim is pretty much nocturnal, so the nightscape is pretty much his palette. There's something about things glowing in the darkness that feels to me really Jim Jarmusch. He's a rock star."<ref name="Jon" />

==Soundtrack==
{{main|Only Lovers Left Alive (soundtrack)}}
[[Jim Jarmusch|Jarmusch]]'s band [[Sqürl]], primarily responsible for the film's score, opens the film with a version of Wanda Jackson's 1961 song "Funnel of Love." Other contributors to the soundtrack are [[Zola Jesus]] and Lebanese vocalist [[Yasmine Hamdan]], while Dutch [[lute]] player [[Jozef van Wissem]]'s compositions formed the core of the film's aural aesthetic.<ref name="Steve">{{cite news|last=Dollar|first=Steve|title=Jozef van Wissem wants to make the lute 'sexy again' and Jim Jarmusch is helping him|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/lifestyle/style/jozef-van-wissem-wants-to-make-the-lute-sexy-again-and-jim-jarmusch-is-helping-him/2014/04/10/5b9734f2-be92-11e3-bcec-b71ee10e9bc3_story.html|access-date=16 May 2014|newspaper=[[The Washington Post]]|date=11 April 2014}}</ref> During the week of the soundtrack album's release, in April 2014, Van Wissem explained:

<blockquote>
I know the way [Jarmusch] makes his films is kind of like a musician. He has music in his head when he's writing a script so it's more informed by a tonal thing than it is by anything else ... I feel that I'm sort of political. Jim's film is anti-contemporary-society. And the lute goes against all technology and against all computers and against all the crap you don't need.<ref name="Steve" /></blockquote>

Van Wissem also described the film as "a very personal film, maybe even autobiographical" and that "Jim is a cultural sponge, he absorbs everything."<ref name="Jon">{{cite news|last=Romney|first=Jonathan|title=Jim Jarmusch: how the film world's maverick stayed true to his roots|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2014/feb/22/jim-jarmusch-only-lovers-left-alive|access-date=16 May 2014|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|date=22 February 2014}}</ref> A concert was held at the [[Santos Party House]] venue in New York City in April 2014 to celebrate the release of Jarmusch's eleventh feature film. During the Santos event, Jesus performed with van Wissem on both a "pseudo-Gregorian" piece from the film's soundtrack and an unrecorded collaboration.<ref name="Steve" />

== Cultural references ==
Jarmusch not only sees himself as a "film nerd,"<ref name=thr120130/> but he has also been called "a cultural sponge" by van Wissem.<ref name="Jon" /> This film is full of cultural references and therefore it has been praised as "intensely curated" and "an elegiac love song to aesthetic originary creation."<ref name="NewYorker">{{cite magazine|last=Brody|first=Richard|title=Jim Jarmusch's Petrified Hipness|url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/richard-brody/jim-jarmuschs-petrified-hipness|access-date=26 January 2016|magazine=[[The New Yorker]]|date=10 April 2014}}</ref><ref name="biblioklept">{{cite web|last=Turner|first=Edwin|title=Curation and Creation in Only Lovers Left Alive, Jim Jarmusch's Vampire Film|url=https://biblioklept.org/2014/05/20/curation-and-creation-in-only-lovers-left-alive-jim-jarmuschs-vampire-film/|access-date=26 January 2016|website=Biblioklept|date=20 May 2014}}</ref> Most of the hints are musical and others refer to science, literature or Jarmusch's work. The implicit vampires (Adam, Eve, and Christopher) are sort of "secret agents of artistic and intellectual achievement throughout history," having created art for others like [[William Shakespeare]] or [[Franz Schubert]] (for whom Adam is said to have written the famous [[wikt:adagio|Adagio]] of the [[String Quintet (Schubert)|cello string quintet D956]], the movement used by Jarmusch in his 2009 film ''[[The Limits of Control]]'').<ref name="NewYorker"/><ref name=allmusic>{{cite web|last=Herzog|first=Kenneth|url=https://www.allmusic.com/album/limits-of-control-soundtrack-mw0000817020|title=Limits of Control [Soundtrack] – Original Soundtrack|website=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=2016-10-07}}</ref> Early in the film, Adam asserts that seventeenth century musician [[William Lawes]] was known for his funeral music, perhaps confusing him with [[Henry Purcell]]. Lawes wrote no funeral music, but Purcell [[Funeral Sentences and Music for the Funeral of Queen Mary|famously]] did.

===The title===
The title pays tribute to the [[Dave Wallis]] science fiction [[Only Lovers Left Alive (novel)|novel of the same name]] from 1964, although the plots have no similarities. A film adaptation was planned in the mid-1960s for director [[Nicholas Ray]] (a picture of him can later be seen in the film) starring [[the Rolling Stones]].<ref>Emma Love, "Silenced cinema," Independent Extra (Brighton), 17 November 2009, p 14</ref><ref>Patrick Humphries, "Music: Bring back the clowns," The Guardian (UK), 11 October 1996, page T12</ref>

===Names===
The names "Adam" and "Eve" can easily be inferred to allude to the biblical creation story but Jarmusch revealed he was originally referring to [[Mark Twain]]'s satirical work ''[[The Diaries of Adam and Eve]]''.<ref name=thr120130/>

For her flight from Tangier to Detroit Eve uses the surname 'Fibonacci,' taken from the Italian mathematician [[Leonardo Fibonacci]].<ref name="NewYorker"/> On their flights to Tangier they use the names [[Stephen Dedalus]] (from [[James Joyce]]'s ''[[A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man]]'' and ''[[Ulysses (novel)|Ulysses]]'') and [[Daisy Buchanan]] (from [[F. Scott Fitzgerald]]'s ''[[The Great Gatsby]]'').<ref name="NewYorker"/> On his two visits to Dr. Watson ([[Jeffrey Wright]]) Adam's name tag shows "Dr. Faust" (from the German legend of [[Faust]], from which Marlowe wrote the play ''[[The Tragical History of the Life and Death of Doctor Faustus]]''). During his visits, Watson jokingly calls him "Dr. Strangelove" (the eponymous character in [[Stanley Kubrick]]'s 1964 film ''[[Dr. Strangelove]]'') and "Dr. Caligari" (an homage to the German silent film ''[[The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari]]'').<ref name="NewYorker"/><ref name="cinema scope">{{cite magazine|last=Tracy|first=Andrew|title=Temps mort: Jim Jarmusch's Only Lovers Left Alive|url=https://cinema-scope.com/features/tiff-2013-cinema-scope-56-preview-only-lovers-left-alive-jim-jarmusch-us/|access-date=26 January 2016|magazine=[[Cinema Scope]]|date=12 January 2014}}</ref>

===Music equipment and technology===
Parallel to Adam's fondness for vintage instruments and audio equipment, Jarmusch originally planned shooting the movie on analog film; budgetary considerations however forced the use of a digital [[Arri Alexa]] Plus with [[Cooke Optics|Cooke]] S4 lenses. Only vinyl (mostly singles) are played and analogue recording is used.<ref name="NewYorker"/> The film opens with a view of a [[Course (music)|ten-course]] renaissance [[lute]] (credited as built by Michael Schreiner of [[Toronto]]). Adam receives four guitars in the beginning from Ian:<ref name="NewYorker"/>
* a white 1959 Supro (then manufactured by [[Valco]]), which he names after [[William Lawes]]
* a silverblue 1966 [[Hagström]]
* an "early sixties" [[Silvertone (instruments)|Silvertone]] in black
* a red [[Gretsch 6120]] "Chet Atkins"; Adam once saw [[Eddie Cochran]] play one

Later, a 1905 [[Gibson Guitar Corporation|Gibson]] L2 is shown while other guitars, violins and cellos can be seen in Adam's studio.<ref name="biblioklept"/> Among Adam's recording equipment is a [[Premier Percussion|Premier]] drum set, a [[Telefunken]] and [[Revox]] tape recorder, a [[Marshall Amplification|Marshall]] and [[Fender Musical Instruments Corporation|Fender]] amps. Eve and her sister are more adapted to modern technology. To communicate via [[Skype]] with her lover, Eve uses her [[iPhone]] while Adam sets up a laptop connected to a wood-cabinet tube television.<ref name="NewYorker"/> Ava uses YouTube to watch the music video of "Soul Dracula" by the French band Hot Blood.

===Scientists and inventors===

===Places===
The home of Adam is originally located at 82 Alfred Street, Detroit. When the protagonists are cruising around the neighbourhood, Adam shows Eve the [[Michigan Theatre (Detroit, Michigan)|Michigan Theater]] -- which is now a parking deck -- and the house where [[Jack White]] grew up with his family.<ref name="NYTimes"/> White previously played a Tesla expert in ''Coffee and Cigarettes''.

==Reception==
===Critical response===
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|86|7.6|210|Worth watching for Tom Hiddleston and Tilda Swinton's performances alone, ''Only Lovers Left Alive'' finds writer-director Jim Jarmusch adding a typically offbeat entry to the vampire genre.|ref=yes|access-date=2 July 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230502101604/https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/only_lovers_left_alive|archive-date=2 May 2023|url-status=live}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 79 out of 100, based on 41 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Only Lovers Left Alive'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/only-lovers-left-alive |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=2 July 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230326035937/https://www.metacritic.com/movie/only-lovers-left-alive |archive-date=26 March 2023 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Scott A. Gray of ''[[Exclaim!]]'' gave the film 8 out of 10, calling it "a visually poetic love story with a wry, jaded sense of humor about finding reasons to wake up every night."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://exclaim.ca/Reviews/TIFF/only_lovers_left_alive-directed_by_jim_jarmusch|title=Only Lovers Left Alive – Directed by Jim Jarmusch|magazine=[[Exclaim!]]|first=Scott A.|last=Gray|date=4 September 2013}}</ref> Calum Marsh of ''[[Slant Magazine]]'' gave it 3 out of 4 stars.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.slantmagazine.com/film/review/only-lovers-left-alive|title=Only Lovers Left Alive|website=[[Slant Magazine]]|first=Calum|last=Marsh|date=12 October 2013}}</ref> Jonathan Romney of ''[[Screen International]]'' commented that it is Jarmusch's most poetic film since ''[[Dead Man]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.screendaily.com/reviews/the-latest/only-lovers-left-alive/5056776.article|title=Only Lovers Left Alive|website=[[Screen Daily]]|first=Jonathan|last=Romney|date=25 May 2013}}</ref>

Todd McCarthy of ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' described the film as "the perennial downtown filmmaker's best work in many years, probably since 1995's ''Dead Man'', with which it shares a sense of quiet, heady, perilous passage."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/lovers-left-alive-cannes-review-558567|title=Only Lovers Left Alive: Cannes Review|magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|first=Todd|last=McCarthy|date=25 May 2013}}</ref> Jonathan Hatfull of ''[[SciFiNow]]'' wrote that it is Jarmusch's best film since ''[[Ghost Dog]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.scifinow.co.uk/reviews/52827/only-lovers-left-alive-film-review/|title=Only Lovers Left Alive film review|magazine=[[SciFiNow]]|first=Jonathan|last=Hatfull|date=14 November 2013}}</ref>

[[Robbie Collin]] from ''[[The Daily Telegraph]]'' awarded the film 4 out of 5 stars and praised the performances of Swinton and Hiddleston: "In the time-honored Jarmuschian fashion, the few things that happen in ''Only Lovers Left Alive'' happen very slowly, but the dialogue is always gloomily amusing, and Swinton and Hiddleston's delivery of the gags is as cold and crisp as footstep in fresh snow."<ref name="DTL">{{cite news |last=Collin |first=Robbie |author-link=Robbie Collin |url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/culture/film/cannes-film-festival/10080087/Cannes-2013-Only-Lovers-Left-Alive-review.html |title=Cannes 2013: Only Lovers Left Alive, review |date=24 May 2013 |access-date=27 May 2013 |newspaper=[[The Daily Telegraph]] |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130526094935/http://www.telegraph.co.uk/culture/film/cannes-film-festival/10080087/Cannes-2013-Only-Lovers-Left-Alive-review.html |archive-date=26 May 2013}}</ref> Jessica Kiang of ''[[IndieWire]]'' gave the film a B+ grade, saying, "The real pleasure of the film is in its languid droll cool and its romantic portrayal of the central couple, who are now our number one role models in the inevitable event of us turning vampiric."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://blogs.indiewire.com/theplaylist/cannes-review-droll-louche-and-languidly-playful-only-lovers-left-alive-is-jarmusch-at-his-most-enjoyable-and-accessible-20130524|title=Cannes Review: Droll, Louche & Languidly Playful "Only Lovers Left Alive" Is Jarmusch At His Most Enjoyable & Accessible|website=[[IndieWire]]|first=Jessica|last=Kiang|date=24 May 2013|access-date=24 October 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131029185435/http://blogs.indiewire.com/theplaylist/cannes-review-droll-louche-and-languidly-playful-only-lovers-left-alive-is-jarmusch-at-his-most-enjoyable-and-accessible-20130524|archive-date=29 October 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Tim Grierson of ''[[Paste (magazine)|Paste]]'' noted that "Hiddleston and Swinton play their characters not as blasé hipsters but, rather, deeply reflective, almost regretful old souls who seem to have decided that love is about the only thing you can count on."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.pastemagazine.com/articles/2013/05/only-lovers-left-alive.html|title=Only Lovers Left Alive (2013 Cannes review)|magazine=[[Paste (magazine)|Paste]]|first=Tim|last=Grierson|date=30 May 2013}}</ref> Peter Bradshaw of ''[[The Guardian]]'' gave the film 3 out of 5 stars, pointing that Adam and Eve look more like "well-born incestuous siblings" in spite of being lovers, while the ''Observer'''s Jonathan Romney concluded that the film is "a droll, classy piece of cinematic dandyism that makes the ''Twilight'' cycle redundant in one exquisitely languid stroke."<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2013/may/24/cannes-2013-only-lovers-left-alive-review|title=Cannes 2013: Only Lovers Left Alive – first look review|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|first=Peter|last=Badshaw|author-link=Peter Bradshaw|date=24 May 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|last=Romney|first=Jonathan |title=Only Lovers Left Alive – review|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2014/feb/23/only-lovers-left-alive-review|access-date=16 May 2014|newspaper=[[The Observer]]|date=23 February 2014}}</ref>

Kurt Halfyard of ''[[Twitch Film]]'' commented: "Retro recording equipment hasn't looked this claustrophobically sexy since ''[[Berberian Sound Studio]]''."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://twitchfilm.com/2013/09/tiff-2013-review-only-lovers-left-alive.html|title=TIFF 2013 Review: Only Lovers Left Alive Brings a Cosmopolitan Maturity to the Ailing Vampire Genre|website=[[Twitch Film]]|first=Kurt|last=Halfyard|date=5 September 2013|access-date=9 January 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140807031749/http://twitchfilm.com/2013/09/tiff-2013-review-only-lovers-left-alive.html|archive-date=7 August 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> Alfred Joyner of ''[[International Business Times]]'' felt that "the melancholy that permeates Motown in the film could be seen as Jarmusch's take on [[American decline|the loss of America's greatness in the 21st century]]."<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.ibtimes.co.uk/lovers-left-alive-film-review-jim-jarmusch-515124|title=Only Lovers Left Alive: Jim Jarmusch's Rock Star Take on Modern Vampires|newspaper=[[International Business Times]]|first=Alfred|last=Joyner|date=19 October 2013}}</ref>
