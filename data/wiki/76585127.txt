'''''Caught by the Tides''''' ({{Langx|zh|风流一代|lit=Romantic Generation}}) is a 2024 Chinese [[drama film]] directed by [[Jia Zhangke]], and written by Wan Jianhuan and Jia Zhangke, based on footage across 22 years, some from Jia's previous films, in an impressionistic non-linear blend of fiction and non-fiction.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Halligan |first=Fionnuala |title='Caught By The Tides': Cannes Review |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/caught-by-the-tides-cannes-review/5193523.article |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=Screen |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":3">{{Cite web |last=Pond |first=Steve |date=18 May 2024 |title='Caught by the Tides' Review: Jia Zhangke Stands Up for China but Watches Things Fall Apart |url=https://www.thewrap.com/caught-by-the-tides-review-jia-zhangke-cannes/ |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=TheWrap |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film was selected to compete for the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival|77th Cannes Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 18 May 2024.

==Plot==
In 2001, in the northern Chinese city of [[Datong]], a working-class woman named Qiao Qiao has a romantic relationship with her manager Guao Bin as she hustles to make a living as a singer, model, and club girl.<ref name=":2" /> Bin leaves Datong to try to earn a living in another province, sending Qiao Qiao a text message that says he will bring her when he has money.<ref name=":2" /><ref name=":4">{{Cite web |last=Rooney |first=David |date=18 May 2024 |title='Caught by the Tides' Review: Jia Zhang-ke's Defining Theme of Modern China in Constant Transformation Yields an Elegiac Love Story |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/caught-by-the-tides-review-jia-zhang-ke-1235899736/ |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=The Hollywood Reporter }}</ref> After a stretch, Qiao Qiao decides to go looking for him,<ref name="Ad Vitam">{{cite web|url=https://www.advitamdistribution.com/films/caught-by-the-tides/|title=Caught by the Tides|website=[[Ad Vitam (company)|Ad Vitam]]|language=fr|access-date=12 April 2024}}</ref> passing through communities that are being displaced by the [[Three Gorges Dam]],<ref name=":2" /> as well as through Guangdong Province.<ref name=":3" /> Meanwhile, Bin tries his hand at a number of businesses, including entering a shady deal with a corrupt politician.<ref name=":4" /> When Qiao Qiao finally finds Bin, she breaks up with him. They eventually re-unite in [[COVID-19 pandemic in China|COVID-era China]], after both have noticeably aged.<ref name=":1" /><ref name=":4" />

==Production==
Jia first alluded to the film in a 2016 interview by stating, "During 1999 and 2000 was the time I had my first DV camera, so I would usually, aimlessly go about shooting footage. This is a habit I still maintain up to this day, and I have a lot of raw footage using different types of cameras and captures throughout the years. I might have a future plan of editing this footage and making it into a film."<ref name = Pearce>{{cite news|url = https://thefilmstage.com/jia-zhangke-and-zhao-tao-on-the-value-of-love-and-capturing-the-future-in-mountains-may-depart/|title = Jia Zhangke and Zhao Tao on the Value of Love and Capturing the Future In ''Mountains May Depart''|last = Newman|first = Nick|work = The Film Stage|date=2016-02-10|access-date=2025-05-04 |language=en-US}}</ref>

The decision to use previous footage was sparked by the COVID-19 pandemic, which made filming in China difficult. As Jia was revisiting two decades of footage, some of which was shot as experimentation with different techniques rather than to capture specific scenes, he realized he could potentially re-use the footage as material for a new film.<ref name=":5">{{Cite web |date=18 May 2024 |title=Chinese director Jia mines leftover footage for top Cannes film |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20240518-chinese-director-jia-mines-leftover-footage-for-top-cannes-film |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=France 24 |language=en}}</ref> 

''Caught by the Tides'' is assembled from 22 years of footage, including many of Jia's previous characters and locations.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Ehrlich |first=David |date=18 May 2024 |title='Caught by the Tides' Review: Jia Zhang-ke's Drifting Portrait of Love Across Time Will Delight Fans and Mystify Newcomers |url=https://www.indiewire.com/criticism/movies/caught-by-the-tides-review-jia-zhang-ke-1235006282/ |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=IndieWire |language=en-US}}</ref> As a result the actors naturally age,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kiang |first=Jessica |date=18 May 2024 |title='Caught by the Tides' Review: Jia Zhangke Weaves a Shimmering New Tapestry from Threads of His Previous Films |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/caught-by-the-tides-review-jia-zhangke-1236005134/ |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref> including the lead character, Qiao Qiao, who is played by Jia's real life wife, actress [[Zhao Tao]].<ref name=":0" /> Part of the film takes place in [[Fengjie County|Fengjie]], the same setting as Jia's [[Golden Lion]]-winning film ''[[Still Life (2006 film)|Still Life]]'' (2006), which also starred Zhao Tao.<ref name=":1">{{Cite news |last=Bradshaw |first=Peter |date=18 May 2024 |title=Caught by the Tides review – two-decade relationship tells story of China's epic transformation |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/article/2024/may/18/caught-by-the-tides-review-two-decade-relationship-tells-story-of-chinas-epic-transformation |access-date=19 May 2024 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>

Footage and outtakes from ''[[Unknown Pleasures (film)|Unknown Pleasures]]'', ''[[Still Life (2006 film)|Still Life]]'', and ''[[Ash Is Purest White]]'' were used in ''Caught by the Tides''.{{sfn|Prakash|2025|p=40}} Approximately 10 scenes from the film have appeared in Jia's previous films.<ref>{{Cite web |date=18 May 2024 |title=Chinese director Jia mines leftover footage for top Cannes film |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20240518-chinese-director-jia-mines-leftover-footage-for-top-cannes-film |access-date=19 May 2024 |website=France 24 |language=en}}</ref> Kodak Vision 2 and 3 film stock were used with Sony DSR-PD150, Sony [[Betacam]], Sony HDV, [[Arricam]] LT 35mm, and [[Arri Alexa]] 35 cameras for the film.{{sfn|Prakash|2025|p=39}} The final section, set in contemporary China, was shot during the pandemic.<ref name=":5" /> Editing was done using [[Final Cut Pro]] and [[DaVinci Resolve]] was used for colour correction.{{sfn|Prakash|2025|p=39}}

==Release==
''Caught by the Tides'' was selected to compete for the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 18 May 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2024/the-screenings-guide-of-the-77th-festival-de-cannes/|title=The Screenings Guide of the 77th Festival de Cannes|date=8 May 2024|website=Festival de Cannes|access-date=8 May 2024}}</ref> The film played at the [[2024 Toronto International Film Festival|Toronto International Film Festival]] on 5 September 2024.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://tiff.net/events/caught-by-the-tides |title=Caught by the Tides |website=[[Toronto International Film Festival]] |access-date=August 19, 2024}}</ref> It was also selected in Gala Presentation at the [[29th Busan International Film Festival]] and screened on 5 October 2024.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.biff.kr/eng/html/program/prog_view.asp?idx=76002&c_idx=398&sp_idx=&QueryStep=2 |title=Caught by the Tides |website=Busan International Film Festival|access-date=September 14, 2024|language=en}}</ref>

The film was theatrically released in China on 22 November 2024.<ref>{{Cite news |date=October 25, 2024 |title=新片｜贾樟柯执导、赵涛主演《风流一代》定档11月22日 |url=https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_29144286 |access-date=13 November 2024 |work=[[The Paper (newspaper)|The Paper]] |language=zh}}</ref>

For home video, the film is set to be released on [[Blu-ray]] and [[DVD]] by [[The Criterion Collection]] in 2026, as part of its Criterion Premieres line of discs.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Caught by the Tides (2024) |url=https://www.criterion.com/films/34797-caught-by-the-tides |access-date=October 26, 2025 |website=The Criterion Collection}}</ref>

==Reception==

===Critical response===
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|98||64|An intimate epic that captures the passage of time with melancholic clarity, ''Caught by the Tides'' might be director Jia Zhangke and star Tao Zhao's most profound collaboration yet.|ref=yes|access-date=30 June 2025}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 87 out of 100, based on 24 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Caught by the Tides'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/caught-by-the-tides |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=30 June 2025 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

Simon Abrams of ''[[RogerEbert.com]]'' gave the film four out of four stars, writing, "At heart, ''Caught By the Tides'' is an experimental romantic drama, though that makes it sound unapproachable and a little gimmicky. It's neither, thankfully, and that's largely thanks to Jia's typical focus on the material signs of time's relentless passage."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/caught-by-the-tides-jia-zhangke-film-review-2025|title=Caught by the Tides|first=Simon|last=Abrams|website=[[RogerEbert.com]]|date=May 2025|access-date=14 May 2025}}</ref>
