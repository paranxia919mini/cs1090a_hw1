'''''Trolls''''' is a 2016 American animated [[musical comedy film]] produced by [[DreamWorks Animation]], based on [[Troll doll|the Good Luck Trolls doll line]]. The film was directed by [[Mike Mitchell (director)|Mike Mitchell]], written by [[Jonathan Aibel and Glenn Berger]], and stars the voices of [[Anna Kendrick]], [[Justin Timberlake]], [[Zooey Deschanel]], [[Christopher Mintz-Plasse]], [[Christine Baranski]], [[Russell Brand]], [[James Corden]], [[Jeffrey Tambor]], [[Ron Funches]], [[Icona Pop]], [[Kunal Nayyar]], [[Quvenzhané Wallis]], [[John Cleese]], and [[Gwen Stefani]]. The film follows two trolls, Poppy (Kendrick) and Branch (Timberlake), who go on a quest to save their village from destruction by the Bergens, giant creatures who eat Trolls.

''Trolls'' premiered at the [[BFI London Film Festival]] on October 8, 2016, and was theatrically released in the United States on November 4, by [[20th Century Studios|20th Century Fox]].{{efn|name=Distributor|In 2018, the film's distribution rights were transferred from [[20th Century Studios|20th Century Fox]] to [[Universal Pictures]], following [[NBCUniversal]]'s acquisition of DreamWorks Animation in 2016.}} The film received mostly positive reviews from critics and grossed $347&nbsp;million against a $125&nbsp;million budget. It received an [[Academy Awards|Academy Award]] nomination for [[Academy Award for Best Original Song|Best Original Song]] for "[[Can't Stop the Feeling!]]".

The film launched a [[Trolls (franchise)|franchise]], which included two television specials, ''[[Trolls Holiday]]'' (2017) and ''[[Trolls: Holiday in Harmony]]'' (2021), two television series, ''[[Trolls: The Beat Goes On!]]'' (2018–2019) and ''[[Trolls: TrollsTopia]]'' (2020–2022), two sequels, ''[[Trolls World Tour]]'' (2020) and ''[[Trolls Band Together]]'' (2023), and six short films.

==Plot==
<!-- Please see WP:FILMPLOT before editing. Per key guideline, the plot summary should be within the desired length of 400-700 words. Current wordcount 670.-->
Trolls are small, colorful, perpetually happy creatures who like to sing, dance and hug all day. They are discovered by the large, hideous and miserable Bergens, who believe they can only feel happy by consuming a Troll. The Bergens imprison the Trolls in a caged tree, and eat them once a year on a special occasion called "Trollstice".

On the year that Bergen Prince Gristle Jr., son of King Gristle Sr., is due to eat his first Troll, the chef in charge of the ceremony discovers that the Trolls, under the leadership of King Peppy, have managed to escape. The King banishes Chef from Bergen Town, and she vows to find the Trolls, secretly plotting to overthrow the [[monarchy]] as revenge.

Twenty years later, Peppy's daughter Princess Poppy organizes a gigantic party to celebrate the [[anniversary]] of their escape. A serious, gray Troll named Branch warns that this could expose their home, but everyone ignores him. However, his forebodings are justified when Chef, who as only Bergen, has spent the last twenty years searching for the Trolls, notices the party [[fireworks]] from her hideout, follows them to Troll Village and kidnaps Poppy's friends, including her <!---please do not add "Secret". The two openly show each other affection.---> crush, a "[[zen]]" Troll named Creek. While the rest of the Trolls take refuge in Branch's survival [[bunker]] uninvited, Poppy sets off alone to rescue her friends. She gets herself into several potentially deadly situations, and is finally rescued by Branch, who thinks her quest is hopeless and only followed her to escape his crowded bunker.

After encountering further challenges, Poppy and Branch meet a mischievous Cloud Guy, who leads them into Bergen Town. The pair sneak into the castle of Gristle Jr, who is now the King. They witness Chef apparently feeding Creek to Gristle, but Poppy still holds out hope that Creek survived. They find the other captives being guarded by a young [[scullery maid]] named Bridget, who is secretly in love with Gristle. Upon learning this, the Trolls agree to help her get a date in exchange for her help in ascertaining whether or not Creek is still alive. However, Branch refuses to sing along with the others in the ensuing musical number, revealing that when he was a child, his loud singing inadvertently led Chef to his home, and his grandmother Rosiepuff was captured. Branch's guilt caused his colors to fade into gray, and he swore never to sing again.

The Trolls use their powers to disguise Bridget as "Lady Glittersparkles", and she and Gristle go on a date at a [[roller rink]]/[[amusement arcade|arcade restaurant]]. During the date, Poppy spots Creek being held captive inside a jewel on Gristle's [[mantle (royal garment)|mantle]]. Afterwards, the Trolls leave Bridget and go to rescue Creek, but are all captured by Chef when Creek sells them out in exchange for his own life being spared. Creek steals a horrified Poppy's [[cowbell]] and uses it to lure the rest of the Trolls out of hiding. They are rounded up by the Bergens, and imprisoned in a large cooking pot. Blaming herself for the situation, Poppy becomes embittered, and her colors turn to gray. Saddened, the other Trolls lose their colors as well.

However, Branch, the only Troll who has not given up, begins singing "[[True Colors (Cyndi Lauper song)#Other versions|True Colors]]" in an attempt to comfort the despairing Trolls; both his and Poppy's colors return, followed by those of the other Trolls. Bridget, grateful to the Trolls for helping her, secretly releases them from the pot while Chef is not looking. Poppy refuses to let Bridget be punished for their escape, so she and the Trolls return to Bergen Town, reveal Bridget's true identity to the Bergens, and explain that she and Gristle are happily in love with each other. This shows the rest of the Bergens that they can find happiness within themselves without having to eat a Troll, and the two sides celebrate together in song, ending their feud, while Chef, who refuses to accept the peace, is sent rolling out of Bergen Town on a flaming [[serving cart]] along with Creek, and King Peppy crowns Poppy the queen of the Trolls.

Meanwhile, the flaming cart rolls to a stop atop what appears to be a hill. Chef is about to eat Creek, when the "hill" reveals itself to be a huge monster, which eats them both.

==Reception==
===Box office===
''Trolls'' grossed $153.9&nbsp;million in the US and Canada and $193.2&nbsp;million in other countries for a worldwide total of $347&nbsp;million, against a budget of $125&nbsp;million.<ref name=BOM /> ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' calculated the film made a net profit of $19&nbsp;million, when factoring together all expenses and revenues.<ref name=DeadlineProfit>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2020/04/trolls-world-tour-vod-box-office-movie-profits-universal-coronavirus-1202903913/|title=Will 'Trolls World Tour' Profit Off Universal's Experimental VOD (& Drive-In Theater) Model?|first=Anthony|last=D'Alessandro|work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=April 10, 2019|access-date=April 10, 2019|archive-date=April 20, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200420180132/https://deadline.com/2020/04/trolls-world-tour-vod-box-office-movie-profits-universal-coronavirus-1202903913/|url-status=live}}</ref>

In the United States and Canada, ''Trolls'' was released alongside the releases of ''[[Doctor Strange (2016 film)|Doctor Strange]]'' and ''[[Hacksaw Ridge]]'', and was projected to gross $35–40&nbsp;million from 4,060 theaters in its opening weekend.<ref name="preview">{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2016/11/doctor-strange-hacksaw-ridge-mel-gibson-trolls-1201846210/ |title='Doctor Strange' To Give Booster Shot To Sleepy Fall Box Office |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=November 2016 |access-date=June 3, 2023 |archive-date=November 2, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161102152935/http://deadline.com/2016/11/doctor-strange-hacksaw-ridge-mel-gibson-trolls-1201846210/ |url-status=live }}</ref> On its first day, the film grossed $12.3&nbsp;million (including $900,000 made from Thursday night previews). It went on to open to $46.5&nbsp;million, finishing second at the box office behind ''Doctor Strange''.<ref name="DeadlineWknd1">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2016/11/doctor-strange-trolls-hacksaw-ridge-weekend-box-office-1201848529/|title='Doctor Strange' Resuscitates Fall Box Office With $81M To $83M+ Opening – Saturday AM Update|last=D'Alessandro|first=Anthony|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=November 6, 2016|access-date=November 6, 2016|archive-date=November 7, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161107220646/http://deadline.com/2016/11/doctor-strange-trolls-hacksaw-ridge-weekend-box-office-1201848529/|url-status=live}}</ref>

===Critical response===
On [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 76% based on 165 reviews, with an average rating of 6.30/10. The site's critical consensus reads: "''Trolls'' brings its instantly recognizable characters to the big screen in a colorful adventure that, while geared toward the younger set, isn't without rewards for parents."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/trolls/|title=Trolls (2016)|work=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|publisher=[[Fandango Media]]|access-date=May 23, 2019|archive-date=February 26, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210226134139/https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/trolls|url-status=live}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], which assigns a normalized rating, the film has a score of 55 out of 100, based on 32 critics, indicating "mixed or average reviews".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/trolls |title=Trolls Reviews |website=[[Metacritic]] |access-date=November 10, 2016 |archive-date=November 10, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161110114847/http://www.metacritic.com/movie/trolls |url-status=live }}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average grade of "A" on an A+ to F scale, while [[PostTrak]] reported filmgoers gave it an 80% overall positive score.<ref name="DeadlineWknd1"/>

Lindsey Bahr of [[Associated Press]] gave the film a positive review and said, "Ultimately, the 'get happy' moral of the story, while trite compared to something like ''[[Inside Out]]'', is sufficiently sweet enough for its audience. Did you expect more from a piece of candy?"<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.torontosun.com/2016/11/02/review-trolls-is-big-hearted-sensory-overload|title=Review: 'Trolls' is big-hearted sensory overload|first1=Lindsey|last1=Bahr|agency=[[The Associated Press]]|date=November 2, 2016|newspaper=[[Toronto Sun]]|access-date=November 3, 2016|archive-date=November 4, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161104141217/http://www.torontosun.com/2016/11/02/review-trolls-is-big-hearted-sensory-overload|url-status=live}}</ref> Bill Zwecker of ''[[Chicago Sun-Times]]'' gave the film three-and-a-half stars out of four and said, "You simply will walk out – or perhaps dance out – of the theater feeling very happy yourself."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://chicago.suntimes.com/entertainment/trolls-review/|title=Delightful 'Trolls' achieves maximum smiles per hour|date=November 2016|access-date=November 4, 2016|archive-date=November 5, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161105033548/http://chicago.suntimes.com/entertainment/trolls-review/|url-status=live}}</ref> Andy Webster of ''[[The New York Times]]'' said, "Exuberant, busy and sometimes funny, DreamWorks Animation's ''Trolls'' is determined to amuse."<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2016/11/04/movies/trolls-review.html|title=Review: The 'Trolls' Embark on a High-Haired Rescue Mission|date=November 3, 2016|work=The New York Times|access-date=March 3, 2017|archive-date=December 28, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161228180434/http://www.nytimes.com/2016/11/04/movies/trolls-review.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

Michael Rechtshaffen, writing for ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'', called the film "an admittedly vibrant-looking but awfully recognizable animated musical comedy concoction."<ref name="THRReview">{{cite news|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/trolls-film-review-936514|title='Trolls': Film Review|author=Rechtshaffen|first=Michael|date=October 8, 2016|work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=November 11, 2019|archive-date=October 21, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191021020326/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/trolls-film-review-936514|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Alonso Duralde]] of ''[[TheWrap]]'' said the film "combines the barely-there characterization and irritating cutesiness of ''[[The Smurfs (film)|The Smurfs]]'' with the hideous character design and awful pop covers of ''[[Strange Magic (film)|Strange Magic]]''."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thewrap.com/trolls-review-justin-timberlake/|title='Trolls' London Review: Justin Timberlake and Anna Kendrick Are Floppin' to Oldies|author=Alonso Duralde|author-link=Alonso Duralde|work=[[TheWrap]]|date=October 8, 2016|access-date=October 8, 2016|archive-date=October 8, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161008194854/http://www.thewrap.com/trolls-review-justin-timberlake/|url-status=live}}</ref> Betsy Bozdech of [[Common Sense Media]] gave the movie 4 stars. She said, "Make no mistake: Kids are going to love this movie. Trolls is cute, it's colorful, it has tons of catchy songs, and the messages are positive and easy to understand (happiness is inside everyone, if you know where/how to find it, and you shouldn't have to change who you are to get someone to like you)."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.commonsensemedia.org/movie-reviews/trolls|title=Trolls Movie Review|first=Betsy|last=Bozdech|date=June 22, 2016|publisher=Common Sense Media|access-date=August 27, 2017|archive-date=August 27, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170827090318/https://www.commonsensemedia.org/movie-reviews/trolls|url-status=live}}</ref>

However, "Can't Stop the Feeling" did win at the [[59th Annual Grammy Awards]] for [[Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media|Best Song Written for Visual Media]].

=== Sequels ===
{{main|Trolls World Tour|Trolls Band Together}}

On February 28, 2017, [[Universal Pictures]], the studio's new distributor and parent company since its acquisition by [[Comcast]] and [[NBCUniversal]] in 2016, and [[DreamWorks Animation]] announced that a sequel titled ''Trolls 2'' would be released on April 10, 2020, with Kendrick and Timberlake reprising their respective roles of Poppy and Branch as well as Aibel and Berger returning to write the script.<ref name=VarietyTrolls2020>{{cite news|last1=Kroll|first1=Justin|title=Universal DreamWorks Animation Announces 'Trolls 2'; Sets for April 10, 2020|url=https://variety.com/2017/film/news/trolls-2-announced-april-2020-dreamworks-animation-1201998983/|access-date=March 5, 2017|work=Variety|date=February 28, 2017|archive-date=February 28, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170228223832/http://variety.com/2017/film/news/trolls-2-announced-april-2020-dreamworks-animation-1201998983/|url-status=live}}</ref> On October 4, 2017, the release date for the sequel was moved up to February 14, 2020, as ''[[Fast & Furious 9]]'' took its original April 10, 2020 slot. Along with the new release date, it was announced that Dohrn will be returning to direct and Shay will return to produce the sequel. On December 6, 2017, the film was pushed back to an April 17, 2020 release. On March 4, 2020, ''[[No Time to Die]]'' got delayed as a result of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]], so the film was pushed to an April 10, 2020 release again through [[premium video on demand]].<ref name="auto">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2017/10/fast-furious-9-easter-weekend-2020-release-date-trolls-2-valentines-day-opening-2020-1202181866/|title='Fast & Furious 9' Drives To Easter Weekend 2020; DreamWorks Animation's 'Trolls 2' Eyes Valentine's Day.|last=D'Alessandro|first=Anthony|date=October 4, 2017|website=Deadline Hollywood|access-date=November 7, 2017|archive-date=November 8, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171108023131/http://deadline.com/2017/10/fast-furious-9-easter-weekend-2020-release-date-trolls-2-valentines-day-opening-2020-1202181866/|url-status=live}}</ref>

In May 2018, it was confirmed that [[Sam Rockwell]], [[Chance the Rapper]], [[Anthony Ramos (actor)|Anthony Ramos]], [[Karan Soni]], [[Flula Borg]], and [[Jamie Dornan]] joined the cast. Corden, Icona Pop, Funches, Stefani and Nayyar will also reprise their roles.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://deadline.com/2018/05/sam-rockwell-chance-the-rapper-trolls-2-animated-movie-1202387559/|title=Sam Rockwell, Chance The Rapper Join DreamWorks' 'Trolls 2'|last=Busch|first=Anita|date=May 10, 2018|website=Deadline Hollywood|access-date=May 10, 2018|archive-date=May 10, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180510222550/http://deadline.com/2018/05/sam-rockwell-chance-the-rapper-trolls-2-animated-movie-1202387559/|url-status=live}}</ref> In October 2018, it was confirmed that [[Kelly Clarkson]] had joined the cast, and will perform an original song.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://deadline.com/2018/10/kelly-clarkson-trolls-2-perform-original-song-1202476507/|title=Kelly Clarkson & Reece McCreless Joins 'Trolls 2' & Will Perform Original Song|magazine=Deadline Hollywood|last1=N'Duka|first1=Amanda|last2=D'Alessandro|first2=Anthony|date=October 4, 2018|access-date=October 5, 2018|archive-date=October 5, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181005014655/https://deadline.com/2018/10/kelly-clarkson-trolls-2-perform-original-song-1202476507/|url-status=live}}</ref>

The title for the sequel, ''Trolls World Tour'', was announced in June 2018.<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/behind-screen/emmys-trolls-holiday-lights-up-animation-race-1119149 |title=Emmys: Trolls Light Up Animation Race With Holiday Special |date=June 12, 2018 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |access-date=June 12, 2018 |archive-date=June 12, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180612232603/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/behind-screen/emmys-trolls-holiday-lights-up-animation-race-1119149 |url-status=live }}</ref> In May 2017, podcasters [[Justin McElroy|Justin]], [[Travis McElroy|Travis]] and [[Griffin McElroy]] began campaigning for roles in the film via a podcast titled "The McElroy Brothers Will Be in Trolls 2" (later renamed "The McElroy Brothers Will Be in Trolls: World Tour" after the sequel's subtitle was revealed).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://trolls2.libsyn.com/|title=The McElroy Brothers Will Be in Trolls 2|website=trolls2.libsyn.com|access-date=16 September 2018|archive-date=September 13, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180913112717/https://trolls2.libsyn.com/|url-status=live}}</ref> Following the podcast's popularity, DreamWorks confirmed in September 2018 that the McElroy brothers would make cameo appearances in ''World Tour''.<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2018/09/trolls-world-tour-mcelroy-brothers-casting |title=The Unbelievable True Story of How Three Podcasters Trolled Their Way into Trolls 2 |last=Bradley |first=Laura |date=September 11, 2018 |website=[[Vanity Fair (magazine)|Vanity Fair]] |access-date=September 11, 2018 |archive-date=September 11, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180911220216/https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2018/09/trolls-world-tour-mcelroy-brothers-casting |url-status=live }}</ref>

In June 2019, along with promotional posters, new cast members were announced, which included: [[J Balvin]], [[Mary J. Blige]], [[Rachel Bloom]], [[George Clinton (funk musician)|George Clinton]], [[Ester Dean]] and [[Gustavo Dudamel]].<ref>{{cite news |last1=Sarto |first1=Dan |title=DreamWorks Animation Reveals 21 Posters and Cast for 'Trolls World Tour' |url=https://www.awn.com/news/dreamworks-animation-reveals-21-posters-and-cast-trolls-world-tour |access-date=19 June 2019 |work=Animation World Network |date=19 June 2019 |language=en |archive-date=June 19, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190619224924/https://www.awn.com/news/dreamworks-animation-reveals-21-posters-and-cast-trolls-world-tour |url-status=live }}</ref>

On April 9, 2020, Timberlake expressed interest in future ''Trolls'' films during his Apple Music takeover, saying, "I hope we make, like, seven Trolls movies, because it literally is the gift that keeps on giving".<ref>{{cite magazine |date= 9 April 2020 |last= Weatherby |first= Taylor |title= Can't Stop the Sequels! See How Many 'Trolls' Movies Justin Timberlake Wants to Make |url= https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/justin-timberlake-trolls-movies-sequels-9355124/ |magazine= Billboard |access-date= 2 January 2021 |archive-date= 1 January 2021 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20210101201658/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop/9355124/justin-timberlake-trolls-movies-sequels/ |url-status= live }}</ref> On November 22, 2021, it was announced that a third film, titled ''Trolls Band Together'', would be released in theaters on November 17, 2023. The film followed Branch and his newly reunited brothers saving another brother.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.instagram.com/p/CWlp8_PNDb7/ |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/iarchive/s/instagram/CWlp8_PNDb7 |archive-date=2021-12-26 |url-access=registration|title=Get Ready for #Trolls3 on 11/17/23! ✨ #DreamWorksTrolls3|website=[[Instagram]]|date=November 22, 2021|access-date=November 23, 2021}}{{cbignore}}</ref>

===Television specials===
{{Main|Trolls Holiday|Trolls: Holiday in Harmony}}

''Trolls Holiday'' is a half-hour<ref name=NBCTrollsSpecial /> television special that aired on November 24, 2017, on [[NBC]].<ref>{{cite web|last1=Connelly|first1=Brendon|title=Frozen, Trolls getting new TV specials|url=https://www.denofgeek.com/uk/movies/transformers/47678/frozen-trolls-getting-new-tv-specials|publisher=Den of Geek!|access-date=March 5, 2017|date=March 3, 2017|archive-date=March 5, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170305034638/http://www.denofgeek.com/uk/movies/transformers/47678/frozen-trolls-getting-new-tv-specials|url-status=dead}}</ref> Poppy realizes that the Bergens have no holidays to celebrate. She enlists Branch and their friends (the Snack Pack) to show their best friend Bridget and other Bergens the importance of holidays. Most of the original cast (including Kendrick, Timberlake, Deschanel, Mintz-Plasse, Corden, Funches, Nayyar and Dohrn) all reprise their roles from the film. The likely-Christmas Special is also directed by Crawford and written by Josh Bycel & Jonathan Fener with music by Jeff Morrow.<ref name=NBCTrollsSpecial>{{cite web|title=Trolls Holiday|url=http://www.nbc.com/trolls-holiday?nbc=1|publisher=NBC|access-date=May 20, 2017|archive-date=May 25, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170525201555/http://www.nbc.com/trolls-holiday?nbc=1|url-status=live}}</ref> Its soundtrack album that features seven songs was released on October 27, 2017.<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.amazon.com/Trolls-Holiday-Various/dp/B0761FKYBM |title=TROLLS Holiday |access-date=October 20, 2017 |archive-date=April 15, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230415145945/https://www.amazon.com/Trolls-Holiday-Various/dp/B0761FKYBM |url-status=live }}</ref> It is now available on Netflix and on DVD by [[Universal Pictures Home Entertainment]]. The bonus features of the DVD also include one episode of ''[[Spirit Riding Free]]'' and two of ''[[Home: Adventures with Tip & Oh]]''.

A second special, ''[[Trolls: Holiday in Harmony]]'', aired on November 26, 2021.
