'''''Punching the World''''' ({{langx|de|Mit der Faust in die Welt schlagen}}) is a 2025 German [[drama film]] directed by Constanze Klaue in her directorial debut. The film based on the novel of the same name by Lukas Rietzschel, follows The childhood of two brothers, Philipp and Tobi, growing up in the East German provinces is scarred by the disintegration of their family.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.berlinale.de/en/2025/programme/202516907.html |title=Punching the World|access-date=4 February 2025|website=Berlinale}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.flare-film.com/de/spielfilme/mit-der-faust-in-die-welt-schlagen-at |title=Punching the World|date=17 April 2024|access-date= 4 February 2025 |website= Flare Film |language=de}}</ref>

It was selected in Perspectives at the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]] and was screened on 16 February 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/472576 |title= The Berlinale unveils the titles playing in its Competition and its Perspectives and Berlinale Special strands|author= Davide Abbatescianni |date=21 January 2025|access-date=4 February 2025 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref><ref name="maincomp:sd">{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/berlin-film-festival-reveals-2025-competition-line-up/5200946.article |title=Berlin film festival reveals 2025 competition line-up 2025 |first= Michael |last= Rosser |date=21 January 2025 |access-date=4 February 2025 |website=[[ScreenDaily]]|language=en}}</ref>

==Synopsis==

Philipp and Tobias experience their formative years in the expansive yet opportunity-scarce province of [[Saxony]]. As time passes, their town faces the challenge of accommodating refugees, leading to a volatile situation. In response, one brother becomes reclusive, while the other channels his anger outward—eventually finding a means to express it.

==Production==

The film directed by Constanze Klaue has screenplay written by herself based on novel of the same name by Lukas Rietzschel. Produced by Flare Film with Chromosom Film, it is funded by MDM (Mitteldeutsche Medienförderung), [[Medienboard Berlin-Brandenburg|MBB]], BKM (Federal Ministry of Culture and Media) and DFFF (German Film Fund).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.mdr.de/presse/mit-der-faust-in-die-welt-schlagen-im-prespectives-wettbewerb-bei-berlinale-100.html |title="Mit der Faust in die Welt schlagen": MDR-Kinokoproduktion im neuen Perspectives-Wettbewerb der Berlinale 2025 |trans-title="Punching the World with a Fist": MDR cinema co-production in the new Perspectives Competition of the Berlinale 2025  |date=22 January 2025|access-date=5 February 2025|website= [[Mitteldeutscher Rundfunk]] |language=de}}</ref>

Principal photography began at the end of February 2023 in [[Görlitz]], [[Upper Lusatia]] for one month.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.saechsische.de/lokales/goerlitz-lk/bautzen-der-mdr-dreht-ab-ende-februar-rietzschel-film-in-der-oberlausitz-SXACE3B2FFOAXOPQDHI2GYGILM.html |title=MDR dreht ab Ende Februar Rietzschel-Film in der Oberlausitz  |trans-title= MDR to shoot Rietzschel film in Upper Lusatia from the end of February |author=Tim Ruben Weimer  |date=8 February 2023|access-date=4 February 2025|website= Saechsische |language=en}}</ref> The filming ended on 15 June 2023 in Umgebung Berlin, [[Upper Lusatia]], [[Görlitz]], [[Hoyerswerda]], [[Brandenburg]], and [[Saxony]].<ref name="ten:crew">{{Cite web|url=https://www.crew-united.com/en/Punching-the-World__302773.html|title=Punching the World: Feature Film, 2023-2024, Drama, Film based on literary source, Germany |website= Crew United |date= 5 December 2024|access-date=5 February 2025|language=en}}</ref><ref name="prod:filmportal">{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmportal.de/en/movie/mit-der-faust-in-die-welt-schlagen_c30e628f6d154be3a480e99a7a15c42c |title=Mit der Faust in die Welt schlagen: Deutschland 2023/2024 Spielfilm|trans-title=Punching the World, Germany 2023/2024 Feature Film |website= filmportal.de |date=5 December 2024|access-date=5 February 2025|language=de}}</ref>

==Release==

''Punching the World'' had its world premiere on 16 February 2025, as part of the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]], in Perspectives.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2025/01/berlin-film-festival-competition-line-up-2025-follow-live-1236262137/|title=Berlin Film Festival Lineup: Richard Linklater, Jessica Chastain, A$AP Rocky & Marion Cotillard Movies Among Vibrant Selection |last=Ntim |first=Zac |date=21 January 2025|access-date=4 February 2025 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film had its theatrical released in Germany on 3 April 2025 by Across Nations.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.flare-film.com/en/news/the-wagner-brothers-wfh8w-l67jb-hcwbl |title=Berlinale: Punching the World |date= 28 January 2025|access-date= 4 February 2025 |website= Flare Film|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmstarts.de/kritiken/307364.html |title= Mit der Faust in die Welt schlagen|trans-title= Punching the World|date=12 September 2024|access-date=5 February 2025 |website= Film Starts |language=de}}</ref>
