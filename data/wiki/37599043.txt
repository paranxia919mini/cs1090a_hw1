The film was produced by [[Grant Hill (film producer)|Grant Hill]] and the Wachowskis, making ''Jupiter Ascending'' Hill's seventh collaboration with the Wachowskis as producer or executive producer. Several more [[The Wachowskis#Frequent collaborators|longstanding Wachowski collaborators]] since the creation of the ''Matrix'' films contributed to the picture,<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.geeksofdoom.com/2014/03/26/new-trailer-jupiter-ascending-channing-tatum-mila-kunis-released |title=New Trailer For 'Jupiter Ascending' With Channing Tatum, Mila Kunis Released |publisher=Geeksofdoom.com |date=March 26, 2014 |access-date=June 9, 2014}}</ref> including production designer Hugh Bateup, visual effects supervisor [[Method Studios|Dan Glass]], [[VFX creative director|visual effects designer]] [[John Gaeta]], standby propman Alex Boswell, sound designer and supervising sound editor [[Dane A. Davis]] and [[costume designer]] [[Kym Barrett]]. Other notable past collaborators include ''[[Speed Racer (film)|Speed Racer]]'' music composer [[Michael Giacchino]], ''[[Cloud Atlas (film)|Cloud Atlas]]'' [[Cinematographer|director of photography]] [[John Toll]] along with its editor Alexander Berner and hair and make-up designer Jeremy Woodhead, who worked on both.

''Jupiter Ascending'' was released in the United States on February 6, 2015, by [[Warner Bros. Pictures]]. It received negative reviews from critics; despite praise for the visual effects, the narrative was criticized as confusing. It grossed $184&nbsp;million against a $176–210 million budget during its theatrical release.

==Plot==
<!--per [[WP:FILMPLOT]], plot summaries should be between 400 and 700 words--> 
Earth and countless other planets were established by families of [[transhuman]] and alien royalty to harvest the resulting organisms to produce a perpetual life-extending [[youth serum]] for the elites on other planets. After the [[matriarch]] of the House of [[Abraxas|Abrasax]], the most powerful of the alien dynasties, dies, her children — Balem, Kalique, and Titus — quarrel over the inheritance. Balem inherits control of the Abrasax business empire, while Kalique inherits the planet Cerise and Titus spends his inheritance on a lavish spaceship. None of them are named Sovereign of Abrasax. Instead, they are styled First, Second and Third Primary.

After Maximilian Jones is killed in a robbery, his wife Aleksa (née Bolotnikov), whom he met in [[Saint Petersburg, Russia]], names their daughter Jupiter after [[Jupiter|his favorite planet]], and they relocate to [[Chicago]] to live with her family. Years later, Jupiter makes a modest living as a housekeeper to her wealthy neighbors. Desiring a telescope, she agrees to sell her [[egg cell]]s and uses her friend Katharine Dunlevy's name as a pseudonym. At Katharine's house, extraterrestrial "Keepers" attack the women. Jupiter photographs them, but they erase both women's memories of the incident immediately afterwards. She later notices the strange photograph on her phone while waiting at an egg donation clinic, but cannot recall anything about it. During the procedure, the doctors and nurses are revealed as Keepers sent to kill her, but Caine Wise, a former soldier dispatched by Titus to bring her to him, rescues her. As Caine and Jupiter flee, a squad of Keepers destroys their spaceship, but he fends off the attack and kills them before hijacking one of their vehicles, realizing that Jupiter must be highly significant to Titus and Balem, who are revealed to have dispatched the Keepers to Earth.

Caine takes Jupiter to the hideout of Stinger Apini, another former soldier living in exile on Earth. After learning she can control the bees in Stinger's residence, Jupiter discovers that she is galactic royalty. Stinger agrees to help Jupiter, but she is captured by Balem's hunters and bribed by Kalique to bring Jupiter to her [[Alcázar|alcazar]] on Cerise. There, Kalique explains that Jupiter is genetically identical to the House of Abrasax's long-dead matriarch, and therefore she is the Earth's rightful owner.

Supported by Captain Diomika Tsing of the intergalactic police force, Aegis, Caine retrieves Jupiter from Kalique and takes her to the capital planet, Orous, to claim her inheritance as Sovereign of Abrasax. Once claimed, Jupiter would gain control of Abrasax Industries and displace Balem as titleholder of Earth. In another attempt to lure Jupiter to him, Balem sends Greeghan to kidnap her family. On the way back to Earth, Titus's henchmen capture Jupiter and detain Caine as punishment for not bringing her to him. Titus reveals to Caine his plan to marry Jupiter, kill her, and claim Earth. Titus then throws Caine into the void of space and attempts to seduce Jupiter, declaring his intention to dismantle the youth serum trade, of which Earth is the next intended source. Caine survives being spaced and returns with Stinger to save Jupiter at the altar before she is married. Jupiter asks to return home but learns that her family has been taken hostage by Balem.

At his refinery inside the [[Great Red Spot]], Balem demands Earth in exchange for Jupiter's family. Realizing that Balem cannot "harvest" Earth without her permission, Jupiter refuses. Caine infiltrates the refinery and damages its gravity hull, causing the refinery to begin collapsing. While the occupants evacuate the refinery, Tsing's ship moves in and rescues Jupiter's family. Jupiter survives the collapsing structures, only to land at the feet of Balem, who tries to kill her; she fights him off before Balem falls to his death. Caine saves her as the refinery is in its final stages of collapse. Tsing opens a portal to Earth and prepares to evacuate, potentially leaving Caine and Jupiter behind. However, she is relieved that they have survived and crossed the portal, along with Tsing's ship. Jupiter's family returns home with no memory of their disappearance, while Jupiter secretly retains ownership of the Earth. Caine's rank in the Legion is restored, and he and Jupiter begin a relationship.

==Production==
===Development===
In 2009, [[Warner Bros. Entertainment|Warner Bros.]] president Jeff Robinov approached the Wachowskis about creating an original intellectual property and [[Media franchise|franchise]]. Development began two years later, with the production and visual effects teams doing [[Film and television production manager|pre-production]] work based on a first [[Draft document|draft]] of the [[Screenplay|script]], while the Wachowskis were shooting the future-set segments of ''[[Cloud Atlas (film)|Cloud Atlas]]''.<ref name="Total Film"/> The story was partly inspired by Lana's favorite book,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nytimes.com/1999/04/05/movies/brothers-unleash-the-comic-book-of-ideas.html |title=Brothers Unleash the Comic Book of Ideas |date=April 5, 1999 |work=The New York Times|access-date=January 30, 2015|last=Weinraub|first=Bernard|page=E1}}</ref> the ''[[Odyssey]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wsj.com/articles/the-matrix-creators-launch-a-new-epic-with-jupiter-ascending-1422548645|title='The Matrix' Creators Launch a New Epic With 'Jupiter Ascending' – WSJ|author=Steinberg|date=January 29, 2015 |work=The Wall Street Journal|access-date=January 30, 2015|first=Don}}</ref> "It was making me super-emotional", Lana has said. "The whole concept of these almost spiritual journeys and you're changed." Another inspiration was ''[[The Wizard of Oz (1939 film)|The Wizard of Oz]]'' which Lana contrasts to the ''Odyssey''. "[[Dorothy Gale|Dorothy]] is pretty much the same at the end as she is at the beginning. Whereas [[Odysseus]] goes through such an epic shift in his identity." The Wachowskis themselves describe the plot of the film as an effort to reverse the classical science-fiction trope of the hero who is "emotionally withholding and strong and stoic". Instead, they tried to create a new form of female space opera hero. "We were, like, 'Can we bring a different kind of female character like Dorothy or [[Alice (Alice's Adventures in Wonderland)|Alice]]? Characters who negotiate conflict and complex situations with intelligence and empathy?' Yes, Dorothy has a protector, [[Toto (Oz)|Toto]], who's always barking at everyone. And that was sort of the origin of Caine."<ref>{{cite news|first=Jim|last=Slotek|url=http://www.torontosun.com/2015/02/04/jupiter-ascending-filmmakers-thrive-on-being-sci-fi-originals|title='Jupiter Ascending' filmmakers thrive on being sci-fi originals |newspaper=[[Toronto Sun]]|date=February 4, 2015}}</ref>

===Production design===
Producer [[Grant Hill (film producer)|Grant Hill]] and visual effects supervisor Dan Glass have noted that the Wachowskis never repeat themselves. Hill has described the design as an original take on the look of space environments, while Glass mentioned it was influenced by cities around Europe rather than science fiction touchstones. Examples include [[Renaissance architecture]], modern glass and Gothic art.<ref name="Total Film"/>

Stereo 3D Production Expertise |publisher=Vision3.tv |access-date=July 29, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140730013805/http://www.vision3.tv/project.php?id=42 |archive-date=July 30, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> An eight-minute-long pursuit sequence, code-named "Fifty-Two Part" by the film's crew, depicts Jupiter and Caine fleeing from aliens and spaceships in downtown Chicago shortly after they first meet. It was the longest sequence in the script, involving some of the film's most difficult stunts. To complete it, Kunis and Tatum had to film every day for six months.<ref name="SFX" />

===Visual effects===
The filmmakers, wherever possible, employed [[Stunts|stuntwork]] rather than [[Computer-generated imagery|CGI]]. Tatum has noted there was minimal use of [[Virtual actor|digital doubles]]. Most stunts were done by the principal actors or stuntmen attempting to match the [[Previsualization#Digital pre-visualization|pre-vis]] sequences.<ref name="GQ"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://collider.com/jupiter-ascending-channing-tatum-spider-man-2-jamie-foxx/ |title=Channing Tatum and Jamie Foxx Talk JUPITER ASCENDING and SPIDER-MAN 2 |website=[[Collider (website)|Collider]]|first=Adam|last=Chitwood|date=June 22, 2013|access-date=June 9, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|first=Todd|last=Gilchrist|date=June 25, 2013|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1709586/channing-tatum-jupiter-ascending/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140605093144/http://www.mtv.com/news/1709586/channing-tatum-jupiter-ascending/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=June 5, 2014 |title='Jupiter Ascending' Takes Channing Tatum To Places He's 'Never Gone Before' |website=[[MTV]] |access-date=June 9, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|first=Peter|last=Gicas|url=http://uk.eonline.com/news/526198/mila-kunis-talks-working-with-channing-tatum-on-jupiter-ascending-he-literally-saved-my-life |title=Mila Kunis Talks Working With Channing Tatum on Jupiter Ascending: He Literally Saved My Life |website=[[E! Online UK]] |date=March 28, 2014 |access-date=June 9, 2014}}</ref> For the scenes of Tatum's character flying using antigravity boots, Glass has stated that his team invented a way to use stuntmen instead of doing them digitally, despite the limited available time to shoot them.<ref>{{cite web|first=Erik|last=Sofge |url=http://www.popsci.com/article/science/real-life-science-behind-summers-most-outrageous-sci-fi-movies |title=The Real-Life Science Behind The Summer's Most Outrageous Sci-Fi Movies |website=[[Popular Science]] |date=May 30, 2014 |access-date=June 9, 2014}}</ref> They created a rig of six cameras, called the Panocam, which was mounted on a helicopter and covered nearly 180 degrees of the action. During [[post-production]], the directors could combine the overlapped filmed footage, essentially creating a camera that could swing around the action independently of the helicopter's actual flying path.<ref>{{cite web|first=Ian|last=Failes|title=Shoot and stitch: making Jupiter Ascending's Chicago chase|url=https://www.fxguide.com/fxfeatured/shooting-and-stitching-making-jupiter-ascendings-chicago-chase/|website=FX Guide|date=February 15, 2015|access-date=October 11, 2020}}</ref> The technique piqued the interest of other directors who have subsequently used it in their own movies. Visual effects company [[Framestore]] used Vicon T40 cameras for pre-vis and [[motion-capture]] purposes, the same camera system they used in the 2013 film ''[[Gravity (2013 film)|Gravity]]'', which was critically acclaimed for its cinematography and visual effects.<ref>{{cite web|first=Nick|last=Dager|url=http://www.digitalcinemareport.com/article/framestore-uses-vicon-t40-cameras-pre-vis-gravity-jupiter-ascending |title=Framestore Uses Vicon T40 Cameras for Pre-Vis on Gravity, Jupiter Ascending |website=Digital Cinema Report |date=November 21, 2013 |access-date=June 9, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131124111039/http://www.digitalcinemareport.com/article/framestore-uses-vicon-t40-cameras-pre-vis-gravity-jupiter-ascending|archive-date=November 24, 2013}}</ref>

===Music===
The [[Film score|film's score]] was composed by [[Michael Giacchino]] and performed by the [[Hollywood Studio Symphony]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://filmmusicreporter.com/2013/03/05/michael-giacchino-to-score-jupiter-ascending/|title=Michael Giacchino to Score 'Jupiter Ascending'|author=|work=filmmusicreporter.com|date=March 5, 2013 |access-date=April 20, 2013}}</ref> On June 10, 2013, Giacchino tweeted that {{ill|Ludwig Wicki|de}}, Robert Ziegler and Tim Simonec were conducting the film's score at [[Abbey Road Studios]] in London.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/m_giacchino/status/344048295557074944 |title=Twitter / m_giacchino: Ludwig Wicki Conducting the |publisher=Twitter.com |date=June 10, 2013 |access-date=February 14, 2014}}</ref> In August, Giacchino stated: "We're actually recording all the music first, before they're even done shooting. It's been done sort of backward, and it's much more freeing doing it that way. I'm not locked down to any specific timings and what the film is doing. I can do whatever I want. It opens up a lot more possibilities."<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sfx.co.uk/2013/08/20/sfx239-preview-composer-michael-giacchino-on-jupiter-ascending/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130820180729/http://www.sfx.co.uk/2013/08/20/sfx239-preview-composer-michael-giacchino-on-jupiter-ascending/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 20, 2013|title=SFX239 Preview: Composer Michael Giacchino On Jupiter Ascending |work=SFX|date=August 20, 2013|access-date=August 20, 2013}}</ref> The Wachowskis first used this approach during production of ''Cloud Atlas'' at the recommendation of co-director [[Tom Tykwer]] who has made all his movies this way, and have since commented they will never again make a movie without recording the music first.<ref>{{cite web |last=Sepinwall |first=Alan |url=http://www.hitfix.com/motion-captured/an-epic-interview-with-the-wachowskis-and-tom-tykwer-from-cloud-atlas-to-jupiter-ascending/3 |title=An epic interview with the Wachowskis and Tom Tykwer: From Cloud Atlas to Jupiter Ascending |website=[[Hitfix.com]] |date=October 10, 2012 |access-date=June 13, 2014 |archive-date=July 14, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140714122303/http://www.hitfix.com/motion-captured/an-epic-interview-with-the-wachowskis-and-tom-tykwer-from-cloud-atlas-to-jupiter-ascending/3 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Dancer Kyle Davis was inspired when listening to Giacchino's score for the film to create a ballet for the [[Pacific Northwest Ballet]] named "A Dark and Lonely Space" that is choreographed to Giacchino's music. The ballet, which premiered November 2, 2018, features 24 dancers that represent planets, celestial bodies, and forces of physics, and was designed as an "[[anthropomorphism|anthropomorphization]] of the birth of a planetary system."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.polygon.com/2018/11/8/18056748/jupiter-ascending-ballet|title=Jupiter Ascending is getting a new life in ballet|first=Susana|last=Polo|date=November 8, 2018|website=[[Polygon (website)|Polygon]]}}</ref>

==Release==
The film was originally set to be released on July 25, 2014,<ref>{{cite web |first=Nikki|last=Finke|url=https://deadline.com/2007/04/caa-loses-high-profile-director-to-uta-2014/ |title=WB Dates Wachowskis' 'Jupiter Ascending', Shifts De Niro-Stallone 'Grudge Match' |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=March 14, 2013|access-date=April 20, 2013}}</ref> but it was later moved up to July 18, 2014,<ref>{{cite web |first=Todd |last=Cunningham|url=https://www.thewrap.com/wachowskis-jupiter-ascending-moves-week-july-18-2014/ |title=Wachowskis' 'Jupiter Ascending' Moves Up a Week to July 18, 2014 |website=[[TheWrap]] |date=December 11, 2013 |access-date=February 14, 2014}}</ref> and on June 3, 2014, the film's release was delayed to February 6, 2015, due to poor test screenings that April and to give additional time needed to complete over 2,000 special effects shots, which increased the final budget from $130&nbsp;million to over US$210&nbsp;million.<ref name="Deadline"/> The film received a "secret screening" at the [[2015 Sundance Film Festival]] which was invitation only and did not include members of the press. [[Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]'s Ramin Setoodeh reported that clusters of seats were empty at the screening.<ref name="sundance"/>

The film was released in [[IMAX 3D]], as was its competitor ''[[Seventh Son (film)|Seventh Son]]'' from [[Universal Pictures]] the same weekend. ''Jupiter Ascending'' had a surprise premiere at the [[2015 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 27, 2015, at the [[Mary G. Steiner Egyptian Theatre]] in [[Park City, Utah|Park City]].<ref name="sundance">{{cite magazine | url = https://variety.com/2015/film/news/jupiter-ascending-debuts-to-muted-crowd-at-sundance-1201417130/ | title = 'Jupiter Ascending' Debuts to Muted Crowd at Sundance | first= Ramin|last=Setoodah | magazine = [[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] | date = January 27, 2015 | access-date = January 28, 2015}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Box office===
''Jupiter Ascending'' grossed $47.4&nbsp;million in the United States and Canada, and $136.5&nbsp;million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $184&nbsp;million, against a budget of $176–210&nbsp;million.<ref name="BOM" /><ref name="Deadline" />

The film grossed $1&nbsp;million from Thursday's preview.<ref name="deadline.com">{{cite web |url=https://deadline.com/2015/02/spongebob-squarepants-box-office-jupiter-ascending-seventh-son-1201366190/ |title='SpongeBob's $53M Pair of Pants; 'Jupiter' & 'Son' Descending – Late Night B.O. Update |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |access-date=February 8, 2015|date=February 9, 2015|last=D'Alessandro|first=Anthony}}</ref> The film earned $6.4&nbsp;million on its opening day, and was later forecast to open at around $18&nbsp;million.<ref>{{cite web |date= February 7, 2015 |first= Ray|last=Subers|url= https://boxofficemojo.com/news/?id=4017&p=.htm |title=Friday Report: 'SpongeBob' Scores Super $15.1 Million |work=[[Box Office Mojo]] |access-date=February 8, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite news | url=https://variety.com/2015/film/news/two-hollywood-flops-in-one-weekend-at-the-box-office-1201427439/ |title=Two Hollywood Flops in One Weekend at the Box Office |first=Stuart |last=Oldham |date=February 7, 2015 |work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |access-date=February 8, 2015}}</ref> The film earned $7.6&nbsp;million for its second day and $5&nbsp;million on its third day,<ref>{{cite web |url= https://boxofficemojo.com/movies/?page=daily&id=jupiterascending.htm |title=Jupiter Ascending Daily Gross |work=[[Box Office Mojo]] |access-date=February 9, 2015}}</ref> for a gross of $18.4&nbsp;million in its opening weekend from 3,181 theaters, an $5,776 per-theatre average.<ref name="BOM"/> It finished in third at the box office behind ''[[The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water]]'' ($55.4&nbsp;million) and ''[[American Sniper (film)|American Sniper]]'' ($23.3&nbsp;million).<ref>{{cite web| url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/weekend/chart/?yr=2015&wknd=06&p=.htm |title=Weekend Box Office Results for February 6–8, 2015|website=[[Box Office Mojo]]|date=February 8, 2015}}</ref>

Despite a disappointing North American debut, the film opened in the top spot internationally, earning US$32.5&nbsp;million playing in theatres of 65 markets in other territories. Among the top markets was Russia, where the film earned a gross of US$4.7&nbsp;million and topped the box office. It also opened in markets such as France (US$2.5&nbsp;million), South Korea (US$2.1&nbsp;million), the UK (US$2&nbsp;million), Brazil (US$1.9&nbsp;million), Mexico (US$1.8&nbsp;million), Germany (US$1.8&nbsp;million), Italy (US$1.2&nbsp;million) and Spain (US$1.1&nbsp;million). The film also debuted in Asian markets, bringing in US$6&nbsp;million in total from Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/news/?id=4019&p=.htm |title=Around-the-World Roundup: 'Jupiter' Ascends to Top Spot Overseas |work=[[Box Office Mojo]]  |access-date=February 8, 2015|date=February 8, 2015|last=Subers|first=Ray}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |first=Ali|last=Jaafar|url=https://deadline.com/2015/02/jupiter-ascending-rises-to-number-one-at-the-international-box-office-1201369098/ |title='Jupiter Ascending' Rises To Number One at the International Box Office |work=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=February 8, 2015|access-date=February 9, 2015}}</ref> The film opened in China in March (US$23.2&nbsp;million) and the opening in China took it to the top spot in the international market for the weekend.<ref>{{cite web |date= March 8, 2015 |first= Ray|last=Subers |url= https://www.boxofficemojo.com/news/?id=4039&p=.htm&1 |title=Around-the-World Roundup: 'Jupiter Ascending' Opens to $23 Million in China |work=[[Box Office Mojo]] |access-date=March 8, 2015}}</ref>

Before its release, the film had been included in the list of "The Riskiest Box Office Bets of 2015" published by [[Screen Rant]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://screenrant.com/movies-box-office-2015-predictions-best-worst/ |title=The Riskiest Box Office Bets of 2015 | work=Screen Rant |date=January 10, 2015 |access-date=February 6, 2015|last=Young|first=Paul}}</ref> It had been forecasted to gross between US$21–23&nbsp;million in its opening weekend.<ref>{{cite news|first=Saba|last=Hamedy|date=February 5, 2015|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/envelope/cotown/la-et-ct-spongebob-american-sniper-jupiter-ascending-seventh-son-20150205-story.html |title='SpongeBob' to soak up box office, push 'American Sniper' out of top spot |newspaper=[[Los Angeles Times]] |access-date=February 8, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://screenrant.com/american-sniper-spongebob-movie-box-office-prediction |title=Box Office Prediction: 'American Sniper' vs. 'The SpongeBob Movie' |work=[[Screen Rant]] |access-date=February 8, 2015 |first=Chris|last=Agar|date=February 3, 2015 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date= February 5, 2015 |first= Ray|last=Subers |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/news/?id=4016&p=.htm | title=Forecast: 'SpongeBob' To Take Down 'Sniper' on First Weekend of February |publisher=IMDb |work=Box Office Mojo |access-date=February 8, 2015}}</ref>

===Critical response===
Review aggregator [[Rotten Tomatoes]] reports that 28% of 272 critics have given the film a positive review. The site's critics consensus reads, "Pleasing to the eye but narratively befuddled, Jupiter Ascending delivers another visually thrilling misfire from the Wachowskis."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/jupiter_ascending_2014 |title=Jupiter Ascending|access-date=August 8, 2026 |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]}}</ref>[[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 40 out of 100, based on 40 critics, indicating "mixed or average" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Jupiter Ascending'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/jupiter-ascending |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=2025-04-23 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average grade of "B−" on an A+ to F scale.<ref name=THRJupiter>{{cite magazine | first= Pamela |last=McClintock | date = February 6, 2015 | title = Box office: ''SpongeBob'' nabs $15.1M Friday for $52M debut; ''Jupiter Ascending'' falters | magazine = [[The Hollywood Reporter]] | url = https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/box-office-spongebob-nabs-151m-767766 | access-date = February 7, 2015}}</ref>

[[David Edelstein]] of [[Vulture.com]] was highly critical of the whole film, calling it "inane from the first frame to last ... it's miraculously unmiraculous."<ref name=vulture_jupiter>{{cite web |last=Edelstein |first=David |author-link=David Edelstein |title=''Jupiter Ascending'' is inane from the first frame to last |website=[[Vulture.com|Vulture]] |date=February 6, 2015 |publisher=New York Media LLC |location=New York, NY |url=https://www.vulture.com/2015/02/movie-review-jupiter-ascending.html |access-date=March 10, 2015}}</ref> Joe McGovern of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' was also critical of the film, giving it a C+, writing that it was "just another incoherent sci-fi spectacle".<ref name="EW_jupiter">{{cite magazine |last=McGovern |first=Joe |date=February 6, 2015 |title=''Jupiter Ascending'' review: Lost in space |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]] |publisher=[[Meredith Corporation]] |location=New York, NY |url=https://www.ew.com/article/2015/02/06/jupiter-ascending-review-lost-space |access-date=March 10, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150207094149/https://www.ew.com/article/2015/02/06/jupiter-ascending-review-lost-space|archive-date=February 7, 2015}}</ref> British film critic [[Mark Kermode]] said, "''Jupiter Ascending'' is a lot of things. Bonkers, all over the place, incoherent, preposterous, ridiculous dialogue that [[George Lucas]] would have thrown in the bin, spectacularly overripe performances. I'm not going to say it's good, but I would be lying if I said I didn't enjoy it."<ref>{{cite AV media |title=Mark Kermode reviews Jupiter Ascending |medium=video |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ErPeyjcukGM |via=YouTube |access-date=March 25, 2016|date=February 9, 2015}}</ref>

Kofi Outlaw of ''[[Screen Rant]]'' called Redmayne's performance "so over-the-top with his effeminate mannerisms and [[Bane in other media|Bane]]-style whisper voice that ''Jupiter Ascending'' devolves into an absurd comedy whenever he's onscreen," and commented that "he may go home with an Oscar AND a [[Golden Raspberry Awards|Razzie]] on the same night".<ref>{{cite web |first=Kofi (Redmayne did in fact "win" a Razzie Award for Worst Supporting Actor, for his performance in ''Jupiter Ascending''.<ref name="CNN"/>) [[Manohla Dargis]] of ''[[The New York Times]]'' called Redmayne's performance "camping",<ref>{{cite news |first=Manohla |last=Dargis |author-link=Manohla Dargis |date=February 5, 2015 |title=Cleaning lady has no idea of her crucial role in the cosmos while Stephanie Merry of ''[[The Washington Post]]'' lambasted him for "screeching his lines" in a way that "is about as intimidating as a toddler", adding, "Unfortunately, you'll never be able to Donna Dickens of ''[[HitFix]]'' noted that the film picked up an enthusiastic following among female science fiction fans, and reported that many viewers found the film attractive for providing "the wish-fulfillment of prepubescent girls".<ref name="HitFix1">{{cite news | first=Donna |last=Dickens | date=February 24, 2015 |title='Jupiter Ascending' is the Sci-Fi movie women were waiting for | work=[[HitFix]] | url=https://uproxx.com/hitfix/jupiter-ascending-is-the-sci-fi-movie-women-were-waiting-for/ |access-date=August 4, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210624192723/https://uproxx.com/hitfix/jupiter-ascending-is-the-sci-fi-movie-women-were-waiting-for/|archive-date=June 24, 2021}}</ref> Dickens explained that where Hollywood typically portrays strong women in action films as "[[Arnold Schwarzenegger]] with boobs", ''Jupiter Ascending'' presents Kunis's character differently: "Women don't always want superhuman robots to look up to.
