'''''Beuys''''' is a 2017 German [[documentary film]] directed by [[Andres Veiel]] about the German artist [[Joseph Beuys]]. It was selected to compete for the [[Golden Bear]] in the main competition section of the [[67th Berlin International Film Festival]].<ref name="Berlin">{{cite web|url=https://www.berlinale.de/en/archiv/jahresarchive/2017/08_pressemitteilungen_2017/08_pressemitteilungen_2017detail_36116.html|title=Berlinale 2017: Competition and Berlinale Special Are Complete|publisher=Berlinale. berlinale.de |type=press release|date=20 January 2017|accessdate=20 January 2018}}</ref>

Noting that the film emphasizes the "social conscience" behind Beuys's art, [[Glenn Kenny]], writing for ''[[The New York Times]]'', called it "an exhilarating portrait of a unique truth-teller".<ref>Kenny, Glenn (16 January 2018). "[https://www.nytimes.com/2018/01/16/movies/joseph-beuys-documentary-review.html Review: In ‘Beuys,’ a Portrait of the Artist as a Socially Conscious Provocateur]". ''New York Times''. nytimes.com. Retrieved 20 January 2018. Print version, 17 January 2018, p.&nbsp;C5, under title "A Conceptual Artist and Provocateur".</ref>
