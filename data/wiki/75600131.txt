'''''In Our Day''''' ({{Korean|우리의 하루}}) is a 2023 South Korean [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]], and the 30th feature film written and directed by [[Hong Sang-soo]], who also produced, photographed, scored and edited the film.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=In Our Day |url=https://www.quinzaine-cineastes.fr/en/film/in-our-day |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Quinzaine des cinéastes |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Pearce |first=Leonard |date=2023-05-16 |title=First Trailer for Hong Sang-soo's 30th Feature Film In Our Day, Premiering at Cannes |url=https://thefilmstage.com/first-trailer-for-hong-sang-soos-30th-feature-film-in-our-day-premiering-at-cannes/ |access-date=2023-12-19 |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film had its world premiere at the [[Directors' Fortnight]] section of the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Goodfellow |first=Melanie |date=2023-04-18 |title=Cannes Directors' Fortnight Unveils 2023 Line-Up: Michel Gondry, Hong Sangsoo, 'Good Time' DoP Sean Price Williams Make Cut – Full List & Film Details |url=https://deadline.com/2023/04/cannes-directors-fortnight-2023-line-up-2-1235329293/ |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> and it was selected for the main slate section of the [[2023 New York Film Festival]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-08 |title=61st New York Film Festival Main Slate Announced |url=https://www.filmlinc.org/daily/61st-new-york-film-festival-main-slate-announced/ |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Film at Lincoln Center |language=en}}</ref>

== Plot ==
Recently returned to South Korea, an actress in her forties is staying with her friend and her cat. Meanwhile, an aging poet in declining health lives alone after the death of his own cat. One day, each receives a visit from a different young aspiring artist, who come equipped with questions about their careers and life itself.

The film was produced by the Jeonwonsa Film Company, and was shot in [[Seoul]] in 2022.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kiang |first=Jessica |date=2023-10-11 |title='In Our Day' Review: Hong Sang-soo Murmurs Sweet Little Somethings About Life's Unseen Connections |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/asia/in-our-day-review-hong-sang-soos-sweet-little-storyof-lifes-unseen-connections-1235752162/ |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Variety |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Release ==
''In Our Day'' premiered at the [[Directors' Fortnight|Directors Fortnight]] main selection of the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival]], on May 25.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Selection 2023 |url=https://www.quinzaine-cineastes.fr/en/selection/2023 |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Quinzaine des cinéastes |language=en}}</ref> It was also shown at the Main Slate section of the [[2023 New York Film Festival]], alongside Hong's other 2023 film feature, ''[[In Water]]''.<ref name=":1" />
