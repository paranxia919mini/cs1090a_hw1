'''''Elbow''''' ({{langx|de|Ellbogen}}) is a 2024 [[Coming-of-age story|coming-of-age]] [[drama film]] co-written and directed by Aslı Özarslan in her directorial debut. The film, an adaptation of the [[Franz-Hessel-Preis]] and [[Klaus-Michael Kühne]] prize-winning German novel ''{{lang|de|Ellbogen}}'' by [[Fatma Aydemir]], is a story about 17-year-old Hazal, who lives in Berlin. Her biggest wish for her 18th birthday is to escape the everyday grind and party with her friends. But a fatal incident changes everything and she is forced to flee.<ref name="elbow:jip">{{Cite web|url=https://jip-film.de/ellbogen/ |title= Elbow |access-date=5 February 2024 |website= jip film & verleih gbr|language=de}}</ref>

The international co-production between Germany, Turkey and France was selected for the Generation 14plus section at the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 17 February and competed for the Crystal Bear for the Best Film.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/454955/ |title= Berlin unveils the entirety of its Generation line-up |first= Vassilis |last= Economou |date=18 January 2024|access-date=5 February 2024 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref> Aslı Özarslan, making her feature directing debut, was nominated for GWFF Best First Feature Award at the festival.<ref name="gwff:berlinale"/>

==Synopsis==

17-year-old Hazal lives in Berlin. She wants a chance in life; her fervent wish is to get one step further than her parents. Her mother pushes her to get a hairdressing apprenticeship, while her aunt Semra, a social worker, encourages Hazal to finish her ''[[Abitur]]''. She works at her mother's bakery and fails to get any response to her hundreds of job applications. She attends job training programs, but encounters discrimination and she finds them to be too time-consuming.

On her 18th birthday, she wants to forget her humdrum life, and party with her best friends. Disappointed after being turned away from a club, they get into a fight with a student in a subway station, and he dies after Hazal shoves him onto the subway tracks. Hazal flees to Istanbul to live with Mehmet, a call centre agent that she regularly flirts with over [[Skype]]. He turns out to be a drug addict and is physically abusive towards her. During a police raid on Mehmet's apartment, during which Mehmet's roommate was to be arrested for political agitation, Hazal's head is injured, but she is able to escape. Hazal contacts Semra, who comes to Istanbul and tells her that she has been charged with [[manslaughter]]. Semra urges Hazal to turn herself in to the police and to finish secondary school in prison. Hazal hugs her aunt and runs away alone.

==Production==

Principal photography began on 15 August 2022 in [[Istanbul]] and Berlin.<ref name="prod:filmportal">{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmportal.de/film/ellbogen_f543d4c380a642e8ad7736d54826f435 |title=Ellbogen, Deutschland Türkei Frankreich 2022-2024 Spielfilm|trans-title= Elbow: Germany Turkey France 2022-2024 feature film |website= filmportal.de |access-date= 5 February 2024|language=de}}</ref> Filming ended on 15 October 2022 with filming locations in various regions of [[Berlin]] and Turkey.<ref name="ten:crew">{{Cite web|url=https://www.crew-united.com/en/Elbow__276673.html |title=Elbow: Feature Film – 2022-2024, Coming of Age, Drama, Germany, Turkey|website= Crew United |access-date=5 February 2024|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://en.unifrance.org/movie/57908/elbow |title=Elbow|access-date=4 February 2024|website= [[Unifrance]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Release==

''Elbow'' had its world premiere on 17 February 2024, as part of the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]], in the Generation 14plus section.<ref name="pan:dl"/><ref name="gen:sd">{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/berlinale-unveils-complete-2024-panorama-generation-forum-sections/5189510.article |title=Berlinale unveils complete 2024 Panorama, Generation, Forum sections |first= Ben |last= Dalton |date=17 January 2024 |access-date=February 5, 2024 |website=[[ScreenDaily]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.berlinale.de/en/2024/programme/202402994.html#screening_dates |title=Elbow |work=Berlinale |date=6 February 2024 |access-date=8 February 2024}}</ref>

==Reception==

Vladan Petkovic, reviewing the film at the Berlinale for [[Cineuropa]], wrote "Özarslan's coming-of-age film set in the Berlin immigrant community and Istanbul stands out for its storytelling economy, unusually dark atmosphere, and thematic variety and depth."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/457119 |title= Review: Elbow |first=Vladan|last=Petkovic |date=19 February 2024|access-date=21 February 2024 |website= Cineuropa |language=en}}</ref> Julia Lorenz of ''[[Die Zeit]]'' called ''Elbow'' "the best youth film in recent German film history."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lorenz |first=Julia |date=7 September 2024 |title="Ellbogen" von Aslı Özarslan: Anklage, Kampfansage, Stoßseufzer |url=https://www.zeit.de/kultur/film/2024-09/ellbogen-asli-oezarslan-film-roman-fatma-aydemir/komplettansicht |access-date=21 December 2024 |website=[[Die Zeit]] |language=de}}</ref>

==Accolades==
The film was selected for the Generation 14plus section at the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]]; it was thus nominated to compete for the Crystal Bear award.
