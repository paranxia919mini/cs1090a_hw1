'''''From Hilde, with Love''''' ({{langx|de|'''In Liebe, Eure Hilde'''}}) is a 2024 German [[biographical film]] about [[Hilde Coppi]], who along with her husband [[Hans Coppi]] belonged to [[Red Orchestra (espionage)|Red Orchestra]], a [[German resistance to Nazism|German resistance group to Nazism]]. Directed by [[Andreas Dresen]], the film stars [[Liv Lisa Fries]] in titular role of Hilde Coppi and Johannes Hegemann as Hans Coppi.<ref name="lead:filmportal">{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmportal.de/film/in-liebe-eure-hilde_19eb0a77a6c7471fae81d88a2c59437f |title=In Liebe, Eure Hilde: Deutschland 2022-2024 Spielfilm|trans-title=From Hilde, With Love: Germany 2022-2024  feature film |website= filmportal.de |access-date= 27 January 2024|language=de}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/global/from-hilde-with-love-andreas-dresen-berlinale-1235915204/|title=Why ‘From Hilde, With Love’ Director Made Nazi-Era Berlin Seem Almost ‘Normal’: ‘I Wanted to Bring It Closer to Our Reality’|publisher=Variety}}</ref>

The film was selected in the Competition at the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]] to be held from 15 to 25 February 2024, where it competed for the [[Golden Bear]] with its first screening on 17 February at Berlinale Palast.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/455195 |title= The Berlinale unveils its Competition and Encounters titles |first=Davide|last=Abbatescianni |date=22 January 2024|access-date=27 January 2024 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Synopsis==

The film is a drama of short life, about love and death based on true events of World War II. In early 1942 in Berlin, [[Hilde Coppi]], a young medical assistant, became part of a rebel group that was later known as the "[[Red Orchestra (espionage)|Red Orchestra]]". There she met and fell in love with [[Hans Coppi]], who later became her husband. They enjoyed a wonderful summer together, despite the danger of their activities and the risk of being caught. Eventually captured by the Gestapo, Hilde was imprisoned while pregnant. She gave birth to a son in [[Barnimstrasse women's prison]], and soon after, she was condemned to death. In her final months, being a mother gave her the strength to face her destiny.

==Production==

[[File:CIC RO Hilde Coppi detail.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Hilde Coppi]] in 1940]]

In May 2022, [[Liv Lisa Fries]] was cast as the lead to play titular role of [[Hilde Coppi]]. Laila Stieler wrote the script for the producers Pandora Film Produktion's Claudia Steffen and Christoph Friedel with Cooky Ziesche's Leuchtstoff (Initiative von RBB und Medienboard Berlin) [de], Regina Ziegler's Ziegler Film GmbH & Co. KG, and Dresen and Andreas Leusink's Iskremas Filmproduktion GmbH [de].<ref name="cast:sd">{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/babylon-berlin-star-liv-lisa-fries-to-lead-from-hilde-with-love-for-beta-cinema-exclusive/5170939.article |title='Babylon Berlin' star Liv Lisa Fries to lead 'From Hilde With Love' for Beta Cinema (exclusive)|first= Geoffrey|last= Macnab |date=21 May 2022 |access-date=27 January 2024 |website=[[ScreenDaily]]|language=en}}</ref>

Principal photography began on 22 August 2022 on locations in Berlin, [[Potsdam]], [[Groß Köris]], [[Ketzin|Ketzin-Schmergow]], and Krahnepuhl.<ref name="lead:filmportal"/> Filming ended on 6 October 2022 with filming locations in the regions of Berlin and [[Brandenburg]].<ref name="ten:crew"/>

''From Hilde, with Love'' had its world premiere on 17 February 2024, as part of the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]], in Competition.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2024/01/berlin-film-festival-2024-competition-lineup-rooney-mara-1235800214/ |title=Berlin Reveals 2024 Competition Lineup: Rooney Mara, Mati Diop, Isabelle Huppert, Abderrahmane Sissako Movies Among Selection|website=[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]] |first=Zac|last=Ntim|date=22 January 2024|access-date=27 January 2024}}</ref><ref name="biff:hr">{{Cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/berlinale-2024-full-lineup-1235794083/ |title= Rooney Mara, Isabelle Huppert, Gael Garcia Bernal Films Set for 2024 Berlinale|author= Scott Roxborough |website= Hollywood Reporter |date= 22 January 2024 |access-date= 29 January 2024|lang=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.berlinale.de/en/2024/programme/202415117.html#screening_dates |title=From Hilde, With Love |work=Berlinale |date=6 February 2024 |access-date=6 February 2024}}</ref>

[[Beta Film|Beta Cinema GmbH]] has international rights to the film.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.betacinema.com/138/pid/320/From-Hilde,-with-Love.htm |title=From Hilde, With Love|access-date=27 January 2024 |website= Beta Cinema|language=en}}</ref> In March, [[Picturehouse (company)|Picturehouse]] took the distribution rights of the film.<ref name="rel:sd">{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/picturehouse-swoops-for-berlin-competition-title-from-hilde-with-love-exclusive/5191185.article |title=Picturehouse swoops for Berlin competition title ‘From Hilde, With Love’ (exclusive) |first= Mona |last= Tabbara |date=4 March 2023 |access-date=5 March 2023 |website=[[ScreenDaily]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Reception==
''From Hilde with Love'' grossed $696,618 at the box office, worldwide.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt20420646/?ref_=bo_se_r_1|title=From Hilde With Love|website=Box Office Mojo|access-date=18 July 2025}}</ref>

On the review aggregator [[Rotten Tomatoes]] website, the film has an approval rating of 80% based on 10 reviews, with an average rating of 6/10.<ref name="score:rotten">{{cite Rotten Tomatoes |title= From Hilde, with Love (2024, Drama/History) |id= from_hilde_with_love |type=m |access-date=17 February 2025}}</ref>

Leslie Felperin reviewing the film for ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' termed it as "Top-drawer humanist cinema," and opined, "From Hilde feels tidy and complete, speaking like its heroine with a soft still voice about the importance of love, and the deep need to do something right, no matter how small, to defy corrupt, monstrous regimes."<ref name="rev:hr">{{Cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/from-hilde-with-love-review-1235827568/ |title= ‘From Hilde, With Love’ Review: Stirring German Drama Spotlights a Heroine of the Anti-Nazi Resistance |author= Leslie Felperin |website= The Hollywood Reporter |date=17 February 2024 |access-date=17 February 2024|lang=en}}</ref>

Catherine Bray writing in ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' praised Liv Lisa Fries, for her "subtle but layered performance." Bray mentioned [[Judith Kaufmann]] writing, "his cinematography, leaning into the sylvan charms of the Red Orchestra’s hangout, underscoring the brutality of the prison environment to which the film constantly returns." Bray praised Jörg Hauschild for doing "a good job balancing the edit between these two kinds of scene, with the prison narrative proceeding in a linear fashion to its grim conclusion, while the vignettes from prior to Hilde’s arrest jump forwards and backwards in time." Concluding her review she opined, "''From Hilde, with Love'' might be set almost eighty years ago, but the themes explored seem destined to remain eternally urgent and relevant."<ref name="rev:variety">{{Cite news|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/from-hilde-with-love-review-1235914701/ |title=‘From Hilde, With Love’ Review: Andreas Dresen Returns With an Affecting Romance Set in 1942 Germany |author= Catherine Bray |work= [[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|date= 17 February 2024 |access-date= 18 February 2024 |language=en}}</ref>

Lee Marshall reviewing for [[ScreenDaily]] at Berlinale wrote, "The story of the German resistance to the Nazi regime has been told before in books, documentaries and fictional features, but rarely so freshly and movingly as in this account..." Praising performance Marshall wrote, "[[Liv Lisa Fries]] gives a compelling performance as a woman who at first, the film suggests, was something of an accidental revolutionary, brought into the anti-Nazi resistance through her love for the man who in 1941 would become her husband." Concluding Marshall said, "... in its final act, the film builds to a dramatic, moving crescendo, it does so not by hammering away at the injustice of Hilde’s fate – that’s a given – but by celebrating the small joys of parenthood, the touch of a lover’s skin and the adrenalin of a common cause."<ref name="rev:sd">{{cite web |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/from-hilde-with-love-review/5190711.article |title=‘From Hilde, With Love’: Review |first=Lee |last= Marshall |date=17 February 2024 |access-date=18 February 2024 |website=[[ScreenDaily]]|language=en}}</ref>

Nicholas Bell in Ion Cinema rated the film with two stars and said, "In our attempts to never forget the atrocities of the Holocaust, should we not make more of a collective effort to steer clear of making forgettable films?"<ref name="rev:ionc">{{Cite news|url=https://www.ioncinema.com/reviews/dresen-from-hilde-with-love-review |title=Germany, Pale Mother: Dresen Locates the Good Germans of WWII|author= Nicholas Bell |work= Ion Cinema |date= 18 February 2024 |access-date= 20 February 2024 |language=en}}</ref>
