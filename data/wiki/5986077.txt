'''''The Bostonians''''' is a 1984 [[historical romance]] [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]] directed by [[James Ivory]]. The screenplay by [[Ruth Prawer Jhabvala]] is based on the 1886 American novel ''[[The Bostonians]]'' by [[Henry James]]. The film stars [[Vanessa Redgrave]], [[Christopher Reeve]], [[Madeleine Potter]], and [[Jessica Tandy]].

''The Bostonians'' was released in the United States on 2 August 1984.<ref name=Maslin-NYT>{{cite news|last=Maslin|first=Janet|authorlink=Janet Maslin|title=The Screen: Redgrave in Jame's 'Bostonians'|newspaper=[[The New York Times]]|date=August 2, 1984|page=15}}</ref> The film received respectable reviews and nominations by the [[Golden Globe Awards]], [[Academy Awards]], [[British Academy Film Awards]], and won [[IFFI Best Film Award|Golden Peacock (Best Film)]] at the [[10th International Film Festival of India]].

==Plot==
Olive Chancellor, a [[Back Bay]] Boston spinster and leader of the [[Women's suffrage|women's suffrage movement]], becomes enamored of Verena Tarrant, an inspirational young speaker, and adopts Verena as her protégée, her friend, and her companion. When Olive's distant relation, the [[Chauvinism|chauvinist]] Southern lawyer Basil Ransom, falls in love with Verena and wishes to marry her — to relegate the young woman to the kitchen and the nursery — Olive and Ransom find themselves competing for Verena's affections. The charismatic Miss Tarrant must then choose whether to get herself to the nunnery of Olive's social cause or submit to the sensual but subservient life promised by Ransom.<ref>{{cite web|title=The Bostonians|url=http://merchantivory.com/bostonians.html|website=[[Merchant Ivory Productions]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20000903014950/http://www.merchantivory.com/bostonians.html|archive-date=September 3, 2000}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
On [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film has an 81% approval rating, based on 16 reviews, with an average rating of 6.90/10.<ref name=rotten>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/bostonians/|title=The Bostonians|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|accessdate=20 September 2023}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], ''The Bostonians'' has a score of 59 out of a 100, based on 10 critics, indicating "mixed or average reviews".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-bostonians|title=The Bostonians (1984)|website=[[Metacritic]]|accessdate=21 August 2021}}</ref>

[[Roger Ebert]] praised the film, giving it 3 out of 4 stars and observing:
<blockquote>
Intelligent and subtle and open to the underlying tragedy of a woman who does not know what she wants, a man who does not care what he wants, and a girl who does not need what she wants.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Ebert|first1=Roger|authorlink=Roger Ebert|url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/the-bostonians-1984|title=The Bostonians|date=1 January 1984|accessdate=21 August 2021}}</ref>
</blockquote>
