'''''Walk Up''''' ({{Korean/auto|hangul=^탑|rr=yes|lit=Tower}})<ref>{{Cite web |last=Garcia Mino |first=David |date=2022-09-23 |title="Walk Up" (2022): con una copa de vino |url=https://cintilatio.com/critica-walk-up/ |access-date=2022-09-30 |website=Cintilatio |language=es}}</ref> is a 2022 South Korean drama<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fulvi |first=Giovanna |title=Walk Up |url=https://tiff.net/events/walk-up |access-date=2022-09-30 |website=TIFF |language=en}}</ref> film written, directed, produced, photographed, scored and edited by [[Hong Sang-soo]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Romney |first=Jonathan |date=2022-09-22 |title='Walk Up': San Sebastian Review |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/walk-up-san-sebastian-review/5174557.article |access-date=2022-09-29 |website=[[Screendaily]] |language=en}}</ref>

== Plot ==
''Walk Up'' follows filmmaker Byung-soo and his estranged daughter Jeong-su as they visit a building owned by Ms. Kim, share a meal, and meet others who live in the building. The same event is seemingly repeated several times in different variations. Byung-soo later begins a relationship with a woman in the building and moves into one of the apartments.

== Production ==
''Walk Up'' was filmed in black and white<ref>{{Cite web |last=Daniels |first=Robert |date=2022-09-16 |title=TIFF 2022: Prisoner's Daughter, What's Love Got to Do with It, Walk Up |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/tiff-2022-prisoners-daughter-whats-love-got-to-do-with-it-walk-up |access-date=2022-09-30 |website=[[RogerEbert.com|Roger Ebert]] |language=en}}</ref> in Seoul in 2021.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Song |first=Seung-hyun |date=2022-08-03 |title=Hong Sang-soo's 'Walk Up' invited to two overseas film fests |url=https://www.koreaherald.com/view.php?ud=20220803000743 |access-date=2022-09-29 |website=[[The Korea Herald]] |language=en}}</ref> It was written, directed, edited, and produced by Hong, with music<ref name=":2" /> and cinematography by Hong.<ref name=":0" /> The production company was Jeonwonsa Film Company.<ref name=":2" /> [[Kim Min-hee (actress, born 1982)|Kim Min-hee]] served as production manager.<ref>{{Cite web |last=강 |first=민경 |date=2022-08-03 |script-title=ko:[공식] 홍상수 감독♥김민희 제작 실장 '탑', 토론토·산세바스티안 영화제 초청 |url=https://www.hankyung.com/entertainment/article/2022080333474 |access-date=2022-10-13 |website=[[Korea Economic Daily|한경닷컴]] |language=ko}}</ref>

== Release ==
''Walk Up'' premiered at the [[2022 Toronto International Film Festival]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hudson |first=David |date=2022-07-29 |title=Toronto and More Fall Lineups |url=https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/7884-toronto-and-more-fall-lineups |access-date=2022-09-29 |website=[[The Criterion Collection]] |language=en}}</ref> where it screened out of competition. It was also shown in competition at the [[70th San Sebastián International Film Festival|70th San Sebastian International Film Festival]] on September 22, 2022<ref name=":2" /> and premiered in the United States at the [[2022 New York Film Festival]].<ref name=":3">{{Cite magazine |last=Brody |first=Richard |date=2022-09-29 |title=Three Highlights from the First Week of the 2022 New York Film Festival |url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/the-front-row/three-highlights-from-the-first-week-of-the-2022-new-york-film-festival |access-date=2022-09-30 |magazine=[[The New Yorker]] |language=en-US}}</ref> ''Walk Up'' premiered in theaters in South Korea on November 3, 2022<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kim |first=Eun-jung |date=2022-10-28 |title=(Movie Review) In 'Walk Up', Hong Sang-soo unfolds metaphors of life in four-story building |url=https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20221028003300315 |access-date=2022-11-10 |website=[[Yonhap News Agency]] |language=en}}</ref> and in the United States on March 24, 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bramesco |first=Charles |date=2023-03-01 |title=Hong Sang-soo gets lost in the funhouse in the first Walk Up trailer |url=https://lwlies.com/articles/walk-up-trailer-hong-sang-soo/ |access-date=2023-04-25 |website=[[Little White Lies (magazine)|Little White Lies]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Brody |first=Richard |author-link=Richard Brody |date=2023-03-23 |title=Hong Sangsoo's "Walk Up" Signals a Break from Routine |url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/the-front-row/hong-sangsoos-walk-up-signals-a-break-from-routine |access-date=2023-04-25 |magazine=[[The New Yorker]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film is distributed internationally by Finecut<ref name=":0" /> and in North America by [[The Cinema Guild|Cinema Guild]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Brzeski |first=Patrick |date=2022-08-09 |title=Cinema Guild Takes North American Rights to Hong Sang-soo's 'Walk Up' |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/cinema-guild-hong-sang-soos-walk-up-1235195259/ |access-date=2022-09-29 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Reception ==

=== Critical response ===
''Walk Up'' has received positive reviews from critics, holding a 97% positive rating on review aggregator [[Rotten Tomatoes]] based on 29 critics, with an average score of 8 out of 10,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Walk Up |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/walk_up |access-date=2023-08-28 |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]}}</ref> and a score of 86 on [[Metacritic]], based on nine reviews, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Walk Up |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/walk-up |access-date=2022-10-04 |website=[[Metacritic]]}}</ref> Critics praised the film for being reflective<ref name=":3" /><ref name=":1" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-10-02 |title='Walk Up' Review: Hong Sangsoo Delivers A Delicate Portrait Of Age & Artistic Pursuit [NYFF] |url=https://theplaylist.net/walk-up-review-hong-sangsoo-delivers-a-delicate-portrait-of-age-artistic-pursuit-nyff-20221002/ |access-date=2022-10-04 |website=The Playlist}}</ref> and complex,<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Garcia |first=Lawrence |date=2022-10-01 |title=Walk Up — Hong Sang-soo [NYFF '22 Review] |url=https://inreviewonline.com/2022/10/01/walk-up/ |access-date=2022-10-04 |website=In Review Online |language=en-US}}</ref> and noted strengths in its acting,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Vestby |first=Ethan |date=2022-09-18 |title=TIFF Review: Hong Sangsoo's Walk Up is Relaxing in its Languid Rhythms |url=https://thefilmstage.com/tiff-review-hong-sangsoos-walk-up-is-relaxing-in-its-languid-rhythms/ |access-date=2022-09-30 |website=The Film Stage |language=en-US}}</ref> music, and tone.<ref name=":2" />
