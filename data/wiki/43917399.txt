'''''One Step Away''''' ({{lang-zh|t=觸不可及}}) is a 2014 Chinese [[thriller (genre)#Suspense|suspense]] [[romance film]] directed by Zhao Baogang. It was released on September 19, 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url= http://english.entgroup.cn/618096/fullcredits/|title= One Step Away (2014) Cast and Crew|accessdate= September 22, 2014|work= english.entgroup.cn|publisher= EntGroup Inc}}</ref>
