'''''The Old Oak''''' is a 2023 [[Drama (film and television)|drama]] film directed by [[Ken Loach]] and written by [[Paul Laverty]]. It follows TJ (Dave Turner), the owner of a small pub, who forms a bond with Syrian refugee Yara (Ebla Mari). It is a co-production between the United Kingdom, France and Belgium.<ref name="unifrance"/>

The film had its world premiere at the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival]] on 26 May, where it competed for the [[Palme d'Or]]. It was released in the United Kingdom by [[StudioCanal UK|StudioCanal]] on 29 September. At the [[77th British Academy Film Awards]], it was nominated for [[BAFTA Award for Outstanding British Film|Outstanding British Film]].

==Premise==
Pub landlord TJ Ballantyne, living in a previously thriving mining community in [[County Durham]], struggles to hold onto his pub and keep it as the one remaining public space where people can meet in the town. Meanwhile, tensions rise when Syrian refugees are placed there, but Ballantyne strikes up a friendship with one of the refugees, Yara.<ref name="screendaily"/><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/what-to-do-in-paris/cinema-series/articles/292188-the-old-oak-by-ken-loach-in-competition-at-the-cannes-film-festival-2023|access-date=24 April 2023|title=The Old Oak by Ken Loach: in competition at the Cannes Film Festival 2023|first=Julie|last=M|date=19 April 2023|website=Sortiraparis.com}}</ref>

==Production==
The film was set and shot in the northeast of England. Loach, who turned 87 prior to its release, told ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' it would "probably" be his last film.<ref>{{Cite web|website=The Hollywood Reporter|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/ken-loach-final-film-the-old-oak-retirement-1235399975|first=Alex|last=Ritman|date=24 April 2023|access-date=24 April 2023|title=Ken Loach Says 'The Old Oak' Likely to be His Final Feature Film (Exclusive)}}</ref> Production companies on the film include UK's Sixteen Films, [[StudioCanal|StudioCanal UK]] and [[BBC Film]], France's [[Why Not Productions]], and Belgium's [[Les Films du Fleuve]].<ref name="cineuropa"/> The script was written by [[Paul Laverty]]. [[Rebecca O'Brien]] produced the film.<ref>{{Cite web|website=Deadline Hollywood|access-date=24 April 2023|title=Ken Loach's 'The Old Oak' Begins Shooting In UK; Studiocanal, Sixteen Films And Why Not Productions Attached To Syrian Refugee Drama Set In North England|first=Jesse|last=Whittock|date=16 May 2022|url=https://deadline.com/2022/05/ken-loachs-the-old-oak-begins-shooting-in-uk-studiocanal-uk-sixteen-films-and-why-not-productions-syrian-refugee-drama-1235024582|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220516115844/https://deadline.com/2022/05/ken-loachs-the-old-oak-begins-shooting-in-uk-studiocanal-uk-sixteen-films-and-why-not-productions-syrian-refugee-drama-1235024582/|url-status=dead|archive-date=16 May 2022}}</ref> The film was scored by [[George Fenton]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://filmmusicreporter.com/2023/04/24/george-fenton-scoring-ken-loachs-the-old-oak/|website=Film Music Reporter|access-date=24 April 2023|title=George Fenton scoring Ken Loach's The Old Oak|date=24 April 2023}}</ref>

===Filming===
Filming began in May 2022,<ref>{{Cite web|title=Ken Loach just started shooting a new film called 'The Old Oak'|first=Phil|last=de Semlyen|date=16 May 2022|access-date=24 April 2023|url=https://www.timeout.com/news/ken-loach-just-started-shooting-a-new-film-called-the-old-oak-051622|website=Time Out}}</ref> and took place over six weeks. Locations used in [[County Durham]] included [[Murton, County Durham|Murton]], [[Horden]], [[Easington, County Durham|Easington]] and [[Durham Cathedral]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Ken Loach inspired by the North East for film The Old Oak|first=Sharuna|last=Sagar|website=BBC Look North|date=8 November 2022|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-tyne-63536292|access-date=24 April 2023}}</ref><ref name=durdio-20231019>{{cite web |url=https://durhamdiocese.org/the-old-oak--an-important-story-for-our-times.php |title=The Old Oak - an important story for our times! |last=Barron |first=Val |website=Diocese of Durham |date=19 October 2023 |access-date=6 December 2023}}</ref> The pub in Murton that became The Old Oak during filming was a disused pub previously known as The Victoria.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.chroniclelive.co.uk/whats-on/film-news/gallery/pictures-set-ken-loach-during-24004812|website=Chroniclelive|access-date=24 April 2023|first1=Barbra|last1=Hodgson|first2=Craig|last2=Connor|title=Pictures on set with Ken Loach during filming of The Old Oak in County Durham|date=19 May 2022}}</ref>{{coord|54.815141|-1.386338|name=The Old Oak, actually The Victoria, Murton, County Durham|type:landmark_region:GB-DUR|display=title}}

==Release==
The film premiered in main competition at the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival]] on 26 May 2023.<ref name="farout">{{Cite web|first=Jonathan|last=Potter|url=https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/ken-loach-movie-announced-cannes-2023/|date=13 April 2023|access-date=24 April 2023|title=Cannes 2023: New Ken Loach movie 'The Old Oak' announced to compete|website=Far Out Magazine}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=mraultpauillac |date=10 May 2023 |title=The Screenings Guide of the 76th Festival de Cannes |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2023/the-screenings-guide-of-the-76th-festival-de-cannes/ |access-date=10 May 2023 |website=Festival de Cannes |language=en-US}}</ref> It was also invited at the [[28th Busan International Film Festival]] in 'Icon' section and was screened on 5 October 2023.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.biff.kr/eng/html/program/prog_all_list.asp?allYear=2023 |title= The 28th Busan International Film Festival: Selection List |website=Busan International Film Festival|date=September 5, 2023|access-date=September 11, 2023|language=en}}</ref>

[[StudioCanal UK]] released the film in the United Kingdom and Ireland on 29 September 2023,<ref name="screendaily"/><ref>{{cite web|title='The Old Oak' Trailer: Ken Loach's Potential 'Last Film' Set For September Release in UK & Ireland Following Cannes World Premiere|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first=Melanie|last=Goodfellow|date=4 July 2023|access-date=4 July 2023|url=https://deadline.com/video/the-old-oak-trailer-ken-loach-cannes/}}</ref> and [[Le Pacte]] released the film in France on 25 October 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.boxofficepro.fr/les-line-ups-sajustent-chez-les-distributeurs-3/|title=Les line-ups s'ajustent chez les distributeurs|date=20 June 2023|website=[[Boxoffice Pro]]|language=fr|access-date=13 July 2023}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical response===
On [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 81% based on 80 reviews, with an average rating of 7/10. The site's critics consensus read: "Imbued with the fiercely humanistic spirit that has defined Ken Loach's filmography, ''The Old Oak'' serves as a fitting - albeit somewhat sentimental - finale to a remarkable career."<ref>{{cite Rotten Tomatoes|title=The Old Oak|type=m|id=the_old_oak|access-date=8 June 2024}}</ref> On ''[[Metacritic]]'', the film has a score of 69 out of 100, based on 23 critics, indicating "generally favourable" reviews.<ref>{{cite Metacritic|id=the-old-oak|type=movie|title=The Old Oak|access-date=8 June 2024}}</ref>

For [[Variety (magazine)|Variety]], Owen Gleiberman wrote the film "Shines a Vital Light on Working-Class British Racism Until It Succumbs to Soft-Hearted Wish-Fulfillment. Dave Turner gives a lived-in performance as a pub proprietor trying to do the right thing, but Loach's latest look at working-class despair turns into too much of a progressive fairy tale."<ref>https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/the-old-oak-review-ken-loach-cannes-film-festival-1235626246/</ref>

In ''[[The Guardian]]'', Peter Bradshaw named it as one of his films of the year, and the performance of Ebla Mari as one of the best in a supporting role on film in 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/dec/22/braddies-peter-bradshaw-film-picks-of-the-year|website=The Guardian|date=22 December 2023|title= And the 2023 Braddies go to … Peter Bradshaw's film picks of the year|first=Peter|last=Bradshaw|access-date=22 December 2023}}</ref> ''[[The Observer]]'''s [[Duncan Campbell (journalist, born 1944)|Duncan Campbell]] called the film - Loach's last - "a stirring and fitting farewell to a remarkable, brave and uncompromising career."<ref>{{cite web|website=The Observer|last=Campbell|first=Duncan|title=‘My short term memory is fading and my eyesight is not what it was’: Ken Loach takes his last film to Cannes|date=14 May 2023|access-date=19 May 2025|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/may/14/ken-loach-last-film-the-old-oak-syrian-refugees}}</ref>
