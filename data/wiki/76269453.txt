'''''Nouvelle Vague''''' is a 2025 [[Comedy drama|comedy-drama film]] directed by [[Richard Linklater]]. Starring Guillaume Marbeck as [[Jean-Luc Godard]], [[Zoey Deutch]] as [[Jean Seberg]], and Aubry Dullin as [[Jean-Paul Belmondo]], it follows the shooting of ''[[Breathless (1960 film)|Breathless]]'' (1960), one of the first feature films of the [[French New Wave]] of cinema.

''Nouvelle Vague'' had its premiere at the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival]] on 17 May 2025. It was theatrically released in France by ARP Sélection on 8 October and received a [[limited theatrical release]] in the United States on 31 October, before streaming on [[Netflix]] on 14 November.

At the [[51st César Awards]], Linklater became the second American filmmaker to win [[César Award for Best Director|Best Director]]. The film was also nominated for [[Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy|Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy]] at the [[83rd Golden Globe Awards]], while Deutch was nominated for [[Independent Spirit Award for Best Supporting Performance|Best Supporting Performance]] at the [[41st Independent Spirit Awards]].

==Plot==
<!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summaries should be 400 to 700 words. -->
In 1959, [[Jean-Luc Godard]], [[François Truffaut]] and [[Claude Chabrol]]—film critics for ''[[Cahiers du Cinéma]]''—along with [[Suzanne Schiffman]] attend the film premiere of ''La Passe du diable'', produced by [[Georges de Beauregard]]. At the afterparty, Godard harshly criticizes the film and pledges to become a film director, as his other colleagues have. Godard attends the [[1959 Cannes Film Festival|Cannes Film Festival]] where Truffaut's film ''[[The 400 Blows]]'' becomes a resounding success, paving the way for aspiring young filmmakers. 

Godard decides to direct a film based on Truffaut's short outline, inspired by Michel Portail, a petty criminal who had stolen a car, and his American journalist girlfriend Beverly Lynette. Meanwhile, Godard casts [[Jean-Paul Belmondo]], who has completed his military service, for the lead role as Michel Poiccard. Back at the office of ''Cahiers du Cinéma'', Italian director [[Roberto Rossellini]] encourages the staff to avoid artistic artifice and shoot efficiently.  

Godard hires Pierre Rissient as the film's assistant director, and receives advice from [[Jean-Pierre Melville]]. Elsewhere, [[Jean Seberg]] promotes her latest film ''[[Bonjour Tristesse (1958 film)|Bonjour Tristesse]]''. Godard meets with Seberg through her husband François Moreuil, and decides to cast her as Patricia. Godard next hires cameraman [[Raoul Coutard]] and expresses his creative vision, desiring a guerilla filmmaking style and to shoot in the [[Academy ratio]]. 

On the first day of production, Godard films only a single scene. As production continues, Godard's continual script rewrites, continued emphasis on spontaneity, disregard for continuity, and the exceptionally short shooting days bewilders the cast and crew members. As Godard shoots scenes between her and Belmondo, Seberg becomes frustrated by Godard's directorial style and briefly considers leaving the production. Belmondo is told his career will be ruined.

After a week of filming, Godard converses with Truffaut and Schiffman over drinks, where he is eager to cast Truffaut as an informant in the film. By the second week, Godard phones Coutard that he has suspended filming for the day, claiming he is not feeling well. Shortly after, Beauregard confronts Godard, angered that production has fallen behind schedule and threatens to cancel the film. Under pressure, Godard expedites the production and films further scenes between Seberg and Belmondo. 

During the middle of the second week, Godard casts himself as the informant after Truffaut has declined the role. He subsequently films a cameo of Jean-Pierre Melville near an airport. Later, Godard films the scene where Belmondo's character dies on the street. He and Seberg have a brief disagreement when Godard wants Seberg's character Patricia to search through the dying Michel's pockets, but Seberg refuses to play the scene that way. Instead, Seberg's Patricia questions what "dégueulasse" (disgusting) means. When Seberg finishes her last scene, she is relieved and heads back to Hollywood to film her next film, ''[[Let No Man Write My Epitaph]]''. After 23 days of filming, Godard wraps the production. 

During post-production, Godard urges editor [[Cécile Decugis]] to make [[jump cut]]s, much to her disagreement. Godard then screens his film ''Breathless'' for Truffaut, Chabrol, Schiffman, and Beauregard who ironically say the film is terrible before giving Godard a round of applause. An epilogue states that ''Breathless'' became one of the most influential films ever made, Belmondo became an international star, and Seberg divorced her husband, appeared in 35 films, and died at the age of 40. Godard's filmmaking career continued for more than 60 years.

At various points, the film employs tableaux to identify and pay tribute to notable figures of the period, including filmmakers, critics, and regular contributors to ''Cahiers du Cinéma''.<ref name="Blake">{{cite magazine |last=Blake |first=Jason |date=January–February 2026 |title=Nouvelle Vague |magazine=[[Limelight (magazine)|Limelight]] |location=Sydney |page=78 |issn=1448-2800}}</ref><ref name="Romney">{{cite magazine |last=Romney |first=Jonathan |date=Winter 2025–26 |title=Richard Linklater's tour of the nouvelle vague: A band apart |magazine=[[Sight and Sound]] |location=London |volume=36 |issue=1 |pages=94–98 |issn=0037-4806}}</ref>

==Production==
In October 2023, Linklater revealed his plans to shoot a film in France about the [[French New Wave]] movement.<ref>{{cite web |accessdate=5 March 2024 |date=October 11, 2023 |first=Rodrigo |last=Perez |title=Richard Linklater Says He's Shooting "Secret" Project With Ethan Hawke & French New Wave Film Shot In Paris |url=https://theplaylist.net/richard-linklater-says-hes-shooting-secret-project-with-ethan-hawke-french-new-wave-film-shot-in-paris-20231011/ |website=The Playlist}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.interviewmagazine.com/film/youre-a-freak-too-gregg-araki-in-conversation-with-richard-linklater|website=Interview Magazine|accessdate=5 March 2024|title= "You're a Freak, Too": Gregg Araki, in Conversation with Richard Linklater|first=Eloise|last=King-Clements|date=October 10, 2023}}</ref> The original script was written by Holly Gent and Vincent Palmo, Jr., and then it was translated to French with dialogue by Michèle Halberstadt and Laetitia Masson.<ref name="Brody&quot;">{{cite magazine |last=Brody |first=Richard |author-link=Richard Brody |date=10 October 2025 |title=For Art's Sake |url=https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2025/10/20/blue-moon-movie-review-nouvelle-vague |url-access=subscription |department=The Current Cinema |magazine=[[The New Yorker]] |volume=101 |issue=32 |pages=68–69 |issn=0028-792X}} (Published online under the title "Art and Life in Richard Linklater's 'Blue Moon' and 'Nouvelle Vague'").</ref> 

The film is Linklater's first project shot entirely in French,<ref name="Tuillier">{{cite web |last=Tuillier |first=Laura |date=8 October 2025 |title=«Nouvelle Vague» Atout de souffle |url=https://www.liberation.fr/culture/cinema/dans-nouvelle-vague-richard-linklater-rejoue-la-legende-du-jeune-godard-20251007_Z6J6XATIEBCATDRWTPSGMMLOEY |url-access=subscription |website=[[Libération]] |language=fr |location=Paris |issue=13757 |page=20 |access-date=10 February 2026}} (Published online a day earlier under the title "Dans «Nouvelle Vague», Richard Linklater rejoue la légende du jeune Godard").</ref> and it was shot by [[David Chambille]] in black and white on [[35 mm movie film|35 mm film]], using the [[Academy ratio]] (1.37:1). In post-production, digital grain, gate weave, and fine scratches were added to evoke the patina of a well-worn archival print.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Frank |first=Alex |date=14 November 2025 |title=Behind the Scenes of ''Nouvelle Vague'': Capturing the Spirit of 1959 Paris |url=https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/nouvelle-vague-behind-the-scenes |access-date=3 April 2026 |website=[[Tudum]] by [[Netflix]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Tsirbas |first=Christos |date=2025-10-31 |title=Linklater’s ‘Nouvelle Vague’ Is More than an Inside Joke about Godard’s ‘Breathless’ |url=https://www.thegrindmag.ca/linklaters-nouvelle-vague-is-more-than-an-inside-joke-about-godards-breathless/ |access-date=2026-04-03 |website=The Grind Magazine |language=en-US}}</ref>

Filming began in Paris on 4 March 2024, and concluded in April 2024.<ref>{{cite news|last=Morán|first=David|date=26 April 2024|url=https://www.abc.es/play/cine/richard-linklater-cine-ahi-vender-palomitas-busca-20240426204640-nt.html|title=Richard Linklater: "El cine estaba ahí para vender palomitas; ahora lo que busca es nuestros datos"|work=ABC|language=es|access-date=9 May 2024}}</ref> The film received an {{ill|avance sur recettes|fr|italic=y}} (advance on takings) grant of France's [[Centre national du cinéma et de l'image animée]] (CNC).<ref>{{cite news|last=Geisinger|first=Gabriella|date=18 March 2024|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/richard-linklaters-nouvelle-vague-among-first-2024-recipients-of-cnc-funding/5191680.article|title=Richard Linklater's 'Nouvelle Vague' among first 2024 recipients of CNC funding|work=Screen Daily|access-date=18 March 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cnc.fr/professionnels/aides-et-financements/resultats-commissions/avance-sur-recettes-avant-realisation--resultats-de-la-commission-du-29-fevrier-2024_2150656|title=Avance sur recettes avant réalisation : résultats de la commission du 29 février 2024|language=fr|publisher=Centre national du cinéma et de l'image animée|date=29 February 2024|access-date=18 March 2024}}</ref>

==Release==
[[File:Nouvelle Vague (2025)-69752.jpg|thumb|Guillaume Marbeck, [[Zoey Deutch]] and [[Richard Linklater]] during the film's premiere at the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival]]]]
In April 2025, ''Nouvelle Vague'' was selected to compete for the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2025 Cannes Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 17 May 2025 and received an 11-minute standing ovation.<ref>{{cite web|title=Cannes Film Festival Unveils 2025 Lineup|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first1=Elsa|last1=Keslassy|first2=Ellise|last2=Shafer|date=10 April 2025|access-date=10 April 2025|url=https://variety.com/2025/film/festivals/cannes-film-festival-2025-lineup-1236364723/}}</ref><ref name="Screenings Guide">{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2025/the-screenings-guide-of-the-78th-festival-de-cannes/|title=The Screenings Guide of the 78th Festival de Cannes|date=8 May 2025|website=Festival de Cannes|access-date=8 May 2025}}</ref>

Following its Cannes premiere, [[Netflix]] bought U.S. distribution rights for $4 million after a heated bidding war; this sum is believed to be the second highest ever paid for a domestic deal for a foreign language film only behind ''[[Emilia Pérez]]'', also purchased at Cannes by Netflix. While release plans were not disclosed, the film was expected to be in theaters for only two weeks in an awards-qualifying run before streaming on Netflix.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=D'Alessandro |first1=Anthony |last2=Wiseman |first2=Andreas |date=2025-05-26 |title=Netflix Buys Richard Linklater's 'Breathless' Homage & Love Letter To Cinema 'Nouvelle Vague' In Record Domestic Deal For A French-Language Movie |url=https://deadline.com/2025/05/netflix-buys-richard-linklater-movie-nouvelle-vague-1236407181/ |access-date=2025-05-26 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref>

It was also screened in the Best of 2025 section of the [[20th Rome Film Festival]]<ref>{{Cite web |date=19 September 2025 |title=Festa del Cinema di Roma 2025, il programma completo (diretta) |trans-title=Rome Film Fest 2025: Full program (live) |url=https://www.ciakmagazine.it/news/festa-del-cienma-di-roma-2025-il-programma-completo-diretta/ |access-date=19 September 2025 |website=[[Ciak Magazine]] |language=it}}</ref> and as a Headline Gala at the [[2025 BFI London Film Festival|69th BFI London Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tabbara |first=Mona |title=BFI London Film Festival unveils 2025 line-up |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/bfi-london-film-festival-unveils-2025-line-up/5208456.article |access-date=2025-11-08 |website=Screen |language=en}}</ref>

It was theatrically released in France by ARP Sélection on 8 October 2025. In the United States, it was released in select theaters on 31 October 2025, before streaming on Netflix on 14 November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/netflix-nouvelle-vague-awards-release-date-categories-1236333826/|title=Netflix Sets 'Nouvelle Vague' Awards Season Release Dates — Theatrical and Streaming — and Categories (Exclusive)|first=Scott|last=Feinberg|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|date=31 July 2025|access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

== Reception ==
On the [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], 92% of 193 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 7.6/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "Seamlessly recreating one of cinema's most groundbreaking productions, ''Nouvelle Vague'' doesn't reinvent the medium the way its subjects did, but it pays tribute to their accomplishment with infectious admiration."<ref>{{cite web |title=''Nouvelle Vague'' |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/nouvelle_vague_2025 |access-date=June 6, 2026 |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]] |publisher=[[Fandango Media]]}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 76 out of 100, based on 42 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Nouvelle Vague'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/nouvelle-vague |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=June 6, 2026 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

Lee Marshall of ''[[ScreenDaily]]'' described the film as "a nostalgic tribute to a time and place of extraordinary creative ferment".<ref>{{Cite web|website=[[ScreenDaily]]|first=Lee|last=Marshall|title='Nouvelle Vague' review: Richard Linklater dramatises the making of Godard's 'Breathless'|url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/nouvelle-vague-review-richard-linklater-dramatises-the-making-of-godards-breathless/5205174.article|date=17 May 2025}}</ref>
