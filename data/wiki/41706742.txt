'''''Story of My Death''''' ({{langx|ca|Història de la meva mort}}) is a 2013 [[slow cinema]] drama film directed by [[Albert Serra]]. It won the [[Golden Leopard]] at the [[66th Locarno Film Festival]]. The film is about an aging [[Giacomo Casanova|Casanova]] and his encounter with [[Count Dracula]]. The title resembles that of Casanova's autobiography, ''[[Histoire de ma vie]]'' ("Story of my Life").

==Plot==
As a mature [[libertine]] Marquis, [[Casanova]], famous for his spectacular sexual conquests, arrives accompanied by his servant, in a peasant village within a thick forests. There within a strange and erotic atmosphere, Casanova encounters the mysterious [[Count Dracula]].

==Production==
The film was shot in seven weeks, in Romania and France, including at the Château de Bourgon in [[Montourtier]] (Mayenne), the Château d'Ansouis in [[Ansouis]] (Vaucluse) and the Château de Beauregard in [[Mons, Var|Mons]] (Var).<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lemercier |first=Fabien |date=9 December 2011 |title=Casanova meets Dracula in ''Story of My Death'' |url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/213231/ |access-date=4 August 2026 |website=[[Cineuropa]]}}</ref>

==Reception==
The film won the [[Golden Leopard]] at the [[66th Locarno Film Festival]].<ref>{{cite web |url= http://archive.pardo.ch/details.do?methodcall=filmDetails&oc=698168|title= Historia de la meva mort|work= archive.pardo.ch|accessdate= 21 January 2014}}</ref>

On [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film has an approval rating of 75% based on 12 reviews, and an average rating of 8.8/10.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/story_of_my_death |title=''Story of My Death'' (2014) |work=[[Rotten Tomatoes]] |publisher=[[Fandango Media]] |accessdate=8 October 2020}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], the film has a weighted average score of 80 out of 100, based on 6 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.metacritic.com/movie/story-of-my-death |title=''Story of My Death'' Reviews |work=[[Metacritic]] |accessdate=16 February 2018}}</ref>
