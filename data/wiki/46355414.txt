'''''Cosmos''''' is a 2015 French-Portuguese [[speculative fiction]] [[Thriller film|thriller]] film written and directed by [[Andrzej Żuławski]] based on [[Cosmos (Gombrowicz novel)|the novel of the same name]] by [[Witold Gombrowicz]].

==Production==
''Cosmos'' was Andrzej Żuławski's first film in 15 years<ref name=uph/> and ended up being his final film; he died in February 2016. It was produced through [[Paulo Branco]]'s Alfama Films in collaboration with the Portuguese company Leopardo Filmes. Principal photography took place from mid-November to late December 2014.<ref name=ce>{{Cite web|last=Lemercier|first=Fabien|date=9 January 2014|url=http://www.cineuropa.org/nw.aspx?t=newsdetail&l=en&did=283003|title=Andrzej Zulawski's ''Cosmos'' in post-production|work=Cineuropa|accessdate=9 May 2021}}</ref>

==Plot synopsis==
A young man, hoping to write a [[novel]], visits a French [[guest house]] with a friend and finds himself distracted by a mystery and the strange inhabitants of the home.<ref name=bbfc/>

==Critical reception and awards==
The film premiered in competition at the 2015 [[Locarno International Film Festival]],<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.indiewire.com/locarno-film-festival-announces-lineup-for-its-68th-year-awards-edward-norton-on-opening-night|title=Locarno Film Festival Announces Lineup for Its 68th Year, Awards Edward Norton on Opening Night |date=15 July 2015 |work=[[Indiewire]] | accessdate=9 May 2021}}</ref> where it won the award for [[Best Direction Award (Locarno International Film Festival)|Best Direction]].<ref name=uph>{{cite web|first=Meredith|last= Taylor |url=https://filmuforia.com/cosmos-2015/|title=Cosmos (2015): Best Director Locarno 2015|website=Filmuphoria|date= 15 August 2016 | access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref>

''[[Time Out (magazine)|Time Out]]'', giving it 4/5 stars, wrote that, "as with all of Zulawski’s work," the film "is essentially unclassifiable," adding that it is, "at times, astonishingly beautiful" as well as "entrancing, frustrating, and utterly singular."<ref name=time>{{cite web|date=15 August 2016|first=Tom|last=  Huddleston|url=https://www.timeout.com/movies/cosmos-1|title=Cosmos|website=[[Time Out (magazine)|Time Out]]| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref> Noting that "the enigma at the centre of Zulawski’s work is that he’s trying to film the unspeakable," ''CinemaScope''{{'}}s Christof Huber stated that the film is "hardly lacking in perversity, sex, and violence, not to mention fierce freak-out acting spectaculars" but "the manic-depressive tone of [the director's] earlier films has been replaced by a state of constant agitation." By ''Cosmos''{{'}}s ending, he concluded, "the equilibrium of existence has been...thoroughly pierced."<ref name=agit>{{cite web|first=Christof |last=Huber|url=https://cinema-scope.com/cinema-scope-online/bleurghing-the-unspeakable-a-stroll-through-andrzej-zulawskis-cosmos/|title=Bleurghing the Unspeakable: A Stroll Through Andrzej Zulawski's ''Cosmos''|date= 2015|website=CinemaScope| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref> ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' reviewer  Peter Debruge opined that the film could be described as "frustrating, yes, but boring, never," containing "enough sexual tension percolating beneath the surface to spark a second [[Big Bang]]." He added, however, that, during its length, "the film grows increasingly difficult to interpret" and that "mainstream [audiences] would sooner stick to more conventional entertainments."<ref name=debruge>{{cite web|first=Peter|last= Debruge |url=https://variety.com/2015/film/reviews/cosmos-film-review-1201569202/|title=Locarno Film Review: ''Cosmos''|date=13 August 2015|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref> In [[Roger Ebert]]'s website, critic Scout Tafoya gave 4/4 stars to a film "shaking with ecstasy, possibility and the torment of being alive." ''Cosmos'', he wrote, is "everything at once too fast, like the broken merry-go-round, sudden [[inebriation]], or a magic trick. Real life is only steps away, but you'll never find anything as brutally fantastic out there."<ref name=abs>{{cite web|first=Scout|last= Tafoya |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/cosmos-2016|title=''Cosmos''|date=17 June 2016|website=[[Roger Ebert]]| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref>

For [[A. O. Scott]] of ''[[The New York Times]]'', the film "feels more like a revival than a new release. It can inspire [the audience] to think fondly back on the work of [[Alain Resnais]]...or [[Raúl Ruiz (director)|Raúl Ruiz]]" and "leaves a curiously paradoxical [[aftertaste]]." It’s "an exercise in nostalgic [[avant garde|avant-gardism]], a [[retro]]-[[modernist]] trip down [[aesthetic]] paths that have long since been blocked off, overgrown or paved over."<ref name=ao>{{cite web|first=A. O. |last=Scott |author-link=A. O. Scott |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2016/06/17/movies/cosmos-review.html|title=''Cosmos'' Is a Madcap Plunge Into Retro Avant-Gardism|date=16 June 2016|website=[[The New York Times]]| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref>

''[[The Guardian]]''{{'}}s Mike McCahill found it to be "a characteristically eccentric outing that descends into impenetrable gibberish" with "even the sharper scenes soon clot[ting] into an impenetrable layer of gibberish [[tic]]s."<ref name=laughs>{{cite web|first=Mike |last=McCahill|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2016/aug/18/cosmos-review-andrzej-zulawski-witold-gombrowicz|title=''Cosmos'' review – absurdism without the laughs|date=18 August 2016|website=[[The Guardian]]| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref> ''Filmuphoria'''s review <ref name=uph/> pointed out that the "[[viscous]] plot and rake-thin premise" of the film are making it "impossible for any of the myriad ideas put forth to take hold with any lasting thematic coherence," and asserted that the film's "crazed [[maximalism]] and heightened [[delirium]] make [it] a dramatic exercise rather than a drama per se."<ref name=uph/>

The [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]] cited a cumulative rating of 81%, and an average rating of 7/10, based on 32 reviews.<ref name=rot>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/cosmos_2016|title=Cosmos (2016)|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]| access-date=9 May 2021}}</ref> [[Metacritic]] assigned the film a weighted average score of 72 out of 100, based on 14 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/cosmos?ftag=MCD-06-10aaa1c | title=Cosmos Reviews | website=[[Metacritic]] }}</ref>
