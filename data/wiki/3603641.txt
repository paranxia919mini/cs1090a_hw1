'''''The Day a Pig Fell Into the Well''''' ({{Korean|hangul=돼지가 우물에 빠진 날}}) is a [[1996 in film|1996]] [[Cinema of South Korea|South Korean]] [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]] and the directorial debut of [[Hong Sang-soo]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://variety.com/1997/film/reviews/the-day-a-pig-fell-into-the-well-2-1200448456/|title=The Day a Pig Fell Into the Well|last=Eisner|first=Ken|date=1997-01-13|work=Variety|access-date=2018-10-15|language=en-US}}</ref> It stars [[Bang Eun-hee]], [[Jo Eun-sook]], [[Park Jin-song]], [[Lee Eung-kyung]] and [[Kim Eui-sung]]. It was also the feature film debut of [[Song Kang-ho]]. The title derives from a 1954 book by [[John Cheever]].

Hong earned the Best Director award at the Korean [[Blue Dragon Film Awards]] for his work as well as awards at [[Rotterdam International Film Festival|Rotterdam]] and [[Vancouver International Film Festival|Vancouver]].

==Synopsis==
The plot focuses on the desires and lives of four characters in diverse circumstances: a poor novelist, a cheating wife, her germophobic husband, and a ticket girl.
