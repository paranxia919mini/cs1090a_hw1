'''''Invisible Nation''''' is a 2023 American–Taiwanese documentary film, directed and produced by Vanessa Hope. It follows the presidency of [[Tsai Ing-wen]], as she struggles to preserve [[Taiwan]]'s democracy and handle the geopolitical forces of the United States and China.

It had its world premiere at the [[Woodstock Film Festival]] on September 29, 2023, and is scheduled to be released in the United States on May 31, 2024, by Abramorama. 

==Premise==
The film explores the presidency of [[Tsai Ing-wen]], Taiwan’s first female president. It provides behind-the-scenes access to her eight-year tenure, chronicling Tsai’s efforts to safeguard Taiwan's democratic institutions and national identity in the face of authoritarian threats and escalating tensions with the People's Republic of China.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2024-05-20 |title=Taiwan: Under President Tsai Ing-wen, eight years of stability in the storm |url=https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2024/05/20/taiwan-under-president-tsai-ing-wen-eight-years-of-stability-in-the-storm_6671969_4.html |access-date=2025-05-04 |language=en}}</ref> It follows critical moments of her administration, capturing her responses to military intimidation, diplomatic isolation, and political interference from Beijing.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=TVBS |title=Documentary explores Taiwan’s fight for recognition │ TVBS World Taiwan |url=https://t.media/2854349 |access-date=2025-05-04 |website=t.media |language=en}}</ref>

Through interviews, archival footage and animated map-based graphics, the documentary delves into Taiwan’s history of colonization, transition from martial law to democracy and its current struggle for [[International recognition of Taiwan|international recognition]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Felperin |first=Leslie |date=2024-12-02 |title=Invisible Nation review – insightful primer for Taiwan’s complex history |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2024/dec/02/invisible-nation-review-useful-primer-for-taiwans-complex-history |access-date=2025-05-04 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref> The documentary also offers a close look at Tsai, with scenes from the [[Presidential Office Building, Taipei|presidential office]] offering a more intimate view of the inner workings of her administration.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite news |last=Hille |first=Kathrin |date=2024-12-04 |title=Invisible Nation review — a tour de force documentary about Taiwan |url=https://www.ft.com/content/30adb225-8698-4924-9072-fefe25d3bd67 |access-date=2025-06-11 |work=Financial Times}}</ref>

==Production==
Following the completion of ''All Eyes and Ears'', which followed the relationship between the United States and China, Vanessa Hope wanted to make a documentary revolving around Taiwan and was moved to do so after the landmark 2016 Taiwanese presidential election, remarking, "The Taiwanese people deeply inspired me."<ref name=":0" />

Hope and [[Geralyn Dreyfous]], secured initial funding through the Compton Foundation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.parkrecord.com/entertainment/slamdance-will-end-with-a-little-hope-for-democracy/|title=Slamdance will end with a little Hope for democracy|website=[[Park Record]]|first=Scott|last=Iwasaki|date=January 20, 2024|access-date=May 2, 2024}}</ref> Vanessa Hope gained access to [[Tsai Ing-Wen]] by filling out a proposal through the presidential office, which took six months for a response. Once they responded, Hope pitched the film, with Ing-Wen agreeing to film, and Hope maintaining editorial control of the project.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/news/invisible-nation-taiwan-doc-vanessa-ted-hope-1235788560/|title='Invisible Nation' Filmmakers Vanessa and Ted Hope on Importance of Taiwan Doc: 'It's 23 Million Lives and Their Democracy That's at Stake!' (EXCLUSIVE)|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Nick|last=Vivarelli|date=November 13, 2023|access-date=May 2, 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://filmmakermagazine.com/123436-interview-vanessa-hope-invisible-nation/|title="Understanding Taiwan on Its Own Terms": Vanessa Hope on Invisible Nation|website=[[Filmmaker (magazine)|Filmmaker]]|first=Lauren|last=Wissot|date=November 15, 2023|access-date=May 2, 2024}}</ref>

[[Principal photography]] began initially in May 2017, with another round of filming following the [[2019-2020 Hong Kong protests]], with Hope living in Taiwan from July 2022 to February 2023, completing post-production on the film.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thewirechina.com/2024/01/14/vanessa-hope-on-taiwan-the-invisible-nation-lai-ching-te/|title=Vanessa Hope on Taiwan, the Invisible Nation|website=[[The Wire China]]|first=Jonathan|last=S. Landreth|date=January 14, 2024|access-date=May 2, 2024}}</ref>
