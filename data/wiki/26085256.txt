'''''Mixed Blood''''' is a 1984 [[black comedy]] film directed by [[Paul Morrissey]] and [[John Leguizamo]]'s film debut.<ref name=":0">{{Cite news |last=Canby |first=Vincent |date=1985-10-18 |title=SCREEN: TOUGH COMEDY |language=en-US |work=The New York Times |url=https://www.nytimes.com/1985/10/18/movies/screen-tough-comedy.html |access-date=2022-10-01 |issn=0362-4331}}</ref>

==Plot==
Rita La Punta ([[Marília Pêra]]) leads a gang of [[Brazil]]ian [[juvenile delinquent]]s  in an attempt to seize control of [[Lower East Side|New York's Lower East Side]]'s [[illegal drug trade]] from a [[Puerto Rico|Puerto Rican]] gang.<ref name="McElhaney 1986">{{cite web | last=McElhaney | first=Joe | title=Paul's Movie: Mixed Blood | website=desistfilm | date=January 3, 1986 | url=https://desistfilm.com/pauls-movie-mixed-blood/ | access-date=August 27, 2022}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The film received generally positive reviews.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kehr |first=Dave |date=1985-10-26 |title=Mixed Blood |url=http://chicagoreader.com/film/mixed-blood-2/ |access-date=2022-10-01 |website=Chicago Reader |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Vincent Canby]] of ''[[The New York Times]]'' wrote:<blockquote>Paul Morrissey continues to be a cinema original and his <nowiki>''Mixed Blood,'' a most unorthodox look at life in the drug trade on New York'</nowiki>s [[Lower East Side]], is successively comic, brutal, primitive and sophisticated—a comedy with the manners of a live-action cartoon for jaded adults.<ref name=":0" /></blockquote>Morrisey's former collaborator [[Andy Warhol]] stated in ''[[The Andy Warhol Diaries]]'':<blockquote>Paul's movie ''Mixed Blood'' is playing midnights at the [[IFC Center|Waverly]] ... And I just loved the movie. It was everything he's done before, but it was photographed well and he seemed to know so much about the Lower East Side and the [[Alphabet City, Manhattan|Alphabet]]—avenues A, B, C, and D—for someone who hadn't been in New York for so long.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Warhol |first=Andy |url=http://archive.org/details/andywarholdiarie00warh |title=The Andy Warhol diaries |last2=Hackett |first2=Pat |date=1989 |publisher=New York, NY : Warner Books |others=The Archive of Contemporary Music |isbn=978-0-446-51426-2 |pages=706 |postscript=Entry date: Friday, January 3, 1986}}</ref></blockquote>Sid Smith wrote for the ''[[Chicago Tribune]]'':<blockquote>Although still fairly crude, the movie has more style than Morrissey's earlier pictures, and the lovely [[Salsa music|salsa]] score provides a biting undertone and subtlety Morrissey once avoided. It<nowiki>'s not a perfect picture, and sometimes it's a boring one, but ''Mixed Blood''</nowiki> is a fairly successful [[Italian neorealism|neo-realist]] look at something most moviemakers wouldn't go near.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Smith |first=Sid |date=May 18, 1986 |title='BLOOD' IS IN CLASSIC MORRISSEY VEIN |work=Chicago Tribune |url=https://www.chicagotribune.com/news/ct-xpm-1986-05-18-8602040792-story.html}}</ref></blockquote>
