'''''A Man Named John''''' ({{langx|it|E venne un uomo}}) is a [[1965 in film|1965]] [[drama film]] directed by [[Ermanno Olmi]] and starring [[Rod Steiger]].<ref name="NY Times">{{cite web |url=https://movies.nytimes.com/movie/83691/E-Venne-un-Uomo/overview |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110520123251/http://movies.nytimes.com/movie/83691/E-Venne-un-Uomo/overview |url-status=dead |archive-date=2011-05-20 |department=Movies & TV Dept. |work=[[The New York Times]] |author=Clarke Fountain |date=2011 |title=New York Times: A Man Named John |accessdate=2008-08-27}}</ref>

==Plot==

The film is a biography of [[Pope John XXIII]], who, however, does not appear in the film as an actual character. Instead [[Rod Steiger]] acts as an "intermediary", telling the Pope's life story while traveling through the places in [[Province of Bergamo|Bergamo]] where he grew up.
