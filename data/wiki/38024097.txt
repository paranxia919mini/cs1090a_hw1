'''''Jonathan''''' is a 1970 West German [[vampire film|vampire]] horror film directed by [[Hans W. Geißendörfer]]. The film stars {{Ill|Jürgen Jung|de}}, {{Ill|Hans-Dieter Jendreyko|de}}, {{Ill|Paul Albert Krumm|de}}, [[Hertha von Walther]] and {{Ill|Oskar von Schab|de}}.<ref name="Browning" />

==Synopsis==
The world of the 19th century has been taken over by [[vampire]]s who are immune to sunlight. Human rebels band together for a battle of life and the control of civilization.

==Release==
''Jonathan'' was released on 17 May 1970 in West Germany.<ref name="filmportal.de">{{cite web|url=http://www.filmportal.de/en/movie/jonathan_ea43d4a6a2295006e03053d50b37753d|work=filmportal.de|title=Jonathan|accessdate=December 25, 2012}}</ref> Director Hans W. Geißendörfer won the Film Award in Gold at the [[Deutscher Filmpreis]] for Best New Direction for his work on ''Jonathan''.<ref name="filmportal.de" />
