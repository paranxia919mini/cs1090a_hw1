'''''Late Night with the Devil''''' is a 2023 [[independent film|independent]] [[postmodernist film|postmodernist]]<ref>[https://consequence.net/2024/03/late-night-with-the-devil-david-dastmalchian/ David Dastmalchian on Late Night With the Devil: Interview|Consequence]</ref> [[supernatural horror film]] written, directed, and edited by brothers [[Colin and Cameron Cairnes]]. It stars [[David Dastmalchian]], [[Laura Gordon]], [[Ian Bliss]], [[Fayssal Bazzi]], [[Ingrid Torelli]], Rhys Auteri, [[Georgina Haig]], and [[Josh Quong Tart]]. Incorporating elements of [[analog horror]], [[Found footage (film technique)|"found footage"]] and documentary filmmaking, the film follows the events of a [[late-night talk show]] episode aired on the night of [[Halloween]] 1977, during which the host attempts to boost ratings by inviting an allegedly [[Spirit possession|possessed]] girl onto the show.<ref>[https://www.dreadcentral.com/editorials/534205/local58-and-the-birth-of-analog-horror/ 'LOCAL58' And The Birth of Analog Horror - Dread Central]</ref><ref name="satanwantsyou">[https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3806650/satanic-panic-sweeps-showbiz-the-real-life-inspirations-behind-late-night-with-the-devil/ Satan Wants You: The Real Life Inspirations Behind 'Late Night with the Devil' – Bloody Disgusting]</ref>

An international co-production of Australia, the United States, and the United Arab Emirates,<ref>{{cite web|publisher=[[Chicago International Film Festival]]|url=https://www.chicagofilmfestival.com/film/late-night-with-the-devil/|title=Late Night with the Devil|work=Cinema Chicago |url-status=live|archive-url=https://archive.today/20240331042738/https://www.chicagofilmfestival.com/film/late-night-with-the-devil/|archive-date=31 March 2024}}</ref> ''Late Night with the Devil'' had its world premiere at the [[South by Southwest Film Festival]] (SXSW) on 10 March 2023. It was released theatrically in the United States on 22 March 2024 by [[IFC Films]] and in Australia on 11 April. The film grossed $16 million and was generally well-received.<ref name="Harvey 2023">{{cite magazine|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/late-night-with-the-devil-review-1235562221/|title='Late Night With the Devil' Review: A '70s Chat Show Becomes Must-Unsee TV|last=Harvey|first=Dennis|date=23 March 2023|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240308162254/https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/late-night-with-the-devil-review-1235562221/|archive-date=8 March 2024}}</ref><ref name="D'Alessandro 2023"/>

==Plot==
The film's prologue is framed as a documentary investigating an unexplained event that occurred on the night of [[Halloween]] 1977, during the live broadcast of a sixth-season episode of the struggling variety [[late-night talk show]] ''Night Owls with Jack Delroy'', which competes for ratings with ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Johnny Carson]]''.<ref name="satanwantsyou"/>

Through his celebrity connections, ''Night Owls'' host Jack Delroy, who is based in New York City, makes regular visits to "[[Bohemian Grove|The Grove]]," an elite California camp for wealthy and powerful men. After his wife Madeleine dies of cancer, ''Night Owls'' halts its production. Jack ultimately returns and, in order to boost the show's low ratings, does a special [[occult]]-themed episode on Halloween. Special guests for the episode include self-proclaimed [[psychic]] and [[Mediumship|medium]] Christou, skeptic and former magician Carmichael Haig, [[parapsychologist]] author Dr. June Ross-Mitchell, and June's latest subject, 13-year-old Lilly, who is purportedly possessed by a demonic spirit. Carmichael promises to give anyone a huge check if they can prove that supernatural events do exist.

During the broadcast, Christou suffers an intense headache while experiencing a premonition about someone named "Minnie". Carmichael dismisses it as a performance, starting a confrontation with the weakened Christou which Jack interrupts by revealing that "Minnie" was his private nickname for Madeleine. This causes Christou to fall ill, projectile vomit a black liquid, and be rushed to the hospital. In the next segment, June introduces Lilly, the sole survivor of a police raid that lead to a [[mass suicide]] by a [[Satanism|Satanic]] church and its leader, Szandor D'Abo, who worshipped [[Abraxas]]. Jack persuades Lilly to communicate with the entity she claims possesses her, which she refers to as "Mr. Wriggles". During a commercial break, Jack's producer Leo informs Jack that Christou has suddenly died from a brain hemorrhage in the ambulance.

During June's conjuring, Lilly becomes possessed and [[Levitation (paranormal)|levitates]] in her chair. The demon notes having previously met Jack "under the tall trees", and reveals that Jack and June are romantically involved. Lilly's possession then subsides and the show continues.

Carmichael challenges June by subjecting Jack's sidekick Gus McConnell to a [[hypnotism]] demonstration, which causes nearly everyone in the studio to see worms pouring out of him before Carmichael ends it with the phrase "Dreamer, Here, Awake". When the production team rewinds the footage, it proves that the demonstration was merely a joint hallucination experienced by nearly everyone in the studio; however, the supernatural phenomena that occurred during June's conjuring are unaltered in the recorded playback. Jack is horrified when he notices Madeleine's ghost standing behind him in the footage, but Carmichael accuses him of orchestrating the events. Lilly becomes possessed again; her head splits open and begins to glow like lava. She then brutally kills Gus and June. Carmichael is finally convinced that what he sees is a true supernatural event and begs for his life, as well as offering Lilly the previously mentioned check, only for him to be burnt as well.

Jack is suddenly transported to a nightmarish version of the show. He relives moments from the show's past before it is revealed that he had a prior connection to the demon possessing Lilly. During a ceremony at The Grove, he made a [[Deal with the Devil|pact with the Devil]], sacrificing his wife's soul in exchange for fame and the success of ''Night Owls''. Thus, he was indirectly responsible for Madeleine's cancer. Madeleine's ghost begs Jack to put her out of her misery, as the cancer is causing her pain. Using the ritual Athame dagger from Lilly's former Satanic cult, he then stabs her to death; and the scene suddenly shifts to the now-empty studio. A horrified Jack realizes he has stabbed and killed Lilly on live television. He stands over the bodies of his dead guests, repeating the phrase "Dreamer, Here, Awake", as police sirens approach in the distance. (At the bottom of the screen, the line "End of Transmission" changes to "So It Is Done".)

==Production==
The film was [[Executive producer|executive produced]] by Australian producer [[Julie Ryan (Australian producer)|Julie Ryan]], soon after forming a new partnership with [[Umbrella Entertainment]] called [[Sanctuary Pictures]].<ref>{{cite web | title=About Us | website=Sanctuary Pictures | date=29 April 2025 | url=https://sanctuarypictures.com.au/about/ | access-date=23 July 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | last=Toth | first=Julianna | title=Umbrella launches production arm Sanctuary Pictures in partnership with Julie Ryan and Cyan Films | website=[[Umbrella Entertainment]]| date=1 March 2023| url=https://www.umbrellaent.com.au/sanctuary_films_announcement/ | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250418182925/https://www.umbrellaent.com.au/sanctuary_films_announcement/ | archive-date=18 April 2025 | url-status=dead | access-date=23 July 2025}}</ref>

Production on ''Late Night with the Devil'' was announced on 13 February 2022, with the Emirati studio [[Image Nation]] and the American genre label [[Spooky Pictures]] attached. Describing their decision to base ''Late Night with the Devil'' on a late-night talk show, writer-director duo Cameron and Colin Cairnes stated: "In the '70s and '80s, there was something slightly dangerous about late-night TV. Talk shows in particular were a window into some strange adult world. We thought combining that charged, live-to-air atmosphere with the supernatural could make for a uniquely frightening film experience."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://variety.com/2022/film/news/image-nation-spooky-pictures-late-night-with-the-devil-1235180296/|title=Image Nation, Spooky Pictures Set For 'Late Night With the Devil' (Exclusive)|last=Vivarelli|first=Nick|date=13 February 2022|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=18 June 2023}}</ref>

The Cairnes brothers offered the role of Jack Delroy to [[David Dastmalchian]] after reading an article Dastmalchian wrote for the magazine ''[[Fangoria]]'' about regional TV [[horror host]]s. Dastmalchian accepted the part after being sent a [[lookbook]] designed to resemble a 1970s ''[[TV Guide (magazine)|TV Guide]]'', along with the film's script.<ref name="Kauzlarich 2023">{{cite web|url=https://www.fangoria.com/original/late-night-with-the-devil-sxsw/|title=Stomach-Ripping And Throat-Slitting: Late Night With The Devil At SXSW|last=Kauzlarich|first=Jason|date=15 March 2023|website=[[Fangoria]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231210103350/https://www.fangoria.com/original/late-night-with-the-devil-sxsw/|archive-date=10 December 2023|url-status=live}}</ref> Dastmalchian's casting was announced on 24 June 2022.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://variety.com/2022/film/news/david-dastmalchian-late-night-with-the-devil-1235301446/|title='Dune' Actor David Dastmalchian to Star in 'Late Night With the Devil' (Exclusive)|last=Vivarelli|first=David|date=24 June 2022|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=18 June 2023}}</ref> [[Ian Bliss]], who played Carmichael Haig, was originally set to play another character, but days prior to filming starting he was told to read for Haig, as the actor who was originally set to play Haig had withdrawn from filming.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Piccoli |first=Liana |date=2024-05-01 |title=Behind the Scenes with Ian Bliss: Exploring the Making of Late Night with the Devil |url=https://www.nida.edu.au/news/behind-the-scenes-with-ian-bliss-exploring-the-making-of-late-night-with-the-devil/ |access-date=2024-11-29 |website=The National Institute of Dramatic Art (NIDA) |language=en-AU}}</ref>

''Late Night with the Devil'' was shot in [[Melbourne]], Australia.<ref name="Wise 2023">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2023/03/late-night-with-the-devil-review-colin-and-cameron-cairnes-1235283828/|title='Late Night With The Devil' Review: Richly Detailed '70s Meta-Horror From Colin And Cameron Cairnes – SXSW|last=Wise|first=Damon|date=10 March 2023|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|access-date=18 June 2023}}</ref> It utilises practical special effects, including [[puppetry]], along with digital visual effects.<ref name="Kauzlarich 2023"/>

=== Box office ===
''Late Night with the Devil'' grossed $10{{nbsp}}million in the United States and Canada, and $6.8{{nbsp}}million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $16.8{{nbsp}}million.<ref name="NUM"/><ref name="BOM"/>

The film opened on 22 March 2024 in 1,034 theaters in the United States and Canada, and earned $2.8 million in its opening weekend, the best debut for an [[IFC Films]] release; additionally, media outlets noted that the total included an estimated $[[Number of the beast|666,666]] gross on its third day.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dick |first=Jeremy |date=24 March 2024 |title=Late Night with the Devil Earns $666K at Sunday Box Office, Setting New Record |url=https://www.cbr.com/late-night-with-the-devil-666-box-office/ |access-date=25 March 2024 |website=[[Comic Book Resources|CBR]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url = https://variety.com/2024/film/box-office/ghostbusters-frozen-empire-box-office-opening-weekend-1235950537|title = 'Ghostbusters: Frozen Empire' Leads Box Office With $45 Million Debut|last = Rubin|first = Rebecca|date = 24 March 2024|accessdate = 24 March 2024|magazine= [[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> Expanding to 1,442 theaters the following weekend, the film made $2.2 million, finishing in seventh.<ref>{{cite web |last=D'Alessandro |first=Anthony |title=They Have Risen: 'Godzilla x Kong' Conquers Easter Box Office With $80M Opening; Legendary Monsterverse Franchise Crosses $2 Billion – Sunday AM Box Office |url=https://deadline.com/2024/03/box-office-godzilla-x-kong-the-new-empire-1235871440/ |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |access-date=March 31, 2024 |date=March 31, 2024 |archive-date=March 30, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240330023616/https://deadline.com/2024/03/box-office-godzilla-x-kong-the-new-empire-1235871440/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Critical response ===
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|97|7.8|239|Delightfully dark, ''Late Night with the Devil'' proves possession horror isn't played out – and serves as an outstanding showcase for David Dastmalchian.|access-date=8 May 2026|ref=y}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 72 out of 100, based on 22 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Late Night with the Devil'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/late-night-with-the-devil |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=31 March 2024 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]''{{'}}s Dennis Harvey commended the film's production design and technical aspects, as well as that of the cast, and wrote that the film's "mix of vintage [[The "Me" Decade and the Third Great Awakening|Me Decade]] showbiz cheese and ''[[The Exorcist|Exorcist]]''-y demonic doings is distinctive, not to mention deftly handled by the [Cairnes] brothers as both writers and directors."<ref name="Harvey 2023" /> Brian Tallerico of ''[[RogerEbert.com]]'' praised the film as inventive and its use of the found footage format as clever, and highlighted Dastmalchian's "phenomenally committed performance [...] really holding the film together as he finds the right tone between smarmy and likable that dominated so much '70s culture."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/sxsw-2023-brooklyn-45-late-night-with-the-devil|title=SXSW 2023: Brooklyn 45, Late Night with the Devil|last=Tallerico|first=Brian|date=14 March 2023|website=[[RogerEbert.com]]|access-date=19 June 2023}}</ref>

Meagan Navarro of [[Bloody Disgusting]] gave the film a score of three-and-a-half out of five, criticizing the film's pacing as rapid but writing that "the ingenuity, the painstaking period recreation, a riveting performance by Dastmalchian, and a showstopper of a finale make for one Halloween event you won't want to miss."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3753964/late-night-with-the-devil-sxsw-review-a-pastiche-salute-to-70s-late-night-television-transforms-into-raucous-horror-ride/|title='Late Night with the Devil' SXSW Review – Salute to Late Night Television Transforms Into a Raucous Horror Ride|last=Navarro|first=Meagan|date=11 March 2023|website=[[Bloody Disgusting]]|access-date=19 June 2023}}</ref> Trace Sauveur of ''[[The Austin Chronicle]]'' also praised Dastmalchian's performance and called the film "totally gimmicky, but the sincere commitment to the conceit is what really makes this work." Sauveur adds that, despite finding the film's conclusion lacking, "''Late Night with the Devil'' is able to mine plenty of effective and fun ideas out of its premise, and it works as a potent examination of the price of success."<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.austinchronicle.com/daily/sxsw/2023-03-16/sxsw-film-review-late-night-with-the-devil/|title=SXSW Film Review: Late Night With the Devil|last=Sauveur|first=Trace|date=16 March 2023|newspaper=[[The Austin Chronicle]]|access-date=19 June 2023}}</ref>

[[Mark Kermode]] of ''[[Kermode and Mayo's Film Review#Kermode and Mayo's Take|Kermode and Mayo's Take]]'' called it "really enjoyable", adding that he thought it was influenced by the 1992 British mockumentary supernatural horror TV film ''[[Ghostwatch]]''.<ref>{{cite AV media |people=[[Mark Kermode]] |date=21 March 2024 |title=Mark Kermode reviews ''Late Night with the Devil'' |type=Podcast |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JRwSsNgZW2g |access-date=23 March 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240323135647/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JRwSsNgZW2g |archive-date=23 March 2024 |time= |via=[[YouTube]] |url-status=bot: unknown }}</ref>

Wendy Ide of ''[[The Observer]]'' gave the film a score of four out of five stars, praising its satire and comedic elements, calling it "[s]mart, cynical and at times devilishly funny, the film delivers a crackle of disruptive static to the demonic possession genre."<ref>{{Cite news |last=Ide |first=Wendy |date=23 March 2024 |title=Late Night With the Devil review – diabolically funny found-footage horror |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2024/mar/23/late-night-with-the-devil-review-cameron-colin-cairnes-diabolically-funny-tv-talkshow-horror |access-date=24 March 2024 |newspaper=[[The Observer]]}}</ref>

John Lui of ''[[The Straits Times]]'' gave the film 4 stars in which he describes the film as "Comedy, horror and homage come together in style in this hugely enjoyable work".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.straitstimes.com/life/entertainment/at-the-movies-late-night-with-the-devil-is-near-perfect-blend-of-horror-comedy-and-homage|title= At The Movies: Late Night With The Devil is near-perfect blend of horror, comedy and homage|last=Lui|first=John|date=18 April 2024|newspaper=[[The Straits Times]]}}</ref> For ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'', Allaire Nuss wrote that ''Late Night with the Devil'', which she described as being presented in a [[mockumentary]] and [[analog horror]] format, was "the first great scary movie of 2024" and that she "adored every second of it".<ref>{{cite magazine |title='Doctor Who' and 'Interview With the Vampire' lead this week's Must List |url=https://ew.com/must-list-doctor-who-interview-with-the-vampire-maxton-hall-8646757 |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]] |access-date=October 24, 2024 |date=May 10, 2024}}</ref>
