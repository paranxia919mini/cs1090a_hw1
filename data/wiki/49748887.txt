'''''Below Deck Mediterranean''''' is an American [[reality television]] series that premiered on [[Bravo (American TV network)|Bravo]] on May 3, 2016. Developed as the first spin-off of ''[[Below Deck]]'', it has aired ten seasons.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ypicrew.com/yacht-work-advice/jobs-on-yachts-description/|title=What are the Jobs Available? {{!}} YPI CREW|website=ypicrew.com|language=en-US|access-date=2018-07-18}}</ref><ref name="about">{{cite web|last=Harnick|first=Chris|title=''Below Deck Mediterranean'' Sets Sails With a Whole Lot of Partying, Hookups and Drama|url=http://www.eonline.com/news/747227/below-deck-mediterranean-sets-sails-with-a-whole-lot-of-partying-hookups-and-drama|work=[[E! Online]]|date=March 9, 2016}}</ref> The eleventh season premiered on June 8, 2026.

The series chronicles the lives of the crew members who work and reside aboard a {{convert|150|ft|m|adj=mid|-plus}} [[superyacht]] during a [[Yacht charter|charter]] season in [[Greece]] (seasons 1 and 9),<ref name="about" /> [[Croatia]] (seasons 2, 6, and 11), [[Italy]] (seasons 3 and 8), [[France]] (season 4), [[Spain]] (seasons 5 and 10) and [[Malta]] (season 7)<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Below Deck Mediterranean Returns with New and Familiar Faces This Summer|url=https://www.bravotv.com/the-daily-dish/below-deck-mediterranean-season-5-premiere-date-trailer|work=Bravo TV Official Site|date=2020-04-27|access-date=2020-04-28|language=en-US}}</ref> which consists of filming for around six weeks with the cast temporarily replacing the conventional crew.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.worldwideboat.com/news/miscellaneous/below-deck-yachts |title=Below Deck Yachts - How Long Are the Below Deck Charters? |work=Worldwide Boat |date=July 27, 2018 |access-date=August 18, 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Bravo Media Expands Diverse Slate with Addition of 10 New and 4 Returning Series Marking Over 40 High-Quality Original Series|url=http://www.thefutoncritic.com/news/2015/03/30/bravo-media-expands-diverse-slate-with-addition-of-10-new-and-4-returning-series-marking-over-40-high-quality-original-series-945513/20150330bravo01/|work=[[The Futon Critic]]|date=March 30, 2015}}</ref> All seasons are currently available on Peacock streaming service.

==Broadcast==
''Below Deck Mediterranean'' airs on the [[Bravo (American TV network)|Bravo]] cable network in the United States; the first episode premiered on Tuesday at 9/8pm [[Effects of time zones on North American broadcasting|ET/PT]] on May 3, 2016. A half-hour preview special of the series aired on March 23, 2016.<ref name="cast">{{cite web|title=Bravo Media Sets Sail with the Premiere of "Below Deck Mediterranean" Tuesday, May 3 at 9PM. Below Deck Mediterranean Season 1 is being repeated on E4 in the United Kingdom ET/PT|url=http://www.thefutoncritic.com/news/2016/03/09/bravo-media-sets-sail-with-the-premiere-of-below-deck-mediterranean-tuesday-may-3-at-9pm-et-pt-130012/20160309bravo01/|work=[[The Futon Critic]]|date=March 9, 2016}}</ref> In 2021, the British [[Channel Four Television Corporation|Channel 4]] acquired the series for its [[E4 (TV channel)|E4]] TV channel, which is aimed at 16–34 year olds, with the first episode on February 22, 2021, at 7:30pm.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.tvzoneuk.com/post/belowdeck-e4|title=E4 ACQUIRE BELOW DECK MEDITERRANEAN|date=February 13, 2021|website=TVZoneUK|accessdate=May 26, 2021}}</ref>
