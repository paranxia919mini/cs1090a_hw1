'''''Tótem''''' is a 2023 [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]] written and directed by [[Lila Avilés]]. The film depicts the story of seven-year-old Sol, who is spending the day at her grandfather's home, for a surprise party for Sol's father, Tonatiuh. As daylight fades, Sol comes to understand that her world is about to change dramatically.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/film/437770/|title=Tótem|last=Avilés|first=Lila|date=11 February 2023|access-date=11 February 2023|website=Cineuropa}}</ref> It was selected to compete for the [[Golden Bear]] at the [[73rd Berlin International Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 20 February 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/berlin-film-festival-reveals-2023-competition-line-up/5178380.article |title= Berlin Film Festival reveals 2023 competition line-up|last=Rosser|first=Michael|website=Screen Daily|date=23 January 2023|access-date=11 February 2023}}</ref> It is a co-production between Mexico, Denmark and France.

It received critical acclaim and was named one of the top five international films of 2023 by the [[National Board of Review]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Lewis|first=Hilary|date=6 December 2023|title=National Board of Review Names 'Killers of the Flower Moon' Best Film of 2023|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/2023-national-board-of-review-winners-list-1235714873/|access-date=6 December 2023|work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref> The film was selected as the Mexican entry for [[Academy Award for Best International Feature Film|Best International Feature Film]] at the [[96th Academy Awards]];<ref>{{cite web|title=Habemus película: 'Tótem' de Lila Avilés representará a México en los Oscar 2024|last=Padilla|first=Greta|date=25 September 2023|url=https://www.sopitas.com/cine-y-tv/pelicula-totem-lila-aviles-representara-mexico-premios-oscar-2024/|website=Sopitas.com|language=es|access-date=25 November 2024}}</ref> it made the shortlist of 15 entries from which the nominees were selected.<ref>{{cite web|title='Tótem', de la mexicana Lila Avilés, precandidata al Óscar a Mejor Película Extranjera {{!}} Video|trans-title='Tótem', by Mexican Lila Avilés, pre-candidate for the Oscar for Best Foreign Film {{!}} Video|url=https://aristeguinoticias.com/2112/kiosko/totem-de-la-mexicana-lila-aviles-precandidata-al-oscar-a-mejor-pelicula-extranjera-video/|access-date=21 December 2023|website=Aristegui Noticias|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://aframe.oscars.org/news/post/96th-oscars-shortlists|title=96th Oscars Shortlists Announced for 10 Categories|date=21 December 2023|access-date=22 December 2023|website=Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences}}</ref>

==Plot==
Sol, a seven-year-old girl, goes to her grandfather's home, where her aunts Nuri and Alejandra are throwing a birthday party for her father. It is probably his last, so in a sense it is also a farewell ceremony. With the onset of dusk, an unfamiliar and unrestrained atmosphere takes over, breaking the family bonds. For Sol, the world is about to change as she learns the essence of letting go and feels the breath of life.

==Production==
On 7 July 2020, it was reported that Lila Avilés was planning for her new film, ''Tótem''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cinemaldito.com/lila-aviles-prepara-su-nueva-pelicula-totem/|title= Lila Avilés prepares her new film Totem|trans-title=Lila Avilés prepara su nueva película Totem|last=Ejarque|first=Cristina|date=7 July 2020|access-date=11 February 2023|website=Cine maldito|language=es}}</ref> The film was among five upcoming feature projects to receive grants of {{€}}53,000 ({{USD}}60,000) from the Hubert Bals Fund (HBF), administered by the [[International Film Festival Rotterdam]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/lila-aviles-second-feature-among-new-hubert-bals-projects/5151376.article|title=Lila Avilés second feature among new Hubert Bals projects|last=Rosser|first=Michael|date=7 July 2020|access-date=11 February 2023|website=[[ScreenDaily]]}}</ref>

''Tótem'' was produced by Limerencia Films and Laterna, in co-production with Danish firm Paloma Productions and France's Alpha Violet Production, who are also distributors. It is the second feature film of Lila Avilés after ''[[The Chambermaid (film)|The Chambermaid]]''. Naíma Sentíes as Sol, Montserrat Marañon as Nuri, Marisol Gasé as Alejandrato and Mateo García Elizondo as Sol's father Tonatiuh are cast in main roles.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/437775/|title=Alpha Violet to boast a Golden Bear candidate in Berlin|last=Lemercier|first=Fabien|date=2 February 2023|access-date=11 February 2023|website=Cineuropa}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical response===
On the [[review aggregator]] [[Rotten Tomatoes]] website, the film has an approval rating of 97% based on 77 reviews, with an average rating of 8.2/10. The website's critics consensus says: "A heartfelt film that tells a poignant story without straying into sentimentality, ''Tótem'' is a life-affirming triumph for writer-director Lila Avilés."<ref name="score:rotten">{{cite Rotten Tomatoes|title=Tótem (Drama 2023)|id=totem_2023|type=m|access-date=4 January 2025}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], it has a weighted average score of 91 out of 100 based on 21 reviews, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{cite Metacritic|id=totem|type=movie|title=Tótem|access-date=4 January 2025}}</ref>

Stephen Saito's review on the website ''The Moveable Fest'' wrote called the film "a thrillingly vital family portrait about a clan that does its very best to celebrate someone they’re about to mourn".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://moveablefest.com/lila-aviles-totem/|title=Berlinale 2023 Review: Lila Avilés' Spellbinding "Tótem" Puts a Hold On You|last= Saito|first=Stephen|date=20 February 2023|access-date=21 February 2023|website=Moveable Fest}}</ref> Jonathan Romney reviewing for [[Screen International]] wrote, "This thematically rich piece offers a set of vivid character studies, while musing on life, death and time – largely from a child’s perspective."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/totem-berlin-review/5179009.article|title='Totem': Berlin Review|last=Romney|first=Jonathan|date=20 February 2023|access-date=21 February 2023|website=[[ScreenDaily]]}}</ref> Marina Ashioti writing in [[Little White Lies (magazine)|''Little White Lies'']] stated, "A tender spirit of warmth and levity permeates the screen, sustaining a buoyancy that keeps the film from sinking into mawkish waters."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://lwlies.com/festivals/totem-first-look-review/|title=Tótem – first-look review|last=Ashioti|first=Marina|date=20 February 2023|access-date=21 February 2023|website=[[Little White Lies (magazine)|Little White Lies]]}}</ref> Fabien Lemercier reviewing for Cineuropa praised the film and wrote, "It’s a film full of life and soul and a modestly deceptive work, for its significance is enormous."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/438976/|title= Review: Tótem|last=Lemercier|first=Fabien|date=20 February 2023|access-date=22 February 2023|website=Cineuropa}}</ref>
