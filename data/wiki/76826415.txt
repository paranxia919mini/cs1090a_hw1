'''''An Unfinished Film''''' ({{lang-zh|一部未完成的电影}}) is a 2024 [[docufiction]] film directed by [[Lou Ye]], and written by Lou and Yingli Ma.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rosser |first=Michael |title=Lou Ye's Cannes title 'An Unfinished Film' heads to Coproduction Office, lands first sales (exclusive) |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/lou-yes-cannes-title-an-unfinished-film-heads-to-coproduction-office-lands-first-sales-exclusive/5192635.article |access-date=2024-05-05 |website=[[Screen International]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Goodfellow |first=Melanie |date=2024-04-22 |title=Oliver Stone, Lou Ye, Michel Hazanavicius Films & 'The Count Of Monte Cristo' Among New Titles Added To Cannes 2024 Official Selection |url=https://deadline.com/2024/04/oliver-stone-lou-ye-new-titles-cannes-2024-official-selection-1235891537/ |access-date=2024-05-05 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

It had its world premiere on 16 May 2024 at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival]], in the Special Screenings section.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Vlessing |first=Etan |date=2024-04-22 |title=Cannes Adds Michel Hazanavicius, Mohammad Rasoulof, Emanuel Parvu Titles to Official Competition |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/cannes-2024-official-competition-titles-1235878805/ |access-date=2024-05-05 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Khaldi |first=Tarik |date=2024-05-16 |title=An Unfinished Film: Lou Ye tells the story of a doomed film project |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2024/an-unfinished-film-lou-ye-tells-the-story-of-a-doomed-film-project/ |access-date=2024-10-04 |website=Festival de Cannes |language=en-US}}</ref> It won both [[Golden Horse Award for Best Narrative Feature|Best Narrative Feature]] and [[Golden Horse Award for Best Director|Best Director]] at the [[61st Golden Horse Awards]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Frater |first=Patrick |date=2024-11-24 |title=Lou Ye’s ‘An Unfinished Film’ Wins Top Honors at Golden Horse Awards |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/news/golden-horse-awards-lou-ye-an-unfinished-film-1236219221/ |access-date=2024-12-04 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Synopsis ==
In January 2020, a film crew reunites near [[Wuhan]] to resume the shooting of a film halted ten years earlier, only to share the unexpected challenges as cities are placed under [[Chinese government response to COVID-19|lockdown]].

== Production ==
The film combines outtakes and behind-the-scenes footage from Lou's previous films—''[[Suzhou River (film)|Suzhou River]]'' (2000), ''[[Spring Fever (2009 film)|Spring Fever]]'' (2009), ''[[Mystery (2012 film)|Mystery]]'' (2012), and ''[[The Shadow Play (2018 film)|The Shadow Play]]'' (2018)—and original footage of a film crew playing versions of themselves. It was originally conceived as a film consisting of old footage of [[Qin Hao]] playing various roles, which Lou felt "reflected the changes in China over the past decade or so", but as the [[COVID-19 pandemic]] began, it became a film centred on filmmaking during the pandemic.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://download.wolf-con.com/AnUnfinishedFilm_Pressbook.pdf|title=Pressbook|publisher=Wolf Consultants|access-date=14 May 2024}}</ref>

{{as of|May 2024}}, a separate film by Lou also consisting of footage from ''Spring Fever'', ''Mystery'', and ''The Shadow Play'' and new footage featuring Qin, Huang Xuan, Liang Ming, and Qi Xi was in production.<ref>{{cite news|last=Wong|first=Silvia|date=18 May 2024|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/lou-ye-to-finish-an-unfinished-film-exclusive/5193686.article|title=Lou Ye to finish 'An Unfinished Film' (exclusive)|work=Screen Daily|access-date=18 May 2024}}</ref>

== Release ==
The film was nominated for the ''[[L'Œil d'or]]'' for best documentary at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival|77th Cannes Film Festival]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 Competition |url=https://loeildor.scam.fr/en/2024-competition/ |access-date=2024-05-10 |website=Scam : Société civile des auteurs multimédia |language=fr-FR}}</ref> where it had its world premiere on 16 May 2024 in the Special Screenings section. It made its North American premiere at the [[2024 Toronto International Film Festival|49th Toronto International Film Festival]] in the Centrepiece Programme.<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 6, 2024 |title=TIFF announces its 2024 Centrepiece programme with titles from over 40 countries |url=https://tiff.net/press/news/tiff-announces-its-2024-centrepiece-programme-with-titles-from-over-40-countries |access-date=2024-12-04 |website=TIFF |language=en}}</ref> It was also selected for the [[MAMI Mumbai Film Festival 2024]] under the World Cinema section.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Deb |first=Deepshikha |date=2024-09-30 |title=MAMI Mumbai Film Festival Unveils 2024 Official Lineup |url=https://www.highonfilms.com/mami-mumbai-film-festival-unveils-2024-official-lineup/ |access-date=2024-10-13 |website=High On Films |language=en-US}}</ref> It was released theatrically in France on 23 October 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Colombani |first=Florence |date=2024-10-28 |title=« Chroniques chinoises », faux documentaire sur l'enfer du confinement |url=https://www.lepoint.fr/culture/chroniques-chinoises-faux-documentaire-sur-l-enfer-du-confinement-28-10-2024-2573865_3.php |access-date=2024-12-04 |website=[[Le Point]] |language=fr}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Mélinard |first=Michaël |date=2024-10-22 |title=« Chroniques chinoises » : Lou Ye dans l’atmosphère anxiogène de Wuhan au début du Covid |url=https://www.humanite.fr/culture-et-savoir/cinema-chinois/chroniques-chinoises-lou-ye-dans-latmosphere-anxiogene-de-wuhan-au-debut-du-covid |access-date=2024-12-04 |website=[[L'Humanité]] |language=fr-FR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Marzolf |first=Hélène |date=2024-10-22 |title=“Chroniques chinoises” : quand Lou Ye dénonce les dérives du confinement |url=https://www.telerama.fr/cinema/chroniques-chinoises-quand-lou-ye-denonce-les-derives-du-confinement_cri-7035098.php |access-date=2024-12-04 |website=[[Télérama]] |language=fr}}</ref>
