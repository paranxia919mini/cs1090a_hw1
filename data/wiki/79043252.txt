'''''Köln 75''''' is a 2025 German music-[[drama film]] directed by Ido Fluk. The film follows the true story of [[Vera Brandes]], teenage patron of the 1970s [[Cologne]] (German: ''Köln'') music scene, as she makes plans to organize a [[Keith Jarrett]] concert in Cologne, which eventually became his album ''[[The Köln Concert]]''. [[Mala Emde]] and [[John Magaro]] star as Brandes and Jarrett, respectively.<ref name="lead:filmportal">{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmportal.de/en/movie/koln-75_15d06f670da24846952d415cfe272173 |title=Köln 75, Deutschland Polen 2023-2025 Spielfilm|trans-title=Köln 75, Germany Poland 2023-2025 Feature Film|website= filmportal.de |date=16 January 2025|access-date=29 January 2025|language=de}}</ref>

The film, released on the 50th anniversary of the concert, was selected in the Berlinale Special Gala section at the [[75th Berlin International Film Festival]], and had its world premiere at the festival on 16 February 2025. Its theatrical release was on March 13, 2025, in German theaters.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/472576 |title= The Berlinale unveils the titles playing in its Competition and its Perspectives and Berlinale Special strands|author= Davide Abbatescianni|date=21 January 2025|access-date=29 January 2025 |website= [[Cineuropa]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
The film follows 18-year-old [[Vera Brandes]] as she defies her conservative parents to organize a 1975 concert by [[Keith Jarrett]] in Cologne. Despite near failure, the impromptu performance's recording becomes the iconic [[The Köln Concert|Köln Concert]], a landmark of 20th-century music.

==Production==

Principal photography began on 5 October 2023 on locations in Germany and Poland. Filming ended on 3 November 2023 in [[North Rhine-Westphalia]].<ref name="ten:crew">{{Cite web|url=https://www.crew-united.com/en/Koeln-75__308559.html |title=Köln 75: Feature Film, 2024-20252023-2024, Art, Biography, Comedy, Drama, Music – Germany, Poland, Belgium |website= Crew United |date= 15 December 2024|access-date=29 January 2025|language=en}}</ref>

==Release==

''Köln 75'' was released on 13 March 2025 theatrically in Germany by Alamode Filmdistribution,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.alamodefilm.de/kino/detail/koeln-75.html |title=Köln 75 |trans-title= Köln 75|date=23 September 2024|access-date=29 January 2025 |website= Alamode Filmdistribution |language=de}}</ref> and in Switzerland by Polyfilm.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.polyfilm.at/film/koeln-75/ |title=Köln 75 |trans-title= Köln 75|date=12 September 2024|access-date=29 January 2025 |website= Polyfilm |language=de}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.procinema.ch/de/statistics/filmdb/1020746.html |title=Köln 75 |trans-title= Köln 75|date=12 September 2024|access-date=29 January 2025 |website= ProCinema |language=de}}</ref> 
On 24 March 2025, it was screened in Kaleidoscope section of the [[Sofia International Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://siff.bg/en/movies/kaleidoscope/koln-75 |title=  Köln 75|date=24 March 2025|access-date=18 September 2025|website= [[Sofia International Film Festival]] |language=en}}</ref>

It was also showcased at the [[53rd Norwegian International Film Festival]] in Main Programme section on 16 August 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.filmfestivalen.no/f/koln-75/1068 |title= Köln 75|date=31 July 2025|access-date=3 August 2025|website= [[Norwegian International Film Festival]] |language=en}}</ref> It was also a part of the films 'Based on A True Story' at the [[2025 Atlantic International Film Festival]] and screened on September 13, 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://2025aiff.eventive.org/films/688bc55e5c948f71b9b64e99 |title=Köln 75 |access-date=13 August 2025|website= [[Atlantic International Film Festival]] |language=en}}</ref> The film was also showcased in 'World Cinema' section at the [[2025 Cinéfest Sudbury International Film Festival]] on 20 September 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://tix.cinefest.com/websales/pages/info.aspx?evtinfo=960164~b95b1856-4626-44b2-885f-ed5089d4e17b&epguid=044dcfcd-a8e0-41ad-b1df-e31bf44dfec7&mdy=9/20/2025& |title=Köln 75|date=22 August 2025|access-date=22 August 2025|website= [[Cinéfest Sudbury International Film Festival]] |language=en}}</ref> On 11 October 2025, it was presented in Galas and special presentations section of [[2025 Vancouver International Film Festival]] as closing film of the festival.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://viff.org/series/special-presentations/ |title= Galas and special presentations|date=27 August 2025|access-date=28 August 2025|website= [[Vancouver International Film Festival]]}}</ref>

It was later released in more European regions, and is planned to be released theatrically in the United States by Zeitgeist Films.<ref>{{cite web|URL=https://deadline.com/2025/05/keith-jarrett-music-biopic-koln-75-scores-us-deal-1236393530/?unapproved=4408220&moderation-hash=f0b63c49221fa91596ed5031e86b4d6e#comment-4408220|title=Keith Jarrett Music Biopic 'Koln 75' Scores U.S. Deal|last=Wiseman|first=Andreas|date=May 12, 2025|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|access-date=June 2, 2025|language=en}}</ref>
