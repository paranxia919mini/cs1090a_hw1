'''''Bending the Arc''''' is a 2017 [[documentary film]]. It tells the story of [[Partners in Health]] and doctors and humanitarians, [[Jim Yong Kim]], [[Ophelia Dahl]], and [[Paul Farmer]], who are devoted to innovative health care in impoverished nations. Directors [[Kief Davidson]] and [[Pedro Kos]] follow their ongoing struggle to treat and eradicate [[tuberculosis]] and [[HIV/AIDS]] in rural areas of [[Haiti]], [[Peru]], and [[Rwanda]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://www.sundance.org/projects/bending-the-arc|title=bending-the-arc|website=www.sundance.org|access-date=2017-04-15|archive-date=2021-01-26|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210126065600/https://www.sundance.org/projects/bending-the-arc|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
[[Paul Farmer]] and [[Jim Yong Kim]] met at [[Harvard Medical School]] in the 1980s and were both drawn to medicine by a shared desire for social justice.<ref>{{Cite journal|last=Burki|first=Talha Khan|date=2017-04-01|title=Bending the Arc|url=http://thelancet.com/pdfs/journals/lanres/PIIS2213-2600(17)30088-7.pdf|journal=The Lancet Respiratory Medicine|language=English|volume=5|issue=4|pages=258|doi=10.1016/S2213-2600(17)30088-7|issn=2213-2600|doi-access=free}}</ref> Along with activist [[Ophelia Dahl]], they decided to try and make quality healthcare available to those in poor countries. They raised funding and opened a clinic in rural Haiti, but realized they needed to incorporate more community work to realize their goals. Through dramatically increased cultural sensitivity, pointed listening skills, local partnerships, and home visits, treatment drastically improved, leading to [[Partners In Health]].<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.impactpartnersfilm.com/films/bending-arc|title=BENDING THE ARC {{!}} Impact Partners Film|website=www.impactpartnersfilm.com|access-date=2017-04-15}}</ref> The film follows their story from their beginnings in Haiti, to treating [[multi-drug-resistant tuberculosis]] in Peru, and advocating for and implementing antiretroviral HIV therapy in Rwanda alongside [[Agnes Binagwaho|Dr. Agnes Binagwaho]].

==Production==
The directors of ''Bending the Arc'', Kief Davidson and [[Pedro Kos]], primarily use a traditional documentary format, mixing earlier video footage and current interviews to tell the story of how Jim Kim, Ophelia Dahl, and Paul Farmer developed [[Partners in Health]].  Where they deviate a bit is in showing older footage to their protagonists, in order to elicit their present-day reactions.

At the [[Greenwich International Film Festival]] in 2017, it won Best Documentary Feature Film.<ref>[https://www.greenwichsentinel.com/2017/06/09/giff-makes-an-impact-especially-with-women/ GIFF Makes an Impact, Especially With Women]</ref>
