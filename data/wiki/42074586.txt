'''''Baxter, Vera Baxter''''' is a 1977 French [[Drama (film and television)|drama]] film written and directed by [[Marguerite Duras]], based on her then-unpublished novel ''Vera Baxter ou les Plages de l'Atlantique''.

==Synopsis==
The film opens with Vera Baxter posing. In the next scene we are inside the bar of a hotel and hear the clerk respond to an inquiring woman about the luxurious seaside house and Vera Baxter. There's also a man named Cayre present, who has failed to get in touch with Vera Baxter. We see Vera again in a grande rental villa. She receives two visitors, and we hear her story about her marriage from these conversations.

==Themes and interpretations==
[[The Criterion Collection]] described ''Baxter, Vera Baxter'' as a "hypnotically unsettling journey into one woman's existential emptiness. Ensconced in a sprawling rental villa, the world-weary Vera Baxter ... receives visits from two women, including a mysterious stranger ... to whom she recounts a shocking story about her marriage, the way she lives, and the reasons for her malaise."<ref>{{cite web |title=Baxter, Vera Baxter |url=https://www.criterion.com/films/31905-baxter-vera-baxter |publisher=[[The Criterion Collection]]}}</ref> Ivone Margulies, also writing for The Criterion Collection, viewed the film as "a harsh take on bourgeois conformity and prostitution, or, in [Duras's] words, 'an infernal circuit that shuttles her from the love of her children to her conjugal duties.'"<ref>{{Cite web |last=Margulies |first=Ivone |title=''India Song'' and ''Baxter, Vera Baxter'': In the Thrall of Duras |url=https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/8082-india-song-and-baxter-vera-baxter-in-the-thrall-of-duras |publisher=[[The Criterion Collection]] |date=28 February 2023}}</ref>

==Home media==
''Baxter, Vera Baxter'' was released by The Criterion Collection on Blu-ray and DVD on 28 February 2023 as part of the ''Two Films by Marguerite Duras'' box set along with ''[[India Song]]'' (1975).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.criterion.com/boxsets/6412-two-films-by-marguerite-duras |title=Two Films by Marguerite Duras |publisher=[[The Criterion Collection]] |access-date=13 March 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221127150824/https://www.criterion.com/boxsets/6412-two-films-by-marguerite-duras |archive-date=27 November 2022}}</ref>
