'''''Teenage Wasteland''''' is a 2025 documentary film which explores how in 1991 to 1997 a group of high school journalists and their teacher in [[Middletown, New York]] exposed the existence of a toxic waste dump in the town. The film was directed by [[Amanda McBaine]] and [[Jesse Moss (filmmaker)|Jesse Moss]].<ref> {{Cite news |date=2025-11-26 |title=‘Teenage Wasteland’ Review: Young in Age, Mature in Reporting |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/26/movies/teenage-wasteland-review.html |access-date=2025-12-03 |language=en}}</ref><ref> {{Cite web |last=MacDonald |first=Joan Vos |date=2025-11-25 |title=Teenage Wasteland: New Documentary Revisits Middletown Students Who Exposed Toxic Dumping |url=https://www.chronogram.com/arts/film/teenage-wasteland-middletown-toxic-landfill-documentary/ |access-date=2025-12-03 |website=Chronogram Magazine |language=en-US}}</ref>
