'''''The Librarians''''' is a 2025 American [[documentary]] film, directed and produced by [[Kim A. Snyder]]. It follows librarians in Texas, Florida, and other states, as they unite to combat [[Book banning in the United States (2021–present)|book banning in the United States]]. [[Sarah Jessica Parker]] serves as an executive producer under her Pretty Matches Productions banner.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/global/sarah-jessica-parker-the-librarians-sundance-executive-producer-1236246900/|title=Sarah Jessica Parker on Producing Sundance Doc About Librarians Fighting Book Bans: 'They Have Put Their Lives at Risk'|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Martin|last=Dale|date=December 12, 2024|access-date=January 26, 2025}}</ref>

The film had its world premiere at the [[2025 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 24, 2025, and was released on October 3, 2025, by 8 Above.

==Premise==
The documentary follows librarians including Carolyn Foote, Suzette Baker, and [[Amanda Jones (librarian)|Amanda Jones]] in [[Texas]], [[Louisiana]], [[Florida]], and other states, as they unite to combat book banning from [[Moms for Liberty]] and other groups in the United States.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/festivals/sarah-jessica-parker-on-the-librarians-doc-book-banning-1235088283/|title=Sarah Jessica Parker on 'The Librarians' Doc About Book Banning: 'If I Didn't Have a Library I Don't Know Who I'd Be Today'|website=[[IndieWire]]|first=Christian|last=Blauvelt|date=January 25, 2025|access-date=January 26, 2025}}</ref>

==Release==
''The Librarians'' had its world premiere at the [[2025 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 24, 2025<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2024/12/sundance-film-festival-lineup-2025-movies-1236200107/|title=Sundance 2025: JLo, Sly Stone, Putin, Ayo Edebiri, André Holland, & Ex-NZ PM Jacinda Ardern Films Among Park City Festival Offerings|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first1=Anthony|last1=D'Alessandro|first2=Dominic|last2=Patten|date=December 11, 2024|access-date=January 26, 2025}}</ref> and a screening on March 7, 2025 at the [[South by Southwest]] film festival.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://schedule.sxsw.com/2025/events/FS18126 |title=The Librarians |website=www.sxsw.com |access-date=February 9, 2025}}</ref>

In September 2025, it was screened and discussed in Oxford, England,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://visit.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/event/sep25/the-librarians |title=The Librarians: Film screening & panel discussion |website=Bodleian Libraries |access-date=2025-10-01}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Clarke |first=Cath |date=24 September 2025 |title=The Librarians review – the heroic women battling against book bans and censorship |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2025/sep/24/the-librarians-review-the-heroic-women-battling-against-book-bans-and-censorship |newspaper=[[The Guardian]] |access-date=1 October 2025}}</ref> and at the [[Zurich Film Festival]] in Switzerland in the festival section ''Hashtag #SaveDemocracy''.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://zff.com/en/movies/the-librarians-98287 |title=The Librarians |website=zff.com |access-date=2025-10-01}}</ref>

The film was released in the United States on October 3, 2025, by 8 Above.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2025/film/news/the-librarians-executive-producer-sarah-jessica-parker-1236509528/|title='The Librarians,' Awards Hopeful Doc About Texas Book Bans From Sarah Jessica Parker, Drops First Trailer and Global Release Plans|website=Variety|first=Giana|last=Levy|date=September 5, 2025|access-date=September 5, 2025}}</ref> It will be screened in the non-competitive 'Freestyle – Arts' section of the [[20th Rome Film Festival]] in October 2025.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ciakmagazine.it/news/festa-del-cienma-di-roma-2025-il-programma-completo-diretta/ |title= Festa del Cinema di Roma 2025, il programma completo (diretta) |trans-title=Rome Film Fest 2025: Full program (live)|date=24 September 2025|access-date=19 September 2025|website= [[Ciak Magazine]] |language=it}}</ref>

It was broadcast by [[BBC Four]] in the UK on 7 October 2025 as part of the ''[[Storyville (TV series)|Storyville]]'' series and is available in some regions on [[BBC iPlayer]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m002kkmj | title= Storyville: The Librarians | access-date=8 October 2025}}</ref>

In October 2025, the film was screened at the 49th edition of the [[São Paulo International Film Festival]], Brazil, as part of the event's 'International Perspective' session.<ref>{{Cite web |title=49ª Mostra Internacional de Cinema em São Paulo |url=https://mostra.org/filmes/os-bibliotecarios |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251019223613/https://mostra.org/filmes/os-bibliotecarios |archive-date=2025-10-19 |access-date=2025-10-20 |website=49ª Mostra Internacional de Cinema em São Paulo |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

[[PBS]] broadcast the documentary as part of ''[[Independent Lens]]'' on February 9, 2026.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2025/12/independent-lens-winter-2025-slate-1236636377/|title=Oscar Contender 'The Librarians' Booked To Anchor Winter Season Of PBS Series 'Independent Lens'|website=Deadline Hollywood|first=Matthew|last=Carey|date=December 4, 2025|access-date=December 6, 2025}}</ref>
