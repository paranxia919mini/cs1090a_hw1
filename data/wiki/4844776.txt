'''''Death & Taxes''''' is a 1993 [[documentary film]] directed by Jeffrey F. Jackson about [[Gordon Kahl]], a [[Tax protester (United States)|tax protester]] who was killed in a shootout with local law enforcement officials in [[Smithville, Arkansas]] in 1983.

==Synopsis==
The documentary focuses on Kahl, a tax protester that was killed as part of a shootout in Arkansas in 1983. It utilized interviews and archive material, as well as footage from Kahl's exhumation and the 1991 [[made-for-TV movie]] ''In the Line of Duty: Manhunt in the Dakotas'' starring [[Rod Steiger]].

==Reception==
''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' reviewed the documentary, writing that it was "a hard-hitting reinvestigation of the 1983 Gordon Kahl case, about which questions still linger. Jackson's unfazed, investigative reporting style approach and inventive handling of familiar material make this a controversial item for fests and progressive webs."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Elley|first1=Derek|title=Review: 'Death & Taxes'|url=https://variety.com/1993/film/reviews/death-taxes-1200435039/|website=Variety|access-date=9 March 2017|date=1 December 1993}}</ref> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' also looked at the film and noted that it "has only a slim potential audience, but provides plenty of fodder for those interested in its subject matter."<ref>{{Cite book|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=xw0EAAAAMBAJ&q=%22Death+%26+Taxes%22+Gordon+Kahl&pg=PA76|title=Death & Taxes (review)|last=Applefeld|first=Catherine|date=1995-09-02|publisher=Billboard|pages=76|language=en}}</ref>
