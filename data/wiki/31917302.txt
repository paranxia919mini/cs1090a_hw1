'''Rich Keeble''' is a British actor and voiceover artist.<ref name="CCP">{{cite news | title = Rich Keeble | url = http://actors.mandy.com/uk/rich.keeble | work = Mandy Actors | publisher = The Mandy Network Ltd. | access-date = 2011-05-28}}</ref> He is known for appearances in TV series including Brian Hetherington in ''[[Rivals (TV series)|Rivals]]'' (2024-present)'','' Mr Arnold in ''[[Good Omens (TV series)|Good Omens]]'' (2023)'','' Trevor in ''[[The Change (TV series)|The Change]]'' (2023-2025), and various characters in ''[[Emily Atack|The Emily Atack Show]]'' (2020-2021). Other TV appearances include ''[[Ghosts (2019 TV series)|Ghosts]]'' (2022), ''[[The Beaker Girls]]'' (2023) and ''[[Trying (TV series)|Trying]] (2022).''

He is also known for his voice acting in video games including Monoco and other characters in ''[[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33]]'' (2025), Fenrico in ''[[Wuthering Waves]]'' (2025)'','' and Frank Fairburne in ''[[Strange Brigade]]'' (2018)''.''

==Early life==
{{expand section|date=June 2026}}
Keeble attended [[Tiffin School]] in [[Kingston upon Thames]] in the 1990s.

==Career==
===Television, film and online===
Between 2010 and 2015, Keeble appeared as various characters in sketches for ''[[Lee Nelson's Well Good Show]]'', ''[[Alan Carr's Specstacular|Alan Carr's Summertime Specstacular]]'', ''[[Alan Carr's New Year Specstacular]]'', hidden camera show ''[[Fool Britannia]]'' (starring [[Dom Joly]]), and had small roles in sitcoms ''[[Birds of a Feather (TV series)|Birds of a Feather]]'' and ''[[The Delivery Man (TV series)|The Delivery Man]].'' In 2013, he appeared in a recurring ident on the [[BBC]] directed by [[Victoria Pile]], playing the character of Gary whose living room is invaded by a panda played by [[Steve Marsh (comedian)|Steve Marsh]].{{citation needed|date=November 2022}}

In 2015 he portrayed Dr. Williams in the drama thriller film ''[[Survivors (2015 film)|Survivors]]''.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.dreadcentral.com/news/91530/cool-new-trailer-signals-coming-survivors/|title=Survivors|work=Dread Central|access-date=2015-03-04|year=2014}}</ref>

Keeble co-writes and stars in the comedy [[web series]] ''Rich Keeble Vanity Project'' which won the "Best UK Series" award at UK Web Fest,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ukwebfest.com/nominees-2015|title=ukwebfest|date=2016-11-01|access-date=2016-11-01|url-status=bot: unknown|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161101230451/http://www.ukwebfest.com/nominees-2015|archive-date=1 November 2016|df=dmy-all}}</ref> and later won "Best British Web Series" at the 2017 Pilot Light TV Festival,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.pilotlightfestival.co.uk/single-post/2017/05/11/Presenting-Our-PLTV-Fest-S2-Award-Winners|title=Pilot Light TV Festival - 4 Days of TV & Web Series|website=Pilot Light TV Festival - 4 Days of TV & Web Series|date=11 May 2017|access-date=2017-09-18}}</ref> and "Best Supporting Actor (comedy)" (for co-star Sam Legassick) at the [[8th Indie Series Awards|Indie Series Awards]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.indieseriesawards.com/2017/04/8th-annual-indie-series-awards-winners.html|title=8th Annual Indie Series Awards Winners|website=www.indieseriesawards.com|access-date=2017-09-18}}</ref> It was also nominated for "Best UK Series" and "Best Writing"<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://raindancefestival.org/programme-2016/|title=Programme 2016 - Raindance Film Festival|newspaper=Raindance Film Festival|language=en-US|access-date=2016-11-01}}</ref> in the web series competition of the [[Raindance Film Festival|24th Raindance Film Festival]].

In November 2016 he regularly appeared on the entertainment television series ''Animal Antics'' on [[Viasat Nature]], directed by Nick Mavroidakis ([[Rude Tube]]).<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://woodcutmedia.com/woodcut-media-announce-production-new-factual-entertainment-series-animal-antics-commissioned-mtg-world-viasat-nature/|title=WOODCUT MEDIA ANNOUNCE PRODUCTION ON NEW FACTUAL ENTERTAINMENT SERIES ANIMAL ANTICS COMMISSIONED BY MTG WORLD FOR VIASAT NATURE - Woodcut Media|date=2016-03-08|newspaper=Woodcut Media|language=en-US|access-date=2016-11-15}}</ref><ref>{{Citation|last=Viasat Channels|title=Polsat Viasat Nature - Zabawne wybryki zwierząt - promo|date=2016-09-30|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ZpV20QONog|access-date=2016-11-15}}</ref> In March 2017 he appeared in [[Comedy Central (UK and Ireland)|Comedy Central's]] ''Bad Snappers''<ref>{{Citation|last=Comedy Central UK|title=Bad Snappers: The Funeral|date=2017-03-23|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YijFn624E-A|access-date=2017-06-19}}</ref> and [[Channel 4]]'s ''[[The Last Leg]],'' appearing as a journalist in a sketch with [[Mr Blobby]], and in another episode as an archaeologist discovering the remains of hosts [[Adam Hills]], [[Josh Widdicombe]] and [[Alex Brooker]]. He appeared in an episode of ''[[The Rebel (2016 TV series)|The Rebel]]'' with [[Simon Callow]] in November 2017.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.comedy.co.uk/tv/the_rebel/episodes/2/2/|title=The Rebel Series 2, episode 2 - Death - British Comedy Guide|website=British Comedy Guide|language=en-GB|access-date=2018-10-01}}</ref>

He provided the voice of the radio DJ on the Halloween special live episode of ''[[Inside No. 9|Inside No.9]]'' broadcast on [[BBC Two]] on 28 October 2018.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.comedy.co.uk/tv/inside_no_9/episodes/2018/1/|title=Inside No. 9 Inside No. 9 Live - Dead Line - British Comedy Guide|website=British Comedy Guide|language=en-GB|access-date=2018-10-29}}</ref> He was 'Richard the lawyer' in the 2018 Christmas special of [[Through the Keyhole]], with [[Keith Lemon]] and [[Christopher Biggins]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.comedy.co.uk/tv/through_the_keyhole/episodes/6/1/|title=Through The Keyhole Series 6 - Through The Christmas Keyhole|website=British Comedy Guide|language=en-GB|access-date=2018-12-23}}</ref>

In March 2019, he portrayed PC Mortimer in Series 2 of ''[[Porters (TV series)|Porters]]'' ([[Dave (TV channel)|Dave]]). He also played three characters in the [[BBC One|BBC1]] sitcom ''[[Warren (TV series)|Warren]]'' - Shop Assistant and two other voice roles (Radio Presenter and Taxi Operator).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.comedy.co.uk/people/rich_keeble/|title=Rich Keeble|website=British Comedy Guide|language=en-GB|access-date=2019-04-18}}</ref> Keeble stars as William in the film ''[[Criminal Audition]]'', which premiered at [[London FrightFest Film Festival|London FrightFest 2019]]. He has received a positive critical reception for his performance.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.thehollywoodnews.com/2019/08/24/criminal-audition-review-dir-samuel-gridley-frightfest-2019/|title='Criminal Audition' Review: Dir. Samuel Gridley [Frightfest 2019]|last=Baldock|first=Luke Ryan|date=2019-08-24|website=THN - The Hollywood News|language=en-GB|access-date=2020-01-25}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nerdspan.com/review-criminal-audition-2019/|title=Review: Criminal Audition (2019)|author=Steve Harcourt |date=2019-07-14|website=NerdSpan|language=en-US|access-date=2020-01-25}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.horrordna.com/movies/criminal-audition-samuel-gridley-movie-review|title=Criminal Audition |last=Beverstock|first=Gareth|website=Horror DNA|language=en-gb|access-date=2020-01-25}}</ref> In December 2019, he appeared as [[Mark Waddington (Doctors)|Mark Waddington]] in the BBC soap opera ''[[Doctors (2000 TV series)|Doctors]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.whatsontv.co.uk/events/doctors-spoilers-clash-of-the-icons/|title=Doctors spoilers: Will Clash Of The Icons be a DISASTER?|last=Timblick|first=Simon|website=What's on TV|language=en|access-date=2020-01-25}}</ref>

In 2022 he appeared as Toby in the fourth series of ''[[Ghosts (2019 TV series)|Ghosts]].''<ref>{{Citation |title=Ghosts - Series 4: 4. Gone Gone |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/iplayer/episode/p0d0mkkc/ghosts-series-4-4-gone-gone |language=en-GB |access-date=2022-09-23}}</ref> That year he also appeared as Tom Taylor the tree surgeon in ''[[Not Going Out]]'', group leader Jared in ''Trying'' on [[Apple TV+|Apple TV]], and as a priest marrying [[Johnny Vegas]] and [[Sian Gibson]]'s characters in ''[[Murder, They Hope]].'' He also appeared in [[Kiell Smith-Bynoe]]'s Channel 4 Blap, ''Red Flag.''

In 2023 he appeared opposite [[Matthew Macfadyen]] in [[ITV (TV network)|ITV]] drama, ''[[Stonehouse (TV series)|Stonehouse]]'', and in an episode of ''[[The Beaker Girls]]'' on [[CBBC]], playing Scott, a disgruntled cafe owner who gets [[Tracy Beaker|Tracey Beaker]] arrested.<ref>{{Citation |title=The Beaker Girls - Series 2: 10. The Last Straw |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/iplayer/episode/m001k4vl/the-beaker-girls-series-2-10-the-last-straw |access-date=2023-06-15 |language=en-GB}}</ref> Later that year he appeared as a room service waiter in ''[[Not Going Out]],'' as Trevor in ''[[The Change (TV series)|The Change]],'' and as Mr Arnold in Series 2 of ''[[Good Omens (TV series)|Good Omens]].''

In 2024 he appeared in ''[[Truelove (TV series)|Truelove]]'' opposite [[Phil Davis (actor)|Phil Davis]] and [[Lindsay Duncan]], ''[[Meet the Richardsons]]'' playing [[Jon Richardson]]'s dentist, [[Kaos (TV series)|''Kaos'']], ''[[Rivals (TV series)|Rivals]]'' playing TV director Brian Hetherington (reunited with ''Good Omens'' co-star [[David Tennant]]), and Christmas comedy film ''[[Bad Tidings]]'' with [[Lee Mack]] and [[Chris McCausland]].

He also appears in the music video for the [[Young Knives]] single "Dissolution".<ref>{{Cite AV media |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pjo6B-fONGs |title=Dissolution (Official video) |date=2024-10-18 |last=The Young Knives |access-date=2025-01-22 |via=YouTube}}</ref>

===Voice work===
He played the character Frank Fairburne in ''[[Strange Brigade]]'', released 28 August 2018.{{citation needed|date=November 2022}}

In 2023 he portrayed the [[Mouth of Sauron]] in ''[[The Lord of the Rings: Gollum|The Lords of the Rings: Gollum]]'' video game.
