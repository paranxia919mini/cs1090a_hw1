'''''National Gallery''''' is a 2014 documentary film edited, co-produced, and directed by [[Frederick Wiseman]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mintzer |first=Jordan |date=2014-05-17 |title=‘National Gallery’: Cannes Review |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/national-gallery-cannes-review-699276/ |access-date=2024-06-29 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163338/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/national-gallery-cannes-review-699276/ |url-status=live }}</ref> The film details operations at the [[National Gallery]] in London.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Kenigsberg |first=Ben |date=May 18, 2014 |title=Cannes: In "National Gallery," Frederick Wiseman observes the London museum's operations |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/cannes-in-national-gallery-frederick-wiseman-observes-the-london-museums-operations |url-status=live |access-date=June 29, 2024 |website=RogerEbert.com |archive-date=June 29, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163336/https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/cannes-in-national-gallery-frederick-wiseman-observes-the-london-museums-operations }}</ref>

== Synopsis ==
The film captures daily activity at the National Gallery in London, depicting both the guests' experience at the museum and behind-the-scenes employee operations.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Cumming |first=Laura |date=2014-12-28 |title=National Gallery review – Frederick Wiseman focuses on the minutiae |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2014/dec/28/national-gallery-film-review-frederick-wiseman-laura-cumming |access-date=2024-06-29 |work=The Observer |language=en-GB |issn=0029-7712}}</ref> Like Wiseman's other films, the film uses only observational footage with no interviews, music, or voiceover narration.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Brody |first=Richard |date=2014-11-06 |title=Frederick Wiseman’s "National Gallery" |url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/richard-brody/frederick-wisemans-relentless-museum-talk |access-date=2024-06-29 |work=The New Yorker |language=en-US |issn=0028-792X |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163339/https://www.newyorker.com/culture/richard-brody/frederick-wisemans-relentless-museum-talk |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite news |last=Dargis |first=Manohla |date=2014-11-04 |title=Framing the Viewers, and the Viewed |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2014/11/05/movies/in-national-gallery-frederick-wiseman-plies-his-art.html |access-date=2024-06-29 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331 |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163339/https://www.nytimes.com/2014/11/05/movies/in-national-gallery-frederick-wiseman-plies-his-art.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

== Release ==
The film premiered at the [[2014 Cannes Film Festival|67th Cannes Film Festival]] through the [[Directors' Fortnight]] program,<ref name=":0" /> and later participated in other festivals such as the [[New York Film Festival]] and [[Toronto International Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=National Gallery |url=https://www.filmlinc.org/films/national-gallery/ |access-date=2024-06-29 |website=Film at Lincoln Center |language=en |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163339/https://www.filmlinc.org/films/national-gallery/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Frederick Wiseman’s National Gallery: Inside the museum |url=https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20140908-the-national-gallerys-secrets |access-date=2024-06-29 |website=www.bbc.com |language=en-GB |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163339/https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20140908-the-national-gallerys-secrets |url-status=live }}</ref>

== Reception ==

=== Critical response ===
On [[review aggregator]] [[Rotten Tomatoes]] the film received a positive response from 95% of critics and 72% of audience members.<ref>{{Cite web |title=National Gallery {{!}} Rotten Tomatoes |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/national_gallery |access-date=2024-06-29 |website=www.rottentomatoes.com |language=en |archive-date=2024-08-25 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240825071019/https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/national_gallery |url-status=live }}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], the film  holds a score of 89 out of 100, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{Cite web |title=National Gallery |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/national-gallery/ |access-date=2024-06-29 |website=www.metacritic.com |language=en |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163339/https://www.metacritic.com/movie/national-gallery/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

[[Manohla Dargis]] of the ''[[The New York Times|New York Times]]'' praised the film for its "cool intelligence and steady camera", calling it an "unexpectedly moving" experience.<ref name=":1" />

=== Box office ===
As of June 2024, the film has an estimated worldwide gross of $354,971 since its release.<ref>{{Cite web |title=National Gallery |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt3720794/credits/ |access-date=2024-06-29 |website=Box Office Mojo |archive-date=2024-06-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240629163339/https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt3720794/credits/ |url-status=live }}</ref>
