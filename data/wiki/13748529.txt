{{nihongo|'''''Zero Focus'''''|ゼロの焦点|'''''Zero no shōten'''''}} is a 1961 Japanese [[Crime film|crime]] [[Drama (film and television)|drama]] film directed by [[Yoshitarō Nomura]], and co-written by [[Shinobu Hashimoto]] and [[Yoji Yamada]]. It is based on the novel of the same name by [[Seichō Matsumoto]].<ref name="kotobank">{{cite web|url=https://kotobank.jp/word/%E3%82%BC%E3%83%AD%E3%81%AE%E7%84%A6%E7%82%B9-719072 |title=ゼロの焦点とは (''Zero Focus'' novel) |website=Kotobank デジタル辞典 |language=ja |access-date=1 February 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://cinenouveau.com/sakuhin/nomurayoshitarou2020/nomurayoshitarou2020.html |title=ゼロの焦点 (101st anniversary of film director Yoshitaro Nomura) |website=Cinenouveau |language=ja |access-date=1 February 2021}}</ref> [[Zero Focus (2009 film)|A remake of the film]] was released in 2009.

==Plot==
One week into newlywed Teiko Uhara's marriage, her husband, ad agency manager Kenichi, leaves on a short business trip to [[Kanazawa]] and doesn't return. With a pair of old photographs she found among his belongings, Teiko travels across Japan to search for him, first with the help of her husband's employer, later on her own. After a series of mysterious deaths, including a reception girl of the agency's Kanazawa branch, who turns out to be Kenichi's common law wife, and Kenichi's alleged suicide, all clues lead to Sachiko Murota, wife of a wealthy business partner of her husband. Teiko confronts Mrs. Murota and blames her for murdering Kenichi and everyone who knew of her past as a prostitute in the [[Post-war|post-war era]]. Yet, as Mrs. Murota's confession reveals, the truth is even more complex than that.
