'''''Grass''''' ({{Korean/auto|hangul=^풀잎들|rr=yes}}) is a 2018 South Korean [[drama film]] written, produced, and directed by [[Hong Sang-soo]].<ref>{{cite web|script-title=ko:풀잎들|url=https://movie.naver.com/movie/bi/mi/basic.nhn?code=167647|website=[[Naver]]|language=ko}}</ref><ref name="grass">{{cite web|title=Grass (2018)|url=http://www.koreanfilm.or.kr/eng/films/index/filmsView.jsp?movieCd=20180401|website=Korean Film Biz Zone}}</ref> It was released theatrically on October 25, 2018.<ref name="release">{{cite web|script-title=ko:홍상수x김민희 '풀잎들', BIFF 공식 일정 성료..25일 국내 개봉|url=http://www.osen.co.kr/article/G1111007289|website=[[Osen (newspaper)|OSEN]]|date=17 October 2018|language=ko}}</ref>

==Premise==
The story about a patron of a Seoul café who observes her fellow customers as they while away their days, drawing inspiration from their conversations for her writing.<ref name="berlin">{{cite web|title=Critics Embrace Hong's Intimate 22nd Work GRASS in Berlin|url=https://www.koreanfilm.or.kr/eng/news/news.jsp?pageIndex=2&blbdComCd=601006&seq=4709&mode=VIEW&returnUrl=&searchKeyword=|website=Korean Film Biz Zone|date=17 October 2018}}</ref><ref name="grass"/>

==Production==
Principal photography began on September 7, 2017, and wrapped on September 21, 2017.<ref>{{cite web|script-title=ko:풀잎들|url=http://www.kobiz.or.kr/new/kor/01_bizmatching/movieInfo/movie_view.jsp?movieCd=20180401|website=Korean Film Biz Zone|language=ko|access-date=2018-10-17|archive-date=2018-10-17|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181017203027/http://www.kobiz.or.kr/new/kor/01_bizmatching/movieInfo/movie_view.jsp?movieCd=20180401|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The film marks the sixth collaboration between actress [[Kim Min-hee (actress, born 1982)|Kim Min-hee]] and director [[Hong Sang-soo]].<ref name="berlin"/>

==Release==
The film premiered at the [[68th Berlin International Film Festival]] on February 16, 2018.<ref name="Variety">{{cite web|last1=Kiang|first1=Jessica|title=Berlin Film Review: 'Grass'|url=https://variety.com/2018/film/festivals/grass-review-berlinale-2018-1202686998/|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|date=16 February 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Ehrlich|first1=David|title='Grass' Review: Hong Sang-soo's First Movie of 2018 Is a Beguiling 66-Minute Charmer for Hardcore Fans Only — Berlinale 2018|url=http://www.indiewire.com/2018/02/grass-review-hong-sang-soo-kim-min-hee-1201931859/|website=[[IndieWire]]|date=23 February 2018}}</ref> In early October 2018, the film was screened in the "Korean Cinema Today – Panorama" program of the [[23rd Busan International Film Festival]].<ref name="release"/>
