'''''Mamma Mia!''''' (promoted as '''''Mamma Mia! The Movie''''') is a 2008 [[jukebox musical]] [[romantic comedy]] film directed by [[Phyllida Lloyd]] and written by [[Catherine Johnson (playwright)|Catherine Johnson]], based on her book from [[Mamma Mia! (musical)|the 1999 musical]]. The film features an [[ensemble cast]], including [[Meryl Streep]], [[Pierce Brosnan]], [[Colin Firth]], [[Stellan Skarsgård]], [[Julie Walters]], [[Dominic Cooper]], [[Amanda Seyfried]], and [[Christine Baranski]]. The film follows a young bride-to-be named Sophie who invites three men to her upcoming wedding, with the possibility that any of them could be her real father.

[[Principal photography]] primarily took place on the Greek island of [[Skopelos]] from August to September 2007, with [[ABBA]] members [[Benny Andersson]] and [[Björn Ulvaeus]] composing the film's score. ''Mamma Mia!'' premiered at the [[Leicester Square]] in [[London]] on June 30, 2008, before being released in the United Kingdom on July 10, and in the United States on July 18, by [[Universal Pictures]]. It received mixed reviews from critics and was a box-office success, grossing $706.4 million worldwide on a $52{{nbsp}}million budget, becoming the [[2008 in film#Highest-grossing films|fifth highest-grossing film of 2008]]. A sequel, titled ''[[Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again]]'', was released on July 20, 2018, with much of the cast returning.

== Plot ==
<!-- Per [[WP:FILMPLOT]], plot summaries are to be between 400-700 words. -->
On the fictional Greek island of Kalokairi ({{langnf|el||summer}}), bride-to-be Sophie Sheridan reveals to her bridesmaids that she has secretly invited three men to her wedding without telling her mother, Donna: Irish-American architect Sam Carmichael, Swedish travel writer Bill Anderson, and British banker Harry Bright. One of the three is Sophie's biological father, though she doesn't know which, and she wants him to give her away at the wedding; she believes that after spending time with each of them, she will learn who fathered her.

When the three men arrive at Kalokairi, Sophie explains she sent the wedding invitations, not Donna, but does not reveal that she believes one of them is her father. She hides the men in Donna's goat house, and they hesitantly agree not to reveal themselves yet, as it is a surprise.

As Donna is working on the goat house, she spies on them. Dumbfounded to find herself facing her former lovers, she demands they leave. Donna confides in her old friends Tanya and Rosie, who have also arrived for the wedding, that she truly does not know which of the three fathered Sophie.

Sophie finds the men aboard Bill's sailboat and they sail around Kalokairi, with the men bonding with Sophie and telling her stories of Donna's carefree youth. Sophie attempts to tell her fiancé Sky about her ploy but loses her nerve.

At Sophie's bachelorette party, Donna is distressed by the three men's presence, but Rosie and Tanya assure her that they will take care of them.
Sophie talks with each man alone.
* Bill learns that Donna received the money for her villa from his great-aunt Sofia whereas he had understood the money was bequeathed "to family"; as a result, they believe he is her father and she asks him to give her away but keep their secret until the wedding.
* Sam deduces (rightly or wrongly) that he is her father and asks who is giving her away. She says "no-one" and Sam offers. They agree to keep it a secret until the wedding.
* Harry similarly deduces (rightly or wrongly) and states that he'll walk her down the aisle, giving her no chance to decline.

In the morning, Donna attempts to comfort Sophie and offers to cancel the wedding; Sophie reacts angrily, saying she wants to avoid her mother's mistakes. Sam attempts to discuss the wedding with Donna, and both realize they still have feelings for each other. Sophie admits her actions to Sky and asks for his help, but he reacts angrily to her deception.

As they prepare for the wedding Donna admits to Sophie that her mother disowned her after she got pregnant, but she could not be more proud of having her. Sophie then asks Donna to give her away, to which Donna happily agrees.

As the bridal party and guests enter the chapel Sam intercepts Donna, who reveals the pain she felt over losing him. At the wedding, Sam reveals that he had ended his engagement, but returned to find Donna had gone off with another man (Bill).

The three men agree with Sophie that they do not want the paternity confirmed, each agreeing to be one-third of a father for Sophie. Sophie suggests to Sky that they postpone their wedding and travel the world. Sam proposes to Donna, revealing that he is divorced and has always loved her. She happily accepts and they marry on the spot.

== Production ==
[[File:Agios ioannis mamma mia chapel.jpg|thumb|The Agios Ioannis chapel during filming of the wedding scene for ''Mamma Mia!''.]]
Most of the outdoor scenes were filmed on location at the small Greek islands of [[Skopelos]] and [[Skiathos]], in [[Thessaly]] (between August 29 and September 2007),<ref name=WPress/><ref>[https://www.skopelostravel.net/mamma-mia/ Film locations, photos and videos]</ref> and the seaside [[Hamlet (place)|hamlet]] of [[Damouchari]] in the [[Pelion]] area of Greece. On Skopelos, Kastani beach on the southwest coast was the film's main location site.<ref name=WPress/> The producers built a beach bar and [[jetty]] along the beach but removed both set pieces after production wrapped.<ref name="WPress">{{cite news| title = Mamma Mia! – Unfazed by the fuss in Skopelos | newspaper = The Telegraph | date = July 15, 2008 | url = https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/artsandculture/2229868/Mamma-Mia---Unfazed-by-the-fuss-in-Skopelos.html| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20080928051539/http://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/artsandculture/2229868/Mamma-Mia---Unfazed-by-the-fuss-in-Skopelos.html| url-status = dead| archive-date = September 28, 2008| first=Paul| last=Mansfield| access-date=May 12, 2010}}</ref> The wedding procession was filmed at the Agios Ioannis Chapel near [[Glossa, Sporades|Glossa]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=January 6, 2016 |title=skopelostravel.net {{!}} Agios Ioannis church, Skopelos |url=http://www.skopelostravel.net/agios-ioannis-church/ |access-date=October 28, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160106000054/http://www.skopelostravel.net/agios-ioannis-church/ |archive-date=January 6, 2016 }}</ref>

A complete set for Donna's Greek villa was built at the [[007 Stage|007 stage at Pinewood Studios]] and most of the film was shot there. Real trees were used for the set, watered daily through an automated watering system and given access to daylight to keep them growing. The part of the film where Brosnan's character, Sam, leaves his New York office to go to the Greek Island was actually filmed at the [[Lloyd's building]] on [[Lime Street, London|Lime Street]] in the [[City of London]]. He dashes down the escalators and through the [[Porte-cochère|porte-cochere]], where yellow cabs and actors representing New York mounted police were used for verisimilitude.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://flamin.filmlondon.org.uk/content.asp?CategoryID=1078&ArticleID=1826|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110816112619/http://flamin.filmlondon.org.uk/content.asp?CategoryID=1078&ArticleID=1826|archive-date=August 16, 2011|title=FLAMIN - Page not found|date=August 16, 2011}}</ref>

== Release ==
Though the world premiere of the film occurred elsewhere, most of the media attention was focused on the Swedish premiere, where [[Anni-Frid Lyngstad]] and [[Agnetha Fältskog]] joined [[Björn Ulvaeus]] and [[Benny Andersson]] with the cast at the Rival Theatre in [[Mariatorget]], [[Stockholm]], owned by Andersson, on July 4, 2008. It was the first time all four members of ABBA had been photographed together since 1986.<ref>{{cite news|last=Wejbro|first=Sandra|title=ABBA återförenades på röda mattan (Swedish)|url=http://www.aftonbladet.se/nojesliv/film/article2836959.ab|newspaper=Aftonbladet|date=July 4, 2008|access-date=July 6, 2008|archive-date=July 11, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080711161437/http://www.aftonbladet.se/nojesliv/film/article2836959.ab|url-status=live}}<!-- Template data translated to English by Kitch on July 7, 2008 --></ref>

=== Home media ===
In November 2008, ''Mamma Mia!'' became the fastest-selling DVD of all time in the UK according to Official UK Charts Company figures. It sold 1,669,084 copies on its first day of release, breaking the previous record (held by ''Titanic'') by 560,000 copies. By the end of 2008, the Official UK Charts Company declared that it had become the biggest-selling DVD ever in the UK, with one in every four households owning a copy (over 5 million copies sold).<ref>{{cite web|last=Sutherland |first=Mark |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/hr/content_display/news/e3i17e8d113d82a5300a568d1520c7ee9ac |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090115195731/http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/hr/content_display/news/e3i17e8d113d82a5300a568d1520c7ee9ac |url-status=dead |archive-date=January 15, 2009 |title='Mamma Mia!' breaks U.K. DVD record |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |date=December 31, 2008 |access-date=July 13, 2017}}</ref> The record was previously held by ''[[Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl]]'' with sales of 4.7 million copies.

In the United States, the DVD made over $30 million on its first day of release. ''Mamma Mia!'' was released on DVD and Blu-ray on December 16, 2008.<ref>{{cite web |author=Varley |first=Eddie |date=December 17, 2008 |title=MAMMA MIA! DVD Takes In 30 Million In First Day Of Sales |url=http://www.broadwayworld.com/article/MAMMA_MIA_DVD_Takes_In_30_Million_In_First_Day_Of_Sales_20081217 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111229062107/http://broadwayworld.com/article/MAMMA_MIA_DVD_Takes_In_30_Million_In_First_Day_Of_Sales_20081217 |archive-date=December 29, 2011 |access-date=December 19, 2016 |publisher=Broadwayworld.com}}</ref> By December 31, 2008, ''Mamma Mia!'' became the bestselling DVD of all time in Sweden with 545,000 copies sold.<ref>{{cite web|title=Nyheterna - tv4.se|url=http://nyheterna.se/1.799701/|publisher=nyheterna.se|access-date=September 20, 2016|archive-date=August 9, 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110809155519/http://www.nyheterna.se/1.799701/|url-status=live}}</ref>

== Reception ==
=== Box office ===
''Mamma Mia!'' grossed $144.3 million in the United States and Canada, and $562.3 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $706.4{{nbsp}}million, against a production budget of $52{{nbsp}}million.<ref name="BOM">{{Cite Box Office Mojo|id=0795421|title=Mamma Mia! (2008)|access-date=December 25, 2025|publisher_hide=yes|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260408191838/https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0795421/|archive-date=April 8, 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="NUM">{{cite web|url=https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Mamma-Mia#tab=summary|website=[[The Numbers (website)|The Numbers]]|title=Mamma Mia! (2008)|access-date=February 5, 2009|archive-date=July 31, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200731153551/https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Mamma-Mia#tab=summary|url-status=live}}</ref> It became the highest grossing live-action musical of all time until it was surpassed by [[Bill Condon]]'s ''[[Beauty and the Beast (2017 film)|Beauty and the Beast]]'' in 2017. It was also the highest-grossing movie directed by a woman until it was surpassed by [[Patty Jenkins]]' ''[[Wonder Woman (2017 film)|Wonder Woman]]'' in 2017.<ref name="marketwatch1">{{cite web |last=Williams |first=Trey |url=http://www.marketwatch.com/story/wonder-woman-passes-mamma-mia-as-highest-grossing-film-by-female-director-2017-06-23 |title='Wonder Woman' passes 'Mamma Mia!' as highest-grossing film by female director |website=MarketWatch |date=June 24, 2017 |access-date=July 5, 2017 |archive-date=June 23, 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170623211432/http://www.marketwatch.com/story/wonder-woman-passes-mamma-mia-as-highest-grossing-film-by-female-director-2017-06-23 |url-status=live }}</ref>  It is the third highest-grossing film of 2008 internationally (i.e. outside North America) with an international total of $458.4 million and the thirteenth highest gross of 2008 in North America (the US and Canada) with $144.1 million.

The film made $9.6 million on its opening day in the United States and Canada, as well as $27.6 million on its opening weekend, ranking #2 at the box office, behind ''[[The Dark Knight]]''.<ref>{{cite web | title = Box Office Mojo | website = [[Box Office Mojo]] | date = July 19, 2008 | url = https://www.boxofficemojo.com/movies/?id=mammamia.htm | access-date = July 19, 2008 | archive-date = January 2, 2010 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20100102050129/http://boxofficemojo.com/movies/?id=mammamia.htm | url-status = live }}</ref> At the time, it made ''Mamma Mia!'' the record-holder for the highest grossing opening weekend for a movie based on a Broadway musical, surpassing ''[[Hairspray (2007 film)|Hairspray]]''{{'}}s box office record in 2007 and later surpassed by ''[[Into the Woods (film)|Into the Woods]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|title="Into the Woods" Slides Into Second Place in Domestic Box-Office Totals, Beating "Unbroken"|last1=Gioia|first1=Michael|last2=Chum|first2=Vichet|magazine=[[Playbill]]|quote=As previously reported, the Rob Marshall-directed film has become the biggest launch of a Broadway adaptation since "Mamma Mia!" ($27.8 million).|date=December 30, 2014|url=http://www.playbill.com/news/article/into-the-woods-slides-into-second-place-in-domestic-box-office-totals-beating-unbroken-338152|access-date=January 5, 2012}}</ref>

In the United Kingdom, ''Mamma Mia!'' grossed £69.2 million as of January 23, 2009; it is the [[List of highest-grossing films in the United Kingdom|thirteenth highest-grossing film of all time at the UK box office]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.25thframe.co.uk/chartspagetemplate.php?source=ukboxoffice|title=UK all time top grossing films|publisher=25thframe.co.uk|access-date=May 25, 2012|archive-date=February 15, 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110215111058/http://25thframe.co.uk/chartspagetemplate.php?source=ukboxoffice|url-status=live}}</ref> The film opened at number one in the UK, taking £6.6 million on 496 screens. It managed to hold on to the top spot for two weeks, narrowly keeping [[Pixar]]'s ''[[WALL-E]]'' from reaching number one in its second week.{{Citation needed|date=January 2017}}

When released on July 3, 2009, in Greece, the film grossed $1.6 million in its opening weekend, reaching number one at the Greek box office.<ref>{{cite web | title=Greece Box Office Index | website=[[Box Office Mojo]] | date=June 10, 2008 | url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/intl/greece/ | access-date=June 10, 2008 | archive-date=May 17, 2008 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080517003040/http://www.boxofficemojo.com/intl/greece | url-status=live }}</ref>

=== Critical response ===

''Mamma Mia!'' received mixed reviews upon release.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Ahad |first=Halima |date=October 8, 2023 |title=In Defence of: Mamma Mia! |url=https://www.redbrick.me/in-defence-of-mamma-mia/ |access-date=February 18, 2025 |work=[[Redbrick (newspaper)|Redbrick]] |quote=Mamma Mia! has always received mixed reviews since its glamorous debut in 2008}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Fretts |first=Bruce |date=December 21, 2017 |title=New Trailer: 'Mamma Mia! The site's consensus states: "This jukebox musical is full of fluffy fun but rough singing voices and a [[Camp (style)|campy]] tone might not make you feel like 'You Can Dance' the whole 90 minutes."<ref name=rt>{{cite web | url=https://rottentomatoes.com/m/mamma_mia/ | title=Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again'' was announced on May 19, 2017, and premiered on July 18, 2018.<ref>{{cite news|last=Alexander|first=Bryan|title='Mamma Mia' sequel 'Here We Go Again' coming next summer|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/movies/2017/05/19/mamma-mia-sequel-here-we-go-again-coming-next-summer/101899234/|work=US Today|date=May 19, 2017|access-date=May 19, 2017|archive-date=August 21, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200821045028/https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/movies/2017/05/19/mamma-mia-sequel-here-we-go-again-coming-next-summer/101899234/|url-status=live}}</ref> It was written and directed by [[Ol Parker]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2017/05/mamma-mia-here-we-go-again-theaters-release-next-summer-1202098572/|title='Mamma Mia: Here We Go Again!' On Universal Pictures' 2018 Schedule|last=Busch|first=Anita|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=May 19, 2017|access-date=May 28, 2017|archive-date=August 4, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200804011533/https://deadline.com/2017/05/mamma-mia-here-we-go-again-theaters-release-next-summer-1202098572/|url-status=live}}</ref> It was announced that Seyfried,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2017/06/amanda-seyfried-joins-mamma-mia-sequel-1202105360/|title=Amanda Seyfried Set For 'Mamma Mia!' Reprise|last=Fleming|first=Mike Jr.|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|date=June 1, 2017|access-date=June 1, 2017|archive-date=June 4, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170604081759/https://deadline.com/2017/06/amanda-seyfried-joins-mamma-mia-sequel-1202105360/|url-status=live}}</ref> Cooper, Streep, Firth and Brosnan would be returning.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.etonline.com/movies/220110_dominic_cooper_dishes_on_returning_for_mamma_mia_2_it_a_phone_call_i_ve_been_waiting_for/|title=Dominic Cooper Dishes on Returning for 'Mamma Mia 2': It's 'a Phone Call I've Been Waiting For'|last=Seemayer|first=Zach|publisher=[[Entertainment Tonight]]|date=June 22, 2017|access-date=June 22, 2017|archive-date=July 21, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180721014227/https://www.etonline.com/movies/220110_dominic_cooper_dishes_on_returning_for_mamma_mia_2_it_a_phone_call_i_ve_been_waiting_for|url-status=live}}</ref> In July 2017, [[Lily James]] was confirmed to portray young Donna.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/868357-mamma-mia-sequel-casts-baby-drivers-lily-james|title=Mamma Mia Sequel Casts Baby Driver's Lily James|last=Busch|first=Jenna|publisher=Comingsoon.net|date=July 12, 2017|access-date=July 16, 2017|archive-date=August 15, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190815190133/https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/868357-mamma-mia-sequel-casts-baby-drivers-lily-james|url-status=live}}</ref> The film took almost five months to film and was released in London and Sweden on July 16, 2018, and was released worldwide on July 20, 2018. The film was a commercial success and made $402 million worldwide with a $75 million budget. Reviews were generally positive, with critics praising the performances and musical numbers. The film was released digitally on October 9, 2018, and on DVD on October 23, 2018. It held the top spot on the charts for the week ending November 3, 2018.
