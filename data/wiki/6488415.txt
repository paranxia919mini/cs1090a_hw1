'''''Guys Next Door''''' is an American sketch comedy television series aired on [[NBC]] in the 1990–91 season as part of its Saturday morning lineup.<ref name=Daytime>{{cite book |last1=Hyatt |first1=Wesley |title=The Encyclopedia of Daytime Television |date=1997 |publisher=Watson-Guptill Publications |isbn=978-0823083152 |url=https://archive.org/details/encyclopediaofda00hyat/page/202/mode/2up|accessdate=22 March 2020|page=203}}</ref> It featured a [[boy band]] performing comedy sketches and spoofs, as a combination of [[New Kids on the Block]] with ''[[The Monkees (TV series)|The Monkees]]''.<ref>{{cite book |last1=Leszczak |first1=Bob |title=Single Season Sitcoms of the 1990s: A Complete Guide |date=2018 |publisher=McFarland & Co |isbn=9781476670775 |page=79 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=C3JuDwAAQBAJ&dq=%22guys+next+door%22&pg=PA79 |accessdate=25 March 2020}}</ref> The show starred Patrick J. Dancy, Eddie Garcia, Bobby Leslie, [[Damon Sharpe]], and Chris Wolf.  Several music videos were created for the individual episodes; they were produced by Joseph Sassone and directed by Dominic Orlando.

As a band, the group was signed to [[SBK Records]] and released a self-titled album, which spawned a single titled "I've Been Waiting for You". The single peaked at #42 on the [[Billboard Hot 100]] singles chart on February 23, 1991.<ref>[https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/1991-02-23/ Billboard Hot 100 Singles, February 23, 1991]</ref>

While Wolf and Leslie dropped out of the public eye after the show ended, Dancy and Garcia are still active as actors.  Sharpe is currently a music producer and songwriter who has worked with such artists as [[Jennifer Lopez]], [[Kylie Minogue]], [[Anastacia (singer)|Anastacia]], [[Kelly Rowland]], [[Big Time Rush (TV series)|Big Time Rush]], [[New Boyz]], and [[Monica (singer)|Monica]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.hitquarters.com/index.php3?page=intrview%2F2003%2FMarch3_15_27_0.html |title=A&R, Record Label / Company, Music Publishing, Artist Manager and Music Industry Directory |website=www.hitquarters.com |access-date=17 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170823072238/http://www.hitquarters.com/index.php3?page=intrview%2F2003%2FMarch3_15_27_0.html |archive-date=23 August 2017 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

The series sparked criticism by parents, teachers, and media experts due to airing a targeted program for young adult audiences on Saturday mornings that was since attended to be a marathon for children, causing the series to be cancelled after one season.<ref>Osborne, Barbara (Summer 1997).
