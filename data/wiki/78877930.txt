'''''Move Ya Body: The Birth of House''''' is a 2025 American [[documentary film]] directed by [[Elegance Bratton]]. It follows [[Disco Demolition Night]] and the beginning of [[house music]].

It had its world premiere at the [[2025 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 26, 2025.

==Premise==
In July 1979, [[Disco Demolition Night]] occurred, where records were destroyed featuring music primarily from Black artists, which led to the beginning of [[house music]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://impactpartnersfilm.com/film/move-your-body-the-birth-of-house/|title=Move Ya Body: The Birth of House|website=[[Impact Partners]]|date=19 September 2024 |access-date=January 13, 2025}}</ref>

==Production==
In June 2023, it was announced [[Elegance Bratton]] would direct a documentary revolving around Disco Demolition Night.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/global/roger-ross-williams-hillary-clinton-disco-demolition-hiddenlight-1235657012/|title=Hillary Clinton's HiddenLight Teams With Roger Ross Williams' One Story Up, Elegance Bratton for Disco Demolition Night Documentary (EXCLUSIVE)|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Manori|last=Ravindran|date=June 29, 2023|access-date=January 13, 2025}}</ref>

==Release==
It had its world premiere at the [[2025 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 26, 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2024/12/sundance-film-festival-lineup-2025-movies-1236200107/|title=Sundance 2025: JLo, Sly Stone, Putin, Ayo Edebiri, André Holland, & Ex-NZ PM Jacinda Ardern Films Among Park City Festival Offerings|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|first1=Anthony|last1=D'Alessandro|first2=Dominic|last2=Patten|date=December 11, 2024|access-date=January 13, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://festival.sundance.org/program/film/675328ef707ccdd5490898a3|title=Move Ya Body: The Birth of House|website=[[Sundance Film Festival]]|access-date=January 13, 2025}}</ref>
