'''''The Stranger and the Fog''''' ([[Persian language|Persian]]: غریبه و مه, <small>[[Romanization of Persian|romanized]]:</small> Gharibe va meh) is a 1974 Iranian [[Historical drama|period drama]] film written and directed by [[Bahram Beyzai]]. The peaceful lives of the people in a village are disrupted when a mysterious, suspicious man with no memory appears in a boat. It stars Khosrow Shoja'zadeh, [[Parvaneh Massoumi]], Manuchehr Farid, Esmat Safavi, Vali Shirandami, Sami Tahassoni, Reza Yaghouti, and Esmail Pourreza.<ref>{{Cite web |title=The Stranger and the Fog {{!}} Wexner Center for the Arts |url=https://wexarts.org/film-video/stranger-and-fog |access-date=2024-11-16 |website=wexarts.org |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=The Stranger and the Fog |url=https://miff.com.au/program/film/the-stranger-and-the-fog |access-date=2024-11-16 |website=MIFF 2024 |language=en}}</ref>

In 2023, the film was restored in 4K by [[Janus Films]] and [[Martin Scorsese]]'s [[The Film Foundation]]. The restored version premiered at the [[2023 New York Film Festival|New York Film Festival]] and the [[2023 BFI London Film Festival|BFI London Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Janus Films — The Stranger and the Fog |url=https://www.janusfilms.com/films/2209 |access-date=2024-11-16 |website=www.janusfilms.com |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Bergeson |first=Samantha |date=2024-08-19 |title=Bahrām Beyzaie's Surreal 'The Stranger and the Fog' Is Restored in 4K for 50th Anniversary — Watch Trailer |url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/trailers/the-stranger-and-the-fog-4k-trailer-bahram-beyzaie-1235037867/ |access-date=2024-11-16 |website=IndieWire |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Plot ==
In a remote seaside village, the peaceful lives of the residents are upended when they spot a mysterious boat drifting toward the shore. Upon pulling it in, they find a weary and wounded stranger named Ayat, who has no memory of how he got there. He only recalls being attacked and barely escaping with his life. As Ayat tries to rebuild his sense of self and uncover the truth about his past, he becomes a source of curiosity and suspicion in the community.
