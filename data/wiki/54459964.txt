'''''High Life''''' is a 2018 [[science fiction film|science fiction]] film directed by [[Claire Denis]], in English, written by Denis and her long-time collaborator [[Jean-Pol Fargeau]]. Starring [[Robert Pattinson]] and [[Juliette Binoche]], it focuses on a group of criminals sent on a space mission toward a [[black hole]] while taking part in scientific experiments.

Physicist and black hole expert [[Aurélien Barrau]] was hired as a consultant, and Danish-Icelandic artist [[Olafur Eliasson]] designed the film's spacecraft.<ref name="Screen" /> ''High Life'' premiered on 9 September 2018 at the [[2018 Toronto International Film Festival|Toronto International Film Festival]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/tiff-2018-lineup-beautiful-boy-life-more-1129445|title=Toronto: Timothee Chalamet Starrer 'Beautiful Boy,' Dan Fogelman's 'Life Itself' Among Festival Lineup|last=Vlessing|first=Etan|work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=2018-07-24|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180802015025/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/tiff-2018-lineup-beautiful-boy-life-more-1129445|archive-date=2 August 2018|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2018/film/news/high-life-bought-a24-robert-pattinson-juliette-binoche-1202939713/|title='High Life,' Starring Robert Pattinson and Juliette Binoche, Nabbed by A24|work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Dave|last=McNary|date=12 September 2018|access-date=15 January 2019}}</ref>

==Plot==
{{hatnote|The film is presented in a [[nonlinear narrative]]. The following is a linear summary of the plot.}}
A group of criminals serving death sentences are sent on a mission in space to [[Penrose process|extract alternative energy from a black hole]]. Each prisoner is treated as a guinea pig by Dibs, a scientist, for her experiments; Dibs is fixated on trying to create a child in space through [[artificial insemination]], but has yet to succeed. Sexual activity between prisoners is prohibited.

The ship is equipped with "The Box", a device in a small room that is obsessively used by the crew to [[masturbation|masturbate]], while Dibs administers sedatives to the passengers. Monte, the only [[celibacy|celibate]] prisoner, rejects Dibs' sexual advances. He is serving a life sentence for [[manslaughter]] as a child. Monte's only friend on the ship is Tcherny, who is drawn to the onboard garden because it reminds him of life back on Earth.

Pregnant prisoner Elektra delivers a baby, who dies later before she does as well. The captain, Chandra, develops [[leukemia]] from radiation and has a stroke before being [[euthanasia|euthanized]] by Dibs. One night, male prisoner Ettore attempts to [[rape]] Boyse. Nansen, the pilot, intervenes, but Ettore attacks and overpowers her. Monte arrives, throws Ettore off Boyse, and beats him. When Monte leads Boyse away to be treated, Mink stabs Ettore to death in the hallway. Dibs begins doubling the amount of sedatives each prisoner receives, later sneaking into Monte's cell and [[Rape of males#Female-on-male rape|raping him]] while he is sedated. She then [[Sperm theft|injects]] his [[semen]] into Boyse, who produces a healthy child that Dibs dotes on, but Monte is unaware that he is the baby's father.

As the ship approaches the black hole, Nansen prepares to pilot a shuttle around it. Unbeknownst to the other prisoners, Boyse kills Nansen with a shovel and takes her place. The shuttle travels through a molecular cloud that alters its trajectory and causes it to dive into the black hole, where Boyse dies gruesomely due to [[spaghettification]]. Mink later attacks Dibs and injures her, but is then killed by Monte. Dibs informs him that the child is his before ejecting herself into space. Tcherny commits suicide and Monte buries his body in the garden according to his wishes. Now the only survivor, Monte removes the bodies from the ship's morgue, dresses them in spacesuits, and releases them into the vacuum of space.

Monte struggles to raise the child, whom he has named Willow. He attempts to make repairs to the ship, but her frantic cries through his helmet speakers cause him to drop a tool and lose it in space. Much later, when Willow has grown into a teenager, they encounter another ship similar to their own. Monte boards the ship, but finds it carrying stray dogs who have survived by eating one another. Willow begs Monte to bring one back, but he refuses, implying contamination could potentially sicken or kill them. The ship moves closer to the black hole, and Willow convinces Monte to board a shuttle with her and execute the [[Penrose process]] that, if successful, is supposed to slingshot them away from the black hole. No longer wearing space suits, Monte takes Willow's hand, and they walk together to a yellow light source that grows ever larger and envelops them.

==Production==
===Background===
[[Claire Denis]] had the idea of the project in her mind for fifteen years.<ref name="Cahiers">{{cite web|url=http://www.robertpattinsonau.com/2017/05/14/print-claire-denis-talks-high-life-robert-pattinson-his-unflinching-desire-and-our-mutual-recognition-have-done-their-work-cahiers-du-cinema/|title=Claire Denis Talks High Life & Robert Pattinson "his unflinching desire and our mutual recognition have done their work"|website=Robertpattinsonau.com|date=14 May 2017|access-date=4 July 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170627015637/http://www.robertpattinsonau.com/2017/05/14/print-claire-denis-talks-high-life-robert-pattinson-his-unflinching-desire-and-our-mutual-recognition-have-done-their-work-cahiers-du-cinema/|archive-date=27 June 2017|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref> She said, "I had a screenplay which was naturally in English, because the story takes place in space and, I don't know why, but for me, people speak English—or Russian or Chinese—but definitely not French in space."<ref name="philip">{{cite web|url=http://www.indiewire.com/2016/03/claire-denis-on-how-philip-seymour-hoffman-inspired-high-life-and-the-heartrending-charisma-of-robert-pattinson-102813/|title=Claire Denis on How Philip Seymour Hoffman Inspired 'High Life' And The "Heartrending Charisma" of Robert Pattinson|last=Jagernauth|first=Kevin|work=[[IndieWire]]|date=25 May 2016|access-date=4 July 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160630234737/http://www.indiewire.com/2016/03/claire-denis-on-how-philip-seymour-hoffman-inspired-high-life-and-the-heartrending-charisma-of-robert-pattinson-102813/|archive-date=30 June 2016|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref>

High Life was initiated by producer Oliver Dungey of London-based The Apocalypse Films Company when he approached Denis to make a film in English on the theme of Femme Fatale.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://filmmakermagazine.com/107167-elementary-force/|title=Elementary Force: Claire Denis on High Life|first=Paul|last=Dallas|website=Filmmakermagazine.com|date=14 March 2019|access-date=6 August 2026}}</ref> The film was co-produced by Alcatraz Films from Paris, The Apocalypse Films Company from London, Pandora Film Produktion from Cologne, and Madants from Warsaw as a French-German-British-Polish co-production, with financing from New York-based Andrew Lauren Productions and various European funds.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kiang |first=Jessica |date=2018-09-10 |title=Film Review: Robert Pattinson in ‘High Life’ |url=https://variety.com/2018/film/reviews/high-life-review-robert-pattinson-1202934199/ |access-date=2026-05-13 |website=Variety.com |language=en-US}}</ref>

Novelist [[Nick Laird]] co-wrote the screenplay for the film along with Denis, Fargeau and Geoff Cox. Laird's wife [[Zadie Smith]] also contributed to early drafts of the English version of the screenplay.<ref name="stage">{{cite web |url=https://thefilmstage.com/news/claire-denis-robert-Pattinson-led-high-life-will-feature-unwanted-insemination-and-black-holes/|title=Claire Denis' Robert Pattinson-Led 'High Life' Will Feature Unwanted Insemination and Black Holes |last=Newman |first=Nick |date=8 February 2016|website=Filmstage.com|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160209173621/https://thefilmstage.com/news/claire-denis-robert-pattinson-led-high-life-will-feature-unwanted-insemination-and-black-holes/|archive-date=9 February 2016|url-status=dead|access-date=4 July 2017|df=dmy-all}}</ref> Due to creative differences between Denis and Smith, Laird and Smith dropped out of the project as screenwriters, but Laird later served as the consultant for the script.<ref>{{cite web |last=Erbland |first=Kate |title='High Life' Director Claire Denis Explains Why Novelist Zadie Smith Departed Film: 'There Was Not a Word We Could Share' |url=https://www.indiewire.com/2018/10/high-life-claire-denis-zadie-smith-left-robert-pattinson-1202009125/ |website=IndieWire |date=3 October 2018}}</ref>

Denis went to the [[European Space Agency]]'s Astronaut Centre in Cologne to learn about human spaceflight exploration before the film started shooting.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://scienceandfilm.org/articles/3169/claire-denis-science-consultant-talks-about-high-life|title=Claire Denis' Science Consultant Talks About High Life|last=Epstein|first=Sonia|date=14 November 2018|website=Sloan Science & Film }}</ref>

Denis explained the practical reasons for the non-linear story, saying "I always wanted it to start with a man and a baby, as a ritual of two living persons with no despair in that moment. And then there would be the flashbacks bringing in all the despair."<ref name="hyperallergic-denis" /> Denis compared Pattinson's character to the knight [[Perceval]] of [[Arthurian legend]], and Binoche's character to [[Medea]] from [[Medea (play)|the Greek tragedy]].<ref name="hyperallergic-denis">{{cite web |date= 16 April 2019 | author = Dan Schindel |title= Claire Denis on Black Holes, Olafur Eliasson, and the Making of High Life |url= https://hyperallergic.com/495099/claire-denis-on-black-holes-olafur-eliasson-and-the-making-of-high-life/ |website=Hyperallergic }}</ref>

===Casting===
During the project's early stages, Denis had [[Vincent Gallo]] in mind for the lead role,<ref name="Cahiers" /> and wrote the film thinking of [[Philip Seymour Hoffman]]. The project later caught the attention of [[Robert Pattinson]]; according to Denis:<blockquote>Pattinson contacted the person who was doing the English casting. I was intrigued but I thought he was too young. Every time I went to London to meet some actors, he was there. His desire to work with me has never faltered. And now he is a little less young and it's perfect. […] It's strange, though, because it would be difficult to imagine anyone more unlike Philip Seymour Hoffman physically, but Robert is very enigmatic, with a powerful presence. He gives off an aura that immediately makes you want to film him.<ref name="philip" /></blockquote>Pattinson described his character as an astronaut and said, "He's a criminal who volunteers for a mission toward a black hole, but he realizes along the way that a doctor on board also wants to do sexual experimentation with humans in space."<ref name="begin" /> In late 2015, [[Patricia Arquette]] and [[Mia Goth]] joined the cast.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.indiewire.com/2015/10/patricia-arquette-and-mia-goth-join-robert-pattinson-in-claire-denis-sci-fi-high-life-plot-details-revealed-109596/|title=Patricia Arquette and Mia Goth Join Robert Pattinson in Claire Denis' Sci-Fi 'High Life,' Plot Details Revealed|last=Jagernauth|first=Kevin|work=[[IndieWire]]|date=26 October 2015|access-date=4 July 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170709131142/http://www.indiewire.com/2015/10/patricia-arquette-and-mia-goth-join-robert-pattinson-in-claire-denis-sci-fi-high-life-plot-details-revealed-109596/|archive-date=9 July 2017|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref> In September 2017, Arquette dropped out while the rest of the cast, including [[Juliette Binoche]] and rapper [[André 3000]], were also announced.<ref name="CastAnnounced">{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2017/film/markets-festivals/toronto-juliette-binoche-andre-benjamin-join-claire-denis-high-life-exclusive-1202551807/|title=Toronto: Juliette Binoche, André Benjamin Join Claire Denis' 'High Life' (EXCLUSIVE)|first=Brent|last=Lang|work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|date=8 September 2015|access-date=8 September 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171002233232/http://variety.com/2017/film/markets-festivals/toronto-juliette-binoche-andre-benjamin-join-claire-denis-high-life-exclusive-1202551807/|archive-date=2 October 2017|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref> Playing the role of baby Willow is Scarlett Lindsey, Pattinson's goddaughter. After Pattinson was unable to bond with the babies cast as Willow, he remembered that Lindsey was friendly around adults. Pattinson asked a favour of Lindsey's father to cast her in the film, which he described as a "massive gamble" that was ultimately paid off by her "incredible" performance.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Kaye|first=Don|date=April 17, 2019|title=Living the High Life with Robert Pattinson|url=https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/living-the-high-life-with-robert-pattinson/|access-date=February 10, 2022|website=[[Den of Geek]]}}</ref>

==Reception==
