'''''A Traveler's Needs''''' ({{Korean|여행자의 필요}}) is a 2024 South Korean [[drama film]] written and directed by [[Hong Sang-soo]], starring [[Isabelle Huppert]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/news/berlin-isabelle-huppert-hong-sang-soo-a-travelers-needs-1235881584/|title=Isabelle Huppert-Starring Berlin Competition Film 'A Traveler's Needs' Picked Up by Finecut|date=22 January 2024 |publisher=Variety}}</ref> It is the third collaboration between the director and the star, following ''[[In Another Country (film)|In Another Country]]'' and ''[[Claire's Camera]]''.

The film had its world premiere in the Main Competition of the [[74th Berlin International Film Festival]], where it won the [[Silver Bear Grand Jury Prize]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.koreaherald.com/view.php?ud=20240123000527|title=Hong Sang-soo's 'A Traveler's Needs' invited to 74th Berlinale|date=23 January 2024 |publisher=The Korea Herald}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/finecut-picks-up-hong-sangsoos-berlin-competition-drama-a-travelers-needs/5189705.article|title=Finecut picks up Hong Sangsoo's Berlin Competition drama 'A Traveler's Needs'|publisher=screendaily.}}</ref>

== Premise ==
A French woman ([[Isabelle Huppert]]), who initially played a child's recorder in a park and faced financial struggles, eventually became a French teacher for two women, finding solace in lying down on rocks and relying on [[makgeolli]] for comfort.<ref>{{Citation |title=A Traveler's Needs (2024) |url=https://letterboxd.com/film/a-travelers-needs/ |access-date=22 February 2024 |language=en}}</ref>
