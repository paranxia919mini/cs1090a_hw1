{{nihongo|'''''The Stranger Within a Woman''''' {{aka}} '''''The Thin Line'''''|女の中にいる他人|Onna no naka ni iru tanin|lead=yes}} is a 1966 Japanese [[Drama (film and television)|drama film]] directed by [[Mikio Naruse]]. It is based on the 1951 novel [[The Thin Line (novel)|''The Thin Line'']] by [[Edward Atiyah]].<ref name="galbraith" />

==Plot==
Isao and Masako Toshiro are what looks like a happily married middle-class couple with two children. One day, Sayuri, wife of close friend Ryukichi Sugimoto, is found strangled. As it turns out, Sayuri had an affair with another man. Isao, struggling with his conscience, confesses to Masako that he was the man Sayuri had the affair with, and was responsible for her death, although inadvertently. To preserve the family's reputation and sheltered life, Masako begs her husband to keep his deed a secret. Isao also confesses to Ryukichi, who slaps him in return, but refrains from bringing charges against him. When Isao eventually announces to turn himself in to the police, which he sees as the only way to find peace and maintain his personal honour, Masako poisons him with a [[Hypnotic|soporific]]. His death is classified as suicide. Some time later, while taking a walk on the beach with her children, Masako wonders how she will be able to live with her secret.

==Release and legacy==
''The Stranger Within a Woman'' was produced and distributed by [[Toho]] and received a [[roadshow theatrical release]] on January 25, 1966. The film was released with English subtitles in the United States by Toho International in July 1967.<ref name="galbraith" />

Edward Atiyah's novel was again adapted for the screen by [[Claude Chabrol]] for ''[[Just Before Nightfall]]'' (1971). ''The Stranger Within a Woman'' was also remade twice for Japanese television in 1981 (again scripted by [[Toshirō Ide]])<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tvdrama-db.com/drama_info/p/id-19014 |title=女の中にいる他人 (The Stranger Within a Woman |website=TV Drama Database |language=ja |access-date=31 January 2021}}</ref> and 2017.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nhk.or.jp/pd/onnanonaka/ |title=女の中にいる他人 (The Stranger Within a Woman) |website=NHK (Japan Broadcasting Corporation) |language=ja |access-date=31 January 2021}}</ref>
