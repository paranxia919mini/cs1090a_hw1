==Plot==
Japanese language teacher Mori arrives in [[Seoul]] to track down Kwon, a South Korean woman he fell for several years ago. Mori arrives in [[Bukchon Hanok Village|Bukchon]], a neighborhood in the center of the city which has a tranquil atmosphere and historical background. Hoping for a chance to see her, he stays at a guesthouse near Kwon's old home, and he's befriended by the elderly owner, Gu-ok, and her broke but sociable nephew Sang-won. Mori begins to frequent ''Jiyugaoka'' ("Hill of Freedom"), a local coffee shop owned by Young-sun, where he writes letters to Kwon. Then even though Young-sun already has a boyfriend, she and Mori become lovers.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Yun|first1=Ina|title=(In Focus) Hill of Freedom: Hong Sang-soo's daring new film, alternately funny and haunting|url=http://koreanfilm.or.kr/webzine/sub/column.jsp?mode=A_VIEW&wbSeq=75|website=Korean Cinema Today|accessdate=2014-10-30|date=22 September 2014}}</ref>
