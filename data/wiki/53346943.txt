{{Nihongo|'''''After the Rain'''''|恋は雨上がりのように|Koi wa Ameagari no Yō ni|{{lit|Love Is Like After the Rain}}|lead=yes}} is a Japanese [[manga]] series written and illustrated by [[Jun Mayuzuki]]. It was serialized in [[Shogakukan]]'s ''[[Monthly Big Comic Spirits]]'' magazine from June 2014 to November 2015, and later in ''[[Weekly Big Comic Spirits]]'' from January 2016 to March 2018. Its chapters were collected in ten {{Transliteration|ja|[[tankōbon]]}} volumes. In North America, [[Vertical (company)|Vertical]] licensed the manga and published it in a five-volume [[omnibus edition]] from September 2018 to September 2019.

A 12-episode [[anime]] television series adaptation by [[Wit Studio]] aired from January to March 2018 on [[Fuji TV]]'s [[Noitamina]] programming block. A [[live-action]] film adaptation was released in May 2018. The anime series has been licensed in English by [[Sentai Filmworks]] for home video and digital release.

By April 2018, the manga had over two million copies in circulation. In 2018, ''After the Rain'' won the 63rd [[Shogakukan Manga Award]] in the general category.

==Synopsis==
Akira Tachibana, a high school student working part-time at a family restaurant, starts falling in love with the manager, Masami Kondo, a forty-five-year-old divorcé with a young son. Akira struggles to determine why she is falling for Masami, and whether or not to reveal her feelings to him.

An [[anime]] television series adaptation was announced in March 2017.<ref name=anime>{{cite web|last=Loo|first=Egan|title=Koi wa Ameagari no You ni Romance Manga Gets Anime|url=http://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2017-03-01/koi-wa-ameagari-no-you-ni-romance-manga-gets-anime/.112838|website=[[Anime News Network]]|access-date=March 1, 2017|date=March 1, 2017|archive-date=March 7, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170307012040/http://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2017-03-01/koi-wa-ameagari-no-you-ni-romance-manga-gets-anime/.112838|url-status=live}}</ref> Produced by [[Fuji Television]], [[Aniplex]], [[DMM.com|DMM pictures]], [[Dentsu]] and [[Wit Studio]]; the series is directed by [[Ayumu Watanabe]], with Deko Akao handling series composition, Yuka Shibata designing the characters and Ryō Yoshimata composing the music.<ref>{{cite web|last=Hodgkins|first=Crystalyn|date=November 2, 2017|title=Koi wa Ameagari no You ni TV Anime Reveals New Visual, Main Staff|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2017-11-02/koi-wa-ameagari-no-you-ni-tv-anime-reveals-new-visual-main-staff/.123502|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180624101613/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2017-11-02/koi-wa-ameagari-no-you-ni-tv-anime-reveals-new-visual-main-staff/.123502|archive-date=June 24, 2018|access-date=January 11, 2018|website=[[Anime News Network]]}}</ref> The opening theme song is "Nostalgic Rainfall" by [[HoneyWorks|CHiCO with HoneyWorks]], while the ending theme song is "Ref:rain" by [[Aimer]]. It ran for 12 episodes from January 12 to March 30, 2018, on Fuji TV's [[Noitamina]] programming block.<ref>{{cite web|last=Pineda|first=Rafael Antonio|date=January 19, 2018|title=After the Rain Anime Listed With 12 Episodes|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/daily-briefs/2018-01-19/after-the-rain-anime-listed-with-12-episodes/.126638|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180119132455/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/daily-briefs/2018-01-19/after-the-rain-anime-listed-with-12-episodes/.126638|archive-date=January 19, 2018|access-date=January 19, 2018|website=[[Anime News Network]]}}</ref>

[[Amazon.com|Amazon]] streamed the series worldwide on their [[Amazon Video]] service. In March 2020, [[Sentai Filmworks]] announced the acquisition of the series for home video and digital release in North America.<ref>{{cite web|last=Sherman|first=Jennifer|title=Sentai Filmworks Licenses After the Rain Anime|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2020-03-05/sentai-filmworks-licenses-after-the-rain-anime/.157182|website=[[Anime News Network]]|access-date=March 5, 2020|date=March 5, 2020|archive-date=May 11, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200511054333/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2020-03-05/sentai-filmworks-licenses-after-the-rain-anime/.157182|url-status=live}}</ref> In the United Kingdom, the series is licensed by [[MVM Films]] and it was released on [[Blu-ray]] on October 26, 2020.<ref>{{cite web|last=Osmond|first=Andrew|author-link=Andrew Osmond (journalist)|title=MVM Will Release Blu-rays of After the Rain and Moribito on October 26|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2020-08-07/mvm-will-release-blu-rays-of-after-the-rain-and-moribito-on-october-26/.162700|website=[[Anime News Network]]|access-date=August 7, 2020|date=August 7, 2020|archive-date=September 10, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200910192849/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2020-08-07/mvm-will-release-blu-rays-of-after-the-rain-and-moribito-on-october-26/.162700|url-status=live}}</ref>

===Live-action film===
A [[live-action]] film adaptation of the manga was announced in November 2017. It stars [[Nana Komatsu]] as Akira Tachibana and [[Yo Oizumi]] as the manager Masami Kondo.<ref name="Cast2"/> The film was distributed by [[Toho]] and premiered on May 25, 2018.<ref>{{cite web|last=Pineda|first=Rafael Antonio|url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2018-01-24/live-action-after-the-rain-film-video-reveals-additional-cast-may-opening/.126841|title=Live-Action After the Rain Film's Video Reveals Additional Cast, May Opening|date=January 24, 2018|website=[[Anime News Network]]|access-date=September 28, 2020|archive-date=October 24, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024100942/https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2018-01-24/live-action-after-the-rain-film-video-reveals-additional-cast-may-opening/.126841|url-status=live}}</ref>
