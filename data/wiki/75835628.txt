'''''Sugarcane''''' is a 2024 [[documentary]] film, directed by [[Julian Brave NoiseCat]] and [[Emily Kassie]] and produced by Emily Kassie and Kellen Quinn. It follows an investigation into the [[Canadian Indian residential school system]], igniting a reckoning in the lives of survivors and descendants.

It had its world premiere at the [[2024 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 20, 2024 where it won the Grand Jury award for Directing.<ref>{{cite web |title=2024 Sundance Film Festival Announces Award Winners - sundance.org |url=https://www.sundance.org/blogs/2024-sundance-film-festival-announces-award-winners |date=26 January 2024 |access-date=February 4, 2024 |archive-date=February 4, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240204184441/https://www.sundance.org/blogs/2024-sundance-film-festival-announces-award-winners |url-status=live }}</ref> It was theatrically released in limited engagements in the United States and Canada  on August 9, 2024, before gradually expanding to other cities starting August 16, by [[National Geographic (American TV channel)|National Geographic Documentary Films]] through [[Variance Films]] in the United States and Films We Like in Canada. It received critical acclaim and won the award for [[National Board of Review Award for Best Documentary Film|Best Documentary]] at the [[National Board of Review]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 Archives |url=https://nationalboardofreview.org/award-years/2024/ |access-date=2024-12-04 |website=National Board of Review |language=en-US |archive-date=December 4, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241204200831/https://nationalboardofreview.org/award-years/2024/ |url-status=live }}</ref> At the [[97th Academy Awards]], it was nominated for the [[Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature Film|Best Documentary Feature Film]] award.

==Premise==
''Sugarcane'' follows an investigation into [[Saint Joseph's Mission (Williams Lake)|Saint Joseph's Indian Residential School]], an [[Canadian Indian residential school system|Indian residential school]] located near [[Williams Lake, British Columbia]], igniting a reckoning in the lives of survivors and descendants.<ref name=":0">{{cite web|url=https://www.impactpartnersfilm.com/films/sugarcane|title=Sugarcane|website=[[Impact Partners]]|access-date=January 17, 2024|archive-date=February 20, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240220025114/https://www.impactpartnersfilm.com/films/sugarcane|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://nationtalk.ca/story/williams-lake-first-nation-focused-film-sugarcane-to-premiere-at-sundance-film-festival|title=Williams Lake First Nation-Focused film 'Sugarcane' to premiere at Sundance Film Festival|website=Nation Talk|date=December 11, 2023|access-date=January 17, 2024}}</ref>
