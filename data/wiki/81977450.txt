'''''André Is an Idiot''''' is a 2025 American documentary film by Tony Benna.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gajewski |first=Ryan |date=November 13, 2025 |title=Sundance Winner ‘André Is an Idiot’ Lands 2026 Release From Joint Venture (Exclusive) |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/andre-is-an-idiot-documentary-release-sundance-1236425746/ |access-date=December 29, 2025 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref>
The film premiered at the [[2025 Sundance Film Festival]], and was given a [[limited theatrical release]] on March 6, 2026, by Joint Venture.

==Summary==
It explores the story of André Ricciardi, who considers himself an idiot for not getting a colonoscopy and gets a terminal cancer diagnosis.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cooper |first=Will |date=March 17, 2025 |title=‘André is an Idiot’ is the best doc from Sundance 2025 |url=https://www.michigandaily.com/arts/sundance-2025-andre-is-an-idiot-is-a-heartbreaking-and-hilarious-doc/ |access-date=December 29, 2025 |website=The Michigan Daily |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Release ==
The film premiered at the [[2025 Sundance Film Festival|Sundance Film Festival]] on January 24, 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Patten |first=Anthony D'Alessandro,Dominic |date=December 11, 2024 |title=Sundance 2025: JLo, Sly Stone, Putin, Ayo Edebiri, André Holland, & Ex-NZ PM Jacinda Ardern Films Among Park City Festival Offerings |url=https://deadline.com/2024/12/sundance-film-festival-lineup-2025-movies-1236200107/ |access-date=February 23, 2026 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |language=en-US}}</ref> The film had a [[limited theatrical release]] in the United States on March 6, 2026, by Joint Venture.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hazelton |first=John |title=Sundance prize winner ‘Andre Is An Idiot’ goes to Joint Venture for North America |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/sundance-prize-winner-andre-is-an-idiot-goes-to-joint-venture-for-north-america/5210950.article |access-date=December 29, 2025 |website=Screen |language=en}}</ref> The film was released a month early in the United Kingdom by [[Dogwoof]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dalton |first=Ben |date=February 23, 2026 |title=UK-Ireland film cinema release dates: latest updates for 2026 |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/uk-ireland-film-cinema-release-dates-latest-updates-for-2026/5211864.article |access-date=February 23, 2026 |website=[[Screen Daily]] |language=en}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical response===
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|97||60|A public service announcement with a disarming sense of humor, ''André is an Idiot'' brings a light touch to a morbid topic and is all the more powerful for it.|ref=yes|access-date=April 5, 2026}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 78 out of 100, based on 13 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''André Is an Idiot'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/ |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date= |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/andre-is-an-idiot/|title=''André Is an Idiot''|website=[[Metacritic]]|access-date=March 21, 2026}}</ref>

Peter Howell of the ''[[Toronto Star]]'' considers the film one of his 10 favorite films from the Sundance Film Festival.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Howell |first=Peter |date=February 6, 2025 |title=Sundance’s best: Our critics picks his 10 favourite movies from the festival |url=https://www.thestar.com/entertainment/movies/sundances-best-our-critics-picks-his-10-favourite-movies-from-the-festival/article_df10e336-df1b-11ef-8536-1fee7d71d406.html |access-date=December 29, 2025 |website=Toronto Star |language=en}}</ref> Leslie Felperin of ''[[The Guardian]]'' rated the film four out of five and called it "a riotously funny, painfully honest film about facing death."<ref>{{Cite news |last=Felperin |first=Leslie |date=February 4, 2026 |title=André Is an Idiot review – a riotously funny, painfully honest film about facing death |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/feb/04/andre-is-an-idiot-film-review-cancer-death |access-date=February 7, 2026 |work=The Guardian |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>
