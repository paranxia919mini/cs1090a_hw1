'''''By the Stream''''' ({{Korean/auto|hangul=^수유천|rr=yes}}, also known as ''Suyoocheon'') is a 2024 [[Cinema of South Korea|South Korean]] [[drama film]] written and directed by [[Hong Sang-soo]].<ref name="var">{{cite news |last1=Frater |first1=Patrick |title=Hong Sang-soo's 'By the Stream' Picked Up by Korea's Finecut Ahead of Locarno Premiere |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/news/hong-sang-soo-locarno-by-the-stream-1236064547/ |access-date=10 July 2024 |agency=Variety}}</ref> In August 2024, the film was selected to compete for the [[Golden Leopard]] at the [[77th Locarno Film Festival]], where [[Kim Min-hee (actress, born 1982)|Kim Min-hee]] won [[Best Performance Award (Locarno International Film Festival)|Pardo for Best Performance Award]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Locarno77: The Pardo d'Oro goes to Akiplėša (Toxic) by Saulė Bliuvaitė, a striking vision of the teenage female body as a battleground|url=https://www.locarnofestival.ch/press/press-releases/2024/palmares-locarno77-pardo-d-oro-to-akiplesa.html|website=[[Locarno Film Festival]]|date=August 17, 2024|access-date=August 18, 2024|archive-date=August 17, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240817213653/https://www.locarnofestival.ch/press/press-releases/2024/palmares-locarno77-pardo-d-oro-to-akiplesa.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

== Premise ==
Art instructor Jeonim asks her uncle Sieon, a former actor, to write and direct a play for the theatre festival at the university where she teaches. Her boss, Jeong, is a fan of Sieon, and the pair develop feelings for each other to Jeonim's dismay.<ref name="plot">{{cite news |last1=Lodge |first1=Guy |title='By the Stream' Review: Hong Sangsoo's Wry, Strangely Sweet Ode to Art, Love and Eel |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/by-the-stream-review-1236108756/ |access-date=19 August 2024 |agency=Variety}}</ref>

== Release ==
''By the Stream'' debuted on 16 August 2024 in the ''Concorso Internazionale'' competition at the [[77th Locarno Film Festival]].<ref name="locarno">{{cite web |title=Suyoocheon |url=https://www.locarnofestival.ch/festival/program/film.html?fid=6407996a-75bb-4889-b731-dbc5d6d7b892&l=en&eid= |website=Locarno Film Festival |access-date=10 July 2024}}</ref> Ahead of the film's premiere, it was acquired for international sales by Finecut,<ref name="var" /> from whom [[The Cinema Guild|Cinema Guild]] purchased North American theatrical distribution rights.<ref name="dist">{{cite news |last1=Frater |first1=Patrick |title=Hong Sang-soo's Locarno-Bound 'By the Stream' Set for North American Release (EXCLUSIVE) |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/news/hong-sang-soo-by-the-stream-north-american-release-1236091199/ |access-date=6 August 2024 |agency=Variety}}</ref>

In September 2024, ''By the Stream'' will screen in the Centrepiece section of the [[Toronto International Film Festival]], marking the film's North American debut.<ref name="tiff">{{cite web |last1=Pond |first1=Steve |title=Toronto Film Festival Adds 43 International Movies From 41 Countries |url=https://www.thewrap.com/toronto-international-film-festival-movies-countries-tiff/ |website=The Wrap |date=6 August 2024 |access-date=6 August 2024}}</ref> The film is also slated to screen at the [[2024 New York Film Festival]].<ref name="nyff">{{cite web |last1=Lattanzio |first1=Ryan |title=NYFF Reveals Main Slate: Sean Baker, Mike Leigh, Brady Corbet Join Lineup
|url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/festivals/nyff-2024-reveals-main-slate-anora-the-shrouds-more-1235032878/ |website=Indiewire |date=6 August 2024 |access-date=6 August 2024}}</ref>
