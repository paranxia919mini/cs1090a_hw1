'''''The Birthday''''' is a 2004 Spanish film directed by [[Eugenio Mira]] starring [[Corey Feldman]], [[Jack Taylor (actor)|Jack Taylor]], and Erica Prior. It features elements of comedy, thriller, horror, and fantasy.<ref name="hoy" />

== Plot ==
{{More plot|section|date=January 2024}}
Set in 1987, the plot follows Norman Forrester, a guy who attends the birthday of his girlfriend's father, involving himself in a conspiracy pertaining to [[doomsday cult]]s.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.elmundo.es/metropoli/2006/11/15/cine/1076367618.html|website=Metrópoli|via=[[El Mundo (Spain)|El Mundo]]|author=A.B|title=Intensidad claustrofóbica|date=17 November 2006}}</ref>

== Production ==
The screenplay was penned by Mikel Alvariño alongside Eugenio Mira.<ref name="fotogramas">{{Cite web|url=https://www.fotogramas.es/peliculas-criticas/a10815/the-birthday/|website=[[Fotogramas]]|date=29 May 2008|title=The Birthday}}</ref> Mira also scored the film.<ref name="ep" /> Shooting locations included [[Terrassa]].<ref name="ep">{{Cite web|url=https://www.europapress.es/cultura/cine-00128/noticia-corey-feldman-protagoniza-the-birthday-primera-pelicula-eugenio-mira-historia-spielberg-david-lynch-20040409130512.html|website=[[Europa Press (news agency)|Europa Press]]|date=9 April 2004|title=Corey Feldman protagoniza 'The Birthday', primera película de Eugenio Mira, una historia "entre Spielberg y David Lynch"}}</ref>

== Release ==
The film premiered in the official selection of the 2004 [[Sitges Film Festival]].<ref name="defez">{{Cite web|url=https://www.elperiodico.com/es/ocio-y-cultura/20230125/the-birthday-eugenio-mira-jordan-peele-nueva-vida-81759311|website=[[El Periódico de Catalunya]]|date=25 January 2023|first=Desirée de|last=Fez|title=Jordan Peele da una nueva vida a 'The birthday', la ópera prima de Eugenio Mira, 20 años después|author-link=Desirée de Fez}}</ref> Its festival run also included screenings at CineMuerte Film Festival, [[Fantasia International Film Festival]], and [[Fantastic Fest]].<ref name="defez" /> It was released theatrically in Spain on 10 November 2006.<ref name="fotogramas" /> In 2021, [[Filmin]] acquired streaming rights to the film.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://as.com/meristation/2021/09/29/betech/1632936862_022600.html|website=Meristation|via=[[As (newspaper)|As]]|date=29 September 2021|title=Estrenos Filmin para octubre 2021: Novedades y estrenos, la polémica Crash|first=César|last=Otero}}</ref> In 2023, [[Jordan Peele]] programmed screenings of ''The Birthday'' at [[Film at Lincoln Center]].<ref name="defez" /><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.screenslate.com/articles/i-avant-garde-im-artist-corey-feldman-conversation-michael-m-bilandic|website=ScreenSlate|title="I Like the Avant-Garde. I'm an Artist.": Corey Feldman in Conversation with Michael M. Bilandic|first=Michael M.|last=Bilandic|date=13 January 2023}}</ref> A 4K restoration of the film was released in [[Alamo Drafthouse Cinema]]s for one-night-only in the United States on 1 October 2024. It was released in select US cities on 11 October 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/trailers/the-birthday-trailer-corey-feldman-cult-film-4k-restoration-1235045806/|title=‘The Birthday’ 4K Restoration Trailer: Corey Feldman’s Unhinged Take on ‘The Shining’ Finally Gets a Theatrical Release|website=[[IndieWire]]|date=10 September 2024|access-date=13 September 2024}}</ref>
