The '''50th annual [[Toronto International Film Festival]]''' was held from September 4 to 14, 2025.<ref name=rogers>Jeremy Kay, [https://www.screendaily.com/news/tiff-rogers-sign-three-year-presenting-partner-deal/5200989.article "TIFF, Rogers sign three-year presenting partner deal"]. ''[[Screen Daily]]'', January 21, 2025.</ref> To mark the festival's 50th anniversary, the festival programmed a special series, The TIFF Story in 50 Films, to screen through June, July and August 2025 at the [[TIFF Lightbox]].<ref name=":0" />

The first programming announcement was that [[Colin Hanks]]' documentary film ''[[John Candy: I Like Me]]'', about the actor [[John Candy]], would open the festival.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ctvnews.ca/entertainment/article/john-candy-i-like-me-to-open-50th-edition-of-toronto-international-film-festival/|title=‘John Candy: I Like Me’ to open 50th edition of Toronto International Film Festival|website=[[CTV News]]|date=June 4, 2025|access-date=June 4, 2025}}</ref> The full program was released on August 12.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Erbland |first=Kate |date=2025-08-12 |title=This Year’s Full TIFF Schedule and Lineup Is Now Here, Complete with Six New Films |url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/festivals/tiff-2025-lineup-schedule-1235144596/ |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=IndieWire |language=en-US}}</ref> 

The festival courted controversy regarding its final programming decisions, specifically with respect to the inclusion, exclusion,<ref name=":3" /> and subsequent re-inclusion of the [[Cinema of Israel|Israeli documentary]] ''[[The Road Between Us: The Ultimate Rescue]]'',<ref name=":2" /> which depicts rescue efforts during the [[October 7 attacks]] in southern [[Israel]].<ref name=":1" />

''[[Hamnet (film)|Hamnet]]'' was the winner of the festival's [[Toronto International Film Festival People's Choice Award|People's Choice Award]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kay |first=Jeremy |title=‘Hamnet’ wins 2025 TIFF People’s Choice Award |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/hamnet-wins-2025-tiff-peoples-choice-award/5208888.article |access-date=2026-02-04 |website=Screen Daily |language=en}}</ref>

==Preparations==
Preparations for the 2025 festival included the renewal of the festival's sponsorship deal with [[Rogers Communications]], for three years through 2027.<ref name=rogers/> The sponsorship deal included a television special to air on [[Citytv]].<ref name=rogers/>

Organizers also discussed the possibility of moving the festival a week later than its current early September placement, in response to industry feedback that the tight scheduling of the [[Venice Film Festival|Venice]], [[Telluride Film Festival|Telluride]] and Toronto film festivals presents a logistical challenge for filmmakers and industry professionals in travelling to all three festivals;<ref name=market/> however, moving the festival would have had a ripple effect on other Canadian film festivals, such as [[Cinéfest Sudbury International Film Festival|Cinéfest Sudbury]], the [[Atlantic International Film Festival]] and the [[Calgary International Film Festival]], which are scheduled overlapping with or very soon after TIFF, and would create some of the same logistical challenges. They ultimately decided to retain the existing schedule.<ref name=market2/>

==Awards==
===Tribute Awards===
The first [[TIFF Tribute Awards|Tribute Award]] recipients were announced on July 31, including [[Guillermo del Toro]] as recipient of the director award, [[Hikari (director)|Hikari]] as recipient of the emerging talent award, [[Jodie Foster]] as recipient of the Share Her Journey groundbreaker award, and [[Lee Byung-hun]] as recipient of a special tribute award. [[Brendan Fraser]] served as honorary chair of the event.<ref>John Hazelton, [https://www.screendaily.com/news/tiff-tribute-awards-for-guillermo-del-toro-jodie-foster-hikari-lee-byung-hun/5207455.article "TIFF Tribute Awards for Guillermo del Toro, Jodie Foster, Hikari, Lee Byung Hun"]. ''[[Screen Daily]]'', July 31, 2025.</ref>

The remainder of the Tribute Award recipients were announced on August 21, including [[Catherine O'Hara]] as recipient of the Norman Jewison Award, [[Nina Hoss]] and [[Channing Tatum]] as recipients of the Performer Award, [[Kazu Hiro]] as winner of the ''Variety'' Artisan Award, [[Idris Elba]] as recipient of the Jeff Skoll Award in Impact Media, and filmmakers [[Zacharias Kunuk]] and [[Jafar Panahi]] as recipients of Special Tribute Awards.<ref>Etan Vlessing, [https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/tribute-awards-toronto-film-fest-1236343152/ "Idris Elba, Channing Tatum, Catherine O’Hara Set for Tribute Awards at Toronto Film Fest"]. ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'', August 21, 2025.</ref>

===Regular awards===
The festival's regular awards for films, including the [[Toronto International Film Festival People's Choice Award|People's Choice Award]], were announced at the conclusion of the festival.<ref name=hamnet>Pete Hammond, [https://deadline.com/2025/09/hamnet-toronto-film-festival-peoples-choice-award-1236530932/ "‘Hamnet’ Wins Oscar-Predictive Toronto Film Festival People’s Choice Award"]. ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'', September 14, 2025.</ref>

At [[2025 Cannes Film Festival|Cannes]], the festival announced plans to introduce a new International People's Choice award, to be presented to the most popular film from outside North America along the same model as the existing [[Toronto International Film Festival People's Choice Award|People's Choice Award]].<ref>Barry Hertz, "On the beaches of Cannes, TIFF maps outs its ambitious future: The Toronto International Film Festival talked up its 50th anniversary and its coming content marketplace". ''[[The Globe and Mail]]'', May 28, 2025.</ref> A new juried award was also introduced for Best Animated Short Film, separately from the existing awards for Canadian and international short films.<ref name=shortcuts>Melanie Goodfellow, [https://deadline.com/2025/08/tiff-shorts-2025-full-lineup-1236481015/ "Idris Elba’s ‘Dust To Dreams’ Starring Seal Set To Premiere In TIFF Shorts + Full Lineup"]. ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'', August 7, 2025.</ref>

==Film market==
As in recent years, the festival also included market screenings for film industry, not open to the general public.<ref>Jeremy Kay, [https://www.screendaily.com/news/features-from-uk-canada-nigeria-among-tiff-industry-selects-line-up/5207954.article "Features from UK, Canada, Nigeria among TIFF Industry Selects line-up"]. ''[[Screen Daily]]'', August 18, 2025.</ref> This is in preparation for the official launch of a full film market at the 2026 festival.

In February the festival announced the first members of the inaugural advisory committee for the market project, including former [[Marché du Film|Cannes Film Market]] head Jerome Paillard, film producers [[Niv Fichman]], Vincent Maraval and Noah Segal, former [[Société de développement des entreprises culturelles|SODEC]] president Monique Simard, [[Creative Artists Agency]] financier Roeg Sutherland, and Kerry Swanson of Canada's Indigenous Screen Office.<ref name=market>Jeremy Kay, [https://www.screendaily.com/news/tiff-unveils-market-advisory-committee-as-talks-continue-on-location-timing-of-2026-event/5202006.article "TIFF unveils market advisory committee as talks continue on location, timing of 2026 event"]. ''[[Screen Daily]]'', February 14, 2025.</ref> Additional advisory committee members were announced in May, including Moses Babatope, [[Michael Barker (film distributor)|Michael Barker]], Arianna Bocco, Janet Brown, Diana Bustamante Escobar, Vicki Dobbs, Jeongin Hong and Laura Michalchyshyn.<ref name=market2>Jeremy Kay, [https://www.screendaily.com/news/tiff-the-market-sets-2026-dates-adds-advisory-board-members/5205104.article "TIFF: The Market sets 2026 dates, adds advisory board members"]. ''[[Screen Daily]]'', May 16, 2025.</ref>

Charles Tremblay, formerly of film distributors Métropole Films and MK2, has been announced as the leader of the film market.<ref>Andrew Tracy, [https://playbackonline.ca/2025/08/29/tiff-taps-distribution-vet-charles-tremblay-as-head-of-market/ "TIFF taps distribution vet Charles Tremblay as head of market"]. ''[[Playback (magazine)|Playback]]'', August 29, 2025.</ref> The film market is expected, but has not been formally confirmed, to be held at the [[Metro Toronto Convention Centre]].<ref name=market2/>

== Controversy ==
On August 13, 2025 (a month before the festival), organizers of the festival withdrew the invitation to screen the Israeli documentary ''[[The Road Between Us: The Ultimate Rescue]]'', which depicts rescue efforts by retired Major General {{ill|Noam Tibon|he|נעם תיבון}} during the [[October 7 attacks]] in southern Israel. The cancellation cited a copyright claim, stating that the filmmakers did not obtain permission to use video footage recorded by Hamas during the attacks and concerns over possible disruptions as the reason for the decision.<ref name=":3">[https://ca.news.yahoo.com/tiff-pulls-invite-documentary-oct-013343952.html "TIFF pulls invite for documentary on Oct. 7 rescue"] – Yahoo News, August 13, 2025</ref> Critics noted that Hamas is designated as a terrorist organization by the Government of Canada,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Canada |first=Public Safety |date=2018-12-21 |title=Currently listed entities |url=https://www.publicsafety.gc.ca/cnt/ntnl-scrt/cntr-trrrsm/lstd-ntts/crrnt-lstd-ntts-en.aspx |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=www.publicsafety.gc.ca}}</ref> and argued that the decision effectively required Israeli filmmakers to seek approval from a group officially recognized as such in order to depict these events.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Welk |first=Brian |date=2025-08-15 |title=TIFF Accepts Oct. 7 Doc ‘The Road Between Us’ After Public ‘Pain and Frustration,’ Cameron Bailey Says |url=https://www.indiewire.com/news/breaking-news/tiff-pulls-invite-documentary-oct-7-the-road-between-us-1235144825/ |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=IndieWire |language=en-US}}</ref> Festival organizers defended their decision noting that they need to protect the festival from any potential copyright issues, and they impose this requirement on all films exhibited at the festival to either show copyright clearances or provide an indemnity to protect the festival from legal liability in the event that a copyright lawsuit is launched over the film's screening.<ref>{{Cite web |last=D'Alessandro |first=Anthony |date=2025-08-13 |title=TIFF Pulls Invite For October 7th Documentary ‘The Road Between Us’; Filmmakers Say Fest Has “Censored Its Own Programming” |url=https://deadline.com/2025/08/tiff-pulls-october-7-documentary-from-lineup-1236484968/ |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> 

On August 14, 2025, following public criticism and discussions with the filmmakers, the organizers reversed their decision and reinstated the documentary in the festival's lineup.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Ng |first=Curtis |date=2025-08-14 |title=TIFF and filmmaker Barry Avrich reach resolution to screen Oct. 7 doc amid outcry |url=https://www.thestar.com/entertainment/movies/tiff-and-filmmaker-barry-avrich-reach-resolution-to-screen-oct-7-doc-amid-outcry/article_3f3fe018-05dc-54f2-9516-c39680a46f13.html |access-date=2025-08-15 |website=[[Toronto Star]] |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":2">{{Cite news |last=Isai |first=Vjosa |date=2025-08-14 |title=In Reversal, Toronto Film Festival Will Screen Documentary About Oct. 7 Attack |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2025/08/14/world/canada/tiff-israel-oct-7-documentary-canceled.html |access-date=2026-02-03 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Loving |first=Casey |date=2025-08-15 |title=TIFF Re-Invites 'The Road Between Us' to Festival After Pulling Oct. 7 Documentary From Lineup |url=https://www.thewrap.com/tiff-reinvites-the-road-between-us/ |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=TheWrap |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=D'Alessandro |first=Anthony |date=2025-08-15 |title=TIFF Officially Selects October 7th Documentary ‘The Road Between Us’ Amid Outcry |url=https://deadline.com/2025/08/tiff-the-road-between-us-in-lineup-1236489170/ |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref>
