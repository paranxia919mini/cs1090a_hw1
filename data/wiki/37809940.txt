'''''Recollections of the Yellow House''''' ({{langx|pt|Recordações da Casa Amarela}}) is a 1989 Portuguese film written and directed by [[João César Monteiro]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.timeout.com/london/film/recollections-of-the-yellow-house|title=Recollections of the Yellow House, directed by João César Monteiro {{!}} Film review|website=Time Out London|language=en|access-date=2019-04-08}}</ref>

==Plot==
João de Deus, a middle-aged man, lives in a cheap boarding house in an old part of Lisbon. He is plagued by illness. He relies on [[Franz Schubert]]'s music and films to stave off misery. Isolated and eccentric, João obsesses over his landlady’s daughter, engaging in odd and sometimes disturbing behaviours such as drinking her bathwater and collecting her hair. After harassing his landlord's daughters, he is thrown out of his room. He is sent to a mental hospital, but he eventually manages to escape through the sewers.<ref>{{Citation |last=Monteiro |first=João César |title=Recordações da Casa Amarela |date=1989-10-12 |type=Adventure, Comedy, Drama |url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0098175/ |access-date=2025-06-27 |others=João César Monteiro, Manuela de Freitas, Ruy Furtado |publisher=Invicta Filmes}}</ref>
