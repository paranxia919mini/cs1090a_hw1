'''''Nam June Paik: Moon Is the Oldest TV''''' is a 2023 [[documentary film]] by Amanda Kim about [[video art|video artist]] [[Nam June Paik]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://mubi.com/en/us/films/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv |title=MUBI |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240907164110/https://mubi.com/en/us/films/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Summary==
The film traces the life of the artist from his privileged childhood in [[Korea under Japanese rule|Japan-occupied Korea]] up until his death in 2006.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gleiberman |first=Owen |title=‘Nam June Paik: Moon Is the Oldest TV’ Review: A Tantalizing Portrait of the Video-Art Visionary |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-review-1235503341/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240923025540/https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-review-1235503341/ |archive-date=September 23, 2024 |access-date=September 7, 2024 |website=Variety}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://brooklynrail.org/2023/06/film/Amanda-Kims-Nam-June-Paik-Moon-is-the-Oldest-TV/ |title=The Brooklyn Rail |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 19, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240919130520/https://brooklynrail.org/2023/06/film/Amanda-Kims-Nam-June-Paik-Moon-is-the-Oldest-TV/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=American Masters – Nam June Paik: Moon Is the Oldest TV |url=https://hyperallergic.com/821965/american-masters-nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240923025444/https://hyperallergic.com/821965/american-masters-nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv/ |archive-date=September 23, 2024 |access-date=September 7, 2024}}</ref>

==Production==
The films uses [[archival footage]], interviews with fellow artists who knew him best and written letters to personal friends [[John Cage]] and others narrated by [[Academy Award]]-nominated actor [[Steven Yeun]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://filmforum.org/film/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv |title=Film Fourm |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240907164111/https://filmforum.org/film/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://hammer.ucla.edu/programs-events/2024/nam-june-paik-moon-oldest-tv |title=Hammer Museum |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240907164110/https://hammer.ucla.edu/programs-events/2024/nam-june-paik-moon-oldest-tv |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/may/17/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-review-video-warhol |title=The Guardian |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 23, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240923025541/https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/may/17/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-review-video-warhol |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2023/03/23/movies/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-review.html |title=The New York Times |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 23, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240923025445/https://www.nytimes.com/2023/03/23/movies/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-review.html |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-movie-review-2023 |title=Roger Ebert.com |access-date=September 7, 2024 |archive-date=September 23, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240923025445/https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/nam-june-paik-moon-is-the-oldest-tv-movie-review-2023 |url-status=live }}</ref>
