'''''Obsession''''' is a 2025<!-- PER WP:NAMING CONVENTIONS DO NOT CHANGE TO 2026 --> American<!-- DO NOT ADD ROMANCE --> [[supernatural horror film]] written, directed, and edited by [[Curry Barker]]. The film follows Bear ([[Michael Johnston (actor)|Michael Johnston]]), a music store employee who buys a supernatural toy that grants his wish for his friend Nikki ([[Inde Navarrette]]) to fall in love with him, which makes her become obsessed. [[Cooper Tomlinson]], [[Megan Lawless]], and [[Andy Richter]] appear in supporting roles.

A [[YouTube]] sketch comedian, Barker uploaded his short horror film ''The Chair'' to his channel in 2023. When producer James Harris approached him to adapt it into a feature film, Barker took the opportunity to pitch ''Obsession'', a different film idea of his partly inspired by ''[[The Simpsons]]'' episode "[[Treehouse of Horror II]]". The film was shot in Los Angeles in October 2024 on a $750,000 budget. It is Barker's second feature-length film, after ''[[Milk & Serial]]'' (2024), and first to be released in theaters.

''Obsession'' premiered on September 5, 2025, at the [[2025 Toronto International Film Festival|Toronto International Film Festival]] (TIFF), where [[Focus Features]] acquired the distribution rights for $14–15 million, the highest price commanded by a [[wikt:genre film|genre film]] in TIFF history, and [[Jason Blum]] came on board as an executive producer under [[Blumhouse Productions]]. The film was theatrically released in the United States on May 15, 2026. It was a critical and commercial success, grossing $517 million worldwide and setting multiple box-office records as the [[2026 in film#Highest-grossing films|tenth highest-grossing film of 2026]] and [[List of highest-grossing horror films|fifth highest-grossing horror film of all time]], not adjusted for inflation.

==Plot==
<!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summary should be between 400 and 700 words. Do not add unnecessary details. -->

Baron "Bear" Bailey harbors romantic feelings for his friend Nikki Freeman. The two work at a music store with their friends Ian and Sarah. Bear returns home one night to find his cat, Sandy, dead after consuming [[oxycodone]]. He later visits a crystal shop to buy Nikki a replacement crystal necklace, but instead buys her a "One Wish [[Willow]]", a [[novelty toy]] that claims to grant someone one wish when broken. After a group hangout that night, Nikki asks Bear if he likes her, but he is unable to admit his feelings. Frustrated, he breaks the One Wish Willow and wishes that Nikki loved him more than anyone in the world. Nikki immediately reappears and asks to stay with Bear, saying her estranged father is dying of cancer. In bed, Nikki kisses Bear but abruptly screams and pulls back.

The next morning, Nikki creates a memorial with Sandy's remains, and later explains her erratic behavior was the result of [[MDMA]]. After confessing their mutual feelings, they begin happily dating. During a date, Ian calls Bear and reveals that Nikki had told Sarah that she saw Bear as her "little brother" shortly before they began dating, and that she lied about her father's cancer. When Bear questions Nikki about this, she reacts with sudden defensiveness and anger. That night, Bear awakens to Nikki watching him sleep, and she cries hysterically, accusing him of not loving her.

The next day, Bear discovers that Nikki has duct taped the door shut in an attempt to keep him home, and later discovers the sandwich she prepared him was made using Sandy's remains. When Bear calls One Wish Willow's customer service to alter his wish, they say the wish will only expire when he dies before putting a screaming Nikki on the line, causing Bear to hang up in panic. He returns home to find that Nikki stood in place all day awaiting his return, and is covered in bodily fluids.

Nikki accompanies Bear to a party at Ian's house, against the latter's wishes. During a [[Jenga]] drinking game, Nikki recites a violent and [[incest]]uous retelling of "[[Hansel and Gretel]]" that she has written. Bear draws a block daring him to kiss the person to his left, who is Sarah. Nikki ousts Sarah from her seat and kisses Bear instead, before suddenly screaming and stabbing herself with a broken bottle. Bear drives Nikki to the hospital, but brings her back home after she refuses treatment.

That night, Sarah texts Bear to meet at the park. As Bear attempts to leave, a lucid Nikki speaks, claiming her obsessive personality is asleep, and begs Bear to kill her. He refuses, offended that she would rather die than continue to be with him. At the park, Sarah reveals that Ian and Nikki had been casually hooking up for two years and expresses concern that Nikki is using him for revenge against Ian. After Sarah hints at her own attraction to Bear, Nikki suddenly smashes the car window and repeatedly slams Sarah's head into a brick, killing her. After they move the body, Nikki instructs Bear to go home while she disposes of it.

Bear buys the remaining One Wish Willows from the shop to reverse his wish, but is unable to break one. He explains everything to Ian and begs him to undo the wish. Ian sarcastically wishes for a billion dollars instead, causing cash to rain from the ceiling. Bear returns home with the last One Wish Willow to get Nikki to reverse his wish and discovers Sarah's mutilated corpse. Nikki, dressed like Sarah, then threatens Bear and herself at gunpoint. Ian arrives and is immediately shot dead by Nikki. Bear locks himself in the bathroom and prepares to shoot himself but is unable to go through with it. He instead consumes the remaining oxycodone before changing his mind and tries to induce vomiting just as Nikki breaks the last One Wish Willow. Under her wish's influence, Bear exits the bathroom and kisses Nikki before dying from the overdose. Heartbroken, Nikki prepares to shoot herself, but Bear's death causes her to regain control of herself. Horrified by the carnage surrounding her, she wails hysterically.

===Casting===
Barker considered casting himself as Bear. In late 2023 or early 2024, the crew shot a screen test for the scene where Bear drops Nikki off at her place and first makes the wish, in which Tomlinson, who had already been cast as Ian, stepped in to play Bear.<ref name="THR-0623-Tomlinson"/> Casting was underway by August 2024.<ref name="VarietyMilkSerial" /> Barker wrote Nikki as both a threat and a victim and sought an actress capable of playing both a normal young woman and a frightening supernatural presence.<ref name="CouchSoup1">{{cite web |last=Carter|first=Andrea|title=The Real Villain of Obsession is Not Nikki|url=https://couchsoup.com/movies/the-real-villain-of-obsession-is-not-nikki/|website=Couch Soup|date=May 20, 2026 |access-date=May 26, 2026}}</ref><ref name="SlashFilm12">{{cite web |last=Bria|first=Bill|title=How Obsession Found The Perfect Actress For Nikki [Exclusive]|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/2173439/how-obsession-movie-found-perfect-actress-nikki/|website=[[/Film]]|date=May 15, 2026 |access-date=May 26, 2026}}</ref> Amid a long casting process, Mercuri championed [[Inde Navarrette]], who had appeared in three Capstone projects.<ref name="Deadline-0607"/> Navarrette was cast as Nikki after Barker felt she "nailed" a balance between crazy and scary and brought a natural, "[[Bro culture|bro]]-y kind of sassy" personality to the role that helped make Nikki believable before the horror elements emerged and made "Bear feel so much like he's in the friend zone".<ref name="Moveable" /><ref name="SlashFilm12"/> After a [[wikt:chemistry read|chemistry read]] with Navarrette, [[Michael Johnston (actor)|Michael Johnston]] was cast as Bear.<ref name="Moveable" />

===Filming===
To plan shots, minimize [[camera coverage]], and achieve practical in-camera effects, Barker and cinematographer Taylor Clemons [[previsualize]]d the film by 3D scanning locations and recreating them as digital models.<ref>{{cite web |last=Musnicky |first=Sarah |title=TIFF 2025: Curry Barker Directs The Camera At Us In ''Obsession'' |url=https://butwhytho.net/2025/09/curry-barker-obsession-interview/ |website=But Why Tho? |date=September 12, 2025 |access-date=June 26, 2026}}</ref> They shot the film "center-composed" with "extra head space" to create shots that felt "uncomfortable".<ref name="Microbudget" /> [[Principal photography]] began in late October 2024.<ref name="VarietyMilkSerial" /> It was filmed in 26 days, including reshoots after an initial 20-day shoot, in the Los Angeles area, covering five to six script pages daily.<ref name="MPA-BarkerInterview">{{cite web|url=https://www.motionpictures.org/2026/05/curry-barkers-obsession-the-indie-horror-that-turned-l-a-into-a-nightmare-playground/|title=Curry Barker's ''Obsession'' or The Indie Horror That Turned L.A.
