'''''Cocote''''' is a 2017 Dominican Republic [[drama film]] directed by [[Nelson Carlo de Los Santos Arias]]. It was selected as the Dominican entry for the [[Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film|Best Foreign Language Film]] at the [[91st Academy Awards]], but it was not nominated.<ref name="Dominican Republic">{{cite web |url=https://listindiario.com/entretenimiento/2018/09/12/532869/cocote-representara-republica-dominicana-en-los-premios-oscar-y-goya |title=“Cocote” representará a República Dominicana en los Premios Oscar y Goya |work=[[Listín Diario]] |first= |last= |date=12 September 2018 |accessdate=12 September 2018 |archive-date=13 September 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180913040306/https://listindiario.com/entretenimiento/2018/09/12/532869/cocote-representara-republica-dominicana-en-los-premios-oscar-y-goya |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="Dominican Republic2">{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/dominican-republic-selects-cocote-foreign-language-oscars-submission-1143374 |title=Oscars: Dominican Republic Selects 'Cocote' for Foreign-Language Category |work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |first=Agustin |last=Mango |date=14 September 2018 |accessdate=15 September 2018}}</ref>
