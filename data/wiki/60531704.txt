'''''Liberté''''' ({{langx|en|'''Liberty'''|italic=yes}}) is a 2019 [[drama film]] written and directed by [[Albert Serra]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/press/press-articles/communique/articles/the-screenings-guide-2019|title=The Screenings Guide 2019|website=[[Cannes Film Festival]]|date=9 May 2019|accessdate=9 May 2019}}</ref> Set in the 18th century, it tells the story of a group of libertines that spend a night of sexual debauchery in the forest. An international coproduction, the film had its world premiere in the [[Un Certain Regard]] section at the [[2019 Cannes Film Festival]] on 18 May 2019.<ref name="cineuropa371661"/><ref name="festival-cannes"/> It was the final film appearance of [[Helmut Berger]] before his death in May 2023.

==Plot==
In the 18th century, libertines spend a night of sexual debauchery in the forest.

==Production==
The film was preceded by an installation at the [[Reina Sofia Museum]] in Madrid and a theater play.<ref name="mubi"/> For the film, [[Albert Serra]] got inspiration from the French painters such as [[Alexandre-Évariste Fragonard]] and [[François Boucher]].<ref name="mubi"/> The cast consists of a mix of professional and non-professional actors.<ref name="mubi">{{cite web|url=https://mubi.com/notebook/posts/freedom-albert-serra|title=Freedom: Albert Serra|website=[[Mubi (streaming service)|Mubi]]|first=Flavia|last=Dima|date=3 July 2019|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref>

Initially the scene in which the men pour milk and semen on the totally naked body of Mademoiselle de Jensling, played by Iliana Zabeth, was supposed to be a little different. "In my main scene, for example, I was supposed to be tied up, but not suspended, this was decided later. Then I did a lot of research on shibari. Then other elements were added to the shooting, such as milk. We explored a lot on set," said Zabeth.<ref>{{cite web |title=" Liberté " : Entretien avec Iliana Zabeth, actrice |work=seances-speciales.fr |url=https://seances-speciales.fr/liberte-avec-iliana-zabeth-actrice/ |access-date=5 August 2022}}</ref>

Talking about the 'golden shower' scene, director Albert Serra revealed that the guy being urinated on by Theodora Marcadé (playing Madame de Dumeval) and by off-screen participants, wasn't a professional actor. "It's played by the guy who does the post-production of all my films, he came on set especially for that, it's the only time I made a request," Serra said.<ref>{{cite web |title=Entretien avec Albert Serra |work=www.lepolyester.com |url=http://www.lepolyester.com/entretien-avec-albert-serra/ |access-date=5 August 2022}}</ref>

The cast consists of a mix of professional and non-professional actors and includes two of the film's technicians. One of them is Laura Poulvet, who is the set decorator but also plays Mademoiselle de Geldöbel's character.<ref>{{cite web |title=Cannes gets steamy with sado-masochistic debauchery |via=PressReader |url=https://www.pressreader.com/cambodia/the-phnom-penh-post/20190522/281887299772927 |access-date=5 August 2022}}</ref>

==Release==
The film had its world premiere in the [[Un Certain Regard]] section at the [[2019 Cannes Film Festival]] on 18 May 2019.<ref name="cineuropa371661">{{cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/371661/|title=Films Boutique pulls out all the stops for Cannes|website=Cineuropa|first=Ioana|last=Florescu|date=3 May 2019|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref><ref name="festival-cannes">{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/festival/actualites/articles/liberte-as-seen-by-albert-serra|title=Liberté as seen by Albert Serra|website=[[Cannes Film Festival]]|first=|last=|date=18 May 2019|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref> It was also screened at the [[Toronto International Film Festival]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tiff.net/events/liberte|title=Liberté|website=[[Toronto International Film Festival]]|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref> the [[New York Film Festival]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.filmlinc.org/nyff2019/films/liberte/|title=Liberté|website=[[Film at Lincoln Center]]|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref> and the [[International Film Festival Rotterdam]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://iffr.com/en/2020/films/liberté|title=Liberté|website=[[International Film Festival Rotterdam]]|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref> It was released in France on 4 September 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allocine.fr/film/fichefilm_gen_cfilm=262678.html|title=Liberté|website=[[AlloCine]]|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical reception===
On review aggregator website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of {{RT data|score}} based on {{RT data|count}} reviews, with an average rating of {{RT data|average}}. The website's critical consensus reads, "It may prove more tedious than titillating for some, but ''Liberté'' boldly blurs the line between filmgoer and voyeur."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/liberte_2020|title=Freedom|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|publisher=[[Fandango Media|Fandango]]|access-date={{RT data|access date|df=dmy}}}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], the film has a weighted average score of 50 out of 100, based on 8 critics, indicating "mixed or average reviews".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/liberte|title=Liberté|website=[[Metacritic]]|publisher=[[CBS Interactive]]|accessdate=11 July 2020}}</ref>

Carson Lund of ''[[Slant Magazine]]'' gave the film 3 out of 4 stars, writing, "Serra's overriding impulse in ''Liberté'' is to bask in the atmospheric peculiarities of his fantasized scenario, a drive that places the film in closer proximity to an immersive museum exhibition than to narrative cinema."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.slantmagazine.com/film/review-liberte-audaciously-revels-in-an-amorphous-night-of-transgression/|title=Review: Liberté Audaciously Revels in an Amorphous Night of Transgression|website=[[Slant Magazine]]|first=Carson|last=Lund|date=5 September 2019|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref> Leslie Felperin of ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' commented that "even as pornography, it's not much of a source of joy, and the dialogue is even more risible than some of the worst efforts out of the San Fernando Valley."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/liberte-review-1212911|title='Liberte': Film Review {{!}} Cannes 2019|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|first=Leslie|last=Felperin|date=22 May 2019|accessdate=14 March 2020}}</ref>
