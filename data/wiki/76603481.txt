'''''Menus-Plaisirs – Les Troisgros''''' ({{lit|Lesser pleasures:{{efn|The French term ''menus-plaisirs'' literally means "minor pleasures," and also [[pun]]s on "[[menu]]". The [[Menus-Plaisirs du Roi]] was an organisation responsible for royal entertainments under the French [[Ancien Régime]].}} [[Troisgros family|The Troisgros [family]]]}}; {{IPA|fr|məny pleziʁ le tʁwaɡʁo|lang}}) is a 2023 [[French language|French-language]] [[documentary film]] written and directed by [[Frederick Wiseman]], in his final film before his death in February 2026. It follows the everyday activities of the French restaurant ''Le Bois sans feuilles''.

The film had its world premiere out of competition at the [[80th Venice International Film Festival]] on 9 September 2023.<ref name="ser" /> It received universal acclaim, being considered one of Wiseman's best works. 

==Synopsis ==
The film focuses on the [[Troisgros family]]'s [[Michelin Guide|Michelin 3-star]] restaurant "Le Bois sans feuilles" ('The Woods Without Leaves') and the farms which provide them the food, with more brief segments focusing on two other Troisgros' restaurants, Le Central and La Colline du Colombier.<ref name="nytimes">{{Cite news |last=Dargis |first=Manohla |author-link=Manohla Dargis |date=23 November 2023 |title=‘Menus-Plaisirs — Les Troisgros’ Review: A Beautiful Collaboration |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2023/11/22/movies/menus-plaisirs-troisgros-review-frederick-wiseman.html |access-date=13 April 2024 |work=[[The New York Times]]}}</ref>

Like Wiseman's other documentaries, the film uses [[Cinéma vérité|cinema verité]] observational footage with no interviews, music, or voiceover narration.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Seitz |first=Matt Zoller |title=Menus-Plaisirs Les Troisgros movie review (2023)|url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/menus-plaisirs-les-troisgros-movie-review-2023 |access-date=2024-03-15 |website=[[RogerEbert.com]] |language=en}}</ref>

== Production ==
The documentary was conceived in 2020, after Wiseman had lunch at the Troisgros restaurant and asked the chef to film a documentary without any prior planning. Filming took place with a four-person crew over 35 consecutive days in 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Nevins |first=Jake |date=2023-12-06 |title="The Drama Is on the Plate": Frederick Wiseman, in Conversation With Jean-Georges |url=https://www.interviewmagazine.com/film/frederick-wiseman-in-conversation-with-jean-georges |access-date=2024-03-15 |website=[[Interview (magazine)|Interview]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Release==
It premiered out of competition at the [[80th Venice International Film Festival|80th edition]] of the [[Venice Film Festival]].<ref name=ser>{{cite news |last1=Leffler |first1=Rebecca |title=The Party bites into Frederick Wiseman’s Venice premiere ‘Menus Plaisirs - Les Troisgros’ |url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/the-party-bites-into-frederick-wisemans-venice-premiere-menus-plaisirs-les-troisgros-exclusive/5184534.article |access-date=13 April 2024 |work=[[Screen International]]|date=31 July 2023 |language=en}}</ref> It was later screened in other festivals, including the [[2023 Toronto International Film Festival|Toronto Film Festival]], the [[28th Busan International Film Festival |Busan Film Festival]], the [[2023 New York Film Festival|New York Film Festival]], the [[36th Tokyo International Film Festival|Tokyo International Film Festival]], and the [[Sydney Film Festival]].<ref name="ser" /><ref>{{cite news |last1=Hudson |first1=David |title=Frederick Wiseman’s Menus-Plaisirs Les Troisgros |url=https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/8275-frederick-wisemans-menus-plaisirs-les-troisgros |access-date=13 April 2024 |work=[[The Criterion Collection|Criterion]] |date=4 October 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Frater |first1=Patrick |title=Tokyo Film Festival Gives Prominence to Chinese Titles in Competition and Gala Selections |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/news/tokyo-film-festival-competition-gala-selection-1235735107/ |access-date=13 April 2024 |work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |date=27 September 2023}}</ref>
