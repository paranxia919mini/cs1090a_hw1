'''''Anselm''''' ({{langx|de|'''Anselm – Das Rauschen der Zeit'''}}) is a 2023 German [[3D film|3D]] [[documentary film]] directed by [[Wim Wenders]], chronicling the art of German painter and sculptor [[Anselm Kiefer]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Bray|first=Catherine|date=17 May 2023|title='Anselm' Review: Wim Wenders Unveils a Magnificent 3D Portrait of German Artist Anselm Kiefer|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/anselm-review-wim-wenders-1235616517/|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref> The film had its world premiere at the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival|76th Cannes Film Festival]] on 17 May 2023 as a special screening, where it competed for the [[L'Œil d'or]].<ref name="loeildor">{{Cite web |title=Les documentaires à Cannes, découvrez la Sélection 2023 de L'Œil d'or |url=https://loeildor.scam.fr/actualites-ressources/les-documentaires-a-cannes-decouvrez-la-selection-2023-de-loeil-dor/ |website=[[L'Œil d'or|loeildor.scam.fr]] |date=12 May 2023 |language=fr}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
The film opens with footage of some of Kiefer's creations inside natural environments - including dresses with tree branches, sticks and glass shards; he has also made some towers out of wall fragments of destroyed rooms.

It shows the artist riding a bicycle around a large [[warehouse]] outside of [[Paris]] that contains many of his works. After a musical montage, the film depicts the artist's childhood drawing houses. Poetry written by [[Paul Celan]], a Ukrainian Jew who was persecuted during the [[The Holocaust|Holocaust]], is read out loud, the film features historic recordings by Celan himself.

Kiefer shows a book with progressively darker paintings symbolizing the progression of [[brain cancer]] in philosopher Heidegger. Footage is shown of Kiefer making a huge painting, to which he has attached He has attached dead leaves and crops. He torches these to create a burnt edge. His crew extinguish the flames immediately after. The finished products are half-burnt, and he has made a collection of them.

In the winter, Kiefer goes to a field and a forest, where he takes pictures with an [[Camera|analog camera]], obtaining inspiration for his next paintings.  He takes more pictures and draws more paintings inside an attic, with the film juxtaposing his current work with archival footage of what he did in the 1970s and 80s.  In the 1980s, while representing Germany at international competitions, his work was criticized for how his paintings invoked [[German folklore|the German legendary heroes]] whom the Nazis had glorified.

After looking through some books, he paints an enormous mural of a winter field with some creeks.  In the 1990s, he spends time doing aerial photography of various regions of Earth, making enormous books with printouts of the images and building an oversized library with them.  Analysts claim he wants his work to [[german war crimes|grapple with Germany's past]], described as an "open wound."

In the 1960s he traveled around Europe taking pictures of himself doing the [[Nazi salute|Nazi Salute]] in front of various European monuments in Nazi-occupied territories. He says it was to educate people about it.  In his childhood he read stories about [[Argonauts|Greek Argonaut]] mythology.  In 1990, after exhibitions in America, he built huge models of historic German  warplanes as the [[Gulf War]] was breaking out.  

Two years later, he and his team moved to a new studio and 200-acre property in the south of France. There he created huge installations of ruined building towers, in addition to enormous warehouses which housed other installations and works in progress. ith all of his works.

The next few scenes juxtapose his younger self playing in enormous mansions and discovering his older self's work, with his older self traveling around aimlessly.  This is until they both find themselves in the same room where his younger self reads poetry from the same book as at the beginning of the film.  Carrying his younger self to the edge of a lake, his older self remarks, "Childhood is an empty room like the beginning of the world."  An old man, standing in front of an art creation, disappears from existence.

==Production==
Described as "immersive",<ref name="Wiseman">{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2023/04/wim-wenders-3d-cannes-anselm-kiefer-documentary-hanway-1235328156/|title=HanWay Boards Wim Wenders 3D Cannes Doc 'Anselm' With Deals For France & Germany In The Bag; First-Look Image & Details|last=Wiseman|first=Andreas|date=17 April 2023|website=[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref> the film focuses on painter and sculptor [[Anselm Kiefer]]. The project illuminates his work, life journey, inspiration, creative process, and the artist's fascination with myth and history. The aim is to "blur" the boundary between film and painting.<ref name="filmsdulosange">{{cite web|url=https://filmsdulosange.com/film/anselm/|title=Anselm|publisher=[[Les Films du Losange]]|language=fr|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref>

Karsten Brünig produced the documentary for [[Road Movies Filmproduktion]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Kay|first=Jeremy|date=28 June 2023|title=Wim Wenders Cannes entry 'Anselm' scores US, international deals|url=https://www.screendaily.com/news/-wim-wenders-cannes-entry-anselm-scores-us-international-deals/5183543.article|magazine=[[Screen International]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref>

Wenders shot the film at [[6K resolution]] and in the [[3D film|3D]] format, which he previously utilized for his 2011 documentary ''[[Pina (film)|Pina]]''. It was shot over the course of two years,<ref name="Ritman">{{cite magazine|last=Ritman|first=Alex|date=28 June 2023|title=Wim Wenders' Cannes-Bowing Doc 'Anselm' Picked Up for U.S. by Sideshow and Janus|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/wim-wenders-anselm-sideshow-janus-us-1235525235/|magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref> in Germany, France and Italy.<ref name="Wiseman" /> Wenders called the film "a labor of love and turned out to take seven shooting periods and altogether three years to become a film like nothing I've ever done before. I think we stretched the possibilities of 3D into an unknown territory."<ref name="Ritman" />

==Release==
''Anselm'' was selected to be screened in the Special Screenings section at the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival|76th Cannes Film Festival]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2023/04/cannes-film-festival-2023-official-selection-list-1235323965/|title=Cannes Film Festival Lineup: Haynes, Anderson, Glazer, Kore-Eda, Wenders In Competition – Full List|last=Tartaglione|first=Nancy|date=13 April 2023|website=[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref> where it had its world premiere on 17 May 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |last=mraultpauillac |date=10 May 2023 |title=The Screenings Guide of the 76th Festival de Cannes |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2023/the-screenings-guide-of-the-76th-festival-de-cannes/ |access-date=10 May 2023 |website=Festival de Cannes |language=en-US}}</ref> On the same occasion, Wenders also received an invitation to the festival's main competition for his feature film ''[[Perfect Days]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Brzeski|first=Patrick|date=13 April 2023|title=Cannes Film Fest Doubles Down on Depps, Wim Wenders and Wang Bing|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/cannes-film-festival-2023-johnny-depp-wim-wenders-wang-bing-1235373242/|magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref>

World sales were acquired by [[HanWay Films]]. DCM Film Distribution secured the distribution rights for Germany, Austria and Switzerland.<ref name="Wiseman" /> It was also invited at the [[28th Busan International Film Festival]] in 'Icon' section and was screened on 6 October 2023.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.biff.kr/eng/html/program/prog_all_list.asp?allYear=2023 |title= The 28th Busan International Film Festival: Selection List |website=Busan International Film Festival|date=5 September 2023|access-date=11 September 2023|language=en}}</ref>

''Anselm'' was theatrically released in Germany by DCM on 12 October 2023,<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Roxborough|first=Scott|date=23 May 2023|title=Cannes: DCM Takes Wim Wenders Festival Double Pack for Germany|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/cannes-2023-wim-wenders-film-sales-perfect-days-anselm-1235498244/|magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=15 August 2023}}</ref> and in France by [[Les Films du Losange]] on 18 October 2023.<ref name="filmsdulosange" /> U.S. distribution rights were acquired by Sideshow and [[Janus Films]].<ref name="Ritman" />

==Reception==
===Critical response===
On [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 98% based on 55 reviews, with an average rating of 7.6/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "Visually thrilling and refreshingly unconventional, ''Anselm'' pays mesmerizing tribute to a brilliant artist's life and legacy."<ref>{{cite Rotten Tomatoes|title=Anselm|type=m|id=anselm|access-date=15 February 2025}}</ref> On [[Metacritic]], the film has a [[weighted average]] score of 82 out of 100, based on 24 critic reviews, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref>{{cite Metacritic|id=anselm|type=movie|title=Anselm|access-date=6 February 2024}}</ref>
