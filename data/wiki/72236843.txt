'''''Pacifiction''''' ({{langx|fr|'''Pacifiction – Tourment sur les îles'''}}) is a 2022 [[political drama]] film written and directed by [[Albert Serra]], and starring [[Benoît Magimel]]. The film premiered at the [[2022 Cannes Film Festival]], where it competed for the [[Palme d'Or]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/festival/films/pacifiction|title=Pacifiction|last= |first= |date= 27 May 2022|website=Festival de Cannes |access-date=12 November 2022 |quote=}}</ref> The film title is a [[portmanteau]] of the words ''Pacific'' and ''fiction.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Wham |first=James |date=15 July 2022 |title=Pacific Fictions |url=https://newleftreview.org/sidecar/posts/pacific-fictions |access-date=16 February 2023 |journal=NLR/Sidecar |doi=10.64590/kns |url-access=subscription }}</ref>''

==Plot==
"On the [[French Polynesia|French Polynesian]] island of [[Tahiti]], the [[List of colonial and departmental heads of French Polynesia|High Commissioner of the Republic]] and French government official De Roller is a calculating man with flawless manners. His somewhat broad perception of his role brings him to navigate the high-end 'establishment' as well as shady venues where he mingles with the locals. A persistent rumour also has been going around: The sighting of a submarine with a ghostly presence could herald the return of French nuclear testing."

==Release==
The film screened at the [[2022 Cannes Film Festival|75th Cannes Film Festival]] on 26 May 2022.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.lavanguardia.com/vida/20220526/8296723/albert-serra-desconcierta-cannes-bizarra-magnetica-pacifiction.html|website=[[La Vanguardia]]|date=26 May 2022|title=Albert Serra desconcierta en Cannes con la bizarra y magnética "Pacifiction|access-date=14 November 2022|first=Magdalena|language=es|last=Tsanis|archive-date=14 November 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221114012455/https://www.lavanguardia.com/vida/20220526/8296723/albert-serra-desconcierta-cannes-bizarra-magnetica-pacifiction.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> Distributed by [[Elastica Films|Elastica]], it was released theatrically in Spain on 2 September 2022.<ref name="cinemania" /> It was released by [[Les Films du Losange]] in France on 9 November 2022.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.francetvinfo.fr/culture/cinema/sorties-de-films/pacifiction-tourment-sur-les-iles-benoit-magimel-surnage-dans-un-thriller-despionnage-en-eaux-trop-calmes_5455795.html|title=''Pacifiction - Tourment sur les îles'' : Benoît Magimel surnage dans un thriller d'espionnage en eaux trop calmes|last=Belsoeur|first=Camille|website=[[France Info (TV channel)|www.francetvinfo.fr]]|date=5 November 2022 |language=fr|access-date=12 November 2022}}</ref>

==Reception==
On [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 87% based on 70 reviews, with an average rating of 7.7/10. The website's consensus reads, "Narratively sparse and visually vast, ''Pacifiction'' is a tsunami of a political thriller that philosophically rewards those willing to weather it."<ref>{{cite Rotten Tomatoes|title=Pacifiction|type=m|id=pacifiction|access-date=31 December 2022}}</ref> According to [[Metacritic]], which assigned a weighted average score of 75 out of 100 based on 18 critics, the film received "generally favorable reviews".<ref>{{cite Metacritic|id=pacifiction|type=movie|title=Pacifiction Reviews|access-date=31 December 2022}}</ref>

Reviewing the film following its Cannes premiere, [[Peter Bradshaw]] of ''[[The Guardian]]'' wrote that he was "captivated by the film and its stealthy evocation of pure evil."<ref>{{cite news|last=Bradshaw|first=Peter|author-link=Peter Bradshaw|date=26 May 2022|title=Pacifiction review – trouble in paradise, in apocalyptic Tahitian mystery|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2022/may/26/pacifiction-review-trouble-in-paradise-in-apocalyptic-tahitian-mystery|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|access-date=12 November 2022}}</ref> Christian Blauvelt of ''[[IndieWire]]'' wrote, "''Pacifiction'' is not a vicarious experience of luxury; it is an experience of life. Set to its own tidal rhythm, it is one of the most beautiful and rigorously introspective movies of this or any year."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/2022/10/pacifiction-review-albert-serra-1234771158/|title='Pacifiction' Review: Here's the Art Film of the Year|last=Blauvelt|first=Christian|date=12 October 2022|website=[[IndieWire]]|access-date=12 November 2022}}</ref> ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]''{{'}}s Leslie Felperin called it "Punishingly slow, though not uninteresting."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Felperin|first=Leslie|date=27 May 2022|title='Pacifiction': Film Review {{!}} Cannes 2022|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-reviews/pacifiction-film-cannes-2022-1235155244/|magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=12 November 2022}}</ref> Guy Lodge of ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' wrote, "Provided they stick with the film's luxuriantly gradual pacing... there are rewards here even for more bemused viewers caught in its tide."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Lodge|first=Guy|date=27 May 2022|title='Pacifiction' Review: Albert Serra's Torpid Tahitian Getaway is Cryptic But Curiously Hypnotic|url=https://variety.com/2022/film/reviews/pacifiction-review-1235278643/|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=12 November 2022}}</ref> Nando Salvá of ''[[Cinemanía]]'' rated the film 4½ out of 5 stars, underscoring the film to be "Romantic. Seductive. Hypnotic. Overwhelming" as a verdict.<ref name="cinemania">{{Cite web|url=https://www.20minutos.es/cinemania/criticas/critica-de-pacifiction-5046674/|first=Nando|last=Salvá|date=31 August 2022|access-date=13 November 2022|title=Crítica de 'Pacifiction'|website=[[Cinemanía]]|via=[[20minutos.es]]}}</ref>
