}}'''''King of Prism''''' is a Japanese media franchise produced by [[Avex Group|Avex Pictures]] and [[Tatsunoko Production]]. The series is a [[spin-off (media)|spin-off]] of the ''[[Pretty Rhythm]]'' multimedia series and focuses on the male characters introduced in ''Pretty Rhythm''{{'}}s third [[anime]] series, ''[[Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live]]'', aiming for an older female demographic.<ref>{{cite episode |title=King of Prism: Pride the Hero |url=https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/en/vod/imagine/20170613/ |url-status=dead |series=Imagine-Nation |network=[[NHK World-Japan]] |date=2017-06-13 |minutes=2:40 |quote='''Narrator (Dominic "Dynamite" Allen):''' The ''KinPri'' series originally came out of a 2013 TV anime for children called ''[[List of Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live episodes|Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live]]''. ''KinPri'' is an official spin-off story centered on a trio unit that appeared in the anime. |access-date=April 15, 2019 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20170613155423/https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/en/vod/imagine/20170613/ |archivedate=June 13, 2017 }}</ref>

''King of Prism'' debuted as a film series in 2016, with the first film, ''[[King of Prism by Pretty Rhythm]]'' releasing on January 9, 2016. It was then followed by ''[[King of Prism: Pride the Hero]]'' releasing on June 10, 2017, ''[[King of Prism: Shiny Seven Stars]]'' releasing on March 2, 2019, ''King of Prism All Stars: Prism Show ☆ Best Ten'' releasing on January 10, 2020, ''King of Prism: Dramatic Prism.1'' releasing on August 16, 2024 and ''King of Prism: Your Endless Call Minna Kirameki! Prism☆Tours'' releasing on June 27, 2025.

===Edel Rose===
{{nihongo|Edel Rose|エーデルローズ|Ēderu Rōzu}} is one of the top dance schools specializing in Prism Stars attached to the all-boys high school Kakyoin Academy and Le Celiana Girls Academy. It is run by [[List of Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live characters#Hijiri Himuro|Hijiri Himuro]]. Edel Rose's famous alumni includes Koji Mihama, Hiro Hayami, and Kazuki Nishina, who formed the group [[List of Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live characters#Over the Rainbow|Over the Rainbow]]. The new students are collectively referred to as {{nihongo|Edel Rose Freshmen|エーデルローズ新入生|Ēderu Rōzu Shinyūsei}}, but by the end of ''Shiny Seven Stars'', they debut as {{nihongo|Septentrion|セプテントリオン|Seputentorion}}.

:{{voiced by|[[Junta Terashima]],<ref name="ann 2015-11-07"/><ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.animatetimes.com/news/details.php?id=1555148268 | script-title=ja:春アニメ『KING OF PRISM -Shiny Seven Stars-』一条シン役・寺島惇太さんインタビュー {{!}} シンにスポットが当たるクライマックスは誰も予想できない怒涛な展開に!? | language=ja | work=[[Animate (retailer)|Animate]] | date=2019-04-15 | accessdate=2019-06-24 | archive-date=April 16, 2019 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190416055930/https://www.animatetimes.com/news/details.php?id=1555148268 | url-status=live }}</ref> [[Sayuri Hara]] (young)}}
:Shin is a 15-year-old aspiring Prism Star who idolizes Hiro Hayami. Unbeknownst to him, Rinne had sealed Shine, the male Prism Messenger, inside him, which also gave him the ability to perform in Prism Shows. As a Prism Star, he performs to "Over the Sunshine!" and has the ability to do a Prism Rush, which excites the audience and reminds them of when they first saw Prism Shows. Shin's Prism Jumps are "Over the Sunshine" (''King of Prism by Pretty Rhythm''), "Shining Splash" (jump 1), "Shin Infinite Hug" (jump 2), and "Rising Sunshine" (jump 3) (''Pride the Hero'').

===Schwarz Rose===
{{nihongo|Schwarz Rose|シュワルツローズ|Shuwarutsu Rōzu}} is a school for Prism Stars led by [[List of Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live characters#Jin Norizuki|Jin Norizuki]], who uses Spartan training to discipline students and has a large influence on media.

;{{nihongo|Louis Kisaragi|如月 ルヰ|Kisaragi Rui}}
:{{voiced by|[[Shouta Aoi]]}}
:Louis is Schwarz Rose's star pupil and Jin's favorite student, who claims to have crossed a millennium to see Shin. He is later revealed to be version 3.01 of the [[List of Pretty Rhythm: Rainbow Live characters#Rinne|Rinne]]-type messenger from the Prism World, who assumed the form of a boy after the Shine-type messenger went faulty. Having watched over Shin for a long time, he falls in love with him, but he remains loyal to Jin, as being in Schwarz Rose is the only way he can accomplish his mission.<ref>{{cite video game |title=King of Prism: Prism Rush! Live |developer=[[Avex Group|Avex Pictures]] |publisher=[[Avex Group|Avex Pictures]] |date=2017-08-05 |platform=[[IOS]] |level=Episode 7, Chapter 2 |quote='''如月 ルヰ:''' それは……。僕には目的があるから。 [Louis: That's because...... I have a goal to accomplish.]}}</ref> As a Prism Star, he performs to "Pride", "Lunatic Destiny", and "I Know Shangri-La." Louis' Prism Jumps are "Starlight Kiss", "Absolute Idol: I Love You", "100% Pure Pure Arrow", "Star Splash", "Bloom-Bloom-Blooming Love Flowers", "Whispering Lunamystic Heaven", "Aurora Rising", "Fluttering Rainbow Tail", "Infinite Hug Eternal", "Can't Stop Fall in Heaven," and "Dawn's Love Again."
;{{nihongo|Alexander Yamato|大和 アレクサンダー|Yamato Arekusandā}}
:{{voiced by|[[Shunsuke Takeuchi]]}}
:Alexander, sometimes called {{nihongo|Alec|アレク}} for short, specializes in street-style performances and views Kazuki as his rival. As a Prism Star, he performs to "[[EZ Do Dance]]." Alec's Prism Jumps are "666 Muscle Bomb", "Iron Six-Pack Core Wonder" and "Rolling Thunderstorm."
;{{nihongo|Joji Takadanobaba|高田馬場 ジョージ|Takadanobaba Jōji}}
:{{voiced by|[[Tomokazu Sugita]]}}
:Joji, whose real name is {{nihongo|Noriyuki Motokawa|本川則之|Motokawa Noriyuki}}, is a student at Schwarz Rose and idolizes Jin. He was formerly the leader of the Prism Star unit The Shuffle before being assigned a solo performer. He was introduced in ''[[King of Prism: Pride the Hero|Pride the Hero]]'' and [[Tomokazu Sugita]] was specifically chosen to voice him due to him promoting ''[[King of Prism by Pretty Rhythm]]'' on his social media.
;{{nihongo|Ace Ikebukuro|池袋 エィス|Ikebukuro Eisu}}
:{{voiced by|Takayuki Kobayashi}}
:Ace is Joji's ghost singer whose voice was featured in ''Pride the Hero'' before being introduced in ''Shiny Seven Stars'' as a new character. Initially Joji's protégé, Ace was later promoted to being The Shuffle's leader, consisting of members {{nihongo|Kokoro Gotanda|五反田 ココロ|Kokoro Gotanda|{{voiced by|Wataru Komada}}}}, {{nihongo|Mond Uguisudani|鶯谷 モンド|Uguisudani Mondo|{{voiced by|Norihito Hase}}}}, {{nihongo|Mitsuba Kanda|神田 ミツバ|Kanda Mitsuba|{{voiced by|Takuhiro Eda}}}}, and {{nihongo|Tsurugi Okachimachi|御徒町 ツルギ|Okachimachi Tsurugi|{{voiced by|Kotaro Hashimoto }}}}.

===Prism World===
;{{nihongo|Shine|シャイン|Shain}}
:{{voiced by|[[Mitsuki Saiga]]}}
:Shine is a male-type Prism Messenger from the Prism World who decides to join Prism Show tournaments under the belief that male Prism Stars are too selfish to spread the Prism Sparkle. Because performing in tournaments will prevent the Prism Sparkle from spreading, the Shine program is deemed defective and Rinne kills him under orders from the Prism World, sealing him inside Shin. As a Prism Star, he performs to the song "Platonic Sword" and is able to perform a Prism Axel, allowing him to perform seven Prism Jumps simultaneously.
;Prism Gods
:{{voiced by|[[Kenta Miyake]] (God I), [[Susumu Chiba]] (God II), [[Risa Hayamizu]] (God III), [[Himika Akaneya]] (God IV), [[Chihiro Suzuki]] (God V), [[Takuma Terashima]] (God VI), [[Miyu Kubota]] (God VII)}}
:The seven gods of the Prism World developed the Rinne/Shine program to lead Prism Stars. The gods consist of God I: M-m.G (Type M), God II: H.B. (Type M), God III: D.A. (Type F), God IV: C.S. (Type F), God V: N.P. (Type M), God VI: P.M. (Type M), and God VII: M.F. (Type F).
