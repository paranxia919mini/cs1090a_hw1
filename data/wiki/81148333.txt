'''''Smog''''' is a 1962 Italian comedy-drama film co-written and directed by [[Franco Rossi (director)|Franco Rossi]]. It premiered in competition at the [[23rd Venice International Film Festival|23rd edition]] of the [[Venice Film Festival]].<ref name="Laura">{{cite journal |last1=Verdone|first1= Mario |title=Smog |url= https://archive.org/details/bianco-e-nero/BN_1962_09_10/page/16/|journal=[[Bianco e Nero]] |date=September 1962 |issue=9 |pages=16–17 |access-date=21 September 2025}}</ref><ref name=hsb>{{cite news |last1=Mosk. |title=Smog |url=https://archive.org/details/variety-1962-09/page/n5/mode/1up?q=smog |access-date=21 September 2025 |work=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |volume=228 |issue=2 |date=September 1962 |page=6}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Celada |first1=Luca |title=Restored by HFPA: "Smog" (1962) - A Long-Lost Gem |url=https://goldenglobes.com/articles/restored-hfpa-smog-1962-long-lost-gem/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |work=[[Golden Globes]] |date=12 July 2022}}</ref> The film is regarded as the first Italian production entirely shot in the United States.<ref name="hsb" /><ref>{{cite news |last1=Gresleri |first1=Lapo |title="Smog" e l'un-american dream di Franco Rossi  |url=https://www.cinefiliaritrovata.it/smog-e-lun-american-dream-di-franco-rossi/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |work=Cinefilia Ritrovata |date=29 June 2023 |language=it-IT}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Cirami |first1=Gabriele |last2=Zancan |first2=Roberto  |date=31 August 2022 |title=Un film "dimenticato" del 1962 che può insegnarci molto sul modernismo |url=https://www.domusweb.it/it/architettura/2022/08/31/le-tre-architetture-moderne-di-la-nel-film-scomparso-di-franco-rossi-del-1962.html |access-date=21 September 2025 |work=[[Domus (magazine)|Domus]] |language=it-IT}}</ref>
