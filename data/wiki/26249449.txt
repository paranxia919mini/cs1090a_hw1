''Touching Me is Dangerous''|lead=yes}} is a [[1966 in film|1966]] [[Japan]]ese film directed by [[Yasuharu Hasebe]] and based on the novel {{nihongo2|三重露出}} by Michio Tsuzuki.<ref name="JMDB">{{cite web |url=http://www.jmdb.ne.jp/1966/cp000530.htm|script-title=ja:俺にさわると危ないぜ|accessdate=2010-02-18|language=Japanese|publisher=[[Japanese Movie Database]]}}</ref><ref>日活アクションの華麗な世界：1954-1971 第十三章無国籍性の持続-爛熟期における小林旭 p.340-359 暴力のプロたち-「夜の勲章」「俺にさわると危いぜ」その他 {{ISBN|4624710878}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
Daisuke Honda, a war photographer in Vietnam, meets Yuriko Sawanouchi, a stewardess on his plane back to Japan. After drinking with her at a Tokyo bar, he becomes involved in saving Yuriko from assassination by [[kunoichi|female ninjas]]. When trying to rescue Yuriko from kidnappers, Daisuke discovers a group of foreigners are hunting for a World War II-era treasure hidden on an island by Yuriko's father.<ref name="JCDB"/><ref>{{cite web |first=Jonathan |last=Crow|url=http://www.allmovie.com/work/black-tight-killers-201934|title=Black Tight Killers: Plot Synopsis|accessdate=2010-02-18|publisher=[[Allmovie]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.kinejun.jp/cinema/id/21862|script-title=ja:俺にさわると危ないぜ(邦画 )|accessdate=2010-02-18|language=Japanese|publisher=[[Kinema Junpo]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120324083742/http://www.kinejun.jp/cinema/id/21862|archive-date=2012-03-24|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Release==
''Black Tight Killers'' was released on February 12, 1966.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nikkatsu.com/movie/20920.html|publisher=[[Nikkatsu]]|title=俺にさわると危ないぜ|accessdate=August 31, 2016|language=Japanese}}</ref> It was released in the U.S. during this era.<ref name="Fentone"/><ref name="Midnight Eye"/> It was released in DVD format in Japan in 2005.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.allcinema.net/prog/show_c.php?num_c=160603|script-title=ja:俺にさわると危ないぜ(1966)|accessdate=2010-02-18|publisher=allcinema.net|language=Japanese}}</ref> [[Image Entertainment]] released the film on DVD in the United States. This DVD has the burned-in subtitles which were part of the film's original U.S. release.<ref name="Midnight Eye">{{cite web |first=Jasper |last=Sharp|url=http://www.midnighteye.com/reviews/blatikil.shtml|title=Black Tight Killers|accessdate=2010-02-18|publisher=midnighteye.com |date=2001-05-10}}</ref> It was released on [[Blu-ray]] by Radiance Films on February 27, 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.slantmagazine.com/dvd/black-tight-killers-blu-ray-review-hasebe-yasuharu/|work=[[Slant Magazine]]|accessdate=September 4, 2025|date=February 26, 2024|first=Derek|last=Smith|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240314073049/https://www.slantmagazine.com/dvd/black-tight-killers-blu-ray-review-hasebe-yasuharu/|archivedate=March 14, 2024|title=Review: Hasebe Yasuharu's Black Tight Killers on Radiance Films Blu-ray}}</ref>

==Critical appraisal==
Jonathan Crow of [[Allmovie]] notes that the influence of Hasebe's mentor [[Seijun Suzuki]] can be seen in ''Black Tight Killers''. Like Suzuki, he uses the tropes of the gangster genre to create "a pop-art dreamscape" with "tail fins, flawless fashion, sudden and unexpected go-go dancing, cool jazz, and freakish violence". Hasebe's quirky use of gaudy color is singled out for comment in the review, which judges the film to be "wild, decadent fun".<ref>{{cite web |first=Jonathan |last=Crow|url=http://www.allmovie.com/work/black-tight-killers-201934/review|title=Black Tight Killers: Review|accessdate=September 4, 2025|publisher=[[AllMovie]]|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160314092352/http://www.allmovie.com/work/black-tight-killers-201934/review|archivedate=March 14, 2016}}</ref>

In his survey of the ''[[pink film]]'' genre, Steve Fentone sums up ''Black Tight Killers'' with, "Chix with guns. What more do ya need?"<ref name="Fentone">{{cite journal |last=Fentone|first=Steve|year=1998|title=The Black Tight Killers; A Rip of the Flesh: The Japanese 'Pink Film' Cycle|journal=She|volume=2|issue=11|page=8}}</ref> [[Midnight Eye|Jasper Sharp]] writes that the plot is not especially impressive, but of Hasebe's visuals, he comments, "there is not a single individual sequence here that fails to deliver enough great dollops of saccharine-coated eye candy to satisfy even the most jaded visual gourmand". He concludes, "this simply magical film is a hoot from start to finish".<ref name="Midnight Eye"/>
