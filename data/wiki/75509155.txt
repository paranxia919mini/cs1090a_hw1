'''''Life of a Shock Force Worker''''' ({{Langx|sh|Slike iz života udarnika}}) is a 1972 [[Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia|Yugoslav]] feature film directed by [[Bahrudin Čengić|Bahrudin Bata Čengić]] and based on a script by [[Branko Vučićević]].<ref>{{Citation |last= |first= |title=Life of a Shock Force Worker - IMDb |url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0124111/ |type= |access-date= |others= |publisher=}}</ref> It belongs to the [[Yugoslav Black Wave]] movement.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Reizi |first=Paulina |date=3 September 2023 |title=A Season of Classic Films: Premiere of ‘Life of a Shock Force Worker’ in Biennale |url=https://ace-film.eu/a-season-of-classic-films-premiere-of-life-of-a-shock-force-worker-in-biennale/ |access-date= |website=Association des Cinémathèques Européennes |language=en-US}}</ref>

The film was inspired by the lives of coal miners who, due to their efforts in the first years of socialist Yugoslavia, were glorified and portrayed as [[national hero]]es. These workers were known as [[Udarnik|udarniks]] (shock force workers).

== Plot ==
[[File:Life of a Shock Force Worker still.jpg|thumb|left|Workers next to Adem's portrait|230x230px]]
The film consists of a series of connected vignettes in the life of Adem, a [[Bosniaks|Bosniak]] miner and his fellow udarniks, starting from his wedding. They are subsequently shown working in a mine, competing over who will mine more coal, and later continuing their numerous other competitions in their spare time. Adem receives honours and recognition for his hard work, but still leads a very modest life. He soon becomes an inspiration to other miners and the people around him. An artist paints Adem's portrait and has it placed right next to portraits of [[Josip Broz Tito|Tito]] and [[Joseph Stalin|Stalin]], which the workers carry around throughout the film. During his prolonged absence, he is suddenly left by his wife. When he and his comrades travel to [[Slovenia]] to receive an award, he begins a relationship with a Slovenian woman, also a shock force worker, and the two get married upon their return. A sculptor makes a [[Bust (sculpture)|bust]] of Adem. While Adem is working, a [[Chetniks|Chetnik]] threatens his wife. Upon learning about the presence of Chetniks nearby, a shootout erupts and Adem and his comrades emerge as victors. Now an official member of the [[League of Communists of Yugoslavia]], Adem spends even less time at home, angering his second wife. He travels to [[Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic|Russia]] as a representative of the workers. When he comes back home he finds her massacred by Chetniks, and gets hospitalised after being found unconscious in the mine. He insists on going back to work and leaves the hospital, reasoning that "a man can bear anything". The film ends in 1972, with an older Adem pondering the state of Yugoslavia's workforce.

== Background ==
The film takes place in the early days of socialist Yugoslavia, before the [[Tito–Stalin split|Tito-Stalin split]], and pays homage to coal miner and shock force worker [[Alija Sirotanović]], who is mentioned in the film. It tries to preserve the memory and legacy Yugoslav miners and the flaws of the system they worked in. The film begins in 1947 and follows the life and work of udarniks from the beginning of the udarnik movement until their retirement.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Slike iz života udarnika / Udruženje filmskih radnika u Federaciji Bosne i Hercegovine |url=https://bhfilm.ba/bs/film/649 |access-date=2023-12-07 |website=bhfilm.ba |language=bs-BA |archive-date=2023-12-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231207200936/https://bhfilm.ba/bs/film/649 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Restoration ==
The film was restored in 2022 by the [[Association of European Film Archives and Cinematheques]] as part of the ''Season of Classic Films'' project. The project was a collaboration between the Slovenian and Croatian cinematheques, the [[Sarajevo Film Center]], and the [[Austrian Film Museum]]. It was carried out under the supervisition of [[Karpo Godina|Karpo Aćimović Godina]], the film's director of photography.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-08-28 |title=Digitalno restaurirani film Slike iz života udarnika Bate Čengića u programu Venice Classics 2023. |url=https://havc.hr/infocentar/novosti/digitalno-restaurirani-film-slike-iz-zivota-udarnika-bate-cengica-u-programu-venice-classics-2023 |access-date=2023-12-07 |website=Havc.hr |language=hr}}</ref>
