'''''Occupied City''''' is a 2023 [[documentary film]] directed and produced by [[Steve McQueen (director)|Steve McQueen]], and based on the book ''Atlas of an Occupied City, Amsterdam 1940–1945'' by [[Bianca Stigter]].<ref name="cannes">{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/occupied-city/|title=OCCUPIED CITY|work=[[Festival de Cannes]]|accessdate=May 9, 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite book |last1=Stigter |first1=Bianca |author-link1= Bianca Stigter | date=2019 |title=Atlas van een bezette stad: Amsterdam 1940–1945 |location=Amsterdam |publisher=Atlas Contact |isbn=9789045029573}}</ref> The film features narration by Melanie Hyams,<ref name="variety"/> describing the [[Netherlands in World War II|occupation of the Netherlands by Nazi Germany]], juxtaposed against footage of modern-day [[Amsterdam]] echoing much of the narration. The film is a co-production between the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, and the United States.<ref name="cineuropa"/><ref name="screen">{{Cite web |last=Halligan |first=Fionnuala |date=17 May 2023 |title='Occupied City': Cannes Review |url=https://www.screendaily.com/reviews/occupied-city-cannes-review/5182060.article |website=[[Screen Daily]]}}</ref>

''Occupied City'' had its world premiere at the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival]] on 17 May 2023 as a special screening,<ref name="cannespremiere"/> where it competed for the [[L'Œil d'or]].<ref name="loeildor">{{Cite web |title=Les documentaires à Cannes, découvrez la Sélection 2023 de L'Œil d'or |url=https://loeildor.scam.fr/actualites-ressources/les-documentaires-a-cannes-decouvrez-la-selection-2023-de-loeil-dor/ |website=[[L'Œil d'or|loeildor.scam.fr]] |date=12 May 2023 |language=fr}}</ref> It was released in the United States on 25 December 2023, and in the United Kingdom and Ireland on 9 February 2024.

==Production==
In January 2022, it was confirmed that [[Steve McQueen (director)|Steve McQueen]] would direct and produce the film about [[Netherlands in World War II|Amsterdam under Nazi occupation during World War II]], with [[A24]] and [[New Regency]] handling the domestic and international and both co-financing. [[Film4]] also provided co-financing and retains United Kingdom linear television rights.<ref>{{Cite web|title=A24 & New Regency Aboard For Steve McQueen WWII Documentary ''Occupied City''|url=https://deadline.com/2022/01/steve-mcqueen-film-a24-new-regency-occupied-city-documentary-world-war-two-1234905955/|last=Wiseman|first=Andreas|date=2022-01-07|website=Deadline|language=en|access-date=2022-01-07}}</ref>

McQueen said that there is a 36-hour version of the documentary and that he shot everything that is covered in the source material, the nonfiction book ''Atlas of an Occupied City, Amsterdam 1940–1945'' by [[Bianca Stigter]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Coyle |first=Jake |date=17 May 2023 |title=In Steve McQueen's 4-hour Cannes entry 'Occupied City,' Holocaust past meets Amsterdam present |url=https://apnews.com/article/cannes-steve-mcqueen-occupied-city-documentary-fc466e5c2fbf76550cd27100563ec315 |website=[[Associated Press]]}}</ref>

==Release==
The film premiered at the [[2023 Cannes Film Festival]] as a special screening on 17 May 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-04-13 |title=Steve McQueen's 'Occupied City' will premiere at Cannes |url=https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/steve-mcqueen-occupied-city-cannes-film-festival/ |access-date=2023-04-13 |website=faroutmagazine.co.uk |language=en-US}}</ref><ref name="cannespremiere"/> It also screened at the [[50th Telluride Film Festival]] on 31 August 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/awards/telluride-lineup-the-bikeriders-rustin-saltburn-1235707476/|title=Telluride Lineup Includes World Premieres of 'Saltburn,' 'The Bikeriders' and 'Rustin' With Tributes for Yorgos Lanthimos and Wim Wenders|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Clayton|last=Davis|date=August 30, 2023|access-date=August 30, 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://telluridecms-production.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/pdfs/50th-TFF-program-guide-FINAL.pdf|title=Program Guide|website=[[Telluride Film Festival]]|access-date=August 30, 2023}}</ref>

A24 released the film in North America and New Regency released it internationally,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ravindran |first=Manoru |date=16 May 2023 |title=Steve McQueen on His 4-Hour Documentary Epic 'Occupied City' and How It's Informing His Apple TV+ Movie 'Blitz' |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/global/steve-mcqueen-blitz-occupied-city-cannes-1235614183/ |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> while September Film released it in the Netherlands.<ref name="fonk"/> The film was released in the United States on 25 December 2023 <ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/video/occupied-city-steve-mcqueen-documentary-trailer-debut/ |title=Watch Trailer For Steve McQueen's 'Occupied City': Epic Doc On Nazi Campaign Against Amsterdam's Jews, Seen From Present Day |last=Carey |first=Matthew |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |date=October 24, 2023 |access-date=October 24, 2023}}</ref> and in the United Kingdom and Ireland on 9 February 2024 through Modern Films, with a nationwide live Q&A from the [[Barbican Centre]] in London.

===Extended cut===
The [[Rijksmuseum]] in [[Amsterdam]] presented a 34-hour version of the film that was projected silently and continuously onto the museum’s south façade from 12 September 2025 until 25 January 2026.

During that time, in the museum’s auditorium, a version with sound and voice-over was shown during museum hours on selected days, including a full continuous screening of the entire 34-hour version that ran from the morning of 11 October 2025 until evening the following day.<ref>{{cite web |title=Steve McQueen to screen 34-hour film Occupied City on the Rijksmuseum's façade |url=https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/press/press-releases/steve-mcqueen-to-screen-34-hour-film-occupied-city-on-the-rijksmuseum-s-facade |website=Rijksmuseum.nl |publisher=[[Rijksmuseum]] |access-date=13 September 2025}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical response===
The [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]] reported an approval rating of 65% based on 37 reviews with an average rating of 6.9/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "''Occupied City'' is an impressively ambitious attempt to connect the past with the present, although its repetitive approach and overwhelming length will be too much for many viewers."<ref>{{Cite Rotten Tomatoes |id=occupied_city|type=movie |access-date=12 January 2024}}</ref> [[Metacritic]] assigned the film a weighted average score of 77 out of 100 based on 14 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".<ref>{{cite web |title=Occupied City Reviews |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/occupied-city |website=[[Metacritic]] |access-date=29 December 2023}}</ref>

Reviewing the film following its Cannes premiere, Peter Bradshaw of ''[[The Guardian]]'' called it a "monumental film" that "allows its emotional implication to amass over its running time."<ref>{{cite news|last=Bradshaw|first=Peter|author-link=Peter Bradshaw|date=May 17, 2023|title=Occupied City review – Steve McQueen's moving meditation on wartime Amsterdam|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/may/17/occupied-city-review-steve-mcqueens-moving-meditation-on-wartime-amsterdam|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|access-date=17 May 2023}}</ref>
