'''''Snow White''''' is a 1916 American [[silent film|silent]] [[romantic fantasy]] film directed by [[J. Searle Dawley]]. [[Winthrop Ames]] adapted it from his own 1912 Broadway play ''[[Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1912 play)|Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs]]'', which was in turn adapted from the [[Snow White|1812 fairy tale]] (as collected by the [[Brothers Grimm]]). The film stars [[Marguerite Clark]] and [[Creighton Hale]], Clark reprising her stage role.<ref name=SF/>

Having seen the film at the age of fifteen, [[Walt Disney]] was inspired to use the Brothers Grimm fairy tale as the subject of [[Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937 film)|his first feature-length animated film]] in 1937.<ref name=SF/>

==Preservation status==
Formerly thought to have been destroyed in a vault fire and presumed [[lost film|lost]], a "substantially complete" print with Dutch intertitles, missing a few scenes, was found in [[Amsterdam]] in 1992 and restored at [[George Eastman House]].<ref name=SF/>

==Home media==
''Snow White'' is included in the boxed DVD set ''[[Treasures From American Film Archives|Treasures from American Film Archives: 50 Preserved Films]]'' (2000).<ref name=silentera/>
