'''''Armand''''' is a 2024 [[Thriller (genre)|thriller]] [[drama (film and television)|drama film]] written and directed by Norwegian filmmaker [[Halfdan Ullmann Tøndel]], in his [[feature directorial debut]].<ref name="Keslassy">{{cite magazine|last=Keslassy|first=Elsa|date=26 June 2023|title='The Worst Person in the World' Producers, Cannes Breakout Star Renate Reinsve Reteam on 'Armand' (EXCLUSIVE)|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/global/joachim-trier-renate-reinsve-the-worst-person-in-the-world-cannes-armand-1235654298/|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=11 May 2024}}</ref> An [[co-production (media)|international co-production]] of Norway, the Netherlands, Germany and Sweden, the film stars [[Renate Reinsve]], [[Ellen Dorrit Petersen]], [[Endre Hellestveit]], Thea Lambrechts Vaulen, Øystein Røger, and Vera Veljovic.

The film had its world premiere in the [[Un Certain Regard]] section at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival|77th Cannes Film Festival]] on 18 May 2024, and won the [[Caméra d'Or]], for Best First Feature Film. It was theatrically released in Norway on 27 September 2024. It was chosen as the [[List of Norwegian submissions for the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film|Norwegian entry]] for [[Academy Award for Best International Feature Film|Best International Feature Film]] at the [[97th Academy Awards]],<ref>{{Cite web |date=19 September 2024 |title=Armand er norsk Oscar-kandidat |url=https://www.nfi.no/nyheter/armand-er-norsk-oscar-kandidat |access-date=19 September 2024 |website=[[Norwegian Film Institute]] |language=no}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Goodfellow |first=Melanie |date=19 September 2024 |title=Oscars: Norway Selects 'Armand' For Best International Feature Film Race |url=https://deadline.com/2024/09/oscars-norway-armand-for-international-best-feature-film-race-1236094000/ |access-date=19 September 2024 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |language=en-US}}</ref> and made the December shortlist.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lattanzio |first=Ryan |date=17 December 2024 |title=Oscars 2025 Shortlists Unveiled: 'Emilia Pérez' and 'Wicked' Nab the Most Spots |url=https://www.indiewire.com/awards/results/oscars-shortlists-2025-revealed-international-feature-docs-1235076882/ |access-date=23 January 2025 |website=[[IndieWire]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Plot==
After an alleged sexual aggression between two 6-year-old boys, the parents and school staff are called in to clarify the incident. They convene Anders and Sarah, the parents of Jon, and Elisabeth, the single mother of Armand, who allegedly assaulted Jon.

The entirety of the movie takes place during the meeting between the school staff and the parents.

==Production==
''Armand'' is the feature directorial debut of [[Halfdan Ullmann Tøndel]], grandson of Norwegian actress [[Liv Ullmann]] and Swedish filmmaker [[Ingmar Bergman]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2024/05/armand-review-renata-reinsve-shines-in-halfdan-ullmann-tondel-intense-school-drama-cannes-film-festival-1235922228/|title='Armand' Review: Renata Reinsve Shines In Halfdan Ullmann Tøndel's Intense School Drama – Cannes Film Festival|last=Wise|first=Damon|date=18 May 2024|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|access-date=18 May 2024}}</ref> It is a production of Eye Eye Pictures, which was launched in 2022 by the former Oslo Pictures CEO-producer Dyveke Bjørkly Graver and producer Andrea Berentsen Ottmar, both of whom worked on [[Joachim Trier]]'s acclaimed 2021 film ''[[The Worst Person in the World]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Pham|first=Annika|date=18 August 2022|title='The Worst Person in the World' Producers Launch Eye Eye Pictures (EXCLUSIVE)|url=https://variety.com/2022/film/global/andrea-berentsen-ottmar-dyveke-bjorkly-graver-1235344413/|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=11 May 2024}}</ref> ''Armand'' is produced by Berentsen Ottmar, in co-production with Keplerfilm (Netherlands), One Two Films (Germany), Prolaps Produktion (Sweden) and [[Film i Väst]] (Sweden).<ref name="Keslassy" /> Trier and Bjørkly Graver served as the executive producers.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/newsdetail/445623/|title=Halfdan Ullmann Tøndel's debut feature, Armand, in production|last=Abbatescianni|first=Davide|website=[[Cineuropa]]|date=29 June 2023 |access-date=11 May 2024}}</ref>
