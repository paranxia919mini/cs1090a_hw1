'''''I Heard It Through the Grapevine''''' is a 1982 documentary film starring [[James Baldwin]] and directed by [[Dick Fontaine]] and [[Pat Hartley]].<ref>{{cite web|date=October 16, 2023|title=I Heard It Through the Grapevine with James Baldwin|publisher=[[Harvard Film Archive]]|url=https://harvardfilmarchive.org/programs/i-heard-it-through-the-grapevine-with-james-baldwin}}</ref><ref name=N>{{cite web|newspaper=[[The New York Times]]|url=https://www.nytimes.com/1982/03/03/movies/film-revisiting-civil-rights-south.html|authorlink=Vincent Canby|author=Canby, Vincent|title=Film: Revisting Civil Rights South|date=3 March 1982|page=23}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|newspaper=[[The Washington Post]]|title=Well-Spoken 'Grapevine'|date=June 29, 1982|author=Remnick, David|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/1982/06/30/well-spoken-grapevine/f2d158b7-6601-4f2a-9bbe-cc7bfaf78abd/}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bam.org/film/2024/cinema-restored-i-heard-it-through-the-grapevine|title=I Heard It Through the Grapevine|date=August 20, 2024|publisher=[[Brooklyn Academy of Music]]|website=bam.org}}</ref>

==Overview==
Documentary film that features writer James Baldwin revisiting his time in the American South during the [[civil rights movement]].

==Critical reception==
''[[The New Yorker]]'' said in its review, "“Grapevine” is a work of political history about the civil-rights movement—and about the ongoing failure of the United States to make good on the promise of justice and equality for Black Americans."<ref>{{cite web|magazine=[[The New Yorker]]|title=James Baldwin’s Anguished Prescience in "I Heard It Through the Grapevine"|authorlink=Richard Brody|author=Brody, Richard|date=January 16, 2024|url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/the-front-row/james-baldwins-anguished-prescience-in-i-heard-it-through-the-grapevine}}</ref>

''[[The New York Times]]'' wrote, "''I Heard It Through the Grapevine,'' a free-form, feature-length documentary that hovers over its subject like a concerned parent who, not knowing quite what to make of things, expresses concern and sympathy."<ref name=N/>
