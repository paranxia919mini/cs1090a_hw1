'''''Fairyland''''' is a 2023 American [[coming of age|coming-of-age]] [[drama (film and television)|drama film]] written and directed by Andrew Durham (in his feature directorial debut) and produced by [[Sofia Coppola]]. It is based on Alysia Abbott's memoir ''Fairyland: A Memoir of My Father''. The film stars [[Emilia Jones]], [[Scoot McNairy]], [[Cody Fern]], [[Maria Bakalova]], Nessa Dougherty, [[Adam Lambert]], and [[Geena Davis]].

''Fairyland'' premiered on January 20, 2023, at the [[2023 Sundance Film Festival]], and was released on October 10, 2025, by [[Lionsgate Films|Lionsgate]] and [[Willa (company)|Willa]].

==Premise==
Shortly after the death of her mother in a car accident in 1973, Alysia Abbott and her gay father Steve move to San Francisco where he can live openly. Steve's bohemian lifestyle clashes with the expectations of being a parent from both the outside world and Alysia herself, who occasionally wishes for less of the independence her father gives her. Their bonds and duty to each other are tested in painful and sudden ways. Years later, she discovers a world of artists and writers, but also the AIDS crisis when people around her contract the virus to which her father also falls victim.

==Production==
In 2013, [[American Zoetrope]] obtained the screen rights for Alysia Abbott's memoir ''Fairyland: A Memoir of My Father'', to be adapted by Sofia Coppola with Andrew Durham and produced by her with her brother [[Roman Coppola]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2013/film/news/sofia-coppola-heading-to-fairlyland-1200965124/|title=Sofia Coppola Heading to 'Fairlyland'|last=McNary|first=Dave|date=December 16, 2013|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=September 6, 2022|archive-date=September 29, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220929184241/https://variety.com/2013/film/news/sofia-coppola-heading-to-fairlyland-1200965124/|url-status=live}}</ref> Durham later became the film's sole writer and director while Coppola remained involved as a producer.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://i-d.co/article/fairyland-sofia-coppola-movie/|title=Sofia Coppola's next movie is an 80s AIDS drama|last=Greenwood|first=Douglas|date=August 12, 2022|website=[[i-D]]|access-date=September 6, 2022|archive-date=September 3, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220903124606/https://i-d.vice.com/en/article/y3pwnx/fairyland-sofia-coppola-movie|url-status=live}}</ref>

In June 2022, production wrapped with [[Emilia Jones]], [[Scoot McNairy]], [[Geena Davis]], [[Adam Lambert]], [[Cody Fern]], Bella Murphy and Nessa Dougherty starring.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/geena-davis-adam-lambert-fairyland-movie-adaptation-1235159730/|title=Emilia Jones, Scoot McNairy Star in 'Fairyland' Adaptation for American Zoetrope|last=Vlessing|first=Etan|date=June 6, 2022|website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=September 6, 2022|archive-date=October 19, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221019180446/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/geena-davis-adam-lambert-fairyland-movie-adaptation-1235159730/|url-status=live}}</ref> In August 2022, [[Maria Bakalova]] was also revealed to be appearing in the film.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deadline.com/2022/08/maria-bakalova-joins-sofia-coppolas-fairyland-jerry-seinfelds-unfrosted-1235089939/|title=Maria Bakalova Joins Sofia Coppola's 'Fairyland', Jerry Seinfeld's 'Unfrosted'|last=Grobar|first=Matt|date=August 11, 2022|website=[[Deadline Hollywood]]|access-date=September 6, 2022|archive-date=August 24, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220824145104/https://deadline.com/2022/08/maria-bakalova-joins-sofia-coppolas-fairyland-jerry-seinfelds-unfrosted-1235089939/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Release==
It premiered on January 20, 2023, at the [[Sundance Film Festival]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/2022/12/sundance-2023-lineup-1234788461/|title=Sundance 2023 Lineup: New Films from Nicole Holofcener, Brandon Cronenberg, Jonathan Majors, & More|website=[[Indiewire]]|first=Kate|last=Erbland|date=December 7, 2022|access-date=December 7, 2022|archive-date=December 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221207182344/https://www.indiewire.com/2022/12/sundance-2023-lineup-1234788461/|url-status=live}}</ref> In August 2025, [[Lionsgate Films|Lionsgate]] and [[Willa (company)|Willa]] acquired distribution rights to the film, and released it on October 10, 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://variety.com/2025/film/news/fairyland-october-release-lionsgate-willa-1236487546/|title='Fairyland' Gets October Release Date From Lionsgate and Willa (EXCLUSIVE)|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first=Katcy|last=Stephan|date=August 12, 2025|access-date=August 12, 2025}}</ref>

== Reception ==
On [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film has an approval rating of 97% based on 33 reviews, with an average rating of 6.5/10. The critics consensus reads, "Drawing on one family's real-life experiences, the powerfully acted ''Fairyland'' pays poignant tribute to love and resilience in the face of adversity and tragedy."<ref>{{Cite web |title=Fairyland |url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/fairyland |access-date=December 10, 2023 |website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]] |publisher=[[Fandango Media]] |archive-date=February 28, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230228172508/https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/fairyland |url-status=live }}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 68 out of 100, based on 9 reviews indicating "generally favorable reviews".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Fairyland |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/fairyland |access-date=March 4, 2023 |website=Metacritic |archive-date=March 4, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230304054809/https://www.metacritic.com/movie/fairyland |url-status=live }}</ref>
