'''''How to Talk to Girls at Parties''''' is a 2017 [[Science fiction film|science fiction]] [[romantic comedy]] film directed by [[John Cameron Mitchell]] from a screenplay he co-wrote with [[Philippa Goslett]], based on the 2006 short story [[How to Talk to Girls at Parties|of the same name]] by [[Neil Gaiman]]. The film stars [[Elle Fanning]], [[Alex Sharp]], [[Ruth Wilson]], [[Matt Lucas (comedian)|Matt Lucas]] and [[Nicole Kidman]]. [[Principal photography]] began on 9 November 2015 in [[Sheffield]].

The film had its world premiere at the [[2017 Cannes Film Festival|Cannes Film Festival]] on 21 May 2017. It was released in the United Kingdom on 11 May 2018 by [[StudioCanal UK]] and in the United States on 25 May 2018 by [[A24]].

==Plot==
A young punk named Enn and his best friends stumble upon a bizarre gathering of teenagers from another planet, visiting Earth to complete a mysterious rite of passage. Enn falls madly in love with Zan, a beautiful and rebellious alien who becomes fascinated with him. Together, they embark on a delirious adventure through the kinetic punk rock world of 1970s London, inadvertently setting off a series of events that leads to the ultimate showdown between punks and aliens.

==Reception==
On the [[review aggregator]] website [[Rotten Tomatoes]], the film holds an approval rating of 47% based on 97 reviews, with an average rating of 5.3/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "''How to Talk to Girls at Parties'' has energy and ambition, but is ultimately too unfocused to do much with either – or develop its themes into a cohesive whole."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/how_to_talk_to_girls_at_parties|title=''How to Talk to Girls at Parties''|website=[[Rotten Tomatoes]]|access-date=22 May 2025}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 50 out of 100, based on 24 critics, indicating "mixed or average" reviews.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/how-to-talk-to-girls-at-parties |title=''How to Talk to Girls at Parties''|website=[[Metacritic]] |access-date=23 May 2018}}</ref>

David Rooney of ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' stated that despite the charming characters of Elle Fanning (a curious alien) and Nicole Kidman (a nihilistic low priestess), its attempts to "add political substance feels less than half-cooked" and in effect sacrifices "narrative cohesion" and "overcomplicates" Neil Gaiman's 18-page story.<ref>{{cite magazine |last= Rooney |first= David |title= 'How to Talk to Girls at Parties': Film Review {{!}} Cannes 2017 |url= https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/how-to-talk-girls-at-parties-cannes-2017-1005973 |magazine= [[The Hollywood Reporter]] |access-date= 20 June 2017 |date= 21 May 2017 |archive-date= January 29, 2018 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20180129050426/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/how-to-talk-girls-at-parties-cannes-2017-1005973 |url-status= live}}</ref>
[[Owen Gleiberman]] of ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' wrote, "The film enunciates its raw themes—punk means individuality! the aliens are all about conformity!—but never begins to figure out how to embody those themes in a narrative that could lure in the audience."<ref>{{cite magazine |date= 21 May 2017 |last= Gleiberman |first= Owen |author-link= Owen Gleiberman |title= Film Review: 'How to Talk to Girls at Parties' |url= https://variety.com/2017/film/reviews/how-to-talk-to-girls-at-parties-review-1202439042/ |magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |access-date= December 29, 2019 |archive-date= December 29, 2019 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20191229232336/https://variety.com/2017/film/reviews/how-to-talk-to-girls-at-parties-review-1202439042/ |url-status= live}}</ref>
