'''''Wake Up Dead Man'''''<!-- The official title per the poster's billing block does not include the subtitle --> (marketed as '''''Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery''''') is a 2025 American [[mystery film]] written and directed by [[Rian Johnson]]. It is the third film in the [[Knives Out (film series)|''Knives Out'' series]]. The film stars [[Daniel Craig]], who reprises his role as master detective Benoit Blanc<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fleming |first=Mike Jr. |date=March 31, 2021 |title=Netflix Near $400M+ Deal For 'Knives Out' 2 & 3; Daniel Craig & Director Rian Johnson Reprising |url=https://deadline.com/2021/03/knives-out-two-sequels-netflix-400-million-plus-deal-daniel-craig-rian-johnson-ram-bergman-1234724575/ |access-date=March 22, 2023 |website=Deadline |language=en-US |archive-date=May 12, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210512172112/https://deadline.com/2021/03/knives-out-two-sequels-netflix-400-million-plus-deal-daniel-craig-rian-johnson-ram-bergman-1234724575/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="VarietyInsight">{{cite web|url=https://www.varietyinsight.com/production_restart_full_view.php?track_id=189665 | title=Film and TV Projects Going Into Production – Knives Out 3|website=[[Variety Insight]] |access-date=April 2, 2021|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210402045050/https://www.varietyinsight.com/production_restart_full_view.php?track_id=189665 |archive-date=April 2, 2021}}</ref> as he investigates the death of a Catholic priest against the backdrop of a missing inheritance. The [[ensemble cast]] also includes [[Josh O'Connor]], [[Glenn Close]], [[Josh Brolin]], [[Mila Kunis]], [[Jeremy Renner]], [[Kerry Washington]], [[Andrew Scott (actor)|Andrew Scott]], [[Cailee Spaeny]], [[Daryl McCormack]], and [[Thomas Haden Church]].

The film had its world premiere at the [[2025 Toronto International Film Festival|Toronto International Film Festival]] on September 6, 2025, and was released in [[Limited theatrical release|select theaters]] on November 26, 2025, before streaming on [[Netflix]] on December 12, 2025. Like its predecessors, it received positive reviews, with particular praise for the performances of Craig, O'Connor, and Close.

==Plot==
<!-- Per [[WP:FILMPLOT]], plot summaries for feature films should be between 400 and 700 words. Current word count: 693 -->	
After punching a fellow [[deacon]], former boxer-turned-Catholic priest Jud Duplenticy is reassigned to Our Lady of Perpetual Fortitude, an [[upstate New York]] parish led by [[Monsignor]] Jefferson Wicks, the grandson of Reverend Prentice Wicks. Duplenticy learns Prentice had amassed a small fortune before dying and that Jefferson's troubled mother Grace had remained at the church in hopes of receiving Prentice's inheritance. When the fortune went missing, she ransacked the church and destroyed its [[crucifix]].  Jud disdains Wicks' reign over the parish, as his incendiary preaching has driven away all but his most loyal and corrupt parishioners. During a [[Good Friday]] service, Wicks collapses in a closet adjoining the chancel. A knife fashioned from a red devil's-head lamp [[finial]] is found in his back, and he is pronounced dead. As Jud had threatened Wicks and drunkenly stolen an identical finial from a bar the day before, he becomes the main suspect in a [[locked-room mystery]].

Police chief Geraldine Scott summons detective Benoit Blanc to investigate. He recruits Jud to assist him, convinced of his innocence. After Wicks' entombment in Prentice's [[mausoleum]], Jud and Blanc question the congregation. They learn that Wicks claimed to have found Prentice's fortune and planned to use it to pursue a career in politics with Cy Draven, his illegitimate son. Cy revealed Wicks' plans to close the church after publicly shaming and exposing the secrets of his followers. Tired of Blanc's dogged pursuit of justice, Jud quits the investigation. That night, he apparently witnesses Wicks exiting the mausoleum and embracing groundskeeper Samson. Jud is knocked out while pursuing them and wakes up next to Samson's corpse, believing he killed him. 

Blanc stops Jud from turning himself in and takes him to parish member Nat Sharp's house, where they find Nat's corpse dissolving in a tub of acid alongside Wicks' body. The following morning, the police and parishioners convene in the church for Jud's confession, only for Blanc to explain that Nat killed Wicks. An accomplice attached a decoy finial to Wicks' matching red [[vestment]] and spiked Wicks' flask with sedatives. As Wicks routinely imbibed during service, he passed out and was found with fake blood on his back, spilled from the finial by remote control. Nat then feigned examining the body and switched out the decoy to fatally stab Wicks. Before Blanc can continue, he appears to have an [[Epiphany (literature)|epiphany]] and abruptly declares that he cannot explain what happened. The congregation leaves.

Blanc explains that he was inspired by Jud's example to allow the killer to come forward. Martha, the church's longtime secretary, returns to confess: she knew Prentice had converted his fortune into a diamond he called "[[Forbidden fruit|Eve's Apple]]". When he took his own life by swallowing it, Wick's mother Grace had ransacked the church in search of it, attacking Martha when she mocks her. Martha had witnessed Prentice's final deed but kept silent until her last [[Confession (religion)|confession]] with Wicks. Terrified by the realization that Wicks would recover Eve's Apple from Prentice's corpse, she conspired with Nat to murder Wicks before he could act.

After Wicks' death, Martha had Samson entombed in Wicks' place to retrieve and destroy Eve's Apple. After finding it, Samson left the tomb dressed as Wicks, staging a resurrection to preserve Wicks' legacy and the church's reputation. Nat dressed as Samson and waited outside the tomb, but when Jud arrived, Nat killed Samson to keep Eve's Apple for himself. Nat then poisoned Martha's coffee with [[pentobarbital]], only for her to swap their cups unnoticed, killing him. Having taken the pentobarbital to commit suicide, Martha lets Jud [[Last rites|absolve her]] after she asks for forgiveness for her treatment of Grace. As she dies, Martha drops the diamond. 

One year later, Jud has carved a new crucifix and is about to reopen the church, now renamed Our Lady of Perpetual Grace. Most of the parishioners have moved on with their lives, though a disgruntled Cy, convinced Jud is hiding Eve's Apple, says he will closely monitor his finances for any traces of it. Blanc bids farewell to Jud. As new parishioners enter for Jud's first [[Mass in the Catholic Church|Mass]], Eve's Apple is seen concealed within the chest of the new crucifix.

In October 2023, following the resolution of the [[2023 Writers Guild of America strike]], Johnson announced he had started working on the script for the third film. The filmmaker stated he had the premise planned, and could begin writing again.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thewrap.com/fair-play-netflix-t-street-rian-johnson-ram-bergman-chloe-domont-interview/|work=The Wrap|title='Fair Play' on T-Street: How 'The Date Movie From Hell' Was Born – Digital Cover|author=Taylor, Drew|date=October 13, 2023|accessdate=October 21, 2023|archive-date=October 20, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231020012210/https://www.thewrap.com/fair-play-netflix-t-street-rian-johnson-ram-bergman-chloe-domont-interview/|url-status=live}}</ref> He considered the script as "definitely the hardest script I've ever written" and spent over eight months contemplating ideas prior to starting the writing process since he wanted to explore the film's religious themes with nuance "while also not being facile about it, or — God forbid — moralistic or irreverent."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Travis |first=Ben |date=October 20, 2025 |title=Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery is the 'hardest script' Rian Johnson has ever written |url=https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/wake-up-dead-man-rian-johnson-hardest-script-exclusive/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251020163643/https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/wake-up-dead-man-rian-johnson-hardest-script-exclusive/ |archive-date=October 20, 2025 |access-date=June 10, 2026 |website=[[Empire (magazine)|Empire]]}}</ref> Johnson realized that he was writing through the "lens of me, now" and that the film's church attendees are a reflection of his experience growing up in [[Orange County, California]]. He felt Spaeny's character as a disabled cellist allowed him to physically express his pain, while Sharp's character symbolized the "notion of bitterness that you're hanging onto," with Draven's political affiliations were based on Johnson's personal change in his political beliefs.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Canfield |first=David |date=November 21, 2025 |title=Rian Johnson Goes Off on AI as He Looks to His Post-‘Knives Out’ Future |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/rian-johnson-interview-knives-out-wake-up-dead-man-1236429539/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251121144437/https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/rian-johnson-interview-knives-out-wake-up-dead-man-1236429539/ |archive-date=November 21, 2025 |access-date=June 10, 2026 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref> Johnson also opted to frame the murder mystery genre as being "gamified" and the "genre itself that we’re inside," which Jud and Blanc were both enamored by. ==== Influences ====
{{further|Locked-room mystery}}
Johnson said his key influences were works of [[Edgar Allan Poe]] and [[John Dickson Carr]], as well as elements of works of [[Agatha Christie]], especially her novel ''[[And Then There Were None]]'' (1939).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3916877/rian-johnson-wake-up-dead-man/|title=Rian Johnson on the Gothic Horror Mystery Influences Behind 'Wake Up Dead Man'|first=Meagan|last=Navarro|publisher=[[Bloody Disgusting]]|language=en-US|date=November 19, 2025|access-date=January 1, 2026}}</ref> Carr's ''[[The Hollow Man (Carr novel)|The Hollow Man]]'' (1935) is repeatedly referenced within the film.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=McCluskey|first=Megan|title=Breaking Down the Twisty Ending of the New Knives Out Mystery Wake Up Dead Man |url=https://time.com/7339826/wake-up-dead-man-knives-out-ending/|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|language=en-US|date=December 12, 2025|access-date=January 1, 2026}}</ref> The flashback scenes depicting Grace Wicks destroying the church were influenced by [[Francis Ford Coppola]]'s ''[[Bram Stoker's Dracula (1992 film)|Bram Stoker's Dracula]]'' (1992).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.cbr.com/knives-out-rian-johnson-francis-ford-coppola-dracula-influence/|title=Knives Out Director Confirms Francis Ford Coppola's Dracula Influences Key Scene in the Third Film|first1=Monica|last1=Coman|first2=Grae|last2=Drake|website=[[Comic Book Resources|CBR]]|language=en-US|date=November 25, 2025|access-date=January 1, 2026}}</ref> Johnson also drew from his own religious upbringing in [[Evangelicalism]], with the atheist Blanc reflecting his feelings on organized religion. However, he chose to have the film focus on [[Catholicism]] for aesthetic reasons, saying the churches he grew up in "kind of looked like [[Pottery Barn]]s" and would therefore serve as a poor visual basis for the film.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Ellwood |first1=Gregory |title=Rian Johnson's Explains How His Religious Upbringing Inspired 'Wake Up Dead Man'|url=https://theplaylist.net/rian-johnsons-explains-how-his-religious-upbringing-inspired-wake-up-dead-man-20251204/|website=The Playlist|access-date=December 6, 2025 |date=December 4, 2025}}</ref>

==Reception==
===Critical response===
{{RT prose|92|7.9|342|Giving Benoit Blanc a worthy mystery with its genuinely soulful fixation on faith and a scene-stealing Josh O'Connor performance, ''Wake Up Dead Man'' is another ''Knives Out'' puzzle that comes together splendidly.|ref=y|access-date=August 27, 2026}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 80 out of 100, based on 59 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''Wake Up Dead Man'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/wake-up-dead-man-a-knives-out-mystery |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=December 16, 2025 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

Damon Wise for ''[[Deadline Hollywood|Deadline]]'' wrote that the film "may be the best one yet" in the ''Knives Out'' franchise due to its more introspective nature.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wise |first=Damon |date=September 7, 2025 |title='Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery' Review: Rian Johnson's Third Whodunit With Daniel Craig Is An Intelligent Gothic Delight – Toronto Film Festival |url=https://deadline.com/2025/09/wake-up-dead-man-a-knives-out-mystery-review-rian-johnson-daniel-craig-1236510199/ |access-date=October 9, 2025 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}}</ref> On the German arthouse portal ''Programmkino.de'', Dieter Oßwald wrote, "this sequel also offers everything a ''Knives''-coup needs: perfectly quirky characters, superb tension, witty twists, star power, and a brilliant Daniel Craig with a screen presence that exudes effortless cool."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fügmann |first=Lina |date=October 14, 2025 |title=Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery |url=https://www.programmkino.de/filmkritiken/wake-up-dead-man-a-knives-out-mystery-2/ |access-date=October 19, 2025 |website=Programmkino |language=de |quote=diese Fortsetzung bietet alles, was ein "Knives"-Coup braucht. Vollendet schräge Figuren. Superbe Spannung. Witzige Wendungen. Star-Auftrieb. Sowie einen grandiosen Daniel Craig mit leinwandpräsenter Lässigkeit. }}</ref> Alissa Wilkinson of ''[[The New York Times]]'' wrote that what makes the film work is that "Father Jud and Benoit Blanc are two peas in a pod, when it comes right down to brass tacks."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wilkinson |first=Alissa |title='Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery' Review: Forgive Them, Father |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/26/movies/wake-up-dead-man-a-knives-out-mystery-review.html |url-access=subscription |website=[[The New York Times]]}}</ref> 

Some reviewers responded to the movie's use of Catholicism with enthusiasm,<ref>{{Cite web |author1=Mick Joest |date=December 22, 2025 |title=Wake Up Dead Man Is Great, But As Someone Raised Catholic, I Really Loved Two Things |url=https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/wake-up-dead-man-is-great-as-someone-raised-catholic-i-really-loved-two-things |access-date=December 29, 2025 |website=Cinemablend |language=en}}</ref> while others found aspects unbelievable.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bianco |first=Megan |date=December 25, 2025 |title=Movie Review: 'Wake Up, Dead Man' plays fast and loose with Catholicism |url=https://timesofsandiego.com/arts/2025/12/25/movie-review-wake-up-dead-man-catholicism/ |access-date=December 29, 2025 |website=Times of San Diego |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Future==
In September 2022, Johnson confirmed his intention to make more films in the series.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Rubin |first1=Rebecca |last2=Sharf |first2=Zack |date=September 10, 2022 |title=Daniel Craig and Rian Johnson Tease More ''Knives Out'' After Twisty, Fun ''Glass Onion'' Slays Toronto Film Festival |url=https://variety.com/2022/film/news/knives-out-2-premiere-tiff-glass-onion-1235362984/ |access-date=September 10, 2022 |website=Variety |language=en-US |archive-date=September 11, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220911152431/https://variety.com/2022/film/news/knives-out-2-premiere-tiff-glass-onion-1235362984/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Later that month, Craig and Johnson separately said they would continue making further films, as long as they were both involved.<ref name="Future_MW">{{Cite web |last=Dick |first=Jeremy |date=September 11, 2022 |title=Daniel Craig and Rian Johnson Don't Want to Ever Stop Making Knives Out Movies |url=https://movieweb.com/daniel-craig-rian-johnson-knives-out-movies-forever/ |access-date=September 11, 2022 |website=MovieWeb |language=en-US |archive-date=September 12, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220912232321/https://movieweb.com/daniel-craig-rian-johnson-knives-out-movies-forever/ |url-status=live}}</ref> In October 2025, Johnson stated that he had nothing planned for a fourth film in the series, though he did express a willingness to make another film as long as he and Craig have fun doing them and audiences enjoy them. He said he finds them challenging to make, especially in terms of developing new plots.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ford |first=Lily |date=October 8, 2025 |title='Knives Out' Creator Rian Johnson Opens London Film Festival in Spooky Style With 'Wake Up Dead Man' — His 'Most Personal' Murder Mystery Yet |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/knives-out-wake-up-dead-man-rian-johnson-bfi-lff-craig-1236395711/ |access-date=October 9, 2025 |website=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref>

It was announced in November 2025 that Johnson and Craig were "starting to formulate ideas" for a potential fourth ''Knives Out'' movie.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Carras |first=Christi |date=November 18, 2025 |title=Will There Be More 'Knives Out' Movies? Rian Johnson and Daniel Craig Are "Already Starting to Formulate" Ideas |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/will-be-more-knives-out-movies-rian-johnson-daniel-craig-1236429890/ |access-date=November 18, 2025 |website=The Hollywood Reporter |language=en-US}}</ref> In January 2026, Johnson said that he had "some basic, elemental, conceptual ideas".<ref>{{Cite news |title='Knives Out 4' Update: Director Rian Johnson Breaks Silence on Netflix Sequel | last1=Griffin | first1=Griff | date=January 9, 2026 |url=https://www.newsweek.com/entertainment/movies/knives-out-4-update-11329644 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260111060353/https://www.newsweek.com/entertainment/movies/knives-out-4-update-11329644 |archive-date=January 11, 2026 |access-date=January 15, 2026 |work=Newsweek |language=en}}</ref>
