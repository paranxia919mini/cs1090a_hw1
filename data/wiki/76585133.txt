'''''The Girl with the Needle''''' ({{Langx|da|'''Pigen med nålen'''|links=yes}}) is a 2024 [[Gothic film|Gothic]] [[Historical drama|historical]] [[psychological horror]]<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tizard |first=Will |date=25 November 2024 |title=Camerimage Winner 'The Girl With the Needle' Honors Classic Psychological Horror, Cinematographer Says |url=https://variety.com/2024/artisans/global/camerimage-the-girl-with-the-needle-1236222387/ |access-date=15 January 2026 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Wheatley |first=Catherine |date=8 January 2025 |title=The Girl with the Needle: Magnus von Horn's grand guignol nightmare of infanticide |url=https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/reviews/girl-with-needle-magnus-von-horns-grand-guignol-nightmare-infanticide |access-date=15 January 2026 |website=[[Sight and Sound]] |publisher=[[British Film Institute|BFI]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Durand |first=Manon |date=15 May 2024 |title=Portrait of The Girl with the Needle, by Magnus Von Horn |url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2024/portrait-of-the-girl-with-the-needle-by-magnus-von-horn/ |access-date=15 January 2026 |publisher=[[Festival de Cannes]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Vorel |first=Jim |date=3 December 2024 |title=The Girl with the Needle Pierces the Horror and Responsibility of Motherhood |url=https://www.pastemagazine.com/movies/horror-movies/the-girl-with-the-needle-review-historical-horror-cinematography-abortion-serial-killer |access-date=15 January 2026 |website=[[Paste (magazine)|Paste Magazine]]}}</ref> film directed by [[Magnus von Horn]], from a screenplay written by von Horn and Line Langebek. Set in 1919, the film stars [[Vic Carmen Sonne]] as a young woman who begins working as a [[wet nurse]] at a secretive adoption agency for disadvantaged mothers, but grows suspicious over the woman who runs the operation. It is very loosely based on the true story of Danish serial killer [[Dagmar Overbye]].

The film was selected to compete for the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival|77th Cannes Film Festival]], where it premiered on 15 May 2024 to critical acclaim. It was named one of the top 5 international films of 2024 by the [[National Board of Review]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 Archives |url=https://nationalboardofreview.org/award-years/2024/ |access-date=4 December 2024 |website=National Board of Review |language=en-US}}</ref> It was nominated for [[Golden Globe Award for Best Foreign Language Film|Best Foreign Language Film]] at the [[82nd Golden Globe Awards]] and for [[Academy Award for Best International Feature Film|Best International Feature Film]] at the [[97th Academy Awards]].

==Plot==
[[Copenhagen]], 1919: Karoline is unable to apply for widow's compensation because her husband Peter, who has not written back to her since [[World War I|being deployed]], has not been officially declared dead. She is forced to move into squalid conditions, working as a seamstress with her friend Frida at a local factory. Peter, who has been [[Facial trauma|brutally mutilated]] in battle, returns after Karoline has begun a romantic relationship with her boss Jørgen and fallen pregnant. Karoline, disgusted by Peter and optimistic about a life of better conditions with Jørgen, rejects him, but Jørgen's mother forbids the two to be married.

Alone and without prospects, Karoline attempts a shoddy [[abortion]] with a large needle at the local [[Public bathing|baths]] but is stopped by a woman called Dagmar and 7-year-old Erena. Dagmar treats Karoline's wounds and instructs her to bring the baby to her sweets shop, where she secretly runs a business finding homes for unwanted babies. Karoline soon discovers Peter working at a [[Freak show|local circus]], and the two begin living together again. Meanwhile, Peter is besieged with violent [[Post-traumatic stress disorder|psychotic episodes]].

Karoline gives birth to a daughter. While Peter wants to keep the baby, sharing he can [[Impotence|no longer have any of his own]], Karoline takes her to Dagmar's sweets shop. Unable to pay Dagmar's fee, Karoline offers herself up as a [[wet nurse]] and begins living at the shop. Karoline settles into her new life, befriending Erena, who she occasionally breastfeeds, and bonding with Dagmar, who seems to share similar feelings of loneliness and guilt. The two women begin recreationally abusing [[diethyl ether|ether]] together.

Dagmar accepts an abandoned baby boy, who she allows Karoline to care for until he can be found a home. As Karoline enjoys her time with him, Frida remains suspicious of Dagmar's operations. One evening, Erena asks to breastfeed. When Karoline rejects her, committed to breastfeeding the baby boy, Erena attempts to smother him, narrowly stopped by Karoline. The next day, Dagmar takes the baby away, claiming to have found him a family. Concerned this is in retaliation for her treatment of Erena, Karoline follows Dagmar and is horrified to witness her [[Strangling|throttling]] the baby to death before dumping his body in a sewer drain. At the sweets shop, Karoline confronts Dagmar, who admits to killing all the babies she has collected, including Karoline's daughter, claiming she does not want to bring up something so innocent in a cruel world. Dagmar begins heavily dosing Karoline with ether.

Frida, having learned of Dagmar's services from Karoline, attends a friend dropping her unwanted baby off at the shop but grows ever more uneasy when she glimpses a disoriented Karoline. Dagmar tries to convince Karoline to kill the baby and the two brawl, accidentally [[Asphyxia|smothering]] the baby to death. Later, the woman returns for her baby, regretting giving it up and threatening to call the police if it isn't returned. As Dagmar panics, Karoline [[Suicide by jumping from height|jumps out of a window]].

Police apprehend Dagmar and send Erena to an orphanage. Karoline, having survived her fall, goes to the traveling circus that Peter now lives with. The two reconcile and Karoline is [[Drug withdrawal|weaned off]] of ether with Peter's support. She lies about the fate of her child. Later, Dagmar is tried in court for her crimes, where it is revealed that doctors believe it unlikely that she is young enough to be Erena's biological mother. Dagmar protects Karoline from association and defends her decision to spare the unwanted babies' lives of poverty. Sometime later, Karoline adopts Erena from the orphanage.

==Production==
''The Girl with the Needle'' is [[Magnus von Horn]]'s third feature after ''[[The Here After]]'' (2015) and ''[[Sweat (2020 film)|Sweat]]'' (2020). He wrote the screenplay with Line Langebek.<ref name="Vourlias">{{cite magazine|last=Vourlias|first=Christopher|date=10 May 2024|title=Magnus von Horn's Cannes Contender 'The Girl With the Needle' Unveils First Clip (Exclusive)|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/global/cannes-magnus-von-horn-the-girl-with-the-needle-first-clip-1235995590/|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=12 May 2024}}</ref> It is very loosely based on the true story of Danish serial killer [[Dagmar Overbye]], who manipulated impoverished mothers into leaving their unwanted children in her care, as adoptees, and subsequently murdered them. She was first sentenced to death in 1921, but it was later changed into a lifetime in prison. Overbye remains Denmark's most prolific serial killer.<ref name="Pham">{{cite magazine|last=Pham|first=Annika|date=18 January 2024|title=The Match Factory Snaps Up 'Sweat' Director Magnus von Horn's First Genre Pic, 'The Girl With the Needle' (Exclusive)|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/global/the-match-factory-magnus-von-horn-1235875447/|magazine=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|access-date=11 April 2024|archive-date=1 March 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240301142417/https://variety.com/2024/film/global/the-match-factory-magnus-von-horn-1235875447/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dr.dk/historie/danmarkshistorien/vidste-du-denne-kvinde-er-danmarkshistoriens-stoerste-seriemorder|title=Vidste du… at denne kvinde er danmarkshistoriens største seriemorder?|last=Lerche Kristiansen|first=Annegerd|date=15 October 2017|publisher=[[DR (broadcaster)|DR]]|language=da|access-date=11 April 2024|archive-date=26 May 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230526094755/https://www.dr.dk/historie/danmarkshistorien/vidste-du-denne-kvinde-er-danmarkshistoriens-stoerste-seriemorder|url-status=live}}</ref> Von Horn described the film as a "fairy tale for grownups" and was motivated "not to just make a straight genre piece [but] to flirt with genre. That became a great lens to look at the story, for me and the cinematographer and the set designers and the costumes."<ref name="Vourlias" /> The film was produced by Malene Blenkov for Nordisk Film Denmark and Mariusz Wlodarski for Lava Films (Poland), in co-production with Nordisk Film Sweden.<ref name="Pham" />

==Release==
[[File:The Girl with the Needle premiere night at 2024 Cannes Film Festival with Vic Carmen Sonne.jpg|thumb|Standing ovation following the world premiere of ''The Girl with the Needle'' in Cannes]]

''The Girl with the Needle'' was selected to compete for the [[Palme d'Or]] at the [[2024 Cannes Film Festival]], where it had its world premiere on 15 May 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2024/the-screenings-guide-of-the-77th-festival-de-cannes/|title=The Screenings Guide of the 77th Festival de Cannes|date=8 May 2024|website=Festival de Cannes|access-date=8 May 2024|archive-date=8 May 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240508200110/https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2024/the-screenings-guide-of-the-77th-festival-de-cannes/|url-status=live}}</ref> It made its North American premiere in the Special Presentations section at the [[2024 Toronto International Film Festival|49th Toronto International Film Festival]] on 5 September 2024.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.tiff.net/events/the-girl-with-the-needle |title=The Girl with the Needle |website=[[Toronto International Film Festival]] |access-date=19 August 2024}}</ref> The film has also been selected for the [[MAMI Mumbai Film Festival 2024]], where it will be screened under the World Cinema section.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Deb |first=Deepshikha |date=30 September 2024 |title=MAMI Mumbai Film Festival Unveils 2024 Official Lineup |url=https://www.highonfilms.com/mami-mumbai-film-festival-unveils-2024-official-lineup/ |access-date=12 October 2024 |website=High On Films |language=en-US}}</ref>

International sales were handled by The Match Factory, whose parent [[Mubi (streaming service)|Mubi]] acquired distribution rights to the film for North and Latin America, United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Italy, Turkey and India a few days after its premiere.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/462114|title=The Match Factory pins its hopes on four competition titles at Cannes|last=Abbatescianni|first=Davide|date=10 May 2024|website=[[Cineuropa]]|access-date=13 May 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Mubi Acquires Magnus von Horn's Chilling Cannes Competition Entry 'The Girl With the Needle'|website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|first1=Elsa|last1=Keslassy|first2=Alex|last2=Ritman|date=18 May 2024|access-date=19 May 2024|url=https://variety.com/2024/film/global/mubi-buys-the-girl-with-the-needle-1236008750/}}</ref> The film premiered in New York and Los Angeles on 6 December 2024.<ref name="DFI">{{Cite web |date=19 September 2024 |title='Pigen med nålen' er Danmarks Oscar-kandidat |url=https://www.dfi.dk/nyheder/pigen-med-naalen-er-danmarks-oscar-kandidat |access-date=29 September 2024 |website=[[Danish Film Institute]] |language=da}}</ref> Gutek Film is set to distribute the film in Poland on 17 January 2025, under the title ''Dziewczyna z igłą''.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Dziewczyna z igłą |url=https://gutekfilm.pl/dziewczyna-z-igla-von-horn |access-date=15 November 2024 |publisher=Gutek Film |language=pl}}</ref> [[Nordisk Film|Nordisk Film Distribution]] is set to release the film in Denmark on 23 January 2025.<ref name="DFI" />

The film was supposed to be featured at the [[8th Malaysia International Film Festival#MIFFest x EUFF (Official Selection|25th European Film Festival]] held in conjunction with [[8th Malaysia International Film Festival]] in July 2025,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.miffest.com.my/film-lineup-2025/# |title=Film Lineup 2025  |date=12 June 2025|access-date=27 June 2025|website= [[Malaysia International Film Festival|MIFFest]] |language=en}}</ref> but the screening was cancelled a week prior due to 'unforeseen circumstances'.<ref>{{cite web | title=Instagram | url=https://www.instagram.com/p/DMUtTS1PIax/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link }}</ref>

==Reception==

===Critical response===
{{Rotten Tomatoes prose|92|3=111|4=Threading its grim tale through sumptuous [[black-and-white photography]], ''The Girl with the Needle'' is a haunting parable made with superb craft.|ref=yes|access-date=19 March 2026}} [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[Weighted arithmetic mean|weighted average]], assigned the film a score of 82 out of 100, based on 22 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".<ref name="Metacritic">{{cite web |title=''The Girl with the Needle'' |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-girl-with-the-needle |website=[[Metacritic]] |publisher=[[Fandom (website)#Fandom, Inc.|Fandom, Inc.]] |access-date=26 March 2025 |archive-url= |archive-date= |url-status=}}</ref>

The reviews from Cannes described the film as "grippingly acted";<ref>{{Cite news |last=Bradshaw |first=Peter |author-link=Peter Bradshaw |date=16 May 2024 |title=The Girl With the Needle review – horrific drama based on Denmark's 1921 baby-killer case |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/article/2024/may/16/the-girl-with-the-needle-review-magnus-van-horne-dagmar-overbye |access-date=16 May 2024 |newspaper=[[The Guardian]] |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref> a "sombre, commercially uncompromising work";<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lodge |first=Guy |date=15 May 2024 |title='The Girl With the Needle' Review: Magnus von Horn's Expressionistic Nightmare of Women Abandoned by Society |url=https://variety.com/2024/film/reviews/the-girl-with-the-needle-review-1236002246/ |access-date=16 May 2024 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |language=en-US}}</ref> and a "poetic and dark fairy tale".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Utichi |first=Joe |date=15 May 2024 |title='The Girl With The Needle' Review: Magnus Von Horn's Dark Fairytale Retelling Of Denmark's Most Infamous Serial Murder Case Is Beguiling – Cannes Film Festival |url=https://deadline.com/2024/05/the-girl-with-the-needle-review-magnus-von-horn-1235912820/ |access-date=16 May 2024 |website=[[Deadline Hollywood]] |language=en-US}}</ref> One reviewer from [[Film International]] called the film "a bold and expressive nightmare", comparing its opening sequence to that of [[Terrence Malick]]'s ''[[Knight of Cups (film)|Knight of Cups]]'' (2015).<ref>{{cite web|title=Odd Blends, in Earnest: More Selections from TIFF 2024 |url=https://filmint.nu/more-selections-from-toronto-international-film-festival-2024-m-sellers-johnson/|first=M.
